accounts

SSH as a service: accounts runs hosts, places each business account on one of them, and keeps the people who sign in to work there. Agents create hosts and accounts, add users and their keys, mark a user an admin of their account, and lock or delete users with the tools, or invite a person to join an account by a link they enroll at themselves. On a host, with its own tools, every user manages their own keys, and an account's admins invite, lock, unlock, delete, promote and demote its users. Every account's files and databases are backed up with nothing to set up, and agents can close a host, put it on a new machine and restore from its backups.{{if .Configured}} People sign in as <user>@<account>.{{.Domain}}.{{end}}
