Complete claim texts:
1. Retrograde pricing is a wholesale pricing method in which the retail price is fixed first and every upstream margin is derived backward from it.
2. Retrograde pricing was formalized by Hella Braun in 1961.
3. Retrograde pricing is standard practice in Scandinavian book publishing.
4. Critics argue that retrograde pricing hides true unit costs.
5. Retrograde pricing remains widely taught.
