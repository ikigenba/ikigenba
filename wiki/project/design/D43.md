# Decision 43 — The home / reset page (`GET /{$}`, no `q`)

> **Re-rooted by D76/D78.** This page is the private tier's scope home
> (`GET /private/{scope}/`, no `q`); the public tier's read-only home is D78.
> "The base" throughout means the tier+scope base.

**Decision.** The state served at the scope base with no query — and the
target the D80 header question form (present on every page) submits to: its
action resolves to `.`, i.e. this base. It is rendered by the home template filling the D80 shell and
reads **only** the D81 `RecentLister`; it touches no `Asker`, no `PageFinder`,
no identity (nginx already gated the request). The page is otherwise a pure
function of service/version/mount.

This is the page D39 mounts at the root; D39's gating, exact-root,
ungated-in-process, and name+version behaviors (`R-LAND-*`) continue to hold
for it — the name+version lives in the shell's **footer** (D80).

**Content, top to bottom, inside the D80 shell** (header bar — which carries
the question form on this page as on every page — above, footer below; both
are D80's):

```html
<!-- the service name as the page title, display type -->
<h1>{{.Service}}</h1>

<!-- Suggested pages: the scope's seven newest subjects (D81).
     OMITTED ENTIRELY when the scope has no subjects. -->
```

- The page body renders **no question form** — asking happens in the D80
  header form, whose GET `?q=…` submission lands back on this base and routes
  to D44 (private) / D78 search (public). Labels and busy feedback stay
  D79's; the header-form composition and single-affordance rule are D80's
  (R-2O6U-BWGC, R-2QMN-3FXQ).
- The **Suggested pages** section is D81's — the scope's seven most recently
  added subjects, newest first, omitted whole when the scope is empty.

**Empty-wiki fall-through.** With nothing ingested the suggested section is
omitted → home is the bare title inside the shell (the header still carries
the question form). Submitting any question routes to D44, which returns the
honest "nothing here" (D9). No special empty-state handling is needed
anywhere.

**Data dependency.** The home handler calls `RecentLister.Recent(ctx, scope, 7)`
and nothing else. The query's cost/correctness is D81's concern; here it is a
single seam call whose result is rendered or omitted.

**Rejected.**
- **An explicit empty-state message** when the suggested list is empty — the
  omitted-section behavior is unambiguous; no copy needed.
- **The orphan index as the home list** — retired; see D81's rejection of it.
- **A JS-driven search box (XHR/fetch the answer in place).** Plain GET
  navigation keeps `?q=` URLs bookmarkable and the back button working;
  fetching in place would forfeit both for no gain. Rejected on those merits —
  script on the surface is otherwise permitted (D79/D80).
- **A question form in the home body** — the prior shape of this page (a
  large centered form under the title), retired by operator direction: the
  D80 header form on every page is the one ask/search affordance, so the
  body keeps only the title and the suggested list.

**Verification.** This Decision carries no ids of its own: the page is pure
composition, and its proof lives with its owners — the suggested-list content
ids are D81's; the shell, header-form, and single-affordance ids are D80's
(R-2O6U-BWGC, R-2PEQ-PO71, R-2QMN-3FXQ).
