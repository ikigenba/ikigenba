# D01-layout-and-run-seam

campaigns is the funnel's stage eight, compose, and the tracking half of stage nine, send: one Go binary that keeps **campaigns**, each a named record the owner creates with a description, the channels it uses (`mail`, `email` or both) and the destination its links forward a visitor to (doc 1); its **members**, businesses named by their candidate id in candidates, each in a campaign once (doc 3), added from a prospect list, one at a time or several by their ids at once (doc 4; funnel rename decision 6; hand-picked outcome 7) and rejected for good (doc 5); its **pieces**, one per channel per member, each planned, delivered by hand or dropped (doc 6 to doc 8), and each carrying a **token** of its own whose link the tracking page forwards to campaigns' guest route `GET /t/<token>` (doc 11, log D10, log D12); every **visit** to that route, kept whole (doc 13), from which **engagement** is read (doc 14); and its **material**, labelled references to files in artifacts (doc 9). All of it lives in the app's own SQLite database, `state/campaigns.db` (`D04-store`). It emits an event on the bus when a member is added or rejected, when a piece is delivered and when a member's link is first visited (doc 16, log D15, hand-picked outcome 13, `D05-events`), records its trail in telemetry (`D09-trail`), offers sixteen MCP tools at `/mcp` (doc 21, hand-picked outcome 7, `D08-tools`), and serves a landing page, a page per campaign, a tools page, an about screen and the not-found page to a signed-in user (doc 22, `D07-pages`). It sends nothing (doc 19, doc 20). Decisions cited as doc N are /home/mgreenly/Documents/ikigenba-campaigns.md; those cited as log DN are /home/mgreenly/Documents/ikigenba-campaigns-decisions.md; those cited as funnel rename decision N are /home/mgreenly/Documents/ikigenba-funnel-rename.md. Decisions cited as hand-picked outcome N are /home/mgreenly/Documents/ikigenba-campaigns-hand-picked.md; those cited as delivery log PN or delivery log S1-N are /home/mgreenly/Documents/ikigenba-campaigns-hand-picked-decisions.md.

This design is the structural ground the others stand on: where the code lives and what each package is for, the seam through which the process is run so that every part can be tested without a real process, the three settings and how they are read, the path, the addresses campaigns builds, where the identity and the display string come from, the binary's wiring, and the hard rule that campaigns sends nothing. It follows candidates' layout and run seam, less what campaigns has no use for: no outbound HTTP client, no background work but the one sweep, run each hour after the start, that sends the opened event of each member of a live campaign whose event was never sent and which waits on one timer of its own, and no sources, passes or enrichers.

The module is `github.com/ikigenba/ikigenba/campaigns`, rooted at the sub-project directory, with `go.mod` beside `specs/`. Its `main` package sits at `cmd/campaigns`, because `devctl build` finds an app as a `package main` in `cmd/<app>` under the directory that holds `etc/manifest.toml`. `cmd/campaigns` is wiring only.

## The dependencies

campaigns depends on one module besides the standard library: `github.com/ikigenba/ikigenba/appkit`, required at a published release with no `replace` directive. Its `db` package brings the pure-Go SQLite driver, which keeps the release build cgo-free. campaigns uses appkit's `db`, `version`, `services`, `page`, `paging`, `identity`, `mcp`, `telemetry`, `events`, `candidates` and `artifacts`, and restates none of their contracts. The release it requires is one whose `candidates` client exports `ProspectList`, which reads a prospect list's members by its `pl_` id (appkit v0.30.0; log D13, log D18), and whose `artifacts` client exports `Head` and `Show`, which `add_material` calls in that order, `Head` first to check the path and then `Show` for the artifact's id, appkit/artifacts Head and Show (D22, v0.29.0; log D23), so appkit is released with them before campaigns is built against it; which release that is, is data that `AGENTS.md` and `go.mod` hold.

## Where the environment is read

`main` makes the one read of the environment that is not `Run`'s own: it calls appkit's `version.Read` exactly once, first, and holds the result, the **identity**, whose `String()` is the **display string**. It hands the identity to `page.New`, whose banner carries its `Release` and `Commit` to every page's footer and to the about screen, and the display string to the `mcp.ServerConfig` its `Process.MCP` builds and to `Run` as `Process.Version`. Nothing below `main` calls `version.Read`. `Run` reads every variable through `Process.LookupEnv`: `DRAIN_SECONDS`, `CAMPAIGNS_DEFAULT_DESTINATION` and `CAMPAIGNS_LINK_PREFIX` once at start, through `settings.Read`; `LISTEN_PID`, `LISTEN_FDS` and `NOTIFY_SOCKET` for the socket and readiness; and `IKIGENBA_SERVICES` once, when it starts to serve. It reads no `IKIGENBA_SANDBOX`: campaigns fetches nothing from outside the suite, so there is nothing for a sandbox to stand in for, and appkit's `candidates` and `artifacts` clients do not read it either. appkit's constructors also read `IKIGENBA_SERVICES` from the process environment; that read is appkit's.

## Packages

The root package, named `campaigns`, holds what the module embeds: `Assets` (the five templates `about.html`, `campaign.html`, `landing.html`, `notfound.html` and `tools.html`), `Etc` (`manifest.toml` and `nginx.conf`) and `Migrations` (the migrations `D04-store` names). `share/icon.svg`, Tabler's `speakerphone` (log D19), is packed beside `bin/` and `etc/` by `devctl build` and is not embedded, as candidates' icon is not (`D10-on-a-space`).

The internal packages hold the concerns, one each; the document named declares each package's shapes and their behavior:

- `internal/cli`: the run seam, `cli.Process` and `cli.Run` (this document); the command, the exit codes, `cli.Manifest` and `cli.Usage` (`D02-cli`); the serve path (`D03-serve`); `cli.NginxConf` (`D10-on-a-space`).
- `internal/settings`: the three settings (this document).
- `internal/server`: a listener's life (`D03-serve`).
- `internal/web`: the route table, `web.Config` and `web.Handler` (`D03-serve`).
- `internal/store`: every record and every read and write of them: campaigns, members, pieces, tokens, material and visits, their ids, the windows and the migrations (`D04-store`).
- `internal/trail`: the four bus events, `trail.Emits` and the bus's two paths (`D05-events`); the telemetry trail as a whole and its secrecy (`D09-trail`).
- `internal/links`: the guest route `GET /t/<token>`, the visit it records and its answers, `links.Handler` and `links.Config`, and the sweep, `links.StartSweep`, which begins one pass that sends the opened event of every member of a live campaign visited and not yet marked, and which `cli.Run` drives each hour after the start (`D06-links`, `D03-serve`).
- `internal/pages`: the templates' data and the page routes, `pages.Handler`, `pages.Config` and `pages.Description` (`D07-pages`); `pages.ServiceName` (this document).
- `internal/tools`: the sixteen tools, `tools.Register` and `tools.Config`, and their refusal sentences (`D08-tools`).
- `internal/urls`: the addresses campaigns builds that are not its own paths: the auth address and the sign-in address a guest is sent to, and the pages on candidates and artifacts that the campaign page links to (this document).

No package is added for the channels, the tokens or engagement: a channel and a piece's state are store records, a token is minted with its piece in the store's write, and engagement is counted from the visits by the store's reads. The guest route is a package of its own, `internal/links`, rather than a page, because it is outside the identity gate, never draws a template and answers every request it is given in one of two shapes (`D06-links`); the sweep lives beside it because a member's first visit sends the same opened event on the live path, and the two share one marker so neither sends it twice. Which package imports which is guidance, not contract.

Template data types, tool argument and result types, copy constants and the constants a package uses only itself are declared by the document that owns the package's behavior, not here.

## The run seam

The run seam is `cli.Run`, which takes a context and a `cli.Process` carrying everything the program would otherwise take from the `os` package, and returns the exit code. It is candidates' seam less `HTTP` and `After`, with `SweepAfter` added: campaigns reaches candidates and artifacts over the sockets the services file names, through appkit's clients, so a `Run`-level test serves a fake candidates on a Unix socket its services file's `candidates` entry names and a fake artifacts on one its `artifacts` entry names, and campaigns reaches no address of its own choosing; and campaigns' one background work is the sweep, which `Run` drives each hour after it starts to serve, never at the start itself, and which does nothing when every visited member is already marked; `SweepAfter` is its one timer, a `func(d time.Duration) <-chan time.Time` a test fires so that no test waits an hour, nil meaning `time.After`, and the occasions on which `Run` calls it are `D03-serve`'s (hand-picked outcome 15, delivery log P8, delivery log S1-2). `Now` is the clock every time campaigns stamps or compares is read from: a campaign's created, a member's added and rejected, a piece's created, delivered and dropped, a material's added, a visit's time, every event's time. `Sleep` is the pause the writer and the emitter wait with between delivery attempts. `Rand` is the source of every value campaigns mints: request ids, campaign ids, piece ids, tokens and bus event ids. `Inherit`, `Now` and `Rand` are the caller's to give whenever `Args` is empty: `Run` has no default for any of them, and the choice of the process's own descriptor 3, `time.Now` and `crypto/rand.Reader` is made once, at the process boundary, by `cmd/campaigns`. The event bus, telemetry, candidates and artifacts sockets reach `Run` through the services file `IKIGENBA_SERVICES` names (`D03-serve`); the absence of a `candidates` or `artifacts` entry never stops campaigns serving (log D19). There are no live tests.

## Settings

campaigns reads three values at start, in this order: `DRAIN_SECONDS`, the space's; `CAMPAIGNS_DEFAULT_DESTINATION`, where the guest route sends a visitor whose token names nothing (log D5, log D11); and `CAMPAIGNS_LINK_PREFIX`, which a piece's token is appended to to make its link (log D22). The last two are campaigns' own, and its manifest's `[env]` declares each with its default (`D02-cli`). An unset or empty one is its default: 5 seconds, `https://michaelgreenly.dev/` and `https://michaelgreenly.dev/c/?t=`. `DRAIN_SECONDS` is spelled as opsctl writes an integer: one or more ASCII digits, no sign, no leading zero, so `0` is refused, and digits beyond what an `int64` holds read as the largest `int64`. Each of the two addresses must be an absolute `http` or `https` URL with a host, printable ASCII without a space; it is kept as given, so the prefix may end with `=`, as its default does, with `/`, or with anything else a URL may end with, and campaigns appends the token to it unaltered. `settings.Read` refuses the first value in that order that is not acceptable with a `*settings.Error` naming it, quoting its value and saying what was wanted, and `Run` writes its text after `campaigns: ` as a usage error before it looks for its socket. The page size, the token length and the refusal sentences are constants of the documents that use them.

## Paths

Relative to `Process.Dir` (the process's working directory when empty) the database is `state/campaigns.db`, opened with `db.Open`, the root package's `Migrations()` and `Process.Now`; `state/` is created when absent. campaigns writes nothing outside `Dir` and nothing under it but the database.

## The addresses

`internal/urls` holds the addresses campaigns builds that are not its own paths. The **auth address** is the `URL` of the services file's entry `auth`, every trailing `/` removed, or, with no such entry, built from the request's scheme and host with any port and a leading `campaigns.` removed (`urls.Auth`); the banner's profile and sign-out links are built on it (`D07-pages`). The **sign-in address** is the auth address, then `/?return=`, then the request's own address query-escaped (`urls.SignIn`): the pages send a guest there, as sites does (`D07-pages`). A **sibling address** is the `URL` of the services file's entry of a sibling's name, every trailing `/` removed, and empty when the file has no such entry (`urls.Sibling`): a relative address would name a page of campaigns' own. On the candidates address are built a candidate's page, `<candidates>/candidates/<candidate>` (`urls.CandidatePage`), and a prospect list's page, `<candidates>/prospect-lists/<prospect list>` (`urls.ProspectListPage`); on the artifacts address an artifact's page, `<artifacts>/<artifact id>` (`urls.ArtifactPage`); each is empty when the address or the id is empty, as the campaign template's data asks (log D19). The page paths `/campaigns/<id>`, `/tools` and `/about` are spelled by the templates, which build them from the data they receive.

## The hard rule

campaigns sends nothing: no email and no mail, in this version (doc 19), and the hard rule binds every later one (doc 20): campaigns, and anything built on it, may send an email only when a human has explicitly approved that specific send, in production, and never in a sandbox, a smoke test, a check script or any development work, with no flag, setting or environment that lets it happen there. This version therefore has no send path at all: no mail or email client, no SMTP, no call to gmail or to any connector, and delivery is marked by hand (doc 8). The design makes the absence observable at three seams. `cli.Process` has no HTTP client, so campaigns reaches nothing outside the suite. `Run` looks up no variable but the seven this document names, so no setting turns sending on. And among its siblings campaigns calls only candidates and artifacts, each through appkit's client for it, besides the event bus and telemetry every app reaches through appkit's sinks; a `Run`-level test that names every other sibling in the services file and exercises every tool and page proves none of them is reached. The tools are listed in `D08-tools`, and none of them sends; a later change that sends email does so under the hard rule, through the gmail connector, and changes this section first.

## Recorded decisions

- The layout follows candidates' run seam, less the concerns campaigns has no part in (reading sources, passes, enrichment and judging), with `internal/links` added for the guest route, as webhooks keeps its public ingress in a package of its own (log D10, log D22). A sibling's own packages are its internals, so this design names none of them.
- **The settings live in this document, as candidates' do** (candidates' `D01` declares `internal/settings`; its `D02` reads the defaults into the manifest). `D02-cli` compares the manifest's `[env]` with `settings.Defaults()`.
- **The two address settings share one acceptance rule and one want constant, `settings.WantURL`.** The brief allows the link prefix to end with `=` or `/`; any absolute http(s) URL with a host may, so the rule needs no case for it, and the prefix is kept verbatim so the token is appended to exactly what the operator wrote. A value holding a space or a byte outside printable ASCII is refused rather than escaped, because the link is printed in a letter and the destination is a `Location` header (`D06-links`); both must be the bytes the operator wrote. A host is the `Hostname()` of the parsed URL, so `https://:80/` is refused (log D22).
- **`Process` keeps `Sleep`, drops `After` and `HTTP`, and adds `SweepAfter`.** The writer and the emitter retry with `Sleep`, so it is used; campaigns has no poll or fetch, so an HTTP client has no use, and the absent client is part of the hard rule (doc 19, doc 20); its one wait is the hour between sweeps, which has a timer of its own, `SweepAfter`, rather than candidates' general `After`, so that a test fires the sweep's wait and nothing else (delivery log S1-2). The sweep pages through the members without a pause between pages, so it waits on nothing else.
- **The sweep is campaigns' one background work, it is `internal/links`', and it runs each hour after the start, never at the start** (hand-picked outcome 15, delivery log P8, delivery log S1-2). The opened event is sent on the live path at a member's first visit, and members visited before this release, or by the previous release after a rollback, are caught by the next sweep, which is a no-op once caught up. It does not run at the start because the bus moves a subscriber past events it does not yet accept and learns what each service accepts only on its own periodic ask, so an opened event sent at campaigns' start on the day of a deploy would be passed over for candidates whatever the order of the restarts; an hour later the bus has learnt candidates' acceptance (delivery log S1-2). A restart within the hour postpones the sweep, and the stop waits for a running sweep before the emitter is flushed (`D03-serve`). Rejected: a sweep at every start (delivery log P8, superseded by delivery log S1-2), a migration, which cannot reach the bus, and a command of its own, which would contradict `D02-cli`'s rule that a command emits nothing and has no precedent.
- **No `IKIGENBA_SANDBOX`.** candidates reads it only to choose its website fetcher; appkit's `candidates` and `artifacts` clients do not read it, and campaigns fetches nothing, so the variable has no use here and stays out of the key set.
- `Process.Inherit`, `Process.Now` and `Process.Rand` are required whenever `Args` is empty, as in candidates; no requirement states the precondition, because no permitted test can observe a default.
- **No `Process` field names a sibling's address:** the services file names candidates' and artifacts' sockets, as it names the bus's and telemetry's, and their absence leaves the tools `unavailable` and the pages' sibling links empty rather than stopping the start (log D19).
- **`internal/urls` gains the sign-in address**, which candidates has no use for (its manifest admits no guest); campaigns' manifest sets `guests = true` (log D10), so its pages send a guest to sign in as sites' do.
- **Departure: the sign-in address is built on the auth address, not as sites' `urls.SignIn` builds it.** sites' takes no services file and keeps the request's port in the auth host; campaigns' `urls.SignIn` starts from `urls.Auth`, which reads the services file's `auth` entry and, without one, drops the port. Reason: the banner's profile and sign-out links and the guest's sign-in link then name the same auth address in every case, one rule rather than two, and a space's services file, which names auth's real address, governs all three alike.
- **`pages.ServiceName` is declared here, as candidates declares it in its `D01`:** `main`, the banner kit, the MCP server and the manifest all need the name, and `D02-cli`, `D03-serve` and `D09-trail` use it; `pages.Description` stays `D07-pages`'.
- **The hard rule is a section and three requirements** (doc 19, doc 20): the `Process` shape, the key set and the sibling set. That nothing in the source names a send path cannot be proved by a permitted test, which never reads source, so it stands in prose; the sibling-set requirement is the observable form of it.

## Consumer tasks

`main` calls `id := version.Read()` and `v := id.String()` and hands `cli.Run` a `cli.Process` whose `Args`, `LookupEnv`, `Unsetenv`, `Pid`, `Stdout` and `Stderr` are the real process's, whose `Version` is `v`, whose `Banner` is `page.New(pages.ServiceName, id).Banner`, whose `Inherit` takes the process's own descriptor 3, whose `Now` is `time.Now`, whose `Rand` is `crypto/rand.Reader`, and whose `MCP` returns `mcp.NewServer(mcp.ServerConfig{Name: pages.ServiceName, Version: v, Telemetry: w})` over the writer `w` it is given, leaving the rest zero; it cancels the context on `SIGTERM` or `SIGINT` with a cause whose `Error` is the signal's name, and exits with what `Run` returns.

`internal/cli`, serving, reads `s, err := settings.Read(p.LookupEnv)`; opens `d, err := db.Open(ctx, db.Config{Path: filepath.Join(p.Dir, "state", "campaigns.db"), Migrations: campaigns.Migrations(), Now: p.Now, Service: pages.ServiceName, Stderr: p.Stderr})`; builds `st := store.New(d, store.Config{Now: p.Now, Rand: rnd})`; reads `list, _ := services.Read(path)` and, when `e, ok := list.Find("candidates")` finds the entry, builds appkit's `candidates.NewClient(candidates.ClientConfig{Service: e, Telemetry: w})`, nil otherwise, and the same for `artifacts`; hands `s.DefaultDestination` and the emitter to the guest route and its sweep and `s.LinkPrefix` to the tools, and `s.Drain()` to `server.Serve` (`D03-serve`).

A page builds `urls.SignIn(r, servicesPath)` for a guest, `urls.Auth(r, servicesPath) + "/"` and `+ "/logout"` for the banner, and, with `c := urls.Sibling(servicesPath, "candidates")` and `a := urls.Sibling(servicesPath, "artifacts")`, `urls.CandidatePage(c, m.Candidate)`, `urls.ProspectListPage(c, m.ProspectList)` and `urls.ArtifactPage(a, x.Artifact)` (`D07-pages`).

A test of the run seam builds a `cli.Process` with a temporary `Dir`, a `LookupEnv` over a map whose `IKIGENBA_SERVICES` names a services file whose `candidates` entry names a Unix socket on which a fake candidates answers appkit's `candidates` client and records each call with its identity headers, and whose `artifacts` entry names one on which a fake artifacts answers appkit's `artifacts` client's `Head` and `Show`, an `Inherit` returning a listener it bound, a `Now`, a `Sleep`, a `SweepAfter` and a `Rand` it controls, and capturing sinks; calls `cli.Run`, waits for `READY=1`, drives `/mcp` with `mcp.Client`, the pages and `/t/<token>` with raw HTTP, cancels, and inspects `Dir`, the fakes' records and the captures. A test of settings calls `settings.Read` with a lookup over a map and compares with `settings.Defaults()` and `Drain()`.
