# D04-store

Decisions cited as doc N are /home/mgreenly/Documents/ikigenba-campaigns.md; those cited as log DN are /home/mgreenly/Documents/ikigenba-campaigns-decisions.md; those cited as funnel rename decision N are /home/mgreenly/Documents/ikigenba-funnel-rename.md.

The records: `internal/store`, which owns campaigns' five tables in its SQLite database, `state/campaigns.db`, and every read and write of them: `campaigns`, one row per campaign, marked and never removed when deleted (doc 1, doc 2, log D22); `members`, one row per business in a campaign, keyed by the campaign and the candidate id (doc 3, log D22); `pieces`, one row per thing sent to one member by one channel, each with its token (doc 6, doc 7, doc 11); `materials`, one row per artifact a campaign references (doc 9); and `visits`, one row per visit to a piece's link, never removed (doc 13). This document declares the store's whole public surface: its records and the words they are spelled with, every method with its signature, the state rules at its seam, how ids and tokens are minted, the windows and the statements that serve them, the errors every consumer tells apart, the one line every consumer answers when the database cannot be reached, and the migrations. Which business gets which piece is the tools' (`D08-tools`, log D16, log D22); what a visit request carries is `D06-links`'; which bus and trail events a change makes is `D05-events`' and `D09-trail`'s. Opening, migrating, transactions and the failure seam are appkit's `db`; `D01-layout-and-run-seam` resolves the path and declares the root package's `Migrations()`.

## One database, one writer

campaigns is the only writer of its database, and the database is the only record of its campaigns, members, pieces, material and visits. `store.New` builds a store over a handle and opens or creates nothing. What a store holds when its handle is closed is what a store over the next `db.Open` of the same path holds; a test proves the records that way, never by reading tables. A store whose handle is failing (`SetFailing(true)`), or closed, answers every call with an error matching `ErrUnreachable` and changes nothing. `Config.Now` is the clock every stamp is read from, read once per call, so every stamp one call writes is the same second; `Config.Rand` is the source campaign ids, piece ids and tokens are drawn from. `Run` hands in `Process.Now` and `Process.Rand`, and the store has no default for either.

## The records

A `Campaign` is a campaign: its id, `camp_` and 16 lowercase hexadecimal digits (log D19); its name, its description (which may be empty) and its channels, a non-empty set of `mail` and `email` always answered in channel order, mail first (doc 1); the destination its links forward a visitor to; when it was created, in UTC to the second; who created it, the user id and the email; and whether it is deleted. Channels are fixed at creation: an edit changes the name, the description and the destination only (log D21, log D22).

A `Member` has no id of its own: it is the campaign id and the candidate id together (log D22), the candidate id kept as it was given (log D1). It records the prospect list it came from, by its `pl_` id (empty when added one at a time, doc 4, funnel rename decisions 5 and 6), when and by whom it was added, and its rejection: whether rejected, by whom, when and why (doc 5).

A `Piece` is one thing sent to one member by one channel (doc 6): its id, `piece_` and 16 lowercase hexadecimal digits (log D22), its campaign, candidate and channel, its state, `planned`, `delivered` or `dropped` (doc 7), its token, when it was made, when, by whom and how it was delivered, `hand` or `system` (doc 8; this version records only `hand`), and when and by whom it was dropped. A piece is made when its member is added, and its creation time is its member's added time.

A `Material` is one artifact a campaign references (doc 9): the artifact's path, unique within the campaign, the label the owner gave it, the artifact id appkit/artifacts Show (D22, v0.29.0; log D23) answered for the path once Head had found it (log D20, log D23), and when and by whom it was added. Material is answered in the order added; re-adding a path replaces its label and artifact id in place.

A `Visit` is one visit to a piece's link (doc 13): a sequence number that orders visits by when they were recorded, the piece, when, and the user agent, referrer and address the request showed, each as given.

Engagement is read off the visits (doc 14): a `PieceEngagement` is a piece's opens, the count of its visits, with the first and last visit's times; a `MemberEngagement` is whether any of the member's pieces was opened, their opens summed, and the earliest first and latest last; `Counts` is a campaign's members (rejected ones included), its delivered pieces, and the members that opened anything. The window rows put a record beside what is read with it: a `CampaignRow` a campaign and its counts, a `MemberRow` a member with its pieces, each a `PieceRow` with its engagement, and the member's engagement, and a `MergeRow` a piece with whether its member is rejected.

## Ids and tokens

A campaign id is minted by `CreateCampaign` and a piece id and a token by `AddMember`, all from `Config.Rand`. An id is 8 bytes per attempt written as 16 lowercase hexadecimal digits after its prefix, drawn again when the store already holds it, at most 8 attempts; a campaign id is checked against every campaign, deleted ones included, so no id is reused. A token is 16 bytes per attempt, encoded in RFC 4648 base32, lowercase, without padding, 26 characters of `a`-`z` and `2`-`7` (log D12), drawn again when any piece holds it, at most 8 attempts; pieces are never removed, so a token is never reused (doc 11). `AddMember` mints its pieces in channel order, and for each piece its id first and then its token. A `Rand` yielding `0x5a` for every byte gives the campaign id `camp_5a5a5a5a5a5a5a5a`, the piece id `piece_5a5a5a5a5a5a5a5a` and the token `ljnfuws2ljnfuws2ljnfuws2li`.

## Campaigns

`CreateCampaign` makes a campaign every time it is called, so creating twice makes two (doc 23). `EditCampaign` takes a pointer per editable part, nil leaving it, and writes nothing when the edited parts equal the stored ones. `DeleteCampaign` marks the campaign deleted (log D22); a campaign holding a delivered piece is refused, since one that has delivered anything is kept forever (doc 2). From then on every method naming the campaign, its members, its pieces or its tokens answers `ErrNotFound`, and the windows leave it out, with one exception: a second delete answers the deleted campaign and false and changes nothing, since log D22 makes delete "idempotent" and doc 23 and log D22 treat a repeat as a success that changes nothing. `GetCampaign` and `Counts` read one campaign; `ListCampaigns` is one window of the campaigns not deleted, newest first (log D22).

## Members and pieces

`AddMember` adds one member with its pieces in one write. The store does not know businesses: the caller passes the channels that get a piece, which the tools work out from the business candidates answers (log D16, log D22's postal-address rule). A member already present is answered as it stands and nothing is written, whatever channels and prospect list were passed (doc 4, doc 23). `ListMembers` is one window of a campaign's members in member order, added time then candidate id (log D19), each with its pieces and engagement; `GetMember` and `GetPiece` read one.

## State rules

Every method that can change records tells its caller whether it did, so the tools emit a bus event only on a change (doc 23, log D22): `EditCampaign`, `AddMember`, `RejectMember`, `DropPiece`, `DeliverPiece`, `AddMaterial`, `RemoveMaterial` and `DeleteCampaign` answer a `bool` just before the error, true exactly when the call wrote; `DeliverChannel` answers the pieces it delivered, empty when it delivered none; `CreateCampaign` changes records whenever it succeeds.

- A reject of a rejected member, a drop of a dropped piece, a delivery of a delivered piece and an add of a present member change nothing and succeed, answering false (log D22). A repeated reject keeps the first why.
- Delivering a dropped piece, delivering a planned piece of a rejected member and dropping a delivered piece are refused `ErrInvalid` (log D22), as is deleting a campaign that holds a delivered piece.
- A reject leaves the member's pieces as they are: a planned piece stays planned and can no longer be delivered, and `DeliverChannel` and the planned merge list pass it over (doc 5, log D4). A delivered piece never changes again (doc 7).
- `DeliverChannel` delivers every planned piece of one channel whose member is not rejected, by hand, and answers them in member order (doc 8).

## Material

`AddMaterial` appends a reference, or replaces the label and artifact id of the path already held in its place, writing nothing when both are equal; `RemoveMaterial` removes one and leaves an absent path alone (doc 9); `Materials` answers a campaign's material whole, in the order added. The store never calls artifacts and only stores the id: the tool checks the path with appkit/artifacts Head and then reads its id with Show (D22, v0.29.0; log D23), and passes the path and the id Show answered.

## Visits and engagement

`Visit` is the settled seam `D06-links` calls: it looks the token up exactly as given, records the visit stamped from `Config.Now`, and answers the campaign, the piece and the campaign's destination as it stands. A token no piece carries, or one whose campaign is deleted, answers `ErrNotFound` and records nothing; a token records visits whatever its piece's state and whether or not its member is rejected (doc 5, log D22). `ListVisits` is one window of a piece's visits, newest recorded first, for `engagement` (log D22). Every visit is kept (doc 13).

## Windows

Each list is ordered and cut by the database on an index (shape's Storage): campaigns `deleted = 0`, `(created, id) < (?, ?)`, `ORDER BY created DESC, id DESC`, on `campaigns_window`; members `campaign = ?`, `(added, candidate) > (?, ?)`, `ORDER BY added, candidate`, on `members_window`; visits `piece = ?`, `seq < ?`, `ORDER BY seq DESC`, on `visits_window`; and the merge list `campaign = ? AND channel = ?`, `(created, candidate) > (?, ?)`, `ORDER BY created, candidate`, on `pieces_merge`, its member reached by primary key and its scope a filter guarded by a bound value. Each window is two fixed statements, the first window and the window after a position, every value bound, exported so a test can ask SQLite for its plan; the campaigns and members windows each have two more, reversed, for the pages' Prev and Last (shape's Listing). A position is the sort key of the last entry: a campaign's created text (`Created` in RFC 3339 UTC, as stored) and id, a member's added text and candidate, a merge row's piece's created text and candidate, a visit's sequence number; an empty id, candidate or a sequence of 0 or less asks for the first window. The store knows nothing of page tokens; the tools and pages encode and decode positions with appkit `paging`, asking for one more entry than they show. Every list answered is never nil.

## Errors

`ErrNotFound`, `ErrInvalid` and `ErrUnreachable` are the store's three errors. `ErrNotFound` answers an absent, malformed or deleted campaign (but to a second `DeleteCampaign`), a member or piece it does not hold, a piece of a deleted campaign and an unknown token. `ErrInvalid` answers a value a person chose that the store refuses, and a change the records forbid. Every other error matches `ErrUnreachable`, and every consumer answers it with `Unreachable` (a tool's `unavailable`), never quoting the cause: a failing or closed handle, a done context, a random source that fails or keeps colliding, and a write given a value no consumer sends (an empty user id, an empty artifact id, a window of fewer than one entry).

## The migrations

The schema is two migrations. The first, `0001_campaigns.sql`, is the first release's: `campaigns` keyed by id, with its window index on the deleted mark, created and id; `members` keyed by campaign and candidate, without rowid, with its window index on campaign, added and candidate; `pieces` keyed by id, unique on token and on campaign, candidate and channel, with the merge index on campaign, channel, created and candidate; `materials` keyed by a sequence, unique on campaign and path; and `visits` keyed by a sequence, with its window index on piece and sequence. Times are RFC 3339 UTC text to the second, the empty text where none.

The second, `0002_prospect_lists.sql`, is the funnel rename's expand step (funnel rename decisions 5, 6 and 17): it adds `members.prospect_list`, text, not null, empty by default, and fills it from the old column, `pl_` and the same 16 hexadecimal digits for every member that came from a list, empty for one added one at a time. It drops and rewrites nothing else. The old column `members.cohort` (L) stays, not null and without a default, so the previous release still reads and writes it; this release never reads it, and every member it adds writes the empty text there, which the previous release takes as its own value for a member added one at a time. The previous release opens a database carrying `0002_prospect_lists.sql`: appkit's `db.Open` notes a migration it does not carry on its stderr and goes on, and its inserts, which do not name `prospect_list`, take the default. `members` is small (30 rows on `us`), so the fill is instant. The old column goes in the contraction release.

## Known limits

A rollback of campaigns to the previous release, on the `0002_prospect_lists.sql` schema, works, and its losses are accepted (funnel rename decision 17: nothing is lost):

- It reads `members.cohort` (L). The members added before the upgrade keep their old list id there and show it, linked to candidates' old list path, which this release's candidates answers with a redirect to the prospect list's page. A member added after the upgrade holds the empty text there, so the previous release shows it as added one at a time.
- It emits its old attribute for the list on `campaign.member.added`, which this release's candidates still accepts (`D05-events`).
- After a roll forward, `0002_prospect_lists.sql` does not run again, so a member the previous release added in between holds an empty `prospect_list` and answers as added one at a time. Its pieces, tokens and visits are whole.

Nothing else is lost: campaigns, pieces, tokens, links, material and visits are in the same tables in both releases.

## Recorded decisions

- **A store fails only through its handle**: a failing (`SetFailing(true)`) or closed handle is how a test provokes a database failure; both answer `ErrUnreachable`, and `D06-links` relies on the closed case answering an error.
- **Delete is a mark** (log D22): `campaigns.deleted` is set and nothing is removed, so a deleted campaign's members, pieces, visits and material stay, its tokens answer `ErrNotFound`, and its id is never minted again. Every method answers `ErrNotFound` for a deleted campaign but one: log D22 reads "Delete is a soft delete, refused `invalid` once any piece is delivered, idempotent", and doc 23 and log D22 treat a repeat as a success that changes nothing, so a second `DeleteCampaign` answers the campaign with `Deleted` true, false and a nil error.
- **One `ErrInvalid` for every refusal** (log D22; the refusal words `not_found`, `invalid` and `unavailable` are a decision of this design, settled for this delivery): a state the records forbid (a dropped piece delivered, a rejected member's piece delivered, a delivered piece dropped, a campaign with a delivered piece deleted) and a value a person chose that is refused (an empty name, why or label, channels empty, unknown or repeated, a destination not absolute http or https with a host, an unknown channel) both match `ErrInvalid`, and the tools map it to `invalid`. No more specific sentinel is added: the tools refuse a bad value before the store with the exported `Valid` functions, which the store applies by the same rule, so an `ErrInvalid` the store answers to a well-formed call is always a state refusal, and the tool knows which from the method it called.
- **The change is a result, not an inference** (doc 23, log D22): each state-changing method answers whether it wrote, a `bool` before the error, or for `DeliverChannel` the pieces it delivered, so a tool emits `campaign.member.added`, `campaign.member.rejected` or `campaign.piece.delivered` (log D15) exactly when it is told a record changed, and a repeated call emits nothing.
- **The store does not know businesses** (log D16, log D22): `AddMember` takes the channels that get a piece; the postal-address and email rule is the tools'. A channel the campaign does not use is refused `ErrInvalid`.
- **A channel the campaign does not use is refused** by `DeliverChannel` and `MergeList` with `ErrInvalid`, as an unknown channel is (decided for this delivery): the campaign has no pieces of it, and an empty answer would hide a mistaken call.
- **Channels are stored and answered in channel order**, mail then email, whatever order they were given in; a repeated channel is refused (a decision of this design, settled for this delivery; log D21 and log D22 fix them at creation).
- **A drop of a rejected member's planned piece is allowed** (decided for this delivery): log D22 lists the refusals exactly, and this is not among them; the piece was planned, and dropping it changes nothing a reject did not already rule out.
- **A repeated reject keeps the first rejection** (root decision 8, log D22: a repeated reject changes nothing; doc 5: a rejection is not undone): who, when and why are not replaced.
- **A re-added path keeps its place and its added stamp** (the place is a decision of this design, settled for this delivery; keeping the added stamp is this design author's own choice): the label and artifact id are replaced; when and by whom it was added stay the first add's, and a re-add with the same label and id writes nothing.
- Departure: **material is listed whole**, not windowed (shape's Listing pages a list a person can add to): a campaign's material is a handful of templates and files the owner keeps with it, and the `campaign` tool and page show it whole (a decision of this design, settled for this delivery).
- Departure: **`DeliverChannel` answers every piece it delivered**, unwindowed: it answers the whole batch of one call, which delivers a batch of letters together (doc 8), and splitting it would leave part of the batch unreported.
- **A piece's created time is its member's added time**, both stamped from the one `Now` read of `AddMember`, so the merge list's member order is the pieces' own `(created, candidate)` and `pieces_merge` serves it without reading members in order.
- **Newest visit is recorded later** (decided for this delivery): visits are ordered by their sequence, so the order is total and survives a clock that steps back; `First` and `Last` are the least and greatest stamped times.
- **Windows by position, each two fixed statements, the reversed ones mirroring them** (shape's Storage and Listing): SQLite cannot take a disjunction guarded by a bound value as an index bound, so the first window and the window after a position are separate statements; the merge list's scope may stay guarded by its bound value (`? = 1 OR ...`), since it is not an index bound. The members join in the merge list is a `CROSS JOIN` so pieces stay the outer loop. Observed while designing with the `sqlite3` CLI 3.46.1 and through appkit's `db` driver (`Read` on a handle `db.Open` returned, SQLite 3.53.4), over this document's `0001_campaigns.sql` with no `ANALYZE`: `CampaignsAfter` `SEARCH campaigns USING COVERING INDEX campaigns_window (deleted=? AND (created,id)<(?,?))` and `CampaignsFirst` the same with `(deleted=?)`; `CampaignsPreceding` `... (deleted=? AND (created,id)>(?,?))` and `CampaignsLast` `(deleted=?)`; `MembersAfter` `SEARCH members USING COVERING INDEX members_window (campaign=? AND (added,candidate)>(?,?))`, `MembersFirst` and `MembersLast` `(campaign=?)`, `MembersPreceding` `(campaign=? AND (added,candidate)<(?,?))`; `VisitsAfter` `SEARCH visits USING COVERING INDEX visits_window (piece=? AND seq<?)` and `VisitsFirst` `(piece=?)`; `MergeAfter` `SEARCH p USING INDEX pieces_merge (campaign=? AND channel=? AND (created,candidate)>(?,?))` then `SEARCH m USING PRIMARY KEY (campaign=? AND candidate=?)`, and `MergeFirst` the same with `(campaign=? AND channel=?)`; no `SCAN` and no `USE TEMP B-TREE` in any (a statement selecting more columns answers `INDEX` where one selecting the key alone answers `COVERING INDEX`, so the requirements bind the end of the text). An index on `(piece, at, seq)` planned `at<?` alone with a temp b-tree for the order, which is why visits are ordered by sequence on `(piece, seq)`. That a method runs its statement rather than another giving the same rows cannot be observed by use; it is accepted unobserved, and a sandbox check's plans and timings on the real binary cover it.
- **Counts and engagement are read, not kept**: they are computed from the pieces and visits at each read, so no write keeps a total in step.
- A write given a value no consumer sends (an empty user id, an empty artifact id) answers `ErrUnreachable` and changes nothing; `ErrInvalid` is kept for what a person chose.
- **The schema grows by expand then contract** (root `AGENTS.md`): `0001_campaigns.sql` had no earlier release to stay compatible with; `0002_prospect_lists.sql` only adds a column with a default and fills it, so the previous release runs on it both ways. Options for the old column on a new member were the empty text or `coh_` and the new id's 16 digits; the empty text is chosen because the column is unused by this release and the empty text is the previous release's own value, so no `coh_` id is minted afresh. The cost is the first Known limit.
- **The fill maps by prefix with `substr`, never `LIKE 'coh_%'`**, because `_` is a `LIKE` wildcard; candidates maps its own lists' ids the same way in its own database, so the two agree without either reading the other's.
- **Migration tests write the previous release's rows through `DB.Write`** and read them back through the store, as candidates' migration tests do: the store is the only reader a test may use, so what survives an upgrade is proved by what the store answers.
- **The store reads every candidate id and prospect list id as given**: `AddMember` checks only their shape; whether candidates holds them is the tools' question (log D1, log D17).

## Consumer tasks

The links handler (`D06-links`) calls `v, err := cfg.Store.Visit(ctx, strings.ToLower(tok), store.VisitInput{UserAgent: ua, Referrer: ref, Address: addr})` for a token-shaped remainder, answers the campaign's `v.Destination` and records `trail.Visited(ctx, w, v.Campaign, v.Piece)` on a nil error, and answers the default destination on `store.ErrNotFound` and on any other error.

The pages (`D07-pages`) call, for `/`, `rs, err := st.ListCampaigns(ctx, created, id, paging.DefaultLimit+1)`, each `r.Campaign` and `r.Counts` filling one entry, and for a before-token or `last` `st.ListCampaignsReversed(ctx, created, id, paging.DefaultLimit+1)` (with `"", ""` for `last`), reversing the window they draw; for `/campaigns/<id>` `c, err := st.GetCampaign(ctx, id)`, `store.ErrNotFound` answering the not-found page, then `n, err := st.Counts(ctx, id)`, `ms, err := st.Materials(ctx, id)` and `rows, err := st.ListMembers(ctx, id, added, candidate, paging.DefaultLimit+1)` or `st.ListMembersReversed`, each `m` of `rows` giving `m.Member`, `m.Engagement.Opened` and, per channel of `c.Channels`, the `PieceRow` of `m.Pieces` whose `Piece.Channel` matches, with its `Engagement`, or none. Positions are `r.Campaign.Created.UTC().Format(time.RFC3339)` and `r.Campaign.ID`, and `m.Member.Added.UTC().Format(time.RFC3339)` and `m.Member.Candidate`, encoded with `paging.Token`.

The tools (`D08-tools`), `c` the caller, call: `campaigns` `st.ListCampaigns(ctx, created, id, n+1)` after `paging.Token{Tool: "campaigns"}.Decode(page, &created, &id)`; `campaign` `st.GetCampaign`, `st.Counts`, `st.Materials` and `st.ListMembers(ctx, a.Campaign, added, candidate, n+1)`; `create_campaign` `st.CreateCampaign(ctx, store.Campaign{Name: a.Name, Description: a.Description, Channels: a.Channels, Destination: a.Destination, CreatedUserID: c.UserID, CreatedUserEmail: c.Email})` after `store.ValidText`, `store.ValidChannels` and `store.ValidDestination`; `edit_campaign` `k, changed, err := st.EditCampaign(ctx, store.CampaignEdit{ID: a.Campaign, Name: a.Name, Description: a.Description, Destination: a.Destination})`; `delete_campaign` `k, changed, err := st.DeleteCampaign(ctx, a.Campaign)`; `add_prospect_list` and `add_member`, having read the business from candidates and chosen the channels by log D22's rule, `row, added, err := st.AddMember(ctx, store.MemberInput{Campaign: a.Campaign, Candidate: id, ProspectList: list, Channels: chans, UserID: c.UserID, UserEmail: c.Email})`, emitting `campaign.member.added` only when `added`; `reject` `row, changed, err := st.RejectMember(ctx, store.Rejection{Campaign: a.Campaign, Candidate: a.Candidate, Why: a.Why, UserID: c.UserID, UserEmail: c.Email})`, emitting only when `changed`; `drop` `st.DropPiece(ctx, store.PieceAction{Piece: a.Piece, UserID: c.UserID, UserEmail: c.Email})`; `deliver` `p, changed, err := st.DeliverPiece(ctx, store.PieceAction{...})`, emitting `campaign.piece.delivered` from `p` only when `changed`; `deliver_channel` `ps, err := st.DeliverChannel(ctx, store.ChannelDelivery{Campaign: a.Campaign, Channel: a.Channel, UserID: c.UserID, UserEmail: c.Email})`, emitting once per entry of `ps`; `add_material` `st.AddMaterial(ctx, store.Material{Campaign: a.Campaign, Path: a.Path, Label: a.Label, Artifact: md.ID, AddedUserID: c.UserID, AddedUserEmail: c.Email})`, `a.Path` the path the caller gave and `md` the `Metadata` appkit/artifacts Show answered after Head found the path (D22, v0.29.0; log D23); `remove_material` `st.RemoveMaterial(ctx, a.Campaign, a.Path)`; `merge_list` `rows, err := st.MergeList(ctx, a.Campaign, a.Channel, a.All, created, candidate, n+1)`, each row's link the link prefix and `row.Piece.Token`; `engagement` `pr, err := st.GetPiece(ctx, a.Piece)` and `vs, err := st.ListVisits(ctx, a.Piece, seq, n+1)`, or `mr, err := st.GetMember(ctx, a.Campaign, a.Candidate)` and `mr.Engagement`. `store.ErrNotFound` answers `not_found`, `store.ErrInvalid` `invalid`, and every other error `unavailable` with `store.Unreachable`.

A test opens its own database with `db.Open` and the root package's `Migrations()` in a temporary directory, builds `store.New(d, store.Config{Now: clock, Rand: rnd})` with a clock it moves and a reader yielding the bytes it chooses, calls the methods, pages each window, flips `d.SetFailing(true)` or closes `d`, and reopens the path to prove what persists. A test of a window's plan runs `d.Read(ctx, func(tx *sql.Tx) error { rows, err := tx.QueryContext(ctx, "EXPLAIN QUERY PLAN "+store.CampaignsAfter, "x", "y", 26); ... })` and reads each row's `id`, `parent`, `notused` and `detail`; a test of a statement's rows runs it the same way and compares its first column with the window method's answer. A test of the migrations reads `fs.ReadFile(campaigns.Migrations(), "0001_campaigns.sql")` and `"0002_prospect_lists.sql"`. A test of the upgrade builds `old := fstest.MapFS{"0001_campaigns.sql": {Data: b}}` from the first file's bytes, opens a database with `db.Open(ctx, db.Config{Path: path, Migrations: old})`, inserts the previous release's rows with `d.Write`, closes it, opens it again with `campaigns.Migrations()` and reads through `store.New`; a test of the previous release's writes opens with `campaigns.Migrations()` first and inserts the same rows.
