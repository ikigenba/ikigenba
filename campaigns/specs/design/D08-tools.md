# D08-tools

The sixteen MCP tools agents reach campaigns by through the MCP gateway: `internal/tools` (doc 21). An agent lists the campaigns with `campaigns` and reads one with `campaign` (its definition, its counts, its material and one window of its members, each with its pieces and engagement); keeps them with `create_campaign`, `edit_campaign` and `delete_campaign`; adds members from a prospect list with `add_prospect_list`, a chosen list of businesses with `add_members` or one business at a time with `add_member`; rejects a member with `reject`; drops a planned piece with `drop`; marks one piece delivered with `deliver`, or every planned piece of one channel with `deliver_channel`; keeps the campaign's material with `add_material` and `remove_material`; produces the merge list of one channel with `merge_list`; and reads the engagement of a piece or a member with `engagement`. Every signed-in caller sees and may change every campaign (log D19); the caller a tool is handed is used only to record who created a campaign, added a member, rejected one, delivered or dropped a piece and added material, and is forwarded to candidates and artifacts by appkit's clients. No tool sends anything (doc 19, doc 20). What each store method answers is `D04-store`'s; the four bus events and the functions that make them are `D05-events`'; the trail as a whole is `D09-trail`'s; where the tools are mounted and how the run builds their configuration is `D03-serve`'s; the tools page, which lists the MCP server's registered tools, `mcp.Server.Tools()`, in the catalog order `Register` registers them in, is `D07-pages`'. ## The tools

In catalog order, each with the effect appkit's `mcp` registers it with:

- `campaigns` (read) takes optional `page` and `limit` and answers `campaigns`, the live campaigns newest first, each with its id, name, channels, created time and its counts (members, delivered pieces, members that opened anything), then `next` (log D22).
- `campaign` (read) takes `campaign` and optional `page` and `limit` and answers the campaign's definition, its counts, its material whole, one window of its members in member order, each with its rejection, its engagement and its pieces, each piece with its state, its delivery and drop fields and its engagement, then `next`. It carries no token and no link: the merge list carries them.
- `create_campaign` (additive) takes `name`, optional `description`, `channels` and `destination` and answers the campaign it made. Creating twice makes two (doc 23).
- `edit_campaign` (additive) takes `campaign` and optional `name`, `description` and `destination`, and answers the campaign as it stands and whether the call changed it. Channels are fixed at creation (log D21, log D22). An edit that changes nothing writes nothing.
- `delete_campaign` (destructive) takes `campaign` and answers the campaign marked deleted and whether the call deleted it; a second delete answers the same campaign and false (log D22). A campaign holding a delivered piece is refused (doc 2).
- `add_prospect_list` (additive) takes `campaign` and `prospect_list`, reads the prospect list's members from candidates, leaves out the excluded ones (log D17), adds every member not already present (doc 4) with the pieces the rule below makes, and answers the members it added, each with the channels whose piece was not made and why, and how many members were already present and how many were excluded.
- `add_member` (additive) takes `campaign` and `candidate`, reads that business from candidates and adds it with no prospect list and its pieces, answering the member, whether the call added it, and the channels whose piece was not made and why. A member already present is answered as it stands, without reading candidates. A merged candidate is added under the id given (log D1).
- `add_members` (additive) takes `campaign` and `candidates`, a list of candidate ids, and adds each business it names that is not already a member, with no prospect list and its pieces, answering `results`, one per distinct id in the order first given: `added`, with the channels whose piece was not made and why as `add_member` answers them; `member`, for one already present, answered without reading candidates; or `unknown`, for one candidates does not know or that is malformed (hand-picked outcome 7, delivery log P6). An id given twice is one result. It has no size limit of its own, and reads every business it needs before it adds any, so a candidates that does not answer changes nothing.
- `reject` (additive) takes `campaign`, `candidate` and `why` and answers the member and whether the call rejected it; a repeat keeps the first rejection (doc 5, log D22).
- `drop` (additive) takes `piece` and answers the piece's record and whether the call dropped it.
- `deliver` (additive) takes `piece` and answers the piece's record and whether the call delivered it, by hand (doc 8).
- `deliver_channel` (additive) takes `campaign` and `channel` and delivers, by hand, every planned piece of that channel whose member is not rejected, answering the records of the pieces it delivered (doc 8).
- `add_material` (additive) takes `campaign`, `path` and `label`, checks the path in artifacts and reads its id, appkit/artifacts Head and Show (D22, v0.29.0; log D23), and answers the material and whether the call changed it (doc 9).
- `remove_material` (destructive) takes `campaign` and `path` and answers the path and whether the call removed it; the artifact itself is never touched (doc 9).
- `merge_list` (read) takes `campaign`, `channel`, optional `all`, `page` and `limit`, and answers `rows`, one per piece of that channel in member order, each with the piece, its candidate, channel and state, whether its member is rejected, its link and the business as candidates answers it now (doc 10, log D16), then `next`. Without `all` the rows are the planned pieces of members not rejected; with it, every piece of the channel (log D22).
- `engagement` (read) takes either `piece`, or `campaign` and `candidate`, and answers whether anything was opened, how many times, first and last (doc 14); for a piece it also answers one window of its visits, newest first, each with its time, user agent, referrer and address, then `next` (log D22).

## Pieces made when a member is added

A member, whichever of `add_prospect_list`, `add_member` and `add_members` adds it, gets one piece per channel the campaign uses, in channel order, except that it gets no mail piece when the business candidates answered lacks any of `Street`, `City`, `State` and `Zip`, and no email piece when its `Email` is empty (doc 6, log D16, log D22). Each channel the campaign uses whose piece was not made is answered as a skip, with the reason word `no_address` or `no_email`. The business is read once, at the add; the campaign keeps only the candidate id (log D16). A member with no piece is still a member.

## Paging

`campaigns`, `campaign` (its members), `merge_list` and `engagement` (a piece's visits) page as the suite's listing convention asks (shape's Listing and Storage), on the contract of appkit's `paging` package, which this document cites and never restates. Each input declares `page` and `limit` as pointer fields carrying `paging.PageDescription` and `paging.LimitDescription`; each answer is its list then `next`, carrying `paging.NextDescription`, present exactly when at least one entry follows. Each tool's token is `paging.Token{Tool: <its name>}` over the key of the last entry the page answered, in the order `D04-store` windows by: for `campaigns` the campaign's created time as RFC 3339 in UTC and its id; for `campaign` the member's added time so formatted and its candidate id; for `merge_list` the piece's created time so formatted and its candidate id; for `engagement` the visit's sequence number. The tool asks the store for one entry more than the page and sets `next` only when it came back. A token binds no campaign, channel, scope or piece, and one tool refuses another's. Before anything else is checked, a `limit` outside 1 to `paging.MaxLimit` is refused with the text of `paging.Window`'s error, then a `page` the tool's token does not decode with `paging.BadPage`; each refusal is that text alone, and is the same while the database is unreachable.

## Answers

Times are RFC 3339 in UTC to the second, the empty string where there is none (a piece never delivered has an empty `delivered`). Every member of every answer is present, a list `[]` when empty, but `next` absent when nothing follows and `visits` absent from a member's engagement. A piece read by `campaign` carries its engagement (`opens`, `first`, `last`); a piece a change answers (`drop`, `deliver`, `deliver_channel`) carries its record alone, as the store's change returns it. Who did something is answered as a user id (`created_by`, `added_by`, `rejected_by`, `delivered_by`, `dropped_by`) beside its email. A merge list row nests the business under `business`, so a piece's `state` and the business's `state` do not collide. A business candidates no longer knows answers an empty business. An answer is product, given to a signed-in caller: the merge list carries addresses, phones, emails and links, `engagement` carries user agents, referrers and addresses; secrecy governs the trail, the bus, the refusals and the logs (`D09-trail`), not the answer.

## Refusals

Apart from the two paging refusals, a refusal is an MCP error result whose text is `<word>: <sentence>`, as candidates' tools answer. The words are three, `tools.RefusalNotFound` (`not_found`), `tools.RefusalInvalid` (`invalid`) and `tools.RefusalUnavailable` (`unavailable`): candidates' set less `busy` and `stopping`, which campaigns has no use for. Each sentence is a copy constant, `tools.Refuse*`, or `store.Unreachable` when the database cannot be read or written (`D04-store`). A refusal names no value the call, the store or a sibling supplied. In every tool, values the caller chose are checked before ids, and ids are checked for shape before the store is read: a malformed id is `not_found` exactly as an unknown one is. `add_members` refuses `not_found` only for its campaign: a candidate id in its list that is malformed or that candidates does not know is answered `unknown`, never refused.

- `not_found`: a campaign, member or piece the store does not hold, deleted ones included, with `RefuseNotFound`; a candidate candidates does not know (`add_member`), or one malformed, with `RefuseNoCandidate`; a prospect list candidates does not know (`add_prospect_list`), or one malformed, a `coh_` id (L) included, with `RefuseNoProspectList`; an artifact path artifacts' `Head` does not find (`add_material`) with `RefuseNoArtifact`.
- `invalid`: a value the caller chose that is refused (a blank name, why, label or path, channels empty, unknown or repeated, a destination that is not an absolute http or https URL with a host, an unknown channel or one the campaign does not use, an artifact path artifacts' `Head` calls bad, `engagement` given neither or both forms) with `RefuseInvalid`; a change the records forbid (delivering a dropped piece or a rejected member's planned piece, dropping a delivered piece, deleting a campaign holding a delivered piece; log D22) with `RefuseState`.
- `unavailable`: the database failing, with `store.Unreachable`; candidates absent from the services file or answering anything but a result or `ErrNotFound` (`ErrUnavailable`, `ErrStopping`, `*Refusal`, `*HTTPError`, any other error), with `RefuseCandidates`; artifacts absent, `Head` answering anything but a result, `ErrBadPath` or `ErrNotFound`, or `Show` answering any error or a metadata with no id after `Head` passed, with `RefuseArtifacts` (log D23).

## Events

A tool emits on the bus, and records in the trail, only through `D05-events`' functions, with its call's context, so each event is told as the tool's caller with the call's cause, and only once the store has reported a change: `trail.MemberAdded` for each member `add_prospect_list`, `add_member` or `add_members` added, with the prospect list for `add_prospect_list` and empty for the other two; `trail.MemberRejected` for a `reject` that rejected, with the why as reason; and `trail.PieceDelivered` for each piece `deliver` or `deliver_channel` delivered, with `hand`. A call the store reports changed nothing (doc 23, log D22), a refused call, a read and every other tool emit nothing. An `add_prospect_list` or `add_members` refused partway, when the database or the campaign fails between adds, keeps the members it added before and their events; an `add_members` add another call won first is answered `member` and emits nothing, so each member is told once. No tool emits `campaign.member.opened`: a visit does (`D06-links`).

## Descriptions

Every tool's description is a copy constant `tools.Describe<Tool>`, whose first line meets appkit's `mcp` rule for a summary and is the line the gateway's `describe` and the tools page show; it may go on after a blank line with guidance for agents. `Register` registers the tools in catalog order, so `mcp.Server.Tools()`, which the tools page lists (`D07-pages`), answers them in that order; `tools.Catalog()` answers each tool's name with its description in the same order, and `tools.FirstLine` cuts a description to its first line.

## Recorded decisions

- **The sibling clients are appkit's concrete types**, `*candidates.Client` and `*artifacts.Client`, each nil for none, as `web.Config` carries them (`D03-serve`, R-PXE4-EPMC), not interfaces of campaigns' own. A test fakes candidates and artifacts on a Unix socket a services entry names and builds the client over it with `NewClient`, as candidates' tests fake scrapes and prompts; the fake answers `ProspectList`, `Candidate`, `Head` and `Show` as the test chooses, fails on demand, and records each request with its identity headers. Reason: the socket fake proves the identity forwarding and the `sibling.called` records with the same calls, and a nil `*Client` held in an interface would not compare nil, so the absent sibling would need a second rule.
- **`tools.Config` names its fields `Store`, `Telemetry`, `Events`, `Candidates`, `Artifacts` and `LinkPrefix`.** `Telemetry` and `Events` are the names `D05-events`' consumer task uses. `LinkPrefix` is `settings.Settings.LinkPrefix` (`D01-layout-and-run-seam`), passed as a string so the tools hold no setting they do not use; `D03-serve` builds it from `cfg.Settings`.
- **Three refusal words** (a choice the root settled for this delivery): `not_found`, `invalid` and `unavailable`, candidates' set less `busy` and `stopping`; campaigns has no pass to be busy with and refuses nothing while draining. The format is candidates' `<word>: <sentence>`; the paging refusals are appkit's text alone (shape's Listing).
- **More than one sentence per word**, unlike candidates' one: a sibling's `not_found` names which thing was missing (`RefuseNoCandidate`, `RefuseNoProspectList`, `RefuseNoArtifact`), a state refusal (`RefuseState`) is told from a bad value (`RefuseInvalid`), and a sibling that does not answer (`RefuseCandidates`, `RefuseArtifacts`) is told from the database (`store.Unreachable`), so an agent knows whether to fix its call, wait for a sibling or give up. Each is still a constant, so no refusal carries a value.
- **A malformed id is `not_found`** (the store's rule, `D04-store`), checked before the store and before any sibling, so a malformed candidate or prospect list id never reaches candidates.
- **`add_member` reads the member before candidates**: a member already present is answered as it stands without a request to candidates, so a repeat changes nothing and needs no sibling (doc 23). A campaign the store does not hold is refused before candidates is reached, for the same reason.
- **`add_prospect_list` answers the members it added, not every member**: those already present and those excluded are counted, as the brief asks, since the present ones are already in the campaign and the excluded ones are not added (log D17). An excluded member is counted excluded whether or not it is present.
- **A prospect list member whose id is not a candidate id refuses the whole `add_prospect_list` `unavailable` before any add**: the answer is not what candidates promises, and adding the rest would leave a partial list the agent cannot see is partial.
- **A list is read with appkit's `candidates.Client.ProspectList`** (appkit v0.30.0; funnel rename decisions 5 and 6), which takes the `pl_` id; `Members` is no longer called. The refusal mapping is the one the earlier read had: `candidates.ErrNotFound` is `not_found` with `RefuseNoProspectList`, anything else `unavailable` with `RefuseCandidates`. Against the previous release of candidates, which has no `prospect_list` tool, appkit's client falls back to the old tool with the old form of the id; that fallback is appkit's, and campaigns neither states nor tests it.
- **`add_prospect_list` takes only a `pl_` id** (options: also accept the old `coh_` form and map it, or refuse it): an agent reads ids from candidates' tools, which answer only `pl_` ids this release, so the old form is refused `not_found` as any malformed id is, and campaigns keeps no mapping of its own outside the migration.
- **`add_members` adds a chosen list in one call** (hand-picked outcome 7, delivery log P6): the owner picks rows on the screen and adds them to a campaign at once, and one `add_member` call per business would leave an agent reconciling many answers. `add_prospect_list` and `add_member` stay as they are. Its answer is one `tools.MemberResult` per distinct id, with a result word, `added`, `member` or `unknown`, that callers parse, so the three words are contract constants, not copy; an added one carries its skips as `add_member` answers them, and no result carries the member record, which `campaign` reads.
- **`add_members` answers `unknown` rather than refusing** an id that is malformed or that candidates answers `ErrNotFound` for: one bad id in a picked list should not cost the rest, and the result names it. A malformed id never reaches candidates.
- **`add_members` reads the members before candidates**, as `add_member` does: an id already a member is answered `member` without a request to candidates, so a call needing no business answers even with no candidates client, and a repeat changes nothing (doc 23).
- **`add_members` reads every business it needs before it adds any**: a candidates that does not answer, or no candidates client, refuses the whole call `unavailable` with nothing changed, so the agent sends the same list again rather than reconciling a partial add. A store failure during the adds keeps what was added, with its events, as `add_prospect_list` does, and a repeat completes the list, answering the earlier ones `member`.
- **An `add_members` add lost to a concurrent add answers `member`**: the store answers false when another call added the member first (`D04-store`), and that call emitted the event, so each member is told on the bus once.
- **`add_members` checks no exclusion**: like `add_member`, it adds whatever business candidates answers; appkit's `candidates.Business` carries no exclusion, and the owner chose the rows.
- **`add_members` is additive** (hand-picked outcome 16): it is a new tool, registered with `mcp.Additive`, and no other tool's input or answer changes, so a dashboard built on the release before keeps working.
- **The skip reasons are words**, `no_address` and `no_email` (doc 6: the campaign says so when the member is added); they are parsed, so they are contract constants, not copy.
- **Mail needs non-empty `Street`, `City`, `State` and `Zip`; email a non-empty `Email`** (log D22), compared as candidates answers them, without trimming.
- **The merge list nests the business** under `business` (a decision of this design): the brief names a piece `state` and a business `state` on one row. The business is read from candidates per row of the window answered, at the call (log D16); a candidate candidates answers `ErrNotFound` for leaves the business empty, every member present; any other failure refuses the call `unavailable`. With no candidates client the call is refused once the store has answered, whether or not the window holds a row, so an absent sibling is always told.
- **`merge_list`'s scope is a boolean `all`**, absent meaning planned pieces of members not rejected (log D22); a token binds no scope, as shape's Listing binds no filter.
- **`engagement` has exactly two forms** (log D22): `piece` alone, or `campaign` and `candidate` together; anything else is `invalid`, checked after the paging rules and before the store. Only the piece form pages visits; the member form answers no `visits` and no `next`, and its `page` and `limit` are checked and otherwise unused.
- **A change answers the piece record, without engagement**: `drop`, `deliver` and `deliver_channel` answer `tools.PieceRecord`s built from what the store's change methods return, reading nothing after the change, so a committed change is never answered with a refusal and every piece a `deliver_channel` delivered is reported by that call. The store's change methods carry no engagement, so these answers leave it out; an agent reads a piece's opens from `campaign` or `engagement`, whose `tools.Piece` carries the record and its engagement.
- **Constants of one kind are declared in one requirement**: the refusal words, the refusal sentences, the skip reasons, the result words and the descriptions are each one Go `const` block of one kind, so each is one declaration, as the design format's own example declares a module's constant and function together and candidates' tools declare their descriptions and sentences; every type and every function has a requirement of its own.
- **`add_material` stores the path the caller gave**, so `remove_material` with the same path removes it; the id is the one `Show` answered (log D20, log D23). A `Show` that answers a metadata with an empty id is `unavailable`, as the store would refuse an empty id as a value no consumer sends (`D04-store`). The campaign is checked before artifacts is reached.
- **`remove_material` refuses an empty path `invalid`**, as `add_material` does, rather than answering false for a path no material can hold.
- **`delete_campaign` and `remove_material` are destructive** and the other changes additive, as the brief lists them: delete hides a campaign and remove forgets a reference, while every other change adds a record or a stamp.
- Departure: **`campaign` answers its material whole** (shape's Listing pages a list a person can add to): material is a handful of files, as `D04-store` keeps it whole.
- Departure: **`deliver_channel` answers every piece it delivered**, unwindowed, as `D04-store`'s `DeliverChannel` answers them: one call delivers one batch of letters together (doc 8), and a split answer would leave part of it unreported.
- Departure: **`add_members` has no size limit and answers every result whole** (delivery log P6), unwindowed, as `deliver_channel` answers its pieces: a campaign has no size limit, the caller chose the list, and one call adds one batch, which a split answer would leave partly unreported.
- **Descriptions may run past one line**, as artifacts' do: the first line is the summary appkit checks and the tools page shows; agents read the rest in `describe`. Their words are copy and change no requirement.

## Consumer tasks

`web.Handler` (`D03-serve`) calls `tools.Register(cfg.MCP, tools.Config{Store: cfg.Store, Telemetry: cfg.Telemetry, Events: cfg.Events, Candidates: cfg.Candidates, Artifacts: cfg.Artifacts, LinkPrefix: cfg.Settings.LinkPrefix})` once. The tools page (`D07-pages`) lists `cfg.MCP.Tools()`, in the order `Register` registered them, which is catalog order; `tools.Catalog()` and `tools.FirstLine` answer the same names and first lines for any other reader.

A test builds a store over its own database, a writer with `telemetry.New` over a `*telemetry.Capture`, an emitter with `events.New` over an `*events.Capture` with `trail.Emits()`, a `*candidates.Client` with `candidates.NewClient(candidates.ClientConfig{Service: services.Entry{Name: "candidates", Socket: sock}, Telemetry: w})` over a fake candidates on a Unix socket in a short temporary directory, which answers the `prospect_list` tool, called with `{"prospect_list": <id>}`, and the `candidate` tool with the prospect lists and businesses the test chooses, refuses or fails on demand and records each request, and likewise a `*artifacts.Client` over a fake artifacts answering `HEAD` and the `show` tool (or nil clients); an `mcp.Server` from `mcp.NewServer`; calls `tools.Register(srv, cfg)`; serves the server inside `events.Middleware` and `identity.Require` with `httptest`; and calls `mcp.Client.CallTool(ctx, caller, name, args)` with `tools.CreateArgs{...}`, `tools.MemberArgs{...}`, `tools.MembersArgs{...}` and the rest encoded as JSON, as two callers. It decodes a success's `structuredContent` into `tools.CampaignList`, `tools.CampaignRead`, `tools.Campaign`, `tools.CampaignChange`, `tools.ProspectListAdd`, `tools.AddedMember`, `tools.MembersAdd` (each holding `tools.MemberResult`s), `tools.MemberChange`, `tools.PieceChange`, `tools.Delivered` (each holding `tools.PieceRecord`s), `tools.MaterialChange`, `tools.Removed`, `tools.MergeList` or `tools.Engagement`, compares it with what the store's own reads answer, walks `next` until it is absent, compares a refusal's text with `tools.RefusalInvalid + ": " + tools.RefuseInvalid` and the rest, with `paging.BadPage` and with the `Error()` of `paging.Window`'s error, flips `SetFailing(true)` on the handle the store was built over to provoke `store.Unreachable`, and inspects the captures for the three bus events the tools emit, their `Cause` and `Depth` under `events.NewContext` or an `X-Event-Cause` header, and for the absence of `campaign.member.opened`.

An agent adding businesses it picked, today and with this design. Today it calls `add_member` once per business, `tools.MemberArgs{Campaign: k, Candidate: c}`, and decodes each `tools.AddedMember`: `Added` true is an add with its `Skipped`, false a member already present, and a business candidates does not know refuses that call `not_found` with `RefuseNoCandidate`; a candidates that does not answer refuses the calls it reaches, after earlier calls have added theirs. With this design it calls `add_members` once, `tools.MembersArgs{Campaign: k, Candidates: ids}`, `ids` a non-nil `[]string` (`[]string{}` when it picks none, since a nil slice marshals as `null`, which the declared `candidates` array refuses), and decodes one `tools.MembersAdd`: each `tools.MemberResult` names its `Candidate`, and its `Result` is compared with `tools.ResultAdded`, `tools.ResultMember` or `tools.ResultUnknown`, an added one's `Skipped` holding `tools.Skip`s whose `Reason` is `tools.SkipNoAddress` or `tools.SkipNoEmail`. A refusal `unavailable` with `RefuseCandidates` means nothing was added and the same list may be sent again; any other refusal after the adds began keeps the earlier adds, which the repeat answers `member`. The captures hold one `campaign.member.added` per `added` result, with an empty `prospect_list`.
