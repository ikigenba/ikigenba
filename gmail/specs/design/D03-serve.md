# D03-serve

The serve path: the settings gmail reads at start, the services file and the sibling clients built from it, what happens between `cli.Run` being called with no arguments and the process being gone, the trail gmail records of itself, the hourly prune of transfers and drafts past keeping, and the route table its handler keeps. It is gdrive's serve path with drafts beside transfers: the start settles what a dead process left (a transfer still `queued` or `running` was cut off and is finished `failed` with reason `interrupted` before ready), the sweeper prunes transfers and drafts once before ready and then each time a wait of `cli.SweepInterval` on `Process.After` ends, and the stop drains transfers as well as requests. `internal/server` is gdrive's, unchanged.

## Settings

gmail reads eight values at start, in this order: `DRAIN_SECONDS`, the space's, and the seven its manifest's `[env]` declares (`D02-cli`): `TRANSFER_MAX_BYTES`, `TRANSFER_MAX_ACTIVE`, `TRANSFER_MAX_QUEUED`, `TRANSFER_KEEP_DAYS`, `TRANSFER_KEEP_COUNT`, `DRAFT_KEEP_DAYS` and `DRAFT_KEEP_COUNT`. Each is spelled as opsctl writes an integer: one or more ASCII digits, no sign, no leading zero, no unit, no white space; `0` is therefore refused for each. An unset or empty one is its default, the manifest's value: 5 seconds, 52428800 bytes, 2 transfers, 10 transfers, 15 days, 100 transfers, 15 days and 100 drafts. Digits beyond what an `int64` holds read as the largest `int64`. `settings.Read` refuses the first value in that order that is not acceptable with a `*settings.Error` naming it, quoting its value and giving its unit, whose text `Run` writes after `gmail: ` as a usage error before it looks for its socket. How the engine applies the transfer settings is `D06-transfers`'; the keep settings are applied by the prune below. The page size, the 64 KiB body cut and Gmail's 35 MiB upload cap are constants of the documents that use them (decision 18); the sweep interval is `cli.SweepInterval`, one hour.

## The services file

`IKIGENBA_SERVICES` names the services file. `Run` reads it once at start with appkit's `services.Read` and looks up the entries `artifacts` and `connections` with `List.Find`: the entry `artifacts` is the `services.Entry` appkit's `artifacts.NewClient` is built with, and `connections` the one `connections.NewClient` is built with, each with the run's telemetry writer, so every sibling call goes over the entry's socket, forwards the caller in the context and is recorded as `sibling.called`. A client is built only over an entry found, never over a zero entry. With `IKIGENBA_SERVICES` unset, naming no file, or naming one `services.Read` refuses, every entry is missing, and that never refuses the start; a missing entry leaves that client nil, and a call that needs it is refused `unavailable` (`D06-transfers`, `D07-drafts`, `D10-tools`). The `URL` of the entry `connections` is where the connect page a `not_connected` refusal names is built (`urls.Connect(urls.Base(u))`, `D01-layout-and-run-seam`), and the entries `artifacts` and `auth` are read afresh per request by the pages (`D08-pages`). The entries `telemetry` and `events` are appkit's sinks' (`D01-layout-and-run-seam`).

## The terms gmail serves on

gmail serves only on a listening socket it inherits, taken as `sd_listen_fds(3)` documents, and reports readiness with `READY=1` to `NOTIFY_SOCKET` as `sd_notify(3)` documents. It trusts `X-User-Id`, `X-User-Email` and `X-Request-Id` as nginx sets them, and trusts the MCP gateway calling it at its socket to have forwarded them (`D11-on-a-space`). Every request has an id: the one nginx set, or 32 lowercase hexadecimal digits appkit's request middleware draws from `Process.Rand`.

The events app reaches gmail at its socket on `/events` and `/declarations`. gmail emits the transfer and draft events of `D09-trail` and accepts no event, so it mounts appkit's `events.DeliveryHandler` with no handlers, and `events.DeclarationsHandler` over its emitter, built with the declarations the trail owns (`trail.Emits()`, `D09-trail`). These two paths sit outside the identity gate; the fragment answers both 404 at the public name (`D02-cli`).

## The route table

Every request goes through appkit's `telemetry.Middleware`. Then, by the URL path alone, compared by hand so that nothing is cleaned and nothing redirected: exactly `/events` and exactly `/declarations` go to the event bus's two handlers; every other request goes through `identity.Require`. Past the gate: exactly `/mcp` goes, through `events.Middleware`, to the MCP server, on which the thirteen tools of `D10-tools` are registered once when the handler is built; a path beginning `/_appkit/` goes to `page.Static()`; and every other path to the page handler (`D08-pages`), which answers `/`, `/drafts/<id>`, `/transfers/<id>`, `/about`, `/tools` and, for everything else, the not-found page.

The handler is built from a `web.Config` carrying what the routes need: the banner source, the MCP server, the services file's path, the store, the transfer engine, the drafts service, the Gmail client, the writer and the emitter. It carries no connections client: the engine and the drafts service each hold appkit's `connections` client and fetch the caller's Google token through it (`D06-transfers`, `D07-drafts`). The drafts service is built first, because the engine takes it, as its `transfers.Drafts`, to check a push's draft.

## The start

With `Args` empty, `Run` checks the settings, then whether exactly one socket was passed in, with the diagnostics below. It removes the three `LISTEN_*` variables, takes descriptor 3, and opens the database with `db.Open` at `state/gmail.db`; a failure is `gmail: cannot open database state/gmail.db: ` and the reason, exit 1. It builds the store, the writer, the emitter, the sibling clients, the Gmail client, the drafts service and then the engine, and records `service.started`, so that it is the first event of every run. Then it settles what a dead process left (`Engine.Recover`, `D06-transfers`): every transfer still `queued` or `running` is finished `failed` with reason `interrupted`. A settling that fails, the database having failed under it, refuses the start with `gmail: ` and `store.Unreachable`, exit 1. Then it prunes once: every transfer past keeping as `store.PruneTransfers` decides and every draft past keeping as `store.PruneDrafts` decides (`D04-store`), with the run's `Now` and the keep settings; a prune that fails is silent and tried again at the next interval. Then `Run` builds the handler, sends `READY=1`, and serves. An `open` draft is never pruned (decision 17), whatever its age, and does not count toward `DRAFT_KEEP_COUNT`; a finished draft goes only when it is both past `DRAFT_KEEP_DAYS` and beyond its user's newest `DRAFT_KEEP_COUNT` finished drafts, as a transfer goes (root ruling A12).

## The sweep

While serving, each time a channel `Process.After` returned for a wait of `cli.SweepInterval` delivers, `Run` prunes transfers and drafts again with the `Now` of that moment, and waits again. A pruned record is gone from the tools and the pages; the artifact, message or draft it made is untouched.

## Stopping

When `ctx` is done (in the binary, on `SIGTERM` or `SIGINT`, the signal's name being the cause) `Run` stops the sweep and calls `Engine.Drain` (`D06-transfers`) with a context that ends at the drain deadline, `DRAIN_SECONDS` after the signal: from then on a `pull` or `push` is refused `stopping` (`D10-tools`); every transfer still `queued` is finished `failed` with reason `interrupted` at once; and every transfer `running` runs on until the deadline and is then cancelled and finished `failed` with reason `interrupted`, the artifacts `Put` in flight leaving nothing and the draft upload in flight replacing nothing (decision 9). At the same time it drains the server for `DRAIN_SECONDS`, waits within the same window for the emitter to deliver what it holds, shuts the writer down with the cause's text as `service.stopping`'s reason, shuts the emitter down, and closes the database. From the drain deadline on, `Run` hands neither sink anything: an event recorded after it reaches standard error as appkit's `lost event` and `undelivered event` lines. A transfer abandoned never makes the exit non-zero; a drain that runs out on requests is `gmail: stopped with <n> request(s) unfinished`, exit 1. A `send` or `discard` in progress at the signal is a request, drained as every request is.

## Recorded decisions

- Settings and the services file are declared here, not in the layout document as gdrive declares them, following the root's document plan; the reading rules are gdrive's, extended with the two draft settings, whose count unit is `drafts`.
- Transfers and drafts are pruned by the start and the sweep calling the store's two prunes directly with the run's `Now` and the keep settings; gdrive routes its prune through the engine, which in gmail owns transfers only, and the drafts' prune has no engine to belong to. The engine keeps `Recover` and `Drain`, which need its queue.
- The settling runs before the first prune, so a transfer the dead process left and that is also past keeping is first finished and then removed in the same start; both finish before `READY=1`.
- A failed settling refuses the start; a failed prune does not: a record past keeping that survives one more hour is harmless. No `Process` hook fails the database after the settling, so the failed prune's silence is not separately required.
- Events at the deadline follow gdrive's cutoff: nothing is handed to a sink once the deadline has passed.
- The drain deadline is `DRAIN_SECONDS` on real time, never `Process.After`, so a drain test runs with `DRAIN_SECONDS=1`.
- `web.Config` carries no clock or random source: the store holds `Process.Now` and `Process.Rand`, and the engine holds `Process.Now`. It carries the services file's path, which the pages read afresh per request.
- `web.Config` names `tools.Config`'s four fields `Transfers`, `Drafts`, `Gmail` and `Store`, which `D10-tools` declares with those types; the tools fetch the caller's token through the engine and the drafts service, never a client of their own.
- No `Process` hook fails the database `Run` opens; a failing store is proved through `web.Handler` over a store whose handle the test fails, by the documents that own each route.

## Consumer tasks

`Run`, serving, calls `s, err := settings.Read(p.LookupEnv)`, checks the socket, unsets the three `LISTEN_*` variables and takes `ln, _ := p.Inherit(3)`; calls `d, err := db.Open(ctx, db.Config{Path: filepath.Join(p.Dir, "state", "gmail.db"), Migrations: app.Migrations(), Now: p.Now, Service: pages.ServiceName, Stderr: p.Stderr})`; builds `st := store.New(d, store.Config{Now: p.Now, Rand: rand})`, the writer `w`, the emitter `em` with `trail.Emits()`, reads `list, _ := services.Read(path)` and builds `artifacts.NewClient` and `connections.NewClient` over the entries `list.Find` finds, `gc := gmail.NewClient(gmail.Config{HTTP: hc, Telemetry: w})` (`D05-gmail`), `dr := drafts.New(drafts.Config{Store: st, Gmail: gc, Connections: cc, Telemetry: w, Events: em, ConnectURL: cu})` (`D07-drafts`) and then `eng := transfers.New(transfers.Config{Store: st, Gmail: gc, Artifacts: ac, Connections: cc, Drafts: dr, Telemetry: w, Events: em, ConnectURL: cu, MaxBytes: s.TransferMaxBytes, MaxActive: s.TransferMaxActive, MaxQueued: s.TransferMaxQueued, Now: p.Now})` (`D06-transfers`), `cu` being `urls.Connect(urls.Base(u))` for the `URL` `u` of the entry `connections`; calls `w.Ready()`; calls `eng.Recover(bg)`, `st.PruneTransfers(bg, p.Now(), s.TransferKeepDays, s.TransferKeepCount)` and `st.PruneDrafts(bg, p.Now(), s.DraftKeepDays, s.DraftKeepCount)`; builds `h := web.Handler(web.Config{Banner: p.Banner, MCP: p.MCP(w), ServicesPath: path, Store: st, Transfers: eng, Drafts: dr, Gmail: gc, Telemetry: w, Events: em})`; sends `READY=1`; and calls `server.Serve(ctx, ln, h, s.Drain(), stop)`, `eng.Drain(drainCtx)` beginning once `ctx` is done. A test of settings calls `settings.Read` with a lookup over a map and compares with `settings.Defaults()` and `Drain()`. A test of the start seeds a temporary `Dir` through its own `db.Open` and a store whose clock it sets back, with transfers left `running` and `queued`, finished transfers and ended drafts past keeping and an old `open` draft; drives `cli.Run` with a listener, a `NOTIFY_SOCKET` it binds, a `Now`, an `After` and a `Rand` it controls, an `HTTP` whose transport is a fake Gmail, a services file naming loopback fakes, and captures; waits for `READY=1`; asks `GET /drafts/<id>` and `GET /transfers/<id>` with the identity headers; delivers on the channel `After` returned; cancels; and reopens the database. A test of the handler builds `web.Handler` over a store on its own database, captures, a server from `mcp.NewServer`, an engine, drafts service and Gmail client pointed at fakes, serves it with `httptest`, and sends raw requests with the identity headers set or left out.
