# D07-tools

The twenty-three MCP tools agents keep ledger's books by, through the MCP gateway: `internal/tools`. They are ledger's only API (seam 3). An agent reads and sets the book with `book` and `set_book`; reads the chart with `accounts` and one account with `account`, and keeps it with `open_account` and `close_account`; posts with `post`, reads one transaction with `transaction`, edits its words with `annotate`, corrects its money with `reverse` and reads every version of its words with `history`; reconciles with `assert_balance`, `retract_assertion` and `check`; attaches notes with `add_note` and `retract_note` and documents with `add_document` and `retract_document`; and reads the reports with `journal`, `register`, `balances`, `balance_sheet` and `income_statement`. There is one set of books, so no tool takes a book. Every signed-in caller reads and writes everything; the caller a tool is handed is used only to record who made a record, by user id, and is carried to artifacts by appkit's client. What each store method answers, and every rule about what a record may hold, how it changes, what "today" is and in what order a list comes, is `D04-store`'s; the bus event and its functions are `D05-events`'; the trail's other names and functions, and the trail as a whole, are `D08-trail`'s; where the tools are mounted and how the run builds their configuration is `D03-serve`'s; the tools page, which lists `mcp.Server.Tools()` in the catalog order `Register` registers them in, is `D06-pages`'. Decisions cited as seam N are the decisions document `/home/mgreenly/projects/ikigenba-ledger.md`.

## The tools

In catalog order, each with the effect appkit's `mcp` registers it with:

- `book` (read) answers the book: its currency and that commodity's precision, the lock date, the fiscal year start, who last changed it and when, and the counts of open accounts (those with no close date), transactions, and assertions failing as of today.
- `set_book` (additive) takes optional `lock_date` (a date, or the empty string to clear it) and `fiscal_year_start` (`MM-DD`) and answers the book as it stands and whether the call changed it.
- `accounts` (read) answers the chart whole, in name order.
- `account` (read) takes `name` and optional `date` and answers the account, its balance as of that date (today when absent) rolled up with its commodity, its own postings count, and its assertions with their status, its notes and its documents, each list whole.
- `open_account` (additive) takes `name` and `date` and answers the account and whether the call opened it; an existing name answers it as it stands and false.
- `close_account` (additive) takes `name` and `date` and answers the account and whether the call closed it.
- `post` (additive) takes `date`, `postings` (each `account` and optional `amount`), optional `flag`, `payee`, `narration`, `tags`, `links`, `metadata` and `key`, and answers the transaction with its postings filled in and whether the call posted it. With a `key` already held it answers that transaction and false without checking the rest of the values the store checks (`D04-store`); the argument shapes, appkit's typing and `metadata`'s members, are checked first.
- `transaction` (read) takes `transaction` and answers it with its postings, its current words and its reversal pointers.
- `annotate` (additive) takes `transaction` and any of `flag`, `payee`, `narration`, `tags`, `links` and `metadata`, writes the next version of the words, and answers the transaction as it stands and whether the call changed it.
- `reverse` (additive) takes `transaction` and optional `date`, `payee`, `narration`, `tags`, `links` and `metadata`, and answers the reversing transaction and whether the call made it; with no `date` the store dates it today.
- `history` (read) takes `transaction` and optional `page` and `limit` and answers the transaction's versions of its words, newest first, then `next`.
- `assert_balance` (additive) takes `account`, `date` and `amount` and answers the assertion with its status, actual balance and difference.
- `retract_assertion` (additive) takes `assertion` and answers it and whether the call retracted it.
- `check` (read) takes optional `page` and `limit` and answers the failing unretracted assertions dated on or before today, in date then account order, then `next`; an empty list means the book checks.
- `add_note` (additive) takes `account`, `date` and `text` and answers the note.
- `retract_note` (additive) takes `note` and answers it and whether the call retracted it.
- `add_document` (additive) takes `account`, `date`, `path` and optional `transaction`, checks the path in artifacts (seam 6), and answers the document with the artifact's id.
- `retract_document` (additive) takes `document` and answers it and whether the call retracted it.
- `journal` (read) takes optional `from`, `to`, `account`, `tag`, `link`, `payee` and `flag` filters and `page` and `limit`, and answers transactions newest first, each with its postings and current words, then `next`.
- `register` (read) takes `account` and optional `from`, `to`, `page` and `limit` and answers the postings of that account and its subtree oldest first, each with its running balance, then `next`.
- `balances` (read) takes optional `date` and `account` and answers the trial balance as of that date, whole, with the total of the lines it answers: the balance of `account` when given, else the five roots summed (B8b, `D04-store`).
- `balance_sheet` (read) takes optional `date` and answers seam 2's lines as of it.
- `income_statement` (read) takes `from` and `to` and answers seam 2's lines over that range and the net.

A tool checks only what appkit's `mcp` cannot type and the store cannot see: the paging members, that `metadata`'s members are all strings and name no key twice, and that `add_document`'s `path` is not empty. Every other value, a date, an account name, an amount, a flag, a key, a tag, an id, is handed to the store, which refuses it with one of its sentinels (`D04-store`); the tool maps the sentinel to a refusal.

## Paging

`history`, `check`, `journal` and `register` page as the suite's listing convention asks (shape's Listing), on the contract of appkit's `paging` package, which this document cites and never restates. Each input declares `page` and `limit` as pointer fields carrying `paging.PageDescription` and `paging.LimitDescription`; each answer is its list then `next`, carrying `paging.NextDescription`, present exactly when at least one entry follows. Each tool's token is `paging.Token{Tool: <its name>}` over the key of the last entry the page answered, in the order `D04-store` windows by: for `history` the version number; for `check` the assertion's date, account and id; for `journal` the transaction's date and sequence number; for `register` the row's date, its transaction's sequence number and its position. The tool asks the store for one entry more than the page and sets `next` only when it came back. A token binds no transaction, account or filter (shape's Listing), and one tool refuses another's. Before anything else is checked, a `limit` outside 1 to `paging.MaxLimit` is refused with the text of `paging.Window`'s error, then a `page` the tool's token does not decode with `paging.BadPage`; each refusal is that text alone, and is the same while the database is unreachable.

`accounts` and `balances` list the chart whole, and `account`'s assertions, notes and documents and the statements' lines are answered whole: these are recorded departures from shape's Listing (below).

## Answers

Times are RFC 3339 in UTC to the second, the empty string where there is none (an account never closed has an empty `closed`). Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD` as the store answers them, the empty string where there is none. Every amount is the decimal text `store.Amount`'s `String` renders at its commodity's precision, beside a `commodity` member. Who did something is answered as a user id (`opened_by`, `posted_by`, `annotated_by`, `written_by`, `added_by`, `retracted_by`, `updated_by`), never an email (`D04-store`). Every member of every answer is present, and listed as required in the output schema, a list `[]` when empty and `metadata` `{}` when the words hold none, but `next` absent when nothing follows. Every change answers the record as the store's change returned it and `changed`, whether this call changed it. An answer is product, given to a signed-in caller: it carries payees, narrations, note text and metadata values; secrecy governs the trail, the bus and the refusals (`D08-trail`), not the answer.

## Refusals

Apart from the two paging refusals, a refusal is an MCP error result whose text is `<word>: <sentence>`. The words are three, `tools.RefusalNotFound` (`not_found`), `tools.RefusalInvalid` (`invalid`) and `tools.RefusalUnavailable` (`unavailable`). Each sentence is a copy constant, `tools.Refuse*`, or `store.Unreachable` when the database cannot be read or written (`D04-store`). A `<word>: <sentence>` refusal names no value the call, the store or artifacts supplied; the paging refusals are appkit's own text, and its limit refusal may name the limit given.

- `not_found`: an id or an account the store reports `store.ErrNotFound` for, a malformed id included, with `RefuseNotFound`; an artifact path artifacts' `Head` does not find (`add_document`) with `RefuseNoArtifact`.
- `invalid`: a value the store reports `store.ErrInvalid` for, a `metadata` whose members are not all strings, an empty `add_document` path and a path artifacts' `Head` calls bad, with `RefuseInvalid`; postings the store reports `store.ErrUnbalanced` for with `RefuseUnbalanced`; a dated change the store reports `store.ErrLocked` for with `RefuseLocked`. Seam 2 refuses the last two `invalid`; their sentences tell the agent what to fix.
- `unavailable`: the database failing, with `store.Unreachable`; artifacts absent from the services file, `Head` answering anything but a result, `ErrBadPath` or `ErrNotFound`, or `Show` answering any error or a metadata with no id, with `RefuseArtifacts`.

## Events

A tool records in the trail, and emits on the bus, only through `D05-events`' and `D08-trail`'s functions, with its call's context, so each event is told as the tool's caller with the call's cause, and only once the store has reported a change. Every mutation records exactly one trail event (seam 5): `open_account` that opened, `trail.AccountOpened`; `close_account` that closed, `trail.AccountClosed`; `post` that posted, `trail.TransactionPosted`, which records `transaction.posted` in the trail and emits it on the bus at once; `annotate` that changed, `trail.TransactionAnnotated` with the new version; `reverse` that made a reversal, `trail.ReversalPosted`, which emits `transaction.posted` on the bus only, naming the original in `reverses`, and `trail.TransactionReversed`, which records `transaction.reversed` in the trail only (the root's ruling on the decisions document's seam 5); `assert_balance`, `trail.AssertionAdded`; `add_note`, `trail.NoteAdded`; `add_document`, `trail.DocumentAdded`; a retraction that retracted, `trail.AssertionRetracted`, `trail.NoteRetracted` or `trail.DocumentRetracted`; and `set_book` that changed, `trail.BookChanged` with the book after the change. A read, a refused call and a call the store reports changed nothing record and emit none of these.

## Descriptions

Every tool's description is a copy constant `tools.Describe<Tool>`, whose first line meets appkit's `mcp` rule for a summary and is the line the gateway's `describe` and the tools page show; it may go on after a blank line with guidance for agents. Every input member but `page` and `limit` carries a description too, a copy constant `tools.Arg<Type><Field>`, `<Type>` its args type's name less `Args` and `<Field>` the Go field's name; appkit's `mcp` reads a property's description only from the field's struct tag, so the tag holds the same words and a requirement binds the two, as spec's design format does for appkit's paging descriptions. `Register` registers the tools in catalog order, so `mcp.Server.Tools()`, which the tools page lists (`D06-pages`), answers them in that order; `tools.Catalog()` answers each tool's name with its description in the same order, and `tools.FirstLine` cuts a description to its first line.

## Recorded decisions

- **The artifacts client is appkit's concrete type**, `*artifacts.Client`, nil for none, as `web.Config` carries it (`D03-serve`), not an interface of ledger's own, as campaigns does. A test fakes artifacts on a Unix socket a services entry names and builds the client over it with `artifacts.NewClient`; the fake answers `HEAD` and the `show` tool as the test chooses, fails on demand, and records each request with its identity headers.
- **`tools.Config` names its fields `Store`, `Telemetry`, `Events` and `Artifacts`** and carries no clock: the store resolves "today" from its own `Now` (`D04-store`), so the tools' defaults (`account`'s and `balance_sheet`'s date, `reverse`'s date, `check`'s horizon, `book`'s failing count) are the store's.
- **Three refusal words**, `not_found`, `invalid` and `unavailable`, as seam 3 names them; the format is `<word>: <sentence>`; the paging refusals are appkit's text alone (shape's Listing).
- **More than one sentence per word**: `RefuseUnbalanced` and `RefuseLocked` share `invalid` with `RefuseInvalid`, as seam 2 refuses all three `invalid`, so an agent knows whether to fix an amount, the postings' sum or the date; `RefuseNoArtifact` tells a missing artifact from a missing record; `RefuseArtifacts` tells artifacts not answering from the database (`store.Unreachable`). Each is a constant, so no `<word>: <sentence>` refusal carries a value; appkit's paging text is appkit's own and may name the limit given.
- **A malformed id is `not_found`**, the store's rule (`D04-store`): the tools check no id's shape themselves.
- Departure: **the tools pre-check only paging, what appkit cannot type, and `add_document`'s path** (campaigns' tools pre-validate every value with the store's validators): the store validates every value and answers one of four refusal sentinels or `store.ErrUnreachable` (`D04-store`), and the tools map them. What appkit's `mcp` cannot type is `metadata`'s members, since appkit has no map schema; an empty `path` is refused before the store because no document can name it; every other path reaches the store's `CheckDocument` before artifacts is asked about it, and `AddDocument` only once artifacts has answered for it.
- Departure: **`metadata` is a `json.RawMessage`** on input and output: appkit's `mcp` derives no schema for a Go map but types a `json.RawMessage` field as an object, refusing anything else before the handler runs. The tool refuses `invalid` an object any of whose members is not a string or which names a member twice (a duplicate is ambiguous, and appkit refuses a duplicate argument the same way); the keys and values themselves are the store's to judge. On output the field is required, so the answer always carries an object, `{}` when there is none.
- Departure: **`changed` in every change answer** (campaigns answers `added` and `removed` beside `changed`): ledger's changes are of one kind, so one member name serves `set_book`, `open_account`, `close_account`, `post`, `annotate`, `reverse` and the three retractions.
- Departure: **argument descriptions are copy constants bound to their tags** (campaigns' arguments carry none; the root's ruling on the brief's "descriptions of every tool and argument are copy constants"): the constant is the copy's home, and the tag repeats it because appkit reads only the tag, as spec does for appkit's paging descriptions; a requirement makes the input schema prove the two equal.
- Departure: **`accounts` and `balances` list whole** (shape's Listing pages a list a person can add to): the chart is configuration-sized, as seam 3 says, and `D04-store` keeps `Accounts` and `Balances` unwindowed.
- Departure: **`account`'s assertions, notes and documents and the statements' lines are answered whole**: the lists on one account are small (seam 3), and a statement split across pages would not sum.
- **The retractions are additive**, like every other change: a retraction stamps the record, which is kept and still answered, and nothing in ledger is deleted (seam 2), so no tool is destructive.
- **Argument shapes are refused before the store's key-first answer**: appkit's `mcp` refuses a mistyped argument before the handler runs, and the tool refuses a `metadata` whose members are not all strings for the same reason, since appkit cannot type it; both come before any store call, so a `post` whose `key` is held but whose arguments are malformed is refused, not answered with the held transaction. The key rule covers the values the store checks.
- **Defaults are the store's** and cited here: `post`'s `key` answer before any other value the store checks, its flag defaulting to `pending`, `reverse`'s date defaulting to today and its words to the original's with flag `cleared`, and an `annotate` that names nothing or only what holds writing nothing (`D04-store`). An absent optional member reaches the store as the empty string or a nil pointer, as each tool's requirement states.
- **`add_document` asks the store before artifacts**: `store.CheckDocument` answers every refusal `AddDocument` would, reading only, so a document the store would refuse never costs a request to artifacts, and the artifact is checked last, with `Head` then `Show` as campaigns' `add_material` does. A `Show` that answers a metadata with an empty id is `unavailable`, since the store keeps the id a document names.
- **A reversal is told twice, once each way** (the root's ruling on seam 5): `trail.ReversalPosted` puts `transaction.posted` on the bus, so subscribers see every posting, reversals included, and `trail.TransactionReversed` records `transaction.reversed` in the trail, so the trail holds one event per mutation.
- **The answer to a change is what the store's change returned**: the tools read nothing after a change, so a committed change is never answered with a refusal.
- **The register's answer type is `RegisterAnswer`**: the contract sheet names it `Register`, which cannot be declared in the package that exports `func Register` (Go allows one declaration per name in a package scope); the name follows the sheet's own `BalanceSheetAnswer` and `IncomeStatementAnswer`. The root accepted it.
- **Constants of one kind are declared in one requirement**: the refusal words, the refusal sentences, the tool descriptions and the argument descriptions are each one Go `const` block of one kind, so each is one declaration, as campaigns' tools declare theirs; every type and every function has a requirement of its own.
- **Descriptions may run past one line**: the first line is the summary appkit checks and the tools page shows; agents read the rest in `describe`. Their words are copy and change no requirement.

## Consumer tasks

`web.Handler` (`D03-serve`) calls `tools.Register(cfg.MCP, tools.Config{Store: cfg.Store, Telemetry: cfg.Telemetry, Events: cfg.Events, Artifacts: cfg.Artifacts})` once. The tools page (`D06-pages`) lists `cfg.MCP.Tools()`, in the order `Register` registered them, which is catalog order, each description cut with `tools.FirstLine`; `tools.Catalog()` answers the same names and descriptions for any other reader.

A test builds a store over its own database with `store.New`, a writer with `telemetry.New` over a `*telemetry.Capture`, an emitter with `events.New` over an `*events.Capture` with `trail.Emits()`, and a `*artifacts.Client` with `artifacts.NewClient(artifacts.ClientConfig{Service: services.Entry{Name: "artifacts", Socket: sock}, Telemetry: w})` over a fake artifacts on a Unix socket in a short temporary directory, which answers `HEAD` and the `show` tool with the artifacts the test chooses, refuses or fails on demand and records each request (or a nil client); an `mcp.Server` from `mcp.NewServer`; calls `tools.Register(srv, cfg)`; serves the server inside `events.Middleware` and `identity.Require` with `httptest`; and calls `mcp.Client.CallTool(ctx, caller, name, args)` with `tools.AccountDateArgs{...}`, `tools.PostArgs{Postings: []tools.PostingArgs{...}}`, `tools.AnnotateArgs{...}`, `tools.ReverseArgs{...}`, `tools.AddDocumentArgs{...}`, `tools.JournalArgs{...}`, `tools.RegisterArgs{...}` and the rest encoded as JSON, as two callers. It decodes a success's `structuredContent` into `tools.Book`, `tools.BookChange`, `tools.AccountList`, `tools.AccountRead`, `tools.AccountChange`, `tools.Transaction`, `tools.TransactionChange`, `tools.History`, `tools.Assertion`, `tools.AssertionChange`, `tools.CheckList`, `tools.Note`, `tools.NoteChange`, `tools.Document`, `tools.DocumentChange`, `tools.Journal`, `tools.RegisterAnswer`, `tools.Balances`, `tools.BalanceSheetAnswer` or `tools.IncomeStatementAnswer`, compares it with what the store's own reads answer (`GetTransaction`, `GetAccount`, `Status` and the rest), walks `next` until it is absent, compares a refusal's text with `tools.RefusalInvalid + ": " + tools.RefuseUnbalanced` and the rest, with `paging.BadPage` and with the `Error()` of `paging.Window`'s error, flips `SetFailing(true)` on the handle the store was built over to provoke `store.Unreachable`, reads the input schemas `ListTools` answers to compare each property's description with its `tools.Arg*` constant, and, after `Flush` on the writer and the emitter, inspects the captures for `trail.EventTransactionPosted` and the other names, their attributes, their `Cause` and `Depth` under `events.NewContext` or an `X-Event-Cause` header.
