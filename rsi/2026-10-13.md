## Next cycle

Take one delivery run on these skills and measure the same table. Expected if the changes worked:

- Codex threads per small job near 8, no sub-coordinator without three or more implementers, serial tail under 15% of wall.
- No author leaf over 150K; context at first edit under 100K.
- No step waits on an unrelated step; one release per completed batch.
- Leaf and verifier threads no longer read fanout.

Watch for harm: design contracts that disagree across documents now that one leaf no longer holds them all, and gate results misreported with only one verifier at the finish.
