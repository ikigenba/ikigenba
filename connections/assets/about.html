How it works

connections is an OAuth broker. A person connects an account at an outside provider once, from this page or from a link an agent hands them, and the broker keeps the grant against their user.

The suite's connectors on this host ask the broker for a short-lived access token for the person they act for, and it refreshes the token with the provider when it is about to expire. The token never leaves the host.

No tool ever returns a credential: list answers each provider and whether you are connected, connect answers the link a person opens to connect, and disconnect revokes the grant at the provider and removes it.

A connection is private to the person who made it, one per provider. One the provider stops honouring shows as broken until it is reconnected.
