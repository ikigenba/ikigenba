Agents read and keep sessions with these tools, through the MCP gateway's call and mutate: they list sessions with their figures, total attention, wall time, agent time, tokens and dollars by week or month, grouped by kind, person, tool or first command, and read and edit the kind rules, the price table and the away limit. Every tool answers data; a change to a rule, a price or the away limit moves the figures at once, without a push.
