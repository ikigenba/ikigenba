# Decision 40 — Adopt the suite lint contract (`root project/design/D30.md`) at tier `strict`

**Decision.** This tree **adopts the suite lint contract** and registers at
tier **`strict`**: a committed one-line `sites/.lint-tier` file containing
`strict`. The contract's content — the tier model and ratchet, the marker
semantics, the strict tier's rule list and thresholds, the
analyzers-skip-tests / gofumpt-everywhere policy, the pinned tool, and the
enforcement points — is the umbrella's and is not restated here; the enforcing
runner (`bin/lint`) and its proof live in `bin` (`[proof: bin]`), so this tree
carries no lint test of its own. This Decision owns only the local facts: the
marker exists, says `strict`, and the tree is clean at that tier.

Strict is the operator-chosen tier for this tree. The ratchet
(`root project/design/D30.md`) promotes a tree `off → cheap → strict` and never
down, and the operator selects the strict step per tree rather than as a uniform
suite-wide flip. Unlike a pure marker flip, reaching a clean strict run here
requires first resolving a small number of strict-only findings — internal
complexity and style items, fixable without changing any exported signature or
seam; the adoption phase carries that refactoring, and the marker flip lands only
once the tree is clean at strict.

**Rejected.**

- *Relying on silence-is-conformance with no local Decision.* Silence covers
  conforming to a contract's ambient semantics; registering at a tier is an
  affirmative act (a committed marker plus a conformance obligation) and gets
  its own visible citation-is-adoption Decision, the same treatment this tree
  gives every other adopted suite contract.
- *Remaining at `cheap`.* Cheap was this tree's entry tier; the operator
  promotes to strict, taking the ratchet's next step. Staying at cheap would
  leave the stricter judgment rules (`unnamedResult`, `paramTypeCombine`) and
  the complexity thresholds unenforced.
- *Minting local ids for the marker or the clean state.* The behaviors are
  the runner's, proven in `bin`; the local obligation is structural and its
  proof is the phase's deterministic exit condition, not a new id.

**Verification.** A structural decision: no ids minted, none cited — the lint
contract carries no per-service ids (`root project/design/D30.md`, Rejected:
*Per-service proof ids*). The checkable local facts are the phase's:
`sites/.lint-tier` contains exactly `strict`, and `bin/lint sites` (run from
the repo root, a read-only check over this tree) exits 0.
