How it works

candidates ingests the records scrapes scrapes into candidate businesses, one per real business with every record that describes it attached as evidence. A record attaches by its source key, else by its normalized name with its state and ZIP; when in doubt it starts a new candidate rather than join the wrong one.

Each candidate carries enrichments and two judgments. The enrichments are the facts the enrichers find: drive time from the base, the fleet verdict (pass, fail or unknown), the truck and driver counts, the headcounts, the website (https or http when one was found, none when the website search ran and found no site, or unknown when no search has run), the government match, the categories and the filing dates. Each is recomputed only when what it was computed from changes, never because it is old, and none removes a candidate. The judge reads the facts and the records and forms the judgments. Its operating confidence, from 0 to 1, says how sure we are that it is a real business operating today; each piece of evidence closes part of the gap to certainty, and with no evidence it is empty, not 0. Its revenue range, low to high with a confidence, combines its size estimates: its headcount times a per-employee band for the kind of business and, for a for-hire or mixed carrier, FMCSA's trucks. PPP gives no estimate of its own; the jobs a loan reported count as a headcount.

An ingest pass starts when scrapes finishes a run: it ingests the run's records and marks gone the records the run no longer holds, and judges nothing. A judge pass judges every candidate again from its facts and its records not gone, once a day when the judge_candidates trigger fires, or when an agent calls judge. ingest starts an ingest by hand: named a scraper, it ingests that scraper's current run as a finished run would; named none, it ingests every scraper's current run, recomputes every fact candidates derives from what it holds, fetching nothing, then judges; it keeps the candidates and the rulings.

An enrich pass follows every ingest that ends done, or starts when an agent calls enrich. It runs each enricher on the candidates whose inputs changed since its last result: first those candidates derives itself, the facts from the records and the drive time from the ZIP, then the website check, which fetches the domain of an email on its own domain once and keeps whether it serves a site over https or over http, with the page it landed on; a domain that serves nothing leaves the website unknown. A site that answers is evidence the business operates; a missing one is never held against it. When it writes a result, a judge pass follows unless one is running.

Site text runs only when an agent calls enrich with a prospect list or one candidate. For each company with a website it runs the caller's site-text prompt in prompts, whose agent reads the company's own site and names the pages that describe the business with add_page; candidates fetches and keeps each page itself. The result records how the run ended and what it cost; a failed run is kept as a result, and force runs a target again. The drive route runs the same way: for each company with a street address it asks Google's Routes API for the drive by car from the base, and the minutes and miles of a route replace the ZIP's estimate while the address and the base stay as they were. Passes run side by side; only a second pass doing the same work, the same kind over the same run, target or corpus, is refused.

The owner reads candidates through prospect lists. A prospect list is a saved search: filters, an order, a size and the candidates rejected from it. Its members, the prospects, are worked out each time it is read: the active, judged candidates that pass every filter, are not rejected from it and are not excluded unless it shows excluded ones, in its order, cut to its size. A filter on a fact that can be unknown has a switch that lets the unknowns in, so none is left out silently. prospect_lists and prospect_list read them; create_prospect_list, edit_prospect_list and delete_prospect_list keep them; reject keeps a candidate out of one prospect list and unreject lets it back.

candidates finds candidates by name, ZIP or phone, and candidate answers one with its evidence, its rulings, its pages and its rejects. decide records the owner's merge, split and not_same rulings, and exclude and include keep a candidate out of every prospect list or bring it back; each ruling takes effect on the candidates it touches at once, and every pass reapplies it and none overwrites it. passes and pass show the passes.

Candidates and prospect lists belong to no one: every signed-in user sees every candidate, prospect list, pass, ruling and reject, and may change every prospect list.
