How it works

candidates turns the records scrapes collects into candidate businesses, one per real business with every record that describes it attached as evidence. A record attaches by its source key, else by its normalized name with its state and ZIP; when in doubt it starts a new candidate rather than join the wrong one.

Each candidate carries two judgments. Its operating confidence, from 0 to 1, says how sure we are that it is a real business operating today; each piece of evidence its records give closes part of the gap to certainty, and with no evidence it is empty, not 0. Its revenue range, low to high with a confidence, combines the size estimates its sources give. Drive time from Sandstone, the fleet verdict (pass, fail or unknown), the truck and driver counts, the government match, the categories and the filing dates are kept beside them as facts; none removes a candidate.

An ingest pass starts when scrapes finishes a run: it resolves the run's records and marks gone the records the run no longer holds, and judges nothing. A judge pass judges every candidate again from its records not gone, once a day when the judge_candidates trigger fires. rebuild ingests every scraper's current run, then judges, and recomputes everything but the candidates and the rulings.

The owner reads candidates through cohorts. A cohort is a saved search: filters, an order, a size and the candidates rejected from it. Its members are worked out each time it is read: the active, judged candidates that pass every filter, are not rejected from it and are not excluded unless it shows excluded ones, in its order, cut to its size. A filter on a fact that can be unknown has a switch that lets the unknowns in, so none is left out silently. cohorts and cohort read them; create_cohort, edit_cohort and delete_cohort keep them; reject keeps a candidate out of one cohort and unreject lets it back.

candidates finds candidates by name, ZIP or phone, and candidate answers one with its evidence, its rulings and its rejects. decide records the owner's merge, split and not_same rulings, and exclude and include keep a candidate out of every cohort or bring it back; each ruling judges the candidates it touches at once, and every pass reapplies it and none overwrites it. passes and pass show the passes.

Candidates and cohorts belong to no one: every signed-in user sees every candidate, cohort, pass, ruling and reject, and may change every cohort.
