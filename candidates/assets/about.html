How it works

candidates turns the records scrapes collects into candidate businesses, one per real business with every record that describes it attached as evidence. A record attaches by its source key, else by its normalized name with its state and ZIP; when in doubt it starts a new candidate rather than join the wrong one.

Each candidate carries two judgments. Its operating confidence, from 0 to 1, says how sure we are that it is a real business operating today; each piece of evidence its records give closes part of the gap to certainty, and with no evidence it is empty, not 0. Its revenue range, low to high with a confidence, combines the size estimates its sources give. Drive time from Sandstone, the fleet test, the government match, the categories and the filing dates are kept beside them as facts; none removes a candidate.

An ingest pass starts when scrapes finishes a run: it resolves the run's records and marks gone the records the run no longer holds, and judges nothing. A judge pass judges every candidate again from its records not gone, once a day when the judge_candidates trigger fires. rebuild ingests every scraper's current run, then judges, and recomputes everything but the candidates and the rulings.

shortlist ranks every candidate the owner has not excluded by operating confidence, highest first, with no evidence last; candidates finds them by name, ZIP or phone, and candidate answers one with its evidence. decide records the owner's rulings, merge, split, not_same, exclude and include, which judge the candidates they touch at once and which every pass reapplies and none overwrites. passes and pass show the passes.

Candidates belong to no one: every signed-in user sees every candidate, pass and ruling.
