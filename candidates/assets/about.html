How it works

candidates turns the records scrapes collects into candidate businesses, one per real business with every record that describes it attached as evidence. A record attaches by its source key, else by its normalized name with its state and ZIP; when in doubt it starts a new candidate rather than join the wrong one.

Each candidate is judged against the fit test: within 120 minutes' drive of Sandstone, a fleet, and ten million dollars or more flowing through it. Each source gives a size estimate with a confidence; the score is the estimate's point times that confidence, weighed down as the newest filing ages past two years, to nothing at five.

A pass starts when scrapes finishes a run: it resolves the run's records, marks gone the records the run no longer holds, and re-scores the candidates they touch. rebuild starts one over every scraper's current run and recomputes everything but the candidates and the rulings.

shortlist ranks the candidates that pass the fit test; candidates finds them by name, ZIP or phone, and candidate answers one with its evidence. decide records the owner's rulings, merge, split, not_same, exclude and include, which every pass reapplies and none overwrites. passes and pass show the passes.

Candidates belong to no one: every signed-in user sees every candidate, pass and ruling.
