Pass

An ingest stores a run's records, and judges only when started by hand over every scraper: rows, new, changed, unchanged and gone count the records against what candidates held, and created and linked count candidates. A judge judges every candidate again: judged counts them and unranged those left with no revenue estimate. An enrich runs every enricher over the candidates whose inputs changed, the derived kinds first: derived counts the results they wrote. For the website check, due counts the candidates due, fetched the domains it fetched, once each, and https, http and none the results by state, none where the check reached no site, which leaves the website unknown. An enrich over a prospect list or one candidate runs one kind over its target, the due ones or, forced, every one; for site text, the brief and the website search, runs counts the prompts runs it started, runs failed those that failed, pages the pages they kept, none for the brief or the website search, and cost what they spent; for the drive route, runs counts the requests it made to the routing service, runs failed those that failed, and cost what they cost. A parks pass derives the industrial parks from the county parcel records: parcels read counts the records, by label, by value and by candidate the industrial parcels by the rule that picked each, and created, updated, gone and grown count the parks.
