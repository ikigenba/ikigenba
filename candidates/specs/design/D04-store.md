# D04-store

The records: `internal/store`, which owns candidates' seven tables in its SQLite database, `state/candidates.db`, and every read and write of them (decision 16): `candidates`, one row per candidate business, never deleted (decision 4); `links`, one row per scrapes row attached to a candidate, addressed by its source key (decision 6); `estimates`, one per source per candidate; `assessments`, one per candidate (decision 8); `decisions`, the owner's rulings (decision 10); `passes`, one row per pass until the prune removes it (decision 11); and `zip_drive`, the ZIP drive-time table, replaced whole at every start (decision 9). This document declares the store's whole public surface: its records, the words they are spelled with, every method with its signature, the errors every consumer tells apart and the one line every consumer answers when the database cannot be reached. It states what the pass records, the ZIP table, the reads, the prune and `db status` do. What a row does to the candidates and links when it is attached, what a gone run does, and what each ruling does (`Attach`, `MarkGone`, `Decide`) are `D12-resolution`'s; what a re-score writes and which candidates the shortlist holds in which order (`Rescore`, `RescoreAll`, the **shortlist**) are `D13-scoring`'s. Opening, migrating, transactions and the failure seam are appkit's `db`; `D01-layout-and-run-seam` resolves the path and declares the root package's `Migrations()`. It is scrapes' store in shape, with a pass in place of a run and the candidates, their evidence and the rulings beside it.

## One database, one writer

candidates is the only writer of its database, and the database is the only record of its candidates, links, estimates, assessments, rulings, passes and drive times; nothing else is kept on disk (decision 1). `store.New` builds a store over a handle and opens or creates nothing. What a store holds when its handle is closed is what a store over the next `db.Open` of the same path holds; a test proves the records that way, never by reading tables. A store whose handle is failing (`SetFailing(true)`) answers every call with `ErrUnreachable` and changes nothing. `Config.Now` is the clock every stamp is read from, and the moment the shortlist's freshness is judged at; `Config.Rand` is the source pass ids and candidate ids are drawn from. `Run` hands in `Process.Now` and `Process.Rand`, and the store has no default for either.

## By key

A pass costs in proportion to its rows, and the pages and the tools answer while it runs (orchestrator ruling, after a pass over the real FMCSA run of 16,706 rows never finished: every batch loaded, copied and compared the whole database under one lock). The store reaches every record the pass path needs by key through SQL, on decision 16's indexes: a link by its source key (the links' unique scraper and source id), the active candidates one of whose links holds a match key by the links' name key, state and ZIP, the oldest first, a candidate's links by the links' candidate, a candidate by its id, its estimates, assessment and rulings by its id, a ZIP's minutes by the ZIP. No write on the pass path reads a whole table, but for a rebuild's `RescoreAll`, which covers every active candidate by definition and so enumerates the active candidates once. An `Attach` touches its batch's rows and the candidates they attach to: an unchanged row costs the lookup of its link and the write of its run, last seen and gone; a candidate's display is re-derived from its own links once per batch for each candidate one of whose links the batch wrote, since a link's last seen and gone order the display (`D12-resolution`). `MarkGone` is one statement over the links of one scraper whose run is not the pass's. `Rescore` reads and writes each candidate it is given by its id, reading the candidate's record, its own links and rulings, and the ZIP table's minutes for its display ZIP, and writing its estimates and assessment.

Every write is one write transaction of appkit's `db`, and the store holds no lock of its own across one: the only lock it keeps guards `Config.Rand`. A read goes through `db.Read`, which answers from what was last committed while a write transaction is open, since appkit's handle reads beside its one writer; so a page or a tool never waits for a batch, a re-score or a ruling. Calls racing still behave as one after another (R-GTSY-N29E, R-4YPM-MZ41), because every write goes through appkit's one writer. Which statements the store issues is not contract; that a read answers while a write is open is, and so is the time a pass over thousands of rows takes (`D06-passes`).

## The records

A **candidate** (`store.Candidate`) is one business: its id, `cand_` and 16 lowercase hexadecimal digits; its status, `active` or `merged`, with the id it was merged into; its display fields (name, every name, street, city, state, ZIP, county as `MN-115`, phone, email, people); its tags (class and cargo); and when it was created and last updated (decision 4). Which link the display fields come from and when they change is `D12-resolution`'s.

A **link** (`store.Link`) is one row's attachment to one candidate: the candidate, the source key (scraper and source id), the run the row was last seen in and its digest, the source's reading of the row as last seen (`sources.Reading`: the keys, the envelope's common fields, the tags, the filing date, the fleet test, the estimate and the exclusion signals), how it attached (`source_key`, `match_key` or `decision`) with its confidence, when it was first and last seen, and whether it is gone (decision 6). A source key is unique among links: a row attaches to one candidate at a time.

An **estimate** is one source's `sources.Estimate` for one candidate, at most one per source per candidate. An **assessment** (`store.Assessment`) is a candidate's one judgment against the fit test as the last re-score wrote it: the fleet test, the combined low, high and confidence, the newest filing date among its links not gone, the drive minutes from its display ZIP (nil when the table lacks it), its exclusions with their reasons and whether it is excluded, the score before freshness, the rules version and when it was scored (decisions 8 and 11). Nothing stored decays: the effective confidence and the score at a moment are computed from the stored values and the clock (`scoring.Effective`, `D13-scoring`).

A **ruling** (`store.Ruling`) is one of the owner's decisions: its kind (`merge`, `split`, `not_same`, `exclude`, `include`), the candidate it names, the other candidate (the other side of a merge or a `not_same`, the new candidate of a split), the source keys a split moved, why, who (user id and email) and when (decision 10). Rulings are never changed or removed.

A **pass** (`store.Pass`) is one resolve and re-score. Its id is `pass_` and 16 lowercase hexadecimal digits. It keeps its **trigger**, the run id a delivery named or `rebuild`; its **status**, one of `running`, `done` and `failed`, and when failed one of three **reasons**, `scrapes`, `interrupted` and `store` (which failure is which is `D06-passes`'); **started** and **finished**, each UTC to the second; the nine counts, each 0 until the pass is done; its starter's user id and email; the request id it was started under; and, for a delivery, the bus event's id, the **delivery**, an internal column no tool or page shows: it is how a redelivered event is told from a new one (`D06-passes`).

Every id the store mints, a pass's or a candidate's, is its prefix and 8 bytes read from `Config.Rand`, written as 16 lowercase hexadecimal digits, drawn again when it is the id of a record of its kind the store holds.

## Passes

A pass has three writes. `AddPass` records it `running`, stamping started and minting the id. It is refused `ErrDelivered` when its delivery is one an existing pass carries, or when its trigger is a run id an existing pass names, whatever that pass's status; and otherwise `ErrBusy` while any pass is `running`, so one pass runs at a time (decision 11). `FinishPass` ends a `running` pass `done` with the nine counts its work gathered; `FailPass` ends a `running` pass `failed` with a reason and leaves everything the pass wrote in place, since every write is idempotent and the next pass or a rebuild repairs it (decision 11). Of two calls racing on the same pass one succeeds and the other is told it is no longer running (`ErrEnded`). Nothing moves a pass back.

The counts a pass finishes with are the sums of the `store.Counts` its writes answered: `Attach` answers `Rows`, `New`, `Changed`, `Unchanged`, `Created` and `Linked` for a batch; `MarkGone` answers `Gone`; `Rescore` and `RescoreAll` answer `Rescored` and `Unranged`. What each counts is the owning document's.

`GetPass` answers any pass, whoever started it. `ListPasses` is every pass newest first, a window at a time; `RunningPasses` every pass still `running`, which the start finishes `failed` with `interrupted` (`D06-passes`). `PrunePasses` removes, in one write, every `done` pass beyond the newest keep count of `done` passes and every `failed` pass beyond the newest keep count of `failed` passes; a `running` pass is neither counted nor removed (decision 16). A pruned pass is gone from every read, its delivery and trigger with it. Candidates, links, estimates, assessments and rulings are never pruned; the prune touches none of them. A re-score replaces a candidate's estimates and assessment (`D13-scoring`); a merge moves the merged candidate's links to the survivor, and the merged candidate otherwise keeps what it had at the merge: its display fields, tags, estimates and last assessment (`D12-resolution`).

## The ZIP table

`ReplaceZipDrive` replaces the whole table with the map it is given in one write; the start hands it the root package's `ZipDrive()`, so the table is always the release's (decision 9). `ZipMinutes` answers one ZIP's minutes. A re-score reads the table for each candidate's display ZIP (`D13-scoring`).

## Reads

`GetCandidate` answers any candidate, active or merged: a merged candidate's record carries its status and the id it was merged into. `Links`, `Estimates`, `GetAssessment` and `Rulings` answer what the store holds for one candidate; each tells an unknown candidate (`ErrNotFound`) from a known one with nothing to show, except `GetAssessment`, which answers `ErrNotFound` for a candidate not yet scored too. Links come not gone first, then newest filing first, so the freshest evidence leads; rulings newest first. `ListCandidates` pages the active candidates newest first, optionally only those one of whose names contains a text, case-insensitively, those at a ZIP, or those one of whose links has a phone key (decision 14). `Shortlist` pages the shortlist under a filter at the moment of the call, each entry with its candidate, its assessment and its effective confidence and score; which candidates, in which order, with which numbers, is `D13-scoring`'s. All lists are never nil.

## Errors

`ErrNotFound`, `ErrBusy`, `ErrDelivered`, `ErrEnded` and `ErrInvalid` are answers about the records; `ErrInvalid` is `Decide`'s alone, a ruling that is malformed or one the records forbid (`D12-resolution`). Every other error satisfies `errors.Is(err, ErrUnreachable)`, and every consumer answers it with `Unreachable` (a tool's `unavailable`, a page's unavailable answer, a delivery's `Fail`, a pass failed `store`), never quoting the cause. A done context, a failing handle, a random source that fails and a write given a value no consumer sends all end there, the last changing nothing.

## `db status`

`candidates db status` writes what appkit's `db.Status` writes for `state/candidates.db` and the root package's `Migrations()`, and creates nothing.

## Recorded decisions

- **A store fails only through its handle's `SetFailing`**, the one way candidates' test discipline provokes a database failure (candidates/AGENTS.md); what a store answers once its handle is closed is not contract, since no test may close the store to make it fail.

- The schema is seven migrations, one per table in decision 16's order, named as the scaffold's `AGENTS.md` names them. `0001_candidates.sql`: `candidates` (`id` primary key, `status`, `merged_into`, `name`, `names` as a JSON array, `names_lower`, `street`, `city`, `state`, `zip`, `county`, `phone`, `email`, `people` as a JSON array, `class`, `cargo` as a JSON array, `created`, `updated`), indexed on `status` and `created`. `0002_links.sql`: `links` (`scraper`, `source_id`, `candidate`, `run`, `digest`, `name_key`, `state`, `zip`, `phone_key`, `domain_key`, `reading` as the reading's JSON, `reason`, `confidence`, `first_seen`, `last_seen`, `gone`), unique on `scraper` and `source_id`, indexed on `name_key`, `state` and `zip`, and on `candidate`. `0003_estimates.sql`: `estimates` (`candidate`, `source`, `low`, `high` nullable, `confidence`, `facts` as JSON, `filed`, `formula`), unique on `candidate` and `source`. `0004_assessments.sql`: `assessments` (`candidate` primary key, `fleet`, `low`, `high` nullable, `confidence`, `filed`, `minutes` nullable, `exclusions` as JSON, `excluded`, `base`, `rules`, `scored`), indexed on `base`, the stored score. `0005_decisions.sql`: `decisions` (`seq` integer primary key, `kind`, `candidate`, `other`, `keys` as a JSON array, `why`, `user_id`, `user_email`, `at`), indexed on `candidate` and on `other`. `0006_passes.sql`: `passes` (`id` primary key, `trigger`, `status`, `reason`, `started`, `finished`, the nine counts, `user_id`, `user_email`, `request_id`, `delivery`), indexed on `started`, unique on `delivery` where it is not empty. `0007_zip_drive.sql`: `zip_drive` (`zip` primary key, `minutes`). Times are RFC 3339 UTC to the second; an empty time is one not set. SQL keywords used as column names (`trigger`, `rows`, `new`, `keys` and the like) are quoted. No requirement states the schema, since a test proves the records through the store; the sandbox check, which sets a pass to `running` by hand, writes these columns.
- A link keeps the source's whole reading of its row as JSON beside the key columns matching reads, so a re-score and a merge need no read of scrapes and a rebuild rewrites both at once (decision 6, "matching runs against the links and never calls scrapes").
- Estimates are answered as `sources.Estimate`, the type a source makes them in; the store adds no type of its own for them, since the candidate is the argument of the read.
- One pass at a time is refused by `AddPass` itself, in its write, so a `rebuild` and a delivery racing cannot both record a pass; the delivery is a column of `passes`, not a table of its own, and is forgotten with its pass at the prune, long after the events app's retries end.
- A redelivery is told by the delivery and also by the trigger: a pass already naming the run is enough, so a second `scrape.finished` for one run, under another bus event, is skipped (decision 11).
- A failed pass keeps its counts at 0, as a failed scrapes run does; what it wrote stays in the candidates and links, which the next pass or a rebuild repairs.
- Every id is minted by the store, 8 bytes at a time, at most 8 attempts, as in scrapes; a pass id and a candidate id are never compared, since their prefixes differ.
- The shortlist's freshness is judged at `Config.Now` read during `Shortlist`, not stored (decision 8); the tools and pages compute the same numbers for one candidate with `scoring.Effective` and their own `Now`.
- A write given a value no consumer sends answers an error matching `ErrUnreachable` and changes nothing, as in scrapes; `ErrInvalid` is kept for the one write whose arguments come from a person, a ruling.
- **Keyed, never whole** (orchestrator ruling): the pass path reads and writes by key on decision 16's indexes, and no index is added: the gone-marking runs on the scraper prefix of the links' unique index, `RescoreAll` on the candidates' index on status, the match on the links' name key, state and ZIP joined to the candidates by id. Only the time bound of `D06-passes` and the read that answers while a write is open (R-SATC-3077) are required; the statements are the build's.
- **Ruling points 1 and 2 carry no requirement of their own** (orchestrator ruling): reads by key, work confined to a batch's rows and their candidates, and a bounded gone-marking cannot be observed by use, since a test never reads tables or counts statements; the cost requirements of `D06-passes` (R-UTW4-OJCI, R-UV41-2B37, R-UWBX-G2TW) are their proof, and the read during a write (R-SATC-3077) is ruling point 4's.
- **An unchanged row still re-derives its candidate's display once per batch**: a link's last seen orders the display (`D12-resolution`, the display link), so the touch an unchanged row makes can change which link shows, and R-GAAK-IQEA requires the display current after every `Attach`; the cost stays one re-derivation per candidate per batch, from that candidate's own links. This goes beyond the ruling's "one lookup and a touch of last seen", deliberately.
- **A read never waits for a write**: appkit's `db.Read` answers from the last commit while `db.Write` holds the writer, which a probe against appkit's released `db` showed (a read returned while a test held a write transaction open), and the store adds no lock of its own across a transaction. The store this replaces held one store-wide lock across every transaction, so a read waited behind a write that was itself waiting for the writer; a test holding a `Write` open while an `Attach` waits tells the two apart. A lock the store took only inside its own write transaction, after appkit's writer is won, would escape that test, and this limitation is accepted: use cannot observe when an `Attach` is inside its own transaction, and a lock held there blocks reads only for as long as the batch's own write transaction, which the time bound of `D06-passes` keeps short. Likewise the test cannot confirm that its waiting `Attach`, `Decide` and `Rescore` have begun waiting before it reads, since the design declares no hook that would tell it: a store lock held across a whole write call is caught only when a waiting call has taken it before the reads end, which the test makes likely, not certain, by starting the writes first and yielding between reads; this limitation is accepted too.
- `ListCandidates`' `phone` filter is matched on the links' phone keys, any link, gone or not, with the filter normalized by `sources.PhoneKey`: a phone is identity evidence, and a gone link still counts for identity (decision 6).

## Consumer tasks

The engine (`D06-passes`), `c` the caller, records a rebuild with `p, err := st.AddPass(ctx, store.Pass{Trigger: store.TriggerRebuild, UserID: c.UserID, UserEmail: c.Email, RequestID: c.RequestID})`, `store.ErrBusy` answering `busy`; a delivery with `st.AddPass(ctx, store.Pass{Trigger: run, UserID: user, RequestID: c.RequestID, Delivery: d.Event.ID})`, `store.ErrDelivered` answering `Skip` and `store.ErrBusy` `Fail`. The work hands each batch to `n, touched, err := st.Attach(ctx, run, rebuild, batch)`, then calls `st.MarkGone(ctx, scraper, run)` and `st.Rescore(ctx, ids)` (or `st.RescoreAll(ctx)` for a rebuild), adds the `store.Counts` they answer, and ends with `st.FinishPass(ctx, p.ID, total)` or `st.FailPass(ctx, p.ID, store.ReasonScrapes)`. The start calls `st.ReplaceZipDrive(ctx, candidates.ZipDrive())` and `st.RunningPasses(ctx)`; the sweep `st.PrunePasses(ctx, s.KeepPasses)`.

The tools and pages (`D10-tools`, `D08-pages`) call `st.Shortlist(ctx, store.ShortlistFilter{MaxMinutes: scoring.DefaultMaxMinutes, MinRevenue: scoring.DefaultMinRevenue}, 0, 30)`, `st.ListCandidates(ctx, store.CandidateFilter{Name: "trucking"}, 0, 26)`, `st.GetCandidate`, `st.Links`, `st.Estimates`, `st.GetAssessment`, `st.Rulings`, `st.ListPasses(ctx, 0, 26)` and `st.GetPass`; `decide` calls `r, cs, err := st.Decide(ctx, store.Ruling{Kind: store.KindMerge, Candidate: a, Other: b, Why: why, UserID: c.UserID, UserEmail: c.Email})`, `store.ErrNotFound` answering `not_found` and `store.ErrInvalid` `invalid`. Any error matching `store.ErrUnreachable` is answered with `store.Unreachable`. A test opens its own database with `db.Open` and the root package's `Migrations()`, builds `store.New(d, store.Config{Now: clock, Rand: rnd})`, replaces the ZIP table with `candidates.ZipDrive()`, attaches observations it builds from `sources.Reading` values, records, finishes and fails passes, flips `d.SetFailing(true)`, and reopens the path to prove what persists. A test of serving holds `d.Write(ctx, fn)` open, its `fn` waiting on a channel the test holds, starts `st.Attach(ctx, run, false, batch)`, `st.Decide(ctx, r)` and `st.Rescore(ctx, ids)` each in a goroutine of its own, and calls `st.GetCandidate`, `st.Links` and `st.Shortlist`, each called a fixed number of times, yielding between calls, and each answering before the test releases `fn`; were a read to wait for the write, the test would never release it and would fail at the package's timeout, so the test waits on no clock.
