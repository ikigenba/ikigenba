# D04-store

The records: `internal/store`, which owns candidates' tables in its SQLite database, `state/candidates.db`, and every read and write of them (decision 16): `candidates`, one row per candidate business, never deleted (decision 4); `links`, one row per scrapes row attached to a candidate, addressed by its source key (decision 6); `estimates`, one per source per candidate; `judgments`, one per judged candidate, holding its two judgments alone (decision 8, funnel rename decisions 12 and 13); `decisions`, the owner's rulings (decision 10); `passes`, one row per pass until the prune removes it (decision 11); `zip_drive`, the ZIP drive-time table, replaced whole at every start (decision 9); `candidate_cargo`, one row per cargo tag of each candidate, kept beside the candidate's cargo for the previous release, which reads it, though nothing in this one does (log 30); `prospect_lists`, one row per prospect list until it is deleted (cohorts decision 23, funnel rename decisions 4 and 5); `rejects`, one row per reject or unreject record, never deleted (cohorts decisions 11 and 23); `enrichments`, one row per enrichment result recorded on a candidate, never deleted (website decisions 20 and 21); `facts`, one row per active candidate holding a result, the projection of its newest result of each kind and of its newest `brief` result whose run is not failed, its website decided by the check or else by a confirmed search and its drive minutes by a route found from its current address and the origin or else by the ZIP table (enrichers decision 5, brief decisions 7 and 8, website search decision 7, drive route outcome 7); `pages`, one row per copy of a web page kept on a candidate, never deleted (site text decision 1); `campaign_uses`, one row per campaign's use of a candidate, kept on the candidate at the end of its merge chain (campaigns decision 17, campaigns log D1); and `opens`, one row per recorded open of a prospect list, never deleted, not even with its prospect list (basis decisions 7, 10 and 12). This document declares the store's whole public surface: its records, the words they are spelled with, every method with its signature, the errors every consumer tells apart and the one line every consumer answers when the database cannot be reached. It states what the pass records, the ZIP table, the reads and their windows, the prospect list, reject and open writes and reads, the enrichment writes and reads, the derived kinds' runs, the targeted walks, the facts rows, the page writes and reads, the campaign use writes and reads, the schema's eighth to nineteenth migrations, the prune and `db status` do. What a row does to the candidates and links when it is attached, what a gone run does, and what each ruling does (`Attach`, `MarkGone`, `Decide`) are `D12-ingest`'s; what a judge writes, the fleet verdict and its counts included (`Judge`, `JudgeAll`), is `D13-judging`'s; what a prospect list is, which validation set each `ValidProspectList` function accepts, and which candidates a prospect list's members and its matching count are, in which order, with which standing rejects, what a candidate's basis is and what `BasisOf` and `KindsOf` answer, and what a read's by basis and its changes since the list's previous open are, are `D14-prospect-lists`'; which pass calls which write is `D06-passes`'; what each kind's inputs are, what a candidate's subject holds, its pages, facts and website check among it, what each kind finds, what makes its found valid and when a prompts kind's result is failed are `D16-enrichers`'; what a website check finds and how a pass uses `DueEnrichments`, `EnrichmentMark` and `EnrichmentAfter` to fetch each inputs once are `D15-website-check`'s; which derived kinds a merge or a split re-runs, and that a merge moves the other candidate's pages to the survivor and a split leaves them, are `D12-ingest`'s; which targets an `enrich_target` walks, what a `site_text`, a `brief` or a `website_search` run records and how its counts are summed are `D06-passes`' and `D16-enrichers`'; which URL `add_page` may keep, and what it fetched, are `D10-tools`' and `D15-website-check`'s; which delivered events record a campaign use and what the delivery answers are `D06-passes`'; how a merge combines two candidates' campaign uses is `D12-ingest`'s; and which candidates the campaign filter admits, by its `min_campaign_age_days` and the uses' effective times, is `D14-prospect-lists`'. Opening, migrating, transactions and the failure seam are appkit's `db`; `D01-layout-and-run-seam` resolves the path and declares the root package's `Migrations()`. It is scrapes' store in shape, with a pass in place of a run and the candidates, their evidence, the rulings and the prospect lists beside it. Beside its tables it keeps, unused by this release's reads, the tables and columns the previous release reads, `cohorts`, `assessments`, `rejects.cohort`, `campaign_uses.cohort` and `passes.rescored`, which `0017_prospect_lists.sql` copies from and leaves in place for a rollback and a later release drops (expand then contract; `0018` and `0019` leave them).

Decisions cited by number are the decisions document that delivered candidates; "judgments decision N" is decision N of the later one, *two judgments and the PPP reader*, which splits each candidate's one ranking number into two judgments and splits storing rows from judging them; "cohorts decision N" is decision N of the next, the cohorts change, which replaces the one ranked list with the owner's saved searches, now prospect lists, and "log N" is entry N of its decisions log; "website decision N" is decision N of the next, *the website check*, which checks each candidate's own domain for a working website, and "website log X" is entry X of its decisions log; "enrichers decision N" is decision N of the next, *candidates enrichers*, which makes every fact an enrichment, derived or fetched, kept in a facts row, and "enrichers settled N" is item N of its settled answers; "site text decision N" is decision N of the next, *candidates — site text*, which adds the `site_text` kind, the pages kept on a candidate, the targeted enrich and its pass kind, and one rule for which passes run together, and "settled for this delivery" marks a choice its delivery made where that document left it open; "brief decision N" is decision N of the next, *candidates — the brief*, which adds the `brief` kind, the pages and facts on a candidate's subject and the facts rows' `brief` and `brief_at`, and "P N" is item N of the decisions log of its delivery; "category evidence decision N" is decision N of the next, *candidates — category evidence*, which lets a good brief with a category decide a candidate's categories alone and gives FMCSA's trucking category only to carriers with for-hire authority; "website search decision N" is decision N of the next, *candidates — website search*, which adds the `website_search` kind, the site it finds and confirms, and the facts rows' `website_source` and `website_search_at`, and "S N" is entry DN of the decisions log of its delivery; "campaigns decision N" is decision N of the latest, *campaigns*, which adds the campaigns app, whose events candidates consumes to keep each business's campaign history and filter prospect lists by it, and "campaigns log DN" is entry DN of the decisions log of its delivery. "Funnel rename decision N" is decision N of the decisions document of the funnel rename, which gives what candidates stores and answers the funnel's stage names: prospect lists, ingest, judgments. "Drive route outcome N" is outcome N of *candidates — exact drive time*, which adds the `drive_route` kind, the drive a routing service finds from the base to a company's street address, and the facts rows' `minutes_source`, `miles`, `route_address` and `drive_route_at`. "Basis decision N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the prospect basis change, which gives every prospect list member a basis and every open the changes since the previous one. "Revenue bands decision N" is decision N of *candidates — PPP jobs and the revenue bands*, which makes the revenue estimate headcount times a band for the kind of business: the facts rows gain the headcounts the links gave and the newest good brief's labels, the judgments gain the headcount a judge picked, and a judge writes a `headcount` estimate of its own beside the sources'.

## One database, one writer

candidates is the only writer of its database, and the database is the only record of its candidates, links, estimates, judgments, rulings, passes, prospect lists, reject records, enrichment results, facts rows, pages, campaign uses and drive times; nothing else is kept on disk (decision 1). `store.New` builds a store over a handle and opens or creates nothing. What a store holds when its handle is closed is what a store over the next `db.Open` of the same path holds; a test proves the records that way, never by reading tables. A store whose handle is failing (`SetFailing(true)`) answers every call with `ErrUnreachable` and changes nothing. `Config.Now` is the clock every stamp is read from, the moment a judge measures freshness at, and the clock a prospect list read measures filing age by (log 3); `Config.Rand` is the source pass ids, candidate ids and prospect list ids are drawn from. `Config.Origin` is the address the `drive_route` kind measures the drive from, which the store puts on every subject it builds, so it is part of that kind's inputs and of which route decides a facts row (drive route outcomes 2 and 3). `Run` hands in `Process.Now`, `Process.Rand` and the origin setting (`D01-layout-and-run-seam`), and the store has no default for any of them: an empty `Origin` gives every candidate empty `drive_route` inputs, so none is due and no route decides.

## By key

A pass costs in proportion to its rows, and the pages and the tools answer while it runs (orchestrator ruling, after a pass over the real FMCSA run of 16,706 rows never finished: every batch loaded, copied and compared the whole database under one lock). The store reaches every record the pass path needs by key through SQL, on decision 16's indexes: a link by its source key (the links' unique scraper and source id), the active candidates one of whose links holds a match key by the links' name key, state and ZIP, the oldest first, a candidate's links by the links' candidate, a candidate by its id, its estimates, judgments and rulings by its id, a ZIP's minutes by the ZIP. No write on the pass path reads a whole table, but `JudgeAll`, which a judge pass and a full ingest run and which covers every active candidate by definition, and so enumerates the active candidates once. An `Attach` touches its batch's rows and the candidates they attach to: an unchanged row costs the lookup of its link and the write of its run, last seen and gone; a candidate's display is re-derived from its own links once per batch for each candidate one of whose links the batch wrote, since a link's last seen and gone order the display (`D12-ingest`). `MarkGone` is one bounded statement per `MarkGone` call, over that call's scraper's links last seen in a run other than that call's run, after which it re-derives by key the display of each candidate whose link it marked, since gone orders the display, bounded by those candidates. `Judge` reads and writes each candidate it is given by its id, reading the candidate's record, its own links, rulings and facts row, and writing its estimates and judgments. An ingest judges nothing, so its cost is its rows alone (judgments decision 23). A ruling works the same way, touching only the candidates it names, each by key (its record, its own links, its rulings, its results and facts row, and the ZIP table's minutes for its display ZIP), so its cost is bounded by those candidates' links: a `not_same` judges none and re-derives nothing; an `exclude` or `include` judges `r.Candidate`; a merge or a split re-derives the display of, re-runs the derived kinds for, and judges the candidates it touches (`r.Candidate` and `r.Other`, or `r.Candidate` and the split's new candidate). It reads no table whole and refreshes no other candidate (`D12-ingest`). A prospect list write touches its one prospect list row, and a reject or unreject its one pair's records, its prospect list and its candidate, each by key. An enrichment write touches the candidates its results name, each by key, and rewrites their facts rows from their newest results; a `Derive` reads one window of the active candidates in id order and, for each, its subject by key (its links, its ZIP's minutes) and its newest result of the kind; and a read of a candidate's results, of the results of one inputs after a mark, or of one window of the due candidates in id order reads by key and by window on `0011`'s and `0012`'s indexes and the candidates' id (`D06-passes` and `D15-website-check` bound the pass's memory). `FillFacts` walks the active candidates once, at the start, writing a row only where one is missing, where the candidate holds a `drive_route` result, a set no larger than the prospects the owner has routed, or where the row's minutes and source disagree, which only a rollback leaves. The upkeep the start and the sweep run touches only its own table (log 36, after candidates was killed at its start on the `us` space under `memory_max = 256M`, the two upkeep writes loading, copying and comparing every candidate, link, estimate, judgments and ruling of a database holding 43,000 candidates and 54,000 links): `ReplaceZipDrive` reads and writes `zip_drive` alone, and `PrunePasses` reads and deletes in `passes` alone, so the memory either takes does not grow with the candidates, links, estimates, judgments or rulings the store holds. A page write touches its one candidate's pages, by key on `0013`'s index on the candidate and the URL, which is also how the cap is counted inside the write; `Pages` and `CountPages` read one candidate's pages on that index and `GetPage` one page by its number; and `DueAmong`, `DeriveAmong` and `Subject` read and write only the candidates whose ids they are given, each by key, so a targeted pass costs in proportion to its targets. A subject built for a `Prompter` kind also reads its candidate's pages, their text included, by key on `0013`'s index, and its newest website check of its own domain, by key on `0011`'s index on the candidate and the kind; no other subject reads them, so no untargeted walk reads a page (P1). No call of the store loads every record.

Every write is one write transaction of appkit's `db`, and the store holds no lock of its own across one: the only lock it keeps guards `Config.Rand`. A read goes through `db.Read`, which answers from what was last committed while a write transaction is open, since appkit's handle reads beside its one writer; so a page or a tool never waits for a batch, a judge, a ruling or a prospect list write. Calls racing still behave as one after another (`D12-ingest`, R-HM7C-HURP, R-O9H7-R42Y), because every write goes through appkit's one writer. Which statements the store issues is not contract; that a read answers while a write is open is, and so is the time a pass over thousands of rows takes (`D06-passes`).

## The records

A **candidate** (`store.Candidate`) is one business: its id, `cand_` and 16 lowercase hexadecimal digits; its status, `active` or `merged`, with the id it was merged into; its display fields (name, every name, street, city, state, ZIP, county as `MN-115`, phone, email, people); its tags (class and cargo); and when it was created and last updated (decision 4). Which link the display fields come from and when they change is `D12-ingest`'s.

A **link** (`store.Link`) is one row's attachment to one candidate: the candidate, the source key (scraper and source id), the run the row was last seen in and its digest, the source's reading of the row as last seen (`sources.Reading`: the keys, the envelope's common fields, the tags, the filing date, the fleet test and the row's fleet verdict and counts, the estimate, the operating evidence, the date the row shows the business existing since, the category and the government name match), how it attached (`source_key`, `match_key` or `decision`) with its confidence, when it was first and last seen, and whether it is gone (decision 6). A source key is unique among links: a row attaches to one candidate at a time.

An **estimate** is one source's `sources.Estimate` for one candidate, at most one per source per candidate; beside the sources' a judge writes one of its own, under the source word `judging.HeadcountSource`, `headcount`, formed from the facts row's headcounts and brief labels and the candidate's categories (revenue bands decision 8, `D13-judging`), which the estimates table holds and `Estimates` answers as any other, in source order (R-5AWM-GOIZ). A candidate's **judgments** (`store.Judgments`) are its two judgments as the last judge wrote them (judgments decision 1, funnel rename decisions 12 and 13): its **operating confidence**, how sure we are that it is a business operating today, with the **operating evidence** behind it, and its **revenue estimate**, a low, a high and a confidence, with the **headcount** it was formed from, a low and a high, equal unless the headcounts disagree, and its basis, the kinds and scrapers that gave it (revenue bands decisions 6 and 7); beside them whether the owner's standing ruling excludes it, the rules version and when they were calculated. They hold no fact: the facts the prospect list filters read (`D14-prospect-lists`) are its facts row's, below, read at every read: the date the business is known since, the government name pattern its name matched, the newest filing among its links not gone, its categories each with its source, the **fleet verdict**, `pass`, `fail` or `unknown`, with the power units and drivers of the FMCSA link it was read from (cohorts decision 13, log 20), the **headcounts** its links gave, each with its scraper, kind, count and date, and the **brief labels**, those of its newest good brief (revenue bands decisions 4 and 5), the **website** state, `https`, `http`, `none` or `unknown`, with the URL the check or the search landed on, empty unless `https` or `http` (website decision 14), and which of the two decided it, `check`, `search` or empty for neither (website search decisions 7 and 10), and the drive minutes, with which result decided them, `route` or `zip`, the route's miles and the address the route was found to (drive route outcome 7). A prospect list's member (`store.Ranked`) carries both, its judgments and its facts, the facts at their unknown values when the candidate has no facts row. The verdict replaces the boolean fleet test in the Go type, the tools and the pages under one name, `fleet_verdict` (log 10); the `fleet` column the store wrote beside it for an earlier release is dropped by `0011` (website decision 22). The judgments are stored in `judgments`, which `0017` fills from the previous release's `assessments` with the judgment columns only, `scored` becoming `calculated`; a judge writes `judgments` and `estimates` and never `assessments`, which the previous release keeps reading and which goes stale after the upgrade (Known limits). Its **operating key** is the operating confidence scaled by a million and rounded to an integer, -1 when it has none: what a prospect list's order compares operating confidence by (`D14-prospect-lists`, `D13-judging`). A judgment the evidence does not support is empty, never 0 (judgments decision 2). What a judge computes is `D13-judging`'s; judgments are written only by a judge: a judge pass, a full ingest, or a ruling re-judging the candidates it touches and no other (`JudgeAll`, `Judge`, `Decide`). An ingest of one run or one scraper stores links and writes no judgment. A read shows what was last written and computes nothing, so a store read between two judges shows each judgment as the last judge wrote it and each fact as the last enrichment wrote it; nothing decays between writes (judgments decision 6). `GetJudgments` answers a merged candidate's last judgments too, since a merge leaves them; a merged candidate has no facts row.

A **ruling** (`store.Ruling`) is one of the owner's decisions: its kind (`merge`, `split`, `not_same`, `exclude`, `include`), the candidate it names, the other candidate (the other side of a merge or a `not_same`, the new candidate of a split), the source keys a split moved, why, who (user id and email) and when (decision 10). Rulings are never changed or removed. The `exclude` and `include` tools and `decide` all record theirs through `Decide`, unchanged (cohorts decision 12).

A **prospect list** (`store.ProspectList`) is one saved search: its id, `pl_` and 16 lowercase hexadecimal digits; its name, free text, not unique; its size; its filters (`store.ProspectListFilters`); its order (`[]store.ProspectListOrder`); who created it (user id and email) and when; and who last changed it and when, which creation sets to the creator and the creation time (cohorts decision 1, log 24). Its members are never stored: `ReadProspectList` works them out at every read (cohorts decisions 3 and 25). The store keeps the filters and the order in their **normal form**, the categories de-duplicated and in byte order, the basis words de-duplicated and in the order records, partial, enriched, and an empty order as the default order, so an edit that changes nothing compares equal and writes nothing (log 8, log 16). An edit (`store.ProspectListEdit`) names the prospect list and any of its name, size, filters and order, nil for each part it leaves alone; a passed filter set or order replaces the stored one whole, so `&ProspectListFilters{}` clears every filter and `&[]ProspectListOrder{}` sets the default order (cohorts decision 6, log 8). A delete removes the row; its reject records and its opens stay.

A **reject record** (`store.Reject`) is one reject or unreject: its kind (`reject` or `unreject`), the prospect list and the candidate it names, why, who and when (cohorts decision 10). Records are never changed or removed, not at an unreject and not when their prospect list is deleted (cohorts decision 11). Which record stands for a prospect list and a candidate is `D14-prospect-lists`' **standing reject**. `ProspectListName` is filled on every read from the prospect list the record names while that prospect list exists, so it is empty once the prospect list is deleted.

An **open** is one recorded open of a prospect list (basis decision 7): a row of `opens` holding the prospect list's id, when it was opened, who opened it (user id and email), the definition it was opened under (its filters, order and size, in normal form), the members it answered, in rank order, each with its basis then, and its **passing** set, the ids of the matching candidates in rank order before the size cut, so a member that joined can be told as having moved up from below the cut or as newly passing (basis decision 9). It is a `store.Open`, each member a `store.OpenMember`, and `Opens` answers a list's opens newest first, a deleted list's included, as `Rejects` answers a candidate's reject records; `ReadProspectList` and `OpenProspectList` read a list's newest open to answer its changes (`D14-prospect-lists`' previous open). No tool and no page exposes `Opens`: it is the store's record, read by the tests, and who opened is answered nowhere else. The row keeps `at` as RFC 3339 UTC to the second and the filters, order, members and passing set as JSON, as the other tables keep theirs; no other release reads them. Opens are never changed or removed, not when their prospect list is deleted, and never pruned (basis decisions 10 and 12). A **basis** is never stored: it is `BasisOf` the candidate's results, worked out at every read (basis decision 1), and an open keeps only the basis each member had then.

An **enrichment result** (`store.Enrichment`) is one thing found about a candidate, from its own links, from the ZIP table or from outside (website decision 20, enrichers decisions 1 and 4): the candidate it is on; its kind, one of `enrichers.Kinds()`, `drive_minutes`, `link_facts`, `website_check`, `website_search`, `site_text`, `brief` and `drive_route` (`D16-enrichers`); its **inputs**, what it was found from, a ZIP, a fingerprint of the candidate's links, a domain, a fingerprint of the candidate's name, city and state, a website URL, a fingerprint of the candidate's pages or a fingerprint of its street, city, state, ZIP and the origin; its source word (`zip_drive`, `links`, the domain checked, `prompts` or `google_routes`); what was found, as the kind's JSON object; what it cost and when it was recorded. Results are history (website decision 21): never changed but by a merge, which moves the other candidate's results to the survivor, and never removed; a redo adds a result, and a candidate's **newest result** of a kind is the one recorded last, as `Enrichments` orders them, not the one with the latest time, so a result moved by a merge keeps its place. A candidate is **due** for a kind when it is active, its inputs for the kind now are not empty, and it has no result of the kind or its newest one was found from other inputs (enrichers decision 3): no age makes a result due.

A **facts row** (`store.Facts`) is the projection of a candidate's newest result of each kind (enrichers decision 5): the link facts from its newest `link_facts` result, the website state and URL and which result decided them from its website decision, below, the drive minutes from its drive decision, below, each kind's facts at their unknown values when it has none, and, per kind written, when the result it came from was recorded; `site_text` gives no fact of its own, only that time (site text decision 11), which a prospect list's member answers as when site text was last written for it (`D14-prospect-lists`). `brief` gives the row's `brief`, the summary of its newest result whose run is not failed, its **newest good brief**, and, when that result gave at least one category, the row's categories: that result's alone, each with the source word `brief`, in place of the link facts' (category evidence decisions 1 and 2); a good brief with no category leaves the link facts' categories standing, and the two sets are never mixed; the newest good brief also gives the row's `brief_labels`, its labels, as `link_facts` gives the row's `headcounts` (revenue bands decisions 4 and 5, R-LQ8Z-ZH75); its time, like every kind's, is that of its newest result, failed or not, which a prospect list's member answers as when the brief was last written for it (brief decisions 6 to 8, `D14-prospect-lists`). The **website decision** of a candidate's results, read newest first, has a source, a state, a URL, a title, a why and a time (R-X4EF-J7W4): when its newest `website_check` result found a site, `https` or `http`, that result decides, with the source `check`; otherwise its newest `website_search` result whose run is not failed and whose confirmation loaded a page, its **confirmed search**, decides, with the source `search`, the confirmation's state, URL and title and the agent's why; otherwise nothing decides, the source, URL and why are empty, and the state, title and time are the newest check's, or `unknown` with none (website search decision 7). So a later check that finds a site takes over, a newer search that finds nothing, fails or names a site that does not load leaves an older confirmed site standing, and a full ingest of the row from the results gives the same answer. The row keeps the decision's state, URL and source, the source as `store.WebsiteByCheck` or `store.WebsiteBySearch`, and `website_search` adds its time as every kind does; the candidate page reads all six parts from the results (`D08-pages`). It is written in the same write as the result that changes it, so it is never behind its results, and it can be rebuilt from them at any time; it is what the prospect lists, the tools and the pages read a candidate's facts from. The **drive decision** (R-32D2-J500) has minutes, a source, miles and a route address: the newest `drive_route` result whose inputs equal the candidate's `drive_route` inputs now, its street, city, state and ZIP with the store's origin, that is not failed and found minutes decides, with the source `route`, its miles and the address the service resolved; otherwise the newest `drive_minutes` result gives the minutes, with the source `zip` when it found some and empty when it found none, and no miles or route address (drive route outcome 7). So a newer ZIP result never displaces a matching route (outcome 9), a route found from an older address or another origin stops deciding at the next write of the row, and a failed or unroutable route leaves an older matching route deciding, as a failed brief leaves the last good one. The words are `store.MinutesByRoute` and `store.MinutesByZip`, as the facts row's `minutes_source` stores them; `drive_route` adds its time as every kind does. Beside the row, `GetFacts` answers the candidate's **records**, the link facts' categories with their sources, read at the read from its newest `link_facts` result and kept in no column, so the categories the records give stay readable when a brief decided (category evidence decision 4).

Which set decided is read from the set, never stored (category evidence decisions 3 and 11): `store.ClassifiedBy` of a set of categories is `brief` when every one carries the source `brief`, empty when there is none, and `links` otherwise, and `store.RecordsDiffer` of the categories and the records is true when the brief decided and the records name other categories than it. Both are pure functions over what the facts row and the records hold, so the `candidate` tool and the candidate page derive the same answer from one `GetFacts`, and a prospect list member's classification is `ClassifiedBy` of its facts' categories, the same categories the category filter reads, so the filter and the label never disagree.

A **page** (`store.Page`) is one copy of a web page kept on a candidate (site text decision 1): the candidate it is on, its **number**, the URL it was fetched from, its title, its text, the bytes of its body read, when it was fetched, and who added it, by user id and email. The store mints the number, which names the copy among every page the store holds, not only the candidate's, so a merge that moves a page keeps its number and its text page's path, `/candidates/<id>/pages/<n>`, names it on the survivor (`D08-pages`, `D12-ingest`). Adding a URL a candidate already holds keeps a newer copy beside the old; the newest copy of each URL, the one with the highest number, is the one read. Pages are never changed but by a merge and never removed.

A **campaign use** (`store.CampaignUse`) is one campaign's use of one candidate, as campaigns' events told it (campaigns decisions 16 and 17): the candidate it is recorded under, the campaign's id, the prospect list the candidate was added from, empty when it was added one at a time, when it was added, when it was rejected and why, and when a piece was last delivered to it. A zero time is one not known: a rejection or a delivery may be told before the add it follows, since the bus retries and does not order. A candidate holds at most one use of a campaign, since a business is in a campaign once (campaigns decision 3). A use's **effective time** is when it was added, or, while no add is known, the earlier of its rejection and its last delivery: the moment the prospect list filter measures a campaign's age from (`D14-prospect-lists`, campaigns decision 18, where used means added). A rejected use is still a use; that a reject is final inside the campaign is campaigns' (campaigns decision 5, campaigns log D4), and it touches no prospect list reject record here.

A **pass** (`store.Pass`) is one ingest, one judge, one enrich or one enrich over a target. Its id is `pass_` and 16 lowercase hexadecimal digits. It keeps its **kind**, what it does: `ingest` stores one run's rows as links and marks the vanished ones gone, or, started by the `ingest` tool, does so for one scraper's current run or, named no scraper, for every scraper's current run and then re-runs every derived kind for every active candidate and judges (a **full ingest**; funnel rename decision 9), `judge` judges every active candidate, `enrich` records what the enrichers find for the candidates due, derived kinds before fetched ones, and `enrich_target` runs one kind for one prospect list's members or one candidate, the due ones or, forced, all of them (`D06-passes`; site text decisions 12 to 14); its **origin**, what started it: `run`, a `scrape.finished` delivery, `cron`, a delivery of the daily trigger's event, `ingest`, the `ingest` tool, `judge`, the `judge` tool, `enrich`, the `enrich` tool, for either enrich kind, or `pass`, another pass (website log A1); its **trigger**, the run id an ingest's delivery named, the trigger's slug `judge_candidates` for a judge the bus started, `ingest` or `judge` for a pass the tool of that name started, `enrich` for an enrich or an `enrich_target` the tool started, or the id of the pass that started it (judgments decision 25, website log A1); for an `enrich_target` its **target**, the prospect list or candidate id it walks, and its **enrich kind**, the kind it walks, both empty for every other pass; for an ingest the `ingest` tool started its **scraper**, the one scraper it ingests, empty for a full ingest and every other pass; its **status**, one of `running`, `done` and `failed`, and when failed one of four **reasons**, `scrapes`, `interrupted`, `store` and `prompts` (which failure is which is `D06-passes`'; `prompts` settled for this delivery, S5); **started** and **finished**, each UTC to the second; the nineteen counts, each 0 until the pass is done, `cost` among them in nano-dollars; its starter's user id and email; the request id it was started under; and, for a delivery, the bus event's id, the **delivery**, an internal column no tool or page shows: it is how a redelivered event is told from a new one (`D06-passes`).

Every id the store mints, a pass's, a candidate's or a prospect list's, is its prefix and 8 bytes read from `Config.Rand`, written as 16 lowercase hexadecimal digits, drawn again when it is the id of a record of its kind the store holds; a prospect list id is drawn again also when a reject record names it, so a deleted prospect list's id is never reused while its records stand (cohorts decision 1).

## Passes

A pass has three writes. `AddPass` records it `running`, stamping started and minting the id. It is refused `ErrDelivered` when its delivery is one an existing pass carries, or when its origin is `run` and its trigger is the run id an existing pass of origin `run` names, whatever that pass's status; and otherwise `ErrBusy` while a pass that blocks it is `running`: one of the same kind over the same **scope**, the scope of an ingest a delivery started being its run, of one the `ingest` tool started its scraper, empty for a full ingest, which is the corpus, of an `enrich_target` its target, and of a judge and an enrich the corpus (site text decision 15). Every other pair runs together: two ingests of different runs, an ingest of one scraper beside a delivered ingest of a run of that scraper and beside a full ingest, a judge beside an ingest, a full ingest or an enrich beside an `enrich_target`, and two `enrich_target` passes over different targets, a candidate and a prospect list holding it among them; two over one target are busy whatever kinds they walk (settled for this delivery, S10). An ingest naming a run another ingest already names is refused `ErrDelivered` before its scope is looked at, so an ingest's scope refuses nothing an ingest delivery can send. A judge's trigger is the same slug every day, so the trigger refuses only run ids, and a redelivered trigger event is told by its delivery alone. `FinishPass` ends a `running` pass `done` with the nineteen counts its work gathered; `FailPass` ends a `running` pass `failed` with a reason and leaves everything the pass wrote in place, since every write is idempotent and the next pass or a full ingest repairs it (decision 11). Of two calls racing on the same pass one succeeds and the other is told it is no longer running (`ErrEnded`). Nothing moves a pass back.

The counts a pass finishes with are the sums of the `store.Counts` its writes answered: `Attach` answers `Rows`, `New`, `Changed`, `Unchanged`, `Created` and `Linked` for a batch; `MarkGone` answers `Gone`; `Judge` and `JudgeAll` answer `Judged` and `Unranged`. So an ingest of a run or of one scraper finishes with `Judged` and `Unranged` 0 and a judge with only those two. `Derive` answers the results it recorded, which a pass sums as `Derived`. An enrich finishes with only `Derived`, `Due`, `Fetched`, `HTTPS`, `HTTP` and `None`, the last five counted by its work itself, since no store write answers them (`D06-passes`, `D15-website-check`); a full ingest with its ingest's and judge's counts and `Derived`; an `enrich_target` with the enrich counts of the kind it walked and, for a prompts kind, `Runs`, `RunsFailed`, `Pages` and `Cost`, which its work counts itself, every other pass leaving those four 0. What each counts is the owning document's.

`GetPass` answers any pass, whoever started it. `ListPasses` is one window of the passes in pass order, newest first: those after a position, the started text and id of the last pass of the window before, at most `n`; `RunningPasses` every pass still `running`, which the start finishes `failed` with `interrupted` (`D06-passes`). `PrunePasses` removes, in one write, every `done` pass beyond the newest keep count of `done` passes and every `failed` pass beyond the newest keep count of `failed` passes; a `running` pass is neither counted nor removed (decision 16). A pruned pass is gone from every read, its delivery and trigger with it. Candidates, links, estimates, judgments, rulings, prospect lists, reject records, enrichment results, pages and campaign uses are never pruned; the prune touches none of them. A judge replaces a candidate's estimates and judgments (`D13-judging`); a merge moves the merged candidate's links to the survivor, and its enrichment results and pages with them, deletes the merged candidate's facts row, and the merged candidate otherwise keeps what it had at the merge: its display fields, tags, estimates, last judgments and reject records (`D12-ingest`).

## The ZIP table

`ReplaceZipDrive` replaces the whole table with the map it is given in one write; the start hands it the root package's `ZipDrive()`, so the table is always the release's (decision 9). `ZipMinutes` answers one ZIP's minutes. `drive_minutes` reads the table for each candidate's display ZIP when it derives (`D16-enrichers`); a judge no longer reads it (`D13-judging`). A new table reaches the facts only through a full ingest, which forces every derived kind, since a ZIP that did not change leaves its candidate's `drive_minutes` inputs unchanged.

## Prospect lists

`CreateProspectList` mints the id, stamps created and changed with the caller and the clock, and keeps the normal form of what it was given. `EditProspectList` replaces each part the edit passes, and when the prospect list that results is the one stored it writes nothing and keeps changed and its user as they were, so an empty edit or a repeated one is the same as none (log 8). `DeleteProspectList` removes the prospect list and answers it as it was; deleting it again is `ErrNotFound` (cohorts decision 7). `GetProspectList` answers a definition without its members. Each write refuses a part a `ValidProspectList` function refuses with `ErrInvalid` before it looks for the prospect list, and an empty user id `ErrUnreachable`, as no consumer sends one (log 7). Creating twice makes two prospect lists, as cohorts decision 18 accepts.

`ReadProspectList` answers the definition, its **matching** count, its **classified** count, how many of its members the brief classified (category evidence decision 5), its **members** in their order, each the candidate, its judgments as the last judge wrote them and its facts the facts row's at the read, and its standing rejects, newest first, each with the prospect list's name, all as `D14-prospect-lists` defines them, at the clock `Config.Now()` gives once for the read (log 3). It also answers how many of the members have each basis, and the changes since the list's previous open (`D14-prospect-lists`), and records nothing: it is the peek (basis decisions 4, 8 and 11). `OpenProspectList` answers exactly what `ReadProspectList` would at its one clock reading and, in the same write, records the open: the time, the caller, the definition, the answered members with their bases and the passing set, so two opens of one list take turns and each compares against the one before (basis decisions 7 and 8). It refuses an empty user id `ErrUnreachable`, as no consumer sends one, and a list it does not hold, a deleted one included, `ErrNotFound`, recording nothing. Membership is one query over the judgments joined to the candidates and, by a left join, to the facts rows, every fact filter and order key reading the facts row and a missing row reading every fact unknown, cut by the size, beside one count (cohorts decision 25); the store holds no more than the members.

`ListProspectLists` and `ListProspectListsReversed` are the prospect list windows the `prospect_lists` tool and the landing read, in **prospect list order**, name then id, each in byte order (log 18), a window after a position or before it, as the passes' are.

`Reject` records a reject or an unreject. A reject of a pair that has no standing reject, or an unreject of one that has, records one record and answers it; a reject of a pair already rejected writes nothing and answers the record that stands; an unreject of a pair with no standing reject writes nothing and answers a record of kind `unreject` naming the prospect list, the candidate and the prospect list's name, with no why, user or time (log 27), so every reject and unreject is safe to repeat (cohorts decisions 10 and 18). It refuses a blank why `ErrInvalid`, a prospect list the store does not hold, a deleted one included, `ErrNotFound`, then a candidate it does not hold `ErrNotFound`, then a merged candidate `ErrInvalid` (log 6). `Rejects` answers every record naming a candidate, active or merged, newest first (cohorts decision 11). A prospect list's standing rejects are listed whatever the candidate's status (`D14-prospect-lists`), so a reject of a candidate that is later merged keeps standing on its prospect list's read and cannot be cleared, since `Reject` refuses a merged candidate `ErrInvalid` either way; a merged candidate is no member of any prospect list, so the row only shows.

## Enrichments

`AddEnrichments` records a pass's fetched results in one write, in the order given, each stamped with the clock, and rewrites each written candidate's facts row in the same write; it skips a result naming a candidate that is merged or unknown, so no result is ever on a merged candidate, as no link is, and it answers what it recorded; were every candidate sharing an inputs merged between the pass's selection and its write, that result is skipped and recorded nowhere, and the inputs may be fetched again for the survivor, in the same pass when its id lies after the cursor, or in a later one. It accepts what the registry's `Valid` accepts for the kind (`D16-enrichers`) with non-empty inputs and a finite, non-negative cost, and keeps `Found` compacted, as given otherwise; anything else, an unknown kind, a found that is not JSON or not the kind's, empty inputs, or a cost that is negative, NaN or infinite (which SQLite would store as NULL and `cost`'s not-null refuses), is a value no consumer sends, refused `ErrUnreachable` with nothing recorded. The store does no UTF-8 repair of its own: the enrichers make `Found` with `json.Marshal`, which already replaces each invalid byte with U+FFFD. That a merge racing it leaves the result moved or unrecorded, never on the merged candidate, is `D12-ingest`'s, which states the store's writes behave as one after another. `Enrichments` answers every result on a candidate, merged or not, newest first, each as it was stored, whichever write recorded it.

`Derive` runs one derived kind over one window of the active candidates in id order after a cursor, in one write: for each it builds the candidate's subject from the records (`D16-enrichers`), and records the kind's result, at the clock, when the inputs are not empty and either the candidate is due or the call is forced, rewriting the facts row beside it. It answers the last id it examined, empty once the walk is over, and how many results it recorded. An enrich derives what is due; a full ingest, and a merge or a split for the candidates it touches, force it, so every derived kind is recomputed whatever its inputs (`D06-passes`, `D12-ingest`). The derived kinds run inside the store (decided for this delivery; enrichers decision 9, which has a merge or a split re-run them in the same call, and decision 15 with its decisions log): a merge or a split re-runs them in the same write as its moves and its re-judge, as the store already judges, and the enrichers package is pure functions the store calls.

`DueEnrichments` is one window of the candidates due for a kind, in id order after a cursor, each answered with its inputs now, so a fetching pass groups them by inputs without reading them again. The store answers whatever inputs the kind names: a display email with no domain key (Gmail and the like) has empty inputs for `website_check` and is never due (website log P12, enrichers decision 3). `EnrichmentMark` answers an opaque mark of what is recorded so far, and `EnrichmentAfter` the last result of a kind with given inputs recorded after a mark, which is how a pass, reading the mark when its work begins, tells inputs it already fetched across batches without holding a set of them (`D15-website-check`).

`DueAmong` and `DeriveAmong` are the targeted forms (site text decision 13): each takes the ids an `enrich_target` walks, a prospect list's members in their order or one candidate, and a force flag. `DueAmong` answers, in the order given and each once, the ids naming an active candidate whose inputs for the kind are not empty and that is due, or every such id when forced, each with its inputs, so a fetched or prompts kind gets its targets without a window; `DeriveAmong` records the derived kind's result for the same set in one write, as a `Derive` window over those ids alone would, and answers how many it recorded. Due and force mean the same for every kind, the same inputs rule a corpus enrich uses, so a forced derived kind records a fresh result for each target as a full ingest does. An id the store does not hold, or a merged candidate's, is skipped rather than refused, since the targets are fixed when the pass is recorded and a ruling may merge one while it runs. `Subject` answers the subject `D16-enrichers` defines for one candidate, its kept pages and its facts row's facts among it (brief decisions 3 and 4), and its display street and phone, its facts row's website source and its newest website check of its own domain (website search decision 3, R-X6U8-ARDI), which a prompts kind makes a run's input from; for `site_text` the inputs are the website URL its facts row holds, so a website check that lands elsewhere, or a search whose site is confirmed, makes the candidate due again, and for `brief` a fingerprint of its pages' URLs and their newest copies' numbers, so a newer copy that `add_page` or a `site_text` run keeps makes it due again and nothing else does (brief decisions 1 and 2).

`GetFacts` answers a candidate's facts row, with its records read beside it, `ErrNotFound` when it has none: no result yet, merged, or not yet filled after `0012`. `FillFacts` writes the missing rows, once, at the start before `READY=1` (`D03-serve`), from the newest results each candidate already holds, and touches no row already written but those of candidates holding a `drive_route` result, which it re-projects at the store's origin, so a changed origin takes effect at the start that changes it, and those whose minutes and source disagree, minutes with no source or a source with no minutes, which only the previous release writes; and it re-projects every row's headcounts and brief labels, so a row written before `0020` gets them at the first start after it, and a row the previous release wrote or rewrote after a rollback gets them back at the start of the roll forward (revenue bands decision 19, R-LRGW-D8XU); it is safe at every start; a failing store answers it `ErrUnreachable` and it changes nothing, as every store call does then (R-OD8H-2EDG). Every member a prospect list read answers carries the facts row's facts, read in the same read, or every fact unknown when there is no row, beside its judgments.

A merge moves the merged candidate's results to the survivor with its links, keeping the order they were recorded in, deletes the merged candidate's facts row, and then records on the survivor a fresh result of every derived kind whose inputs are not empty; a split leaves the results on the candidate it split and records fresh results, likewise, for both; every other write but `AddEnrichments`, `Derive` and `DeriveAmong` leaves the results alone, and only those, `FillFacts` and a merge or a split write a facts row (website decision 21, enrichers decision 15, `D12-ingest`).

## Pages

`AddPage` records one copy of a page on an active candidate (site text decisions 1 to 3): the URL, title, text and size the caller fetched, who added it, the clock as when it was fetched, and a number the database mints. It is refused `ErrNotFound` for a candidate the store does not hold, `ErrInvalid` for a merged one, and `ErrPageCap` when the URL is new to the candidate and the candidate already holds `PageCap` (20) distinct URLs; a URL it already holds is always accepted, since a newer copy adds no URL. The cap is counted inside the write, so two adds racing cannot carry a candidate past it. An empty URL or user id or a negative size is a value no consumer sends. `AddPage` writes nothing else, no enrichment result, no facts row and no pass, since a page is tied to no pass and no run (site text decision 4). The tool that calls it checks the host, the cap and the fetch first and answers each with its own sentence (`D10-tools`, `D15-website-check`).

`Pages` answers a candidate's newest copy of each URL, newest first by number, without the text, which the `candidate` tool and the candidate page list; `GetPage` one copy with its text, by the candidate and its number, `ErrNotFound` when that number is not on that candidate, so a number a merge moved is reached only through the survivor; and `CountPages` the distinct URLs and the copies a candidate holds, which a `site_text` run's result and its pass's `Pages` count read when the run ends (`D06-passes`; settled for this delivery, S6). A merge moves the other candidate's pages to the survivor, keeping each number, and a split leaves them on the candidate it split (`D12-ingest`); no other write touches a page. The subject's pages are a second read of the same rows, with their text: the newest copy of each URL, in the order the URLs were first kept (`D16-enrichers`, brief decision 3), which `Pages`, newest first and without the text, does not answer. It is no method of its own, only part of the subject, and the store reads it only for a `Prompter` kind (P1, recorded below).

## Campaign uses

`AddCampaignUse`, `RejectCampaignUse` and `DeliverCampaignUse` record what a delivered campaigns event told (`D06-passes`): the candidate, the campaign, the prospect list or the reason the event carried, and the event's own time, which the store keeps in UTC to the second. Each first follows `merged_into` from the candidate it is given to the **active end** of its chain, the active candidate it was merged into last, and records there, since campaigns keeps the id it was given and the history must land on the business as it stands now (campaigns log D1). Each creates the use when the candidate holds none of that campaign, so a reject or a delivery told before its add still leaves a use; and each changes only its own part of the use: an add keeps the earliest add, with the prospect list that add named; a reject keeps the first rejection, with its reason; a delivery keeps the latest delivery. So a repeated event changes nothing, and events told in any order leave the same use (campaigns decision 16). An empty candidate or campaign is refused `ErrInvalid` and a candidate the store does not hold `ErrNotFound`, each recording nothing; a zero time is a value no consumer sends, since every bus event carries its time.

`CampaignUses` answers the uses recorded under exactly the id it is given, following no pointer: a merge this release records moves the merged candidate's uses to the survivor (`D12-ingest`), so it answers none after that merge (a merge the previous release recorded leaves them behind, Known limits), and the candidate tool and page show the survivor's. They come newest add first, a use with no add known after every other, ties by campaign id, and the read is one candidate's rows on the table's key. No other write touches a use but a merge, which combines the merged candidate's uses into the survivor's (`D12-ingest`); a split, an exclude and an include leave them where they are, and nothing removes one.

## Reads

`GetCandidate` answers any candidate, active or merged: a merged candidate's record carries its status and the id it was merged into. `Links`, `Estimates`, `GetJudgments`, `Rulings` and `Rejects` answer what the store holds for one candidate; each tells an unknown candidate (`ErrNotFound`) from a known one with nothing to show, as `Pages` and `CampaignUses` do, except `GetJudgments`, which answers `ErrNotFound` for a candidate not yet judged too, and `GetFacts`, which answers `ErrNotFound` for a candidate with no facts row. A candidate's judgments are the last judge's, and its facts are `GetFacts`' at the read. Links come not gone first, then newest filing first, so the freshest evidence leads; rulings and reject records newest first. `ListCandidates` is one window of the active candidates newest first, those after a position, the created text and id of the last candidate of the window before, at most `n`, optionally only those one of whose names contains a text, case-insensitively, those at a ZIP, or those one of whose links has a phone key (decision 14). Beside `ListPasses` and `ListProspectLists` sit their reversed windows, which the landing's backward step and its last window read (`D08-pages`): `ListPassesReversed` answers at most `n` of the passes that precede a position in pass order, nearest first, and `ListProspectListsReversed` likewise in prospect list order; an empty id there means the end of the order, so the reversed window from no position is the oldest passes, or the prospect lists last in order, last first. The position need not be any entry's. The tools never call them. Each window is ordered and cut by the database on an index (appkit's D25, the suite's Storage convention): a read holds at most the window it was asked for, and the tools and pages ask for one more than they show to learn whether more follow. The store knows nothing of tokens; the tools and pages encode and decode them with `appkit/paging` from the position fields an entry carries (`D10-tools`, `D08-pages`). All lists are never nil.

## Errors

`ErrNotFound`, `ErrBusy`, `ErrDelivered`, `ErrEnded`, `ErrInvalid` and `ErrPageCap` are answers about the records; `ErrInvalid` is the answer of the writes whose arguments come from a person: `Decide`'s, a ruling that is malformed or one the records forbid (`D12-ingest`), `CreateProspectList`'s and `EditProspectList`'s, a part a validator refuses, `Reject`'s, a blank why or a merged candidate, `AddPage`'s, a merged candidate, and the campaign use writes', an empty candidate or campaign, which the delivery skips before it calls (`D06-passes`). `ErrPageCap` is `AddPage`'s alone, a URL past the cap, which the tool answers with a sentence naming the cap (`D10-tools`). Every other error satisfies `errors.Is(err, ErrUnreachable)`, and every consumer answers it with `Unreachable` (a tool's `unavailable`, a page's unavailable answer, a delivery's `Fail`, a pass failed `store`), never quoting the cause. A done context, a failing handle, a random source that fails and a write given a value no consumer sends all end there, the last changing nothing. No error variable is added for prospect lists.

## `db status`

`candidates db status` writes what appkit's `db.Status` writes for `state/candidates.db` and the root package's `Migrations()`, and creates nothing.

## Known limits

- **A rollback across `0015` shows search results unmarked** (website search decision 14): to the previous release a `website_search` result is a row of a kind its registry lacks, which its enrich never walks and its candidate page and `candidate` tool show as the found object's members as stored, never marked failed.
- **A rollback across `0015` drops a searched site from the row it re-projects**: the previous release's own re-projection of a candidate takes `website` and `website_url` from the newest check alone, so a site the search found drops out of that row, and out of the `website` filter, until this release next writes it; until then its judge reads a found site in that row as the check's, live evidence this release's judge would not count (`D13-judging`).
- **A roll forward across a rollback may leave a stale source**: a row the previous release rewrote may carry a `website_source` that no longer agrees with its site, `search` beside the check's `none`, or empty beside a site it wrote, until the candidate's next result or an `ingest`, whose forced `link_facts` walk re-projects every row (`D06-passes`); so an `ingest` follows a roll forward across a rollback.
- **A rollback across `0016` loses campaign history meanwhile**: the previous release accepts no campaigns event, so what campaigns tells while it runs is recorded nowhere, and a business added to a campaign then shows no use and passes the campaign filter after the roll forward until a later event of that campaign names it.
- **A merge on the previous release leaves the uses behind**: it does not know `campaign_uses`, so a merge it records leaves the merged candidate's uses under the merged id, where `CampaignUses` of the survivor does not see them and the campaign filter does not count them; a later write follows the pointer to the survivor as usual.
- **A prospect list's campaign filter is unknown to the previous release**: it decodes a list's stored filters into its own filters type, which ignores `min_campaign_age_days`, so it reads such a prospect list as though the filter were absent, and an edit it makes of that prospect list's filters writes them back without it.
- **`force` cannot clear a wrong found site** (website search decision 7): a search that finds nothing never unseats a confirmed one; only a later confirmed search replaces it, or a check that finds a site.
- **A facts row's drive is stale between an address change and the next result**: an `Attach` that changes a candidate's display street, city, state or ZIP changes no facts row (R-OU7I-97OR), so a route found from the old address keeps deciding the row's minutes until the next write of the row, which the ingest's follow-on enrich makes when its derived kinds record a result on the candidate, the changed links giving `link_facts` other inputs (`D06-passes`); a read in between shows the old route.
- **A rollback across `0018` can leave a stale route label** (drive route outcome 15, decided by the delivery): the previous release does not know `drive_route`; its facts upsert writes `minutes` from its newest `drive_minutes` result and `drive_route_at` as for any kind among the candidate's results, and leaves `minutes_source`, `miles` and `route_address` as this release wrote them (R-38GK-FZPH). So a row it rewrites while it runs may read ZIP minutes beside the source `route` and the old route's miles and address, its drive time filter reading the ZIP minutes; a row it inserts or fills with minutes carries no source; and a row whose minutes it clears, its ZIP gone from the table, keeps the source `zip`. Each stays so after the roll forward until the candidate's next result is recorded or the start's `FillFacts`, which re-projects every candidate holding a `drive_route` result and every row whose minutes and source disagree (R-LRGW-D8XU); those cover every row the previous release can leave wrong, so every row is right again once the roll forward is up. A `drive_route` result is, to the previous release, a row of a kind its registry lacks, which its enrich never walks and its candidate page and `candidate` tool show as the found object's members as stored, never marked failed.

- **A rollback across `0017` hides what this release wrote** (funnel rename decision 17, D6 of its delivery log): the previous release reads `cohorts`, `rejects.cohort`, `campaign_uses.cohort` and `assessments`, all intact as of the upgrade. Invisible to it are the prospect lists created or edited since; the rejects made since, which it lists for their candidate with an empty list and no name and which stand in no list's reject set; the campaign uses recorded since, which it reads with an empty list, since this release writes the list into `campaign_uses.prospect_list` and leaves `cohort` empty (R-B9KK-F52P); and the judgments written since, `assessments` staying as of the upgrade until its own daily judge rewrites it. A prospect list deleted since reappears to it. While it runs, it records the adds the new campaigns tells with an empty list, since that `campaign.member.added` carries `prospect_list`, an attribute it does not read. It answers the passes recorded since with this release's kind, origin and trigger words and its own judged count as 0. Nothing is lost: every row stays in this release's tables.

- **A roll forward after a rollback does not copy again**: `0017` has run and does not run twice, so what the previous release wrote meanwhile is invisible or stale here: the lists it created and the edits it made to migrated lists, written into `cohorts` and unseen here, so a migrated list it deleted reappears here, the rejects it wrote into `rejects.cohort`, the uses it recorded with an empty `prospect_list`, the passes it recorded, answered with its kind, origin and trigger words and `judged` 0 until the prune removes them, and the judgments, stale until the next judge. The recipe is to run `judge`, or a full `ingest`, once the roll forward is up.

- **A rollback across `0019` ignores the basis filter** (basis decision 3): the previous release decodes a list's stored filters into its own filters type, which has no `basis`, so it reads a list filtered by basis as though the filter were absent and shows members of every basis.
- **An edit under the rollback drops the basis filter for good**: an edit the previous release makes of a list's filters re-encodes them from its own type, which drops `basis`, so the list keeps no basis filter after the roll forward until the owner sets it again (R-3KRM-IU9T).
- **The previous release records no open**: its page and tool open a list without writing `opens`, so the first open after the roll forward compares against the last open recorded before the rollback, and its changes span the whole rollback.
- **Opens are unbounded** (basis decision 10): one row per open, each holding the answered members and the passing set, the whole matching set before the size cut and so the bulk of a row, never pruned until a retention policy is set, so a list opened often grows its history by one row each time.

ORDER BY name DESC, id DESC LIMIT ?`. They are named for the end they start from and the side of the position they read, mirroring `First` and `After`. Both read the forward windows' indexes in the other direction. Observed as above: `StartedPreceding` `SEARCH passes USING COVERING INDEX passes_window ((started,id)>(?,?))` for a statement selecting the id alone, `StartedLast` `SCAN passes USING INDEX passes_window`, `ProspectListsPreceding` `SEARCH prospect_lists USING INDEX prospect_lists_window ((name,id)<(?,?))`, `ProspectListsLast` `SCAN prospect_lists USING INDEX prospect_lists_window`; no `USE TEMP B-TREE` in any. They answer in the reverse of list order, the order a keyset read from a position backwards takes; the landing page reverses the window it draws. An `n` below 1 is refused `ErrUnreachable`, as the forward windows refuse it. The plans are required of the four (R-PZ73-DFQV, R-NVLB-NNRT) and their rows to be their methods' windows (R-VKEI-HL3G); that a method runs its statement rather than another giving the same rows is accepted unobserved, as for the forward windows.
- **The name filter compares Go's lowering, not SQL's.** `names_lower` is written as Go's `strings.ToLower` of the names joined by line feeds, and SQLite's `lower()` folds only ASCII, so the filter is lowered with `strings.ToLower` before it reaches SQL and found with `instr(names_lower, ?) > 0`, a plain substring test: no `LIKE`, so `%` and `_` need no escape, and no `LIKE` pattern-length limit (50,000 bytes by default) can fail a long filter. The brief's `names_lower LIKE '%' || lower(?) || '%'` with an escape would differ from the Go semantics on non-ASCII letters; this does not. The one difference from the Go semantics the store had is the line feed that joins the names: a filter holding a line feed matches no candidate, where it matched a name holding the same line feed before; no source writes one into a name.
- **What still reads whole tables.** The windows and every read a page or a tool makes read by key or by window: `GetCandidate`, `Links`, `Estimates`, `GetJudgments`, `Rulings`, `Rejects`, `GetProspectList`, `GetPass`, `ZipMinutes`, `Pages`, `GetPage` and `CountPages` read one candidate's, one prospect list's, one pass's or one page's rows by index. `ReadProspectList` reads the judgments of the active candidates its filters do not rule out, ordered by its order and cut by its size, and counts them, which `D14-prospect-lists`' definition asks of it; its standing rejects come from `rejects_prospect_list`. `RunningPasses` is no page's or tool's read: the start calls it once, and with no index on `status` it scans `passes`, whose size is the configured retention plus the passes recorded since the last sweep. What still reads a table whole is the judge path and upkeep: `JudgeAll`, which covers every active candidate by definition, and `PrunePasses` and `ReplaceZipDrive`, each its own table alone (log 36). `Decide` reads and writes by key the candidates it touches alone (orchestrator ruling, after the sandbox found one ruling taking eight minutes over 43,058 candidates and 53,787 links, past the MCP gateway's 50 seconds; `D12-ingest`'s requirements bind it). The engine's redelivery check (`D06-passes`, R-YRFA-2BM8) does not list the passes: `FindDelivered` asks for one pass carrying the delivery's event id or naming its run, on `passes_delivery` and on `passes_trigger`, which `0008` adds; for a judge delivery the engine passes no run, so only the delivery arm can match. `passes_delivery` is partial (`WHERE delivery <> ''`, `0006`), so the delivery arm states `delivery <> ''` for SQLite to use it; and since two passes can match at once (one by delivery, another by run), the first in pass order is chosen with `ORDER BY started DESC, id DESC LIMIT 1`. With the guard, `EXPLAIN QUERY PLAN` on SQLite 3.46.1 shows the two arms as `SEARCH passes USING INDEX passes_delivery (delivery=?)` and `SEARCH passes USING INDEX passes_trigger (trigger=?)`. That a list or page read holds no more than its window cannot be observed by use, since a test never counts statements or reads tables (the tests of R-XWJK-VR9D and the plan requirements run only the statements the store exports, never SQL of their own); it carries no requirement of its own, and the sandbox check measures it.
- **`candidate_cargo` is still written, and observed by nothing** (log 30): every write of a candidate's `cargo` replaces that candidate's `candidate_cargo` rows in the same transaction, so a release that still filters on cargo reads it right after a rollback; `JudgeAll` no longer re-derives the table, the repair having served only the interim ranked list (`D13-judging`). - **Enrichment results are generic** (enrichers decision 4): `store.Enrichment` carries the kind, the inputs, the source, what was found as raw JSON, the cost and the time, one shape for every kind, and the registry's `Valid` is what the store checks a found against (`D16-enrichers`); the website check's fields left the store type and are decoded from `Found` by the consumers that show them into a `website.Result`. - **The facts row is the newest result of each kind** (enrichers decision 5): each kind sets its own columns and its own `_at` from its newest result, and a kind with no result leaves its columns unknown. A candidate whose inputs for a kind become empty (its links all gone from the store, its ZIP cleared, its domain lost) records nothing new for that kind and keeps the facts of its last result, as `D13-judging` once kept a candidate that stops being checked at its last result; this departs from enrichers decision 3's "the facts projection shows unknown", because a facts row must stay the projection of the results, re-made from them alone (enrichers decision 5, enrichers settled 9). `drive_minutes` records a result with no minutes for a ZIP the table lacks, so a ZIP moving to an unknown one clears the minutes and is not examined again at every pass (`D16-enrichers`). `brief`, the website and the drive minutes are the facts that come from another result than a kind's newest: the brief's text and categories from its newest good brief, recorded next, the website from the website decision, recorded after it, and the minutes from the drive decision, recorded under the facts row.
- **The facts take the newest good brief's categories, not the newest brief result's** (a departure from the letter of brief decision 7, which names the newest `brief` result): a failed run carries no categories (brief decision 6), the text comes from the newest result whose run is not failed (brief decision 8), and the candidate page shows that same result with its categories (P5); reading the categories from the newest result while the text came from an earlier one would set one run's summary beside another's categories, or, after a failure, leave a summary with none of its own. So the row's `brief` and its brief categories always come from one result, the newest good brief (R-X5MB-WZMT), and the Verification's "a failed run ... no category from it is added" holds, a failed run leaving both as the last good one left them. `brief_at` still names the newest result, failed or not, as every `_at` does (enrichers settled 8), so a member's brief time moves on a failed run while the text and categories do not.
- **The website is the check's, else the newest confirmed search's** (website search decision 7, S4): a check that found a site decides whatever the searches found, since the search runs only where no site is on record and a later check that finds the domain serving a site takes over; among the searches the newest confirmed one decides, not the newest search, so a forced search that finds nothing, fails, or names a site that does not load leaves the site an earlier search confirmed standing, as a failed brief leaves the last good one. The decision reads every result, as the newest good brief does, so the row stays a function of the results alone and a full ingest gives the same answer. `force` cannot clear a wrong found site (Known limits). The words are `store.WebsiteByCheck` and `store.WebsiteBySearch`, `check` and `search`, as the facts row stores them and the tools answer them, declared in the store because the store writes them and every other document refers to them by name.
- **The website source is read from the facts row** (website search decision 10): `store.Facts`' `WebsiteSource` is answered at the read from the facts row, as its other facts are, and no judge writes a source. A merged candidate has no facts row and answers no facts; the candidate page shows its website from its results.
- **The brief's set or the link facts', never both** (category evidence decisions 1 to 3 and 11): a good brief with at least one category gives the row's categories alone, each with the source `brief`, and otherwise the row holds the link facts' categories as release-46 built them; a good brief with no category decides nothing, so a company whose website check found the wrong site is not made unknown by it.
