# D08-pages

What candidates draws as HTML: `internal/pages`, the ten templates `landing`, `prospect_list`, `candidate`, `page`, `pass`, `parks`, `park`, `tools`, `about` and `notfound` and the data each is executed with, and the handler that answers `/`, `/prospect-lists/<id>`, the old list path, which it redirects there for one release (funnel rename decision 5), `/candidates/<id>`, `/candidates/<id>/pages/<n>`, `/passes/<id>`, `/parks`, `/parks/<id>`, `/tools`, `/about` and every other path the route table (`D03-serve`) hands it (decision 15). It is scrapes' pages with the prospect lists and the passes on the landing page, and a page per prospect list, per candidate and per pass below it, and below a candidate's page one per page of its website kept on it (site text decision 2). Beside them it draws the industrial parks candidates derives from the county parcel records scrapes holds (`D18-parks`): a page of them at `/parks` and one per park at `/parks/<id>`, below which nothing is drawn; a park is a place, never a candidate, and the landing shows only how many there are (parks decision 6). The pages say nothing of their own about what a prospect list holds: who is a member, what `matching` counts and which reject stands are `D14-prospect-lists`' terms, the store's read answers them (`D04-store`), and the pages format what it answers.

Decisions cited by number are the decisions document that delivered candidates; "cohorts decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the cohorts change, and "log N" entry N of its decisions log. "Enrichers decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the enrichers change, "website log X" entry X of the decisions log of the website-check change, "site text decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the site-text change, "brief decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the brief change, "P N" entry N of the decisions log of the brief change's delivery, "category evidence decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the category-evidence change, "website search decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the website-search change, "S N" entry DN of the decisions log of that change's delivery, "campaigns decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the campaigns change, and "campaigns log DN" entry DN of the decisions log of its delivery. "Funnel rename decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the funnel rename, and "drive route decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the exact-drive-time change. "Drive route links outcome N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the drive route links change, and "links log DN" entry DN of the decisions log of its delivery. "Basis decision N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the prospect basis change. "Revenue bands decision N" is decision N of the decisions document of the revenue bands change, which makes a candidate's revenue its headcount times a per-employee band for its kind of business. "Form 5500 decision N" is candidates decision N of the decisions document of the Form 5500 change, which adds Form 5500 filings as a third source and matches a filing to a candidate by its EIN. "Website states outcome N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the website states change, which makes a website `none` only when the website search ran and found nothing. "OSHA ITA outcome N" is outcome N of the reader's section of the intent document of the OSHA ITA change, which adds OSHA's Injury Tracking Application 300A summaries as a fourth source, and "OSHA ITA P N" entry PN of the decisions log of its delivery. "Headcount trend decision N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the headcount series and trend change, which has the judge lay a candidate's dated headcounts out by year, one figure per kind, and read a trend from them, growing, flat, shrinking or unknown. "Parks decision N" is decision N of the decisions document of the parks change, which derives industrial parks from the parcel records scrapes holds so the owner can name one for a later walk. "Empty sites outcome N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the empty sites change, which runs the website search again when site text kept no pages from the website on record, and "empty sites decision N" decision N of its delivery.

## The pages

The landing page shows one window of the prospect lists, `paging.DefaultLimit` of them (appkit's D25) in the store's prospect list order, by name and then by prospect list id, each in byte order, each linked to its prospect list's page; then one window of the passes, `paging.DefaultLimit` newest first in the store's pass order, each linked to its page. A deleted prospect list is on no window. Both lists grow, so each navigates (`shape/`, Listing; `design/`, the listing entry and its two-list form). Two lists share the page, so each has its own query parameter, `prospect_lists_page` and `passes_page`, each the first value of its name in the query, and nothing else is read from the query; the detail routes read none. A list's **page value** asks for one of four windows, and **decodable** names all four. The empty value asks for the **first window**. The token the list's tool issues as `next`, an **after-token** (`prospect_lists` and `passes`, `D10-tools`), asks for the window after the entry it names, exactly what that tool answers for the same `page` with no `limit`, read through the store's positioned windows (`ListProspectLists`, `ListPasses`, `D04-store`). The way back is a token the page issues itself, since neither tool has a backward step: a **before-token** is `paging.Token{Tool: "prospect_lists-before"}` or `paging.Token{Tool: "passes-before"}` over the same two key fields, the name and prospect list id or the started text and pass id, carried in the same parameter, the checksum appkit's token carries keeping it apart from the after-token; it asks for the `paging.DefaultLimit` entries that precede the one it names in the list's order, drawn in that order, whether or not an entry still has that key. The literal word `last`, which decodes as no token, asks for the **last window**, the last `paging.DefaultLimit` prospect lists by name or the oldest `paging.DefaultLimit` passes, drawn in the list's order. The page reads a before-token's window and the last window from the store's reversed windows, `ListProspectListsReversed` and `ListPassesReversed` (`D04-store`), from the token's position or, for `last`, from the end of the list, asking for one entry more than the window and drawing the first `paging.DefaultLimit` of them reversed, so it learns whether the window **underflows**: when at most `paging.DefaultLimit` entries precede a before-token's position, or the list holds at most `paging.DefaultLimit` entries in all for `last`, the window asked for is that list's first window, and the page answers a 303 whose `Location` is `/` with the other list's parameter kept as the request carried it, so each list's first window keeps one address and paging one list never resets the other; when both lists underflow, both parameters are dropped. Neither parameter changes the other list's window. The template receives, for each list, its page value as the request carried it (`ProspectListsPage`, `PassesPage`: empty on the first window, `last` on the last), the before-token of the window's first entry (`ProspectListsPrev`, `PassesPrev`: empty on the first window and on an empty after-window), and the after-token of its last entry when more follow (`ProspectListsNext`, `PassesNext`: on an after-window exactly the tool's `next`, on a before-window always set, on the last window empty); what it draws from them, the two navs with First, Prev, Next and Last included, is the asset's. A value either parameter cannot decode, one list's token in the other's parameter, a token another tool issued, or a word such as `bogus` or `LAST`, is the not-found page, never a first window. A prospect list row carries the prospect list's id, name and size, who created it and when it last changed; the landing shows no member of any prospect list, so it reads no clock and no judgments. The number of rows shown follows `paging.DefaultLimit`, and the template states no count of its own. No row of either list shows a phone or an email.

## Who started a pass

A pass says what started it by its `Origin` (`D04-store`): an `ingest` or `judge` call's pass, origin `ingest` or `judge`, shows its caller's email, or user id when the caller has no email; an ingest a scrapes run's delivery started, origin `run`, shows its trigger, the run id, linked to the run's page at scrapes, and the template names scrapes as its starter; a judge the daily cron trigger's fire started, origin `cron`, shows its trigger, the slug `judge_candidates`, unlinked; an `enrich` call's pass, origin `enrich`, of kind `enrich` or `enrich_target`, shows its caller as an `ingest` call's does; a pass another pass started, origin `pass` (an enrich an ingest started, or a judge an enrich started, `D06-passes`), shows its trigger, the starting pass's id, which the template links to that pass's page; an `edit_park_rules` call's parks pass, origin `rules`, shows its caller as an `ingest` call's does; a `walk` call's walk, origin `walk`, shows its caller as an `ingest` call's does; a parks pass a scrapes parcel run's delivery started, origin `run`, shows its trigger linked to the run's page as an ingest's does; and a parks pass candidates started at start, origin `start`, shows its trigger, the run id, unlinked, and the template names candidates as its starter. The data carries `Kind` and `Origin` as stored, `StartedBy` (an `ingest`, `judge`, `enrich`, `edit_park_rules` or `walk` call's caller's email, or its user id when the email is empty; empty for any other origin) and `RunPage` (set only for origin `run`, whatever the kind); the template keys the starter on `Origin`, and the words are the template's. The landing's pass rows carry `Kind`, `Trigger` and `Judged`, and no origin.

## Numbers as shown

The templates receive numbers already formatted, as their comments name them: dollars as `$` and the whole number of dollars with a comma between each group of three digits, for a revenue range, an estimate's low and high and a prospect list's revenue bounds alike; operating and revenue confidences, evidence weights, an estimate's confidence and a link's confidence with two decimals, though the tools answer the stored values, the operating confidence stored rounded to six decimal places (`D10-tools`, `D13-judging`); a route's miles, on the facts view and on a `drive_route` row, with one decimal, as `strconv.FormatFloat` writes it with the format `f` and precision 1, empty when absent; what a derived kind's result found as one key and value per member, in key order, the value as compact JSON text, as an estimate's facts are; filing dates, the known-since date, the newest filing and the date a headcount is as of as `YYYY-MM-DD` in both the `datetime` and the text; other times RFC 3339 to the second for a `time` element's `datetime`, and a `...Text` field in the page's layout, `pages.MinuteLayout` for the landing's prospect lists and passes, a link's first and last seen, a ruling's time, a reject's time, an enrichment result's time, a listed page's fetched time, a campaign row's added, delivered and rejected times and a member's site text and brief times, `pages.SecondLayout` for a candidate's created and updated, when the result its website stands on was found, when each kind wrote its facts and when its brief was found, the judgments' calculated time, a prospect list's created and changed times on its page, the pass page and a text page's fetched time. An empty judgment gives empty strings, never `0.00` or `$0`, so the template can show that there is no evidence or no estimate. Counts, sizes, ranks, `matching` and the rules version are integers. A time not yet set gives both strings empty. A member row shows a candidate's distinct category words, each once in ascending order; the candidate page shows each category with the source that gave it, in the order stored; a prospect list's category filter shows its words as stored. The fleet verdict is shown as stored, `pass`, `fail` or `unknown`, and a website state likewise, `https`, `http`, `none` or `unknown`. An enrichment result's status code, a website check's or a website search's confirmation's, is decimal text, empty when no response came, and its bytes read an integer, as is a page's; a website search's run's exit code is decimal text, empty when the run has none. A cost, a pass's total and a result's, is its **cost text**: `$` and the nano-dollars as US dollars to six decimal places, rounded to the nearest micro-dollar with a half rounding up, as prompts shows a cost (`$0.023714`); a pass always carries one, `$0.000000` when nothing was spent, and a result that cost nothing carries none. A park's building value, a parcel's and the rules' value threshold are each its **value text**, its dollars, with a `-` before the `$` for a negative value, which no parcel record is expected to hold; a parcel whose record holds no building value shows none. A park's center is its latitude and longitude in degrees to four decimal places, its radius and the rules' distance whole meters, the radius rounded to the nearest, and its drive minutes decimal text, empty when its ZIP is not in the ZIP table; its walked time is a date, and its created and updated times are in `pages.SecondLayout`.

## Pages show what is stored

Pages make no call to scrapes or campaigns and change no record but one: a `GET` or `HEAD` of a prospect list's page records an open of it, which changes nothing but what the next open of that list reports as its changes (basis decision 7). A text page shows the copy the store holds, a pass's page shows the status the store holds, a candidate's campaign rows are the uses the store recorded from campaigns' events (`D04-store`, `D07-events`), each link to campaigns built from the services file alone, the two judgments are the stored judgments', as the last judge, full ingest or ruling wrote them (`D13-judging`), the facts are the facts row's, as the enrichers last wrote it (`D04-store`, `D16-enrichers`), and a prospect list's page shows what the store's read of it answers, its members' facts from the facts row and their judgments from the judgments. Nothing decays: a fact changes only when an enricher writes a new result, in an enrich whose inputs changed, a full ingest, or a merge or split, and a judgment when a judge runs. The candidate card's website is the website decision of the candidate's results (`D04-store`), which is also what the facts row's website, address and source hold, so the card and a prospect list's member row agree as soon as a result is recorded, without a judge between. The one date a page depends on is the read date of a prospect list read, by which its filing-age and campaign-age filters measure (`D14-prospect-lists`) and at which its open is recorded, and that is the store's: `OpenProspectList` reads the store's own clock (`D14-prospect-lists`, `D04-store`), so no page reads a clock of its own and `pages.Config` carries none. The parks pages show what the last derivation applied (`D04-store`, `D06-passes`): they start no pass, read no parcel and ask scrapes nothing, the derived run's link being built from the services file alone. Candidates, prospect lists, rejects, passes and parks belong to no one (decision 14; cohorts decision 8), so every signed-in user sees the same pages.

## Guests and methods

The manifest says `guests = false` (`D02-cli`), so nginx sends a request with no credential to sign in before it reaches candidates, and `D03-serve` mounts this handler inside `identity.Require`. A request to a page path whose method is not `GET` or `HEAD` is 405 with `Allow: GET, HEAD`, decided before the store is read. On `/` the two page values are decided next, both before the store is read: an undecodable one is the not-found page, the same with the store unavailable. On `/parks` the walked, gone, sort and page values are decided the same way, before the store is read; the county only after, since only the store knows which counties hold a park. A path that is not a well-formed prospect list, candidate, pass, park or page path is the not-found page without a store read. The landing with decodable values, the parks page with a decodable view, the prospect list, candidate, pass, park and text pages answer 503 with a read refusal (`D03-serve`) when the store cannot be read, the landing drawing no window and making no redirect; the about, tools and not-found pages are served without reading it. A prospect list page whose open the store cannot record is the same 503, and records no open: the page never shows a list whose open was not recorded.

## Known limits

- After a rollback to the previous release and a roll forward, a facts row the previous release rewrote may carry a stale `website_source` until the candidate's next result or a full `ingest` (`D04-store`). The prospect list row reads that row while the candidate card computes the decision from the results, so the two can differ for that candidate until then.
- Until the one full `ingest` after the website states release, a facts row may still hold the `none` a check wrote, or `unknown` where a search found nothing, while the card computes the new decision from the results, so a prospect list's member row and the card can differ for that candidate until the ingest rewrites its row (`D04-store`). During a rollback the previous release's card reads the results by the old rule.
- A wrong site the search confirmed stays on the card and the prospect list row until a later confirmed search replaces it, as `D04-store`'s known limits record.
- After a rollback to the previous release and a roll forward, the facts row's drive decision may be stale until the candidate's next result or a full `ingest`, as `D04-store`'s recorded decisions say; the card and the prospect list row both read that row, so they agree with each other through it. How the previous release draws a `drive_route` result during a rollback is `D04-store`'s concern: it knows no such kind.
- How the previous release draws a `website_search` result during a rollback is `D04-store`'s known limit.
- During a rollback to release-71, its card decides the website by its old rule, so a candidate whose confirmed search found a different site than the check it ran against shows the check's site, and its search rows draw without why they ran, the site on record, the site text or the case, since it reads the stored value without the new members (empty sites decision 4, `D04-store`).
- During a rollback to release-72, its card judges a takeover by the stored case, so a search recorded before this release whose named site redirected back onto the site on record shows its URL with the source `search` again, while one this release recorded reads `stands` and decides nothing there (empty sites decision 17, `D04-store`).
- Every `GET` and `HEAD` of a prospect list page records an open, a reload or a browser's prefetch included, so the next open reports changes since that one. The page has no peek; an agent that must not reset "since" reads through the `prospect_list` tool with `peek` (`D10-tools`).
- During a rollback the previous release's page records no open, so an open after the roll forward reports changes since the last open the current release recorded (`D04-store`).
- During a rollback to the previous release, `/parks` and every park path are the not-found page, and the previous release draws a parks pass on the landing and its pass page by its kind and trigger as stored, without the parks counts (`D04-store`).
