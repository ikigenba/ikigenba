# D08-pages

What candidates draws as HTML: `internal/pages`, the eight templates `landing`, `prospect_list`, `candidate`, `page`, `pass`, `tools`, `about` and `notfound` and the data each is executed with, and the handler that answers `/`, `/prospect-lists/<id>`, the old list path, which it redirects there for one release (funnel rename decision 5), `/candidates/<id>`, `/candidates/<id>/pages/<n>`, `/passes/<id>`, `/tools`, `/about` and every other path the route table (`D03-serve`) hands it (decision 15). It is scrapes' pages with the prospect lists and the passes on the landing page, and a page per prospect list, per candidate and per pass below it, and below a candidate's page one per page of its website kept on it (site text decision 2). The pages say nothing of their own about what a prospect list holds: who is a member, what `matching` counts and which reject stands are `D14-prospect-lists`' terms, the store's read answers them (`D04-store`), and the pages format what it answers.

Decisions cited by number are the decisions document that delivered candidates; "cohorts decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the cohorts change, and "log N" entry N of its decisions log. "Enrichers decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the enrichers change, "website log X" entry X of the decisions log of the website-check change, "site text decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the site-text change, "brief decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the brief change, "P N" entry N of the decisions log of the brief change's delivery, "category evidence decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the category-evidence change, "website search decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the website-search change, "S N" entry DN of the decisions log of that change's delivery, "campaigns decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the campaigns change, and "campaigns log DN" entry DN of the decisions log of its delivery. "Funnel rename decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the funnel rename, and "drive route decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the exact-drive-time change. "Drive route links outcome N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the drive route links change, and "links log DN" entry DN of the decisions log of its delivery. "Basis decision N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the prospect basis change.

## The pages

The landing page shows one window of the prospect lists, `paging.DefaultLimit` of them (appkit's D25) in the store's prospect list order, by name and then by prospect list id, each in byte order, each linked to its prospect list's page; then one window of the passes, `paging.DefaultLimit` newest first in the store's pass order, each linked to its page. A deleted prospect list is on no window. Both lists grow, so each navigates (`shape/`, Listing; `design/`, the listing entry and its two-list form). Two lists share the page, so each has its own query parameter, `prospect_lists_page` and `passes_page`, each the first value of its name in the query, and nothing else is read from the query; the detail routes read none. A list's **page value** asks for one of four windows, and **decodable** names all four. The empty value asks for the **first window**. The token the list's tool issues as `next`, an **after-token** (`prospect_lists` and `passes`, `D10-tools`), asks for the window after the entry it names, exactly what that tool answers for the same `page` with no `limit`, read through the store's positioned windows (`ListProspectLists`, `ListPasses`, `D04-store`). The way back is a token the page issues itself, since neither tool has a backward step: a **before-token** is `paging.Token{Tool: "prospect_lists-before"}` or `paging.Token{Tool: "passes-before"}` over the same two key fields, the name and prospect list id or the started text and pass id, carried in the same parameter, the checksum appkit's token carries keeping it apart from the after-token; it asks for the `paging.DefaultLimit` entries that precede the one it names in the list's order, drawn in that order, whether or not an entry still has that key. The literal word `last`, which decodes as no token, asks for the **last window**, the last `paging.DefaultLimit` prospect lists by name or the oldest `paging.DefaultLimit` passes, drawn in the list's order. The page reads a before-token's window and the last window from the store's reversed windows, `ListProspectListsReversed` and `ListPassesReversed` (`D04-store`), from the token's position or, for `last`, from the end of the list, asking for one entry more than the window and drawing the first `paging.DefaultLimit` of them reversed, so it learns whether the window **underflows**: when at most `paging.DefaultLimit` entries precede a before-token's position, or the list holds at most `paging.DefaultLimit` entries in all for `last`, the window asked for is that list's first window, and the page answers a 303 whose `Location` is `/` with the other list's parameter kept as the request carried it, so each list's first window keeps one address and paging one list never resets the other; when both lists underflow, both parameters are dropped. Neither parameter changes the other list's window. The template receives, for each list, its page value as the request carried it (`ProspectListsPage`, `PassesPage`: empty on the first window, `last` on the last), the before-token of the window's first entry (`ProspectListsPrev`, `PassesPrev`: empty on the first window and on an empty after-window), and the after-token of its last entry when more follow (`ProspectListsNext`, `PassesNext`: on an after-window exactly the tool's `next`, on a before-window always set, on the last window empty); what it draws from them, the two navs with First, Prev, Next and Last included, is the asset's. A value either parameter cannot decode, one list's token in the other's parameter, a token another tool issued, or a word such as `bogus` or `LAST`, is the not-found page, never a first window. A prospect list row carries the prospect list's id, name and size, who created it and when it last changed; the landing shows no member of any prospect list, so it reads no clock and no judgments. The number of rows shown follows `paging.DefaultLimit`, and the template states no count of its own. No row of either list shows a phone or an email.

`/prospect-lists/<id>` is one prospect list as one `OpenProspectList` of the store answers it (`D04-store`), an open by the request's caller that the store records (basis decision 7): its definition (name, size, each filter it carries with its unknown switch, the campaign-age filter among them, which has none (campaigns decision 18), and the basis filter, which has none either (basis decision 3), its order, who created and last changed it and when), its `matching`, how many of its members the brief classified (category evidence decision 5), how many of its members stand on each basis (basis decision 4), what changed since the previous recorded open of the list, by the page or the `prospect_list` tool, which parts of its own definition changed, who joined and why and who left and why, a member merged away or no longer judged leaving as gone with no basis now (basis decisions 8, 9 and 10, `D14-prospect-lists`), its members in rank order, ranked from 1, and its standing rejects, newest first, each with the candidate, who, when and why (`D14-prospect-lists`). Each member row has the shape the landing's earlier ranked rows had, with its basis, `records`, `partial` or `enriched` (basis decisions 1, 2 and 5), the fleet verdict in place of the fleet test, the website fact, the address it landed on and which result decided it, the check or the search (website search decision 11), when site text was last written for it, empty when never (site text decision 19), when its brief was last written, empty when never, whether or not that run failed (brief decisions 11 and 12), what classified its categories, `brief`, `links` or nothing, read from the sources of the categories the filter reads (category evidence decision 3), the drive minutes and which result decided them, so that a routed drive is marked (drive route decision 11), and the known-since date and the newest filing beside them, as the candidate's facts row holds them when the prospect list is read (`D04-store`, `D16-enrichers`), whether the owner excluded it, and links to its candidate's page. The page is a listing: it carries no form, and a prospect list is made, changed and groomed through the tools (`D10-tools`). The members are worked out at each read, so two requests minutes apart can show different members when a judge, an enrichment, a ruling or a reject came between them, and the second shows the difference as its changes. Every `GET` and `HEAD` of the page records one open; the page never peeks (basis decision 11). A first open shows no changes.

`/candidates/<id>` is one candidate: its display fields and tags, its website as the website decision of its results decides it (`D04-store`, website search decision 7): the state, the address it landed on and the page title of its newest `website_check` result when that found a site, else of its newest `website_search` result whose site was confirmed, with the reason the search's agent gave, which of the two decided and when that result was found, else its newest check's state, title and time, or `unknown` when it has no check (website search decision 11, S11); its facts as its facts row holds them, the projection of the newest result of each enricher's kind (`D04-store`, `D16-enrichers`): the known-since date, the newest filing, the categories with their sources, what classified them, `brief` when the brief's categories decide, `links` when the link facts' do and nothing when there are none, and, when the brief decided and what the records say differs, the newest `link_facts` result's categories with their sources (category evidence decisions 1, 3 and 4), the government match, the fleet verdict, the truck and driver counts, the drive minutes with which result decided them, the route or the ZIP estimate, and, when the route decided, its miles and the address the routing service resolved the request to (drive route decisions 7 and 11), and when each kind last wrote the row, or, when it has no row, that it has no facts; beside them its basis and which of the four targeted kinds it has a result of and which it lacks, `store.BasisOf` and `store.KindsOf` of its results (`D04-store`, `D14-prospect-lists`, basis decision 5), the same basis its member row shows on a prospect list page; its brief under the facts, from its newest `brief` result whose run did not fail, the one whose summary the facts row holds (`D04-store`, `D16-enrichers`): the summary, the categories it gave, each with the reason given, the labels and when it was found, with the template's note that it gave no categories, drawn from its own empty categories, when it gave none (category evidence decision 2), or, when it has no such result, that it has no brief (brief decision 12, P5); apart from those enrichments (funnel rename decision 13), its judgments, the two judgments as the last judge, full ingest or ruling wrote them, with the operating evidence behind it; each estimate with the facts it used, each link with its last seen and gone, linked to its row's page at scrapes, the rulings that name it, the pages of its website kept on it, the newest copy of each URL, newest first, each with its URL, title, bytes read, when it was fetched and who added it, linked to its text page (site text decisions 1, 2 and 19), every enrichment result on it, of every kind, newest first, each with its kind, its source, what it cost and its time, a `website_check` result with its state, landed address, title, status code, bytes read and error, a `website_search` result with the URL its agent named, the reason given, the one fetch that confirmed that URL or found it would not load, with the same six parts as a check's, the sites set aside, each with its reason, and its run (website search decisions 5, 6 and 11), a `drive_route` result with its minutes, its miles, the address the service resolved the request to, how precisely it placed it, the service's own status, and why nothing was routable or the failed request's error (drive route decisions 5 and 11), a result of any other kind with what it found, member by member, and a result of a prompts kind, `site_text`, `brief` or `website_search`, whose run failed, or a `drive_route` result whose request failed, marked so by its kind's rule (site text decision 9, brief decisions 6 and 12, website search decision 6, drive route decision 11) (`D04-store`, `D15-website-check`, `D16-enrichers`), and every reject and unreject record that names it, each with its prospect list's id, the prospect list's name while the prospect list exists, who, when and why; and every campaign the business was put in, as campaigns told candidates on the bus (campaigns decisions 16 and 17, `D07-events`), one row per campaign in the order the store's `CampaignUses` answers them, newest added first and those whose added event has not arrived last (`D04-store`), each with the campaign's id, linked to its page at campaigns when the services file names a `campaigns` entry (`D01-layout-and-run-seam`), the prospect list it was added from, with that prospect list's name and a link to its page while the prospect list exists, or nothing when it was added by hand, when it was added, when something was last delivered to it there, and whether the owner rejected it there, when and why (campaigns decision 5, campaigns log D4). A merged candidate's page shows its id, status, the candidate it was merged into (linked), its created and updated times, its rulings and its reject records, and no display field, tag, website, fact, brief, page, enrichment result, judgments, estimate, link or campaign (a merge moves the results, the pages and the campaign uses to the survivor, `D04-store`, `D12-ingest`, campaigns log D1): the store keeps its display fields as they were at the merge, and the page data blanks them, so the page says only what it merged into (the template's comment). An active candidate not yet judged shows that it is not judged: an ingest stores its rows and judges nothing. Facts and the judgments are apart, so a candidate an enrich has reached and no judge has shows its facts beside saying it is not judged, and one no enrich has reached shows that it has no facts.

`/candidates/<id>/pages/<n>` is one kept page, the **text page**: one copy of a page of the candidate's website as `add_page` fetched and kept it (`D10-tools`), with the candidate that holds it and that candidate's display name, its URL, title, bytes read, when it was fetched, who added it, and its text (site text decisions 1 and 2). `<n>` is the copy's number, which the store mints for each copy and keeps across a merge (`D04-store`); any copy is served, the newest of its URL or an older one, so an older copy keeps its address after a newer one is kept, while the candidate page links each URL to its newest copy. A number that names no page, or a page another candidate holds, is the not-found page, as is the path of a candidate that does not exist; a merged candidate holds no page, since its pages moved to the survivor with the merge.

`/passes/<id>` is one pass's record in full, an enrich pass's six counts (derived, the results the derived kinds wrote, `D06-passes`; then due, fetched, https, http and none, whose meaning is `D15-website-check`'s) among them, which the template shows for an `enrich` and an `enrich_target`; for an `enrich_target`, the pass a targeted `enrich` starts (site text decision 14), its target, linked to that prospect list's or candidate's page, the kind it walks, and the prompts runs it started, those that failed, the pages they added and what they cost, in dollars (site text decision 19), or, for a `drive_route`, the requests it made to the routing service, those that failed and what they cost, in the same `Runs`, `RunsFailed` and `Cost`, with `Pages` 0 (`D06-passes`), so the pass page's data is unchanged; when it failed, the reason's sentence, which the template chooses from `Reason`, `prompts` among the reasons (`D06-passes`); when a scrapes run's delivery started it, its trigger linked to the run's page at scrapes; when another pass started it, its trigger, that pass's id, linked to that pass's page by the template. A pass still `running` shows its record as it stands; the page does not stream.

An unknown candidate id, an unknown or pruned pass id, an unknown or deleted prospect list id, a text page's path whose candidate is unknown or whose number names no page of that candidate, and a path whose id is not a well-formed candidate, pass or prospect list id or whose number is not a well-formed page number are answered alike, the not-found page with 404, byte for byte the not-found page of any other path.

The tools page lists the eighteen tools in the catalog's order (`D10-tools`), each with its one-line description. The about screen shows the service's name, the banner's `Release` and `Commit` and `pages.Description`. Any other path is the not-found page, 404, whatever the method.

The landing page is the root of the banner's trail; a prospect list page is one level below it, named by the prospect list's id at `/prospect-lists/<id>`; a candidate page one, named by the candidate's id at `/candidates/<id>`; a pass page one, named by the pass's id at `/passes/<id>`; and the tools page and the about screen one, `pages.ToolsLevel` and `pages.AboutLevel`; a text page is two below it, its candidate's id at `/candidates/<id>` and then its number at its own path. The banner's user is the caller's email, with auth's profile and sign-out addresses built on `urls.Auth` (`D01-layout-and-run-seam`).

## Who started a pass

A pass says what started it by its `Origin` (`D04-store`): an `ingest` or `judge` call's pass, origin `ingest` or `judge`, shows its caller's email, or user id when the caller has no email; an ingest a scrapes run's delivery started, origin `run`, shows its trigger, the run id, linked to the run's page at scrapes, and the template names scrapes as its starter; a judge the daily cron trigger's fire started, origin `cron`, shows its trigger, the slug `judge_candidates`, unlinked; an `enrich` call's pass, origin `enrich`, of kind `enrich` or `enrich_target`, shows its caller as an `ingest` call's does; and a pass another pass started, origin `pass` (an enrich an ingest started, or a judge an enrich started, `D06-passes`), shows its trigger, the starting pass's id, which the template links to that pass's page. The data carries `Kind` and `Origin` as stored, `StartedBy` (an `ingest`, `judge` or `enrich` call's caller's email, or its user id when the email is empty; empty for any other origin) and `RunPage` (set only for origin `run`); the template keys the starter on `Origin`, and the words are the template's. The landing's pass rows carry `Kind`, `Trigger` and `Judged`, and no origin.

## Numbers as shown

The templates receive numbers already formatted, as their comments name them: dollars as `$` and the whole number of dollars with a comma between each group of three digits, for a revenue range, an estimate's low and high and a prospect list's revenue bounds alike; operating and revenue confidences, evidence weights, an estimate's confidence and a link's confidence with two decimals, though the tools answer the stored values, the operating confidence stored rounded to six decimal places (`D10-tools`, `D13-judging`); drive minutes on the prospect list page and the candidate page, a prospect list's drive-minutes maximum, filing-age days and campaign-age days, and a candidate's truck and driver counts, as decimal text, empty when absent; a route's miles, on the facts view and on a `drive_route` row, with one decimal, as `strconv.FormatFloat` writes it with the format `f` and precision 1, empty when absent; what a derived kind's result found as one key and value per member, in key order, the value as compact JSON text, as an estimate's facts are; filing dates, the known-since date and the newest filing as `YYYY-MM-DD` in both the `datetime` and the text; other times RFC 3339 to the second for a `time` element's `datetime`, and a `...Text` field in the page's layout, `pages.MinuteLayout` for the landing's prospect lists and passes, a link's first and last seen, a ruling's time, a reject's time, an enrichment result's time, a listed page's fetched time, a campaign row's added, delivered and rejected times and a member's site text and brief times, `pages.SecondLayout` for a candidate's created and updated, when its website was checked, when each kind wrote its facts and when its brief was found, the judgments' calculated time, a prospect list's created and changed times on its page, the pass page and a text page's fetched time. An empty judgment gives empty strings, never `0.00` or `$0`, so the template can show that there is no evidence or no estimate. Counts, sizes, ranks, `matching` and the rules version are integers. A time not yet set gives both strings empty. A member row shows a candidate's distinct category words, each once in ascending order; the candidate page shows each category with the source that gave it, in the order stored; a prospect list's category filter shows its words as stored. The fleet verdict is shown as stored, `pass`, `fail` or `unknown`, and a website state likewise, `https`, `http`, `none` or `unknown`. An enrichment result's status code, a website check's or a website search's confirmation's, is decimal text, empty when no response came, and its bytes read an integer, as is a page's; a website search's run's exit code is decimal text, empty when the run has none. A cost, a pass's total and a result's, is its **cost text**: `$` and the nano-dollars as US dollars to six decimal places, rounded to the nearest micro-dollar with a half rounding up, as prompts shows a cost (`$0.023714`); a pass always carries one, `$0.000000` when nothing was spent, and a result that cost nothing carries none.

## Pages show what is stored

Pages make no call to scrapes or campaigns and change no record but one: a `GET` or `HEAD` of a prospect list's page records an open of it, which changes nothing but what the next open of that list reports as its changes (basis decision 7). A text page shows the copy the store holds, a pass's page shows the status the store holds, a candidate's campaign rows are the uses the store recorded from campaigns' events (`D04-store`, `D07-events`), each link to campaigns built from the services file alone, the two judgments are the stored judgments', as the last judge, full ingest or ruling wrote them (`D13-judging`), the facts are the facts row's, as the enrichers last wrote it (`D04-store`, `D16-enrichers`), and a prospect list's page shows what the store's read of it answers, its members' facts from the facts row and their judgments from the judgments. Nothing decays: a fact changes only when an enricher writes a new result, in an enrich whose inputs changed, a full ingest, or a merge or split, and a judgment when a judge runs. The candidate card's website is the website decision of the candidate's results (`D04-store`), which is also what the facts row's website, address and source hold, so the card and a prospect list's member row agree as soon as a result is recorded, without a judge between. The one date a page depends on is the read date of a prospect list read, by which its filing-age and campaign-age filters measure (`D14-prospect-lists`) and at which its open is recorded, and that is the store's: `OpenProspectList` reads the store's own clock (`D14-prospect-lists`, `D04-store`), so no page reads a clock of its own and `pages.Config` carries none. Candidates, prospect lists, rejects and passes belong to no one (decision 14; cohorts decision 8), so every signed-in user sees the same pages.

## Guests and methods

The manifest says `guests = false` (`D02-cli`), so nginx sends a request with no credential to sign in before it reaches candidates, and `D03-serve` mounts this handler inside `identity.Require`. A request to a page path whose method is not `GET` or `HEAD` is 405 with `Allow: GET, HEAD`, decided before the store is read. On `/` the two page values are decided next, both before the store is read: an undecodable one is the not-found page, the same with the store unavailable. A path that is not a well-formed prospect list, candidate, pass or page path is the not-found page without a store read. The landing with decodable values, the prospect list, candidate, pass and text pages answer 503 with a read refusal (`D03-serve`) when the store cannot be read, the landing drawing no window and making no redirect; the about, tools and not-found pages are served without reading it. A prospect list page whose open the store cannot record is the same 503, and records no open: the page never shows a list whose open was not recorded.

## Recorded decisions

- Candidate pages at `/candidates/<id>` and pass pages at `/passes/<id>` (decision 15). A path whose id fails `store.ValidCandidateID` or `store.ValidPassID` is not looked up.
- **Prospect list pages at `/prospect-lists/<id>`** (cohorts decision 21). A path whose id fails `store.ValidProspectListID` is not looked up. The prospect list path is defined in a requirement of its own beside the candidate and pass paths, so the existing definition keeps its id and only the requirements that list the page paths change.

- **The old list path redirects for one release** (funnel rename decisions 5 and 17): the previous release's campaigns links a member's list at `/cohorts/` followed by its old id, so `/cohorts/` followed by a well-formed old id answers 303 to `/prospect-lists/` followed by the mapped `pl_` id, with an empty body, reading no store and dropping the query; any other id under `/cohorts/` is the not-found page. 303 is the precedent of the landing's paging redirects, and nothing permanent should be cached, since the redirect goes in the next release. A guest is sent to sign in, as on every page.
- `ServiceName` and `Description` are `D01-layout-and-run-seam`'s; the layouts are contract constants with values here, as in scrapes. The landing shows `paging.DefaultLimit` of each list, the window the matching tool answers for the same `page` and no `limit`, so the page and the tool agree on every window.
- One data type per thing a template draws, each field list the template comment's in its order. Every value a template receives is a string, a bool, an `int64`, a slice or a pointer to a struct; the template comments' `int` fields are `int64`, as the store holds counts.
- **The interim ranked list left the landing** (cohorts decisions 15 and 20), with its parameter, its window, its ranks and the offset count behind them, and its row type. The ranked view of everything is a prospect list with no filters, on its own page. A URL still carrying that parameter names nothing the landing reads, so it is answered as the landing with that parameter absent; URLs are not persisted contract, so there was no expand period. The parameter `prospect_lists_page` follows the same rule: it replaces the earlier name outright, an old bookmark landing on the first window.
- **The landing lists prospect lists by name, then id, in byte order** (cohorts decision 20, log 18), the order `ListProspectLists` windows and the `prospect_lists` tool pages in. A prospect list row carries no rank: the list is alphabetical, not ranked.
- **Every judgment is the store's** (`store.Judgments`, `D13-judging`): the prospect list page and the candidate page show the judgments the last judge, full ingest or ruling wrote, so a page and the tools agree, and a judgment moves once per judge rather than per request. Freshness is measured when a judge runs, not when a page is drawn, so the pages show no freshness of their own.
- **The page reads no clock of its own** (cohorts decision 4, log 3): the filing-age filter is measured on the day a prospect list is read, and the date is the store's, read from its own `Config.Now` inside `OpenProspectList`, which also stamps the open with it. `pages.Config` gains nothing, so the prospect list page and the `prospect_list` tool measure the same day under one clock.
- **The landing's forward tokens are the tools'**: an after-token in `prospect_lists_page`, and `ProspectListsNext`, are `paging.Token{Tool: "prospect_lists"}` over a prospect list's name and id, and an after-token in `passes_page`, and `PassesNext`, `paging.Token{Tool: "passes"}` over a pass's started text and id (`D04-store`), so a window on the page and the tool's answer for the same `page` are one window, read through the same store window, and a token on the page can be handed to an agent. They are strings, empty on the first window and when nothing follows, so the template tests them as it tests any other field.
- **The page steps back and jumps to the end** (`design/`'s listing entry, with Prev and Last, decided 2026-10-13): each parameter also carries a before-token the page issues under its tool's name with `-before` appended, `prospect_lists-before` or `passes-before`, or `last`, and the landing data carries `ProspectListsPrev` and `PassesPrev`. `prospect_lists` and `passes` decode only their own tokens, so they refuse a before-token and `last` with `paging.BadPage` as any page they did not issue (`D10-tools`), and the agreement with the tools holds for the empty value and after-tokens only. `last` is no token: appkit's `paging` (v0.26.0) encodes a token as unpadded URL-safe base64 of a payload and a four-byte checksum and refuses a decoded value shorter than four bytes, and `last` decodes to three; the definitions test it only after both tokens regardless. Both lists' tokens now carry two strings, so only the checksum over the tool's name keeps one list's token out of the other's parameter, and the definitions say so.
- **An underflow answers 303**, rather than drawing the first window under another address, so each list's first window has one URL and its page value is always empty. The redirect drops the underflowing list's parameter and keeps the other's as the request carried it, which is URL-safe since only a decodable value gets this far and appkit's tokens are URL-safe base64; when both underflow at once both are dropped, so one redirect lands on the first windows. The 303 is the listing entry's.
- **The last window is the last `paging.DefaultLimit` prospect lists by name, and the oldest `paging.DefaultLimit` passes**, aligned to the end of the list, not the final window of the Next walk: the two are equal only when the entries number a multiple of `paging.DefaultLimit`. Prev from the last window steps back in windows aligned to the end until one underflows.
- **One parameter per list** (`design/`'s two-list form): `prospect_lists_page` and `passes_page`, never `page`, and the page data carries each list's own token so the asset's navs carry the other list's along. An undecodable value in either is the existing not-found answer, decided after identity, path and method and before any store read, so a URL carrying a token the page never issued names no page and the answer is the same with the store unavailable.
- **The prospect list page is one `OpenProspectList`** (cohorts decisions 3 and 21; basis decision 7): its definition, `matching`, the counts by basis, the changes, its members and its standing rejects come from one store call, which also records the open, so the page never shows a member count from one moment and members from another. Its members are ranked from 1 in the order the read answers them; the rank is the place in the prospect list, which is the whole list, since a prospect list is read whole.
- **The prospect list page lists its members and its standing rejects whole, unpaged**, a departure from `shape/`'s Listing rule, which pages every list an agent or a person can add to. A prospect list is read whole in one answer (cohorts decision 2): its members are at most its size, and its size at most `store.ProspectListSizeMax`, so the members are bounded; its standing rejects are made by hand, one candidate at a time, and the `prospect_list` tool answers them whole too (cohorts decision 17), so the page and the tool show the same list. The changes' joined and left members are answered whole too; `D14-prospect-lists` extends this departure to them. The candidate page lists its reject records whole, as it lists its rulings.

## Known limits

- After a rollback to the previous release and a roll forward, a facts row the previous release rewrote may carry a stale `website_source` until the candidate's next result or a full `ingest` (`D04-store`). The prospect list row reads that row while the candidate card computes the decision from the results, so the two can differ for that candidate until then.
- A wrong site the search confirmed stays on the card and the prospect list row until a later confirmed search replaces it, as `D04-store`'s known limits record.
- After a rollback to the previous release and a roll forward, the facts row's drive decision may be stale until the candidate's next result or a full `ingest`, as `D04-store`'s recorded decisions say; the card and the prospect list row both read that row, so they agree with each other through it. How the previous release draws a `drive_route` result during a rollback is `D04-store`'s concern: it knows no such kind.
- How the previous release draws a `website_search` result during a rollback is `D04-store`'s known limit.
- Every `GET` and `HEAD` of a prospect list page records an open, a reload or a browser's prefetch included, so the next open reports changes since that one. The page has no peek; an agent that must not reset "since" reads through the `prospect_list` tool with `peek` (`D10-tools`).
- During a rollback the previous release's page records no open, so an open after the roll forward reports changes since the last open the current release recorded (`D04-store`).
