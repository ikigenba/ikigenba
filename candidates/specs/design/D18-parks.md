# D18-parks

Parks: what an industrial park is, as terms the rest of the design reads. candidates reads the Minnesota parcel records scrapes holds, the rows of the scraper `mn_parcels`, and derives industrial parks from them, so the owner can name a park for a later walk of the businesses in it. A **park** is a derived fact about a place, never a candidate: reading parcels creates no candidate, link, fact or enrichment, and the judge, the enrichers, the prospect lists and every tool that existed before parks answer exactly as before (parks outcome 9). Nothing is fetched and nothing is paid: no polygon, no Places call, no geocoding; a park's center, radius, town and ZIP come from the parcel records alone, and its drive minutes from the ZIP table the store already holds.

This document owns the words and the pure package `internal/parks`, which it declares whole, as `D16-enrichers` declares `internal/enrichers` and `D17-drive-route` `internal/routes`: a **parcel** and how one is read from a row (`parks.ParcelOf`), the **address key** (`parks.AddressKey`), the **rules** (`parks.Rules`) and the **rule** that picked a parcel, `label`, `value` or `candidate` (`parks.Classify`), the **distance** between two points (`parks.Distance`), the **clusters** of a county's industrial parcels with each one's center, radius, town, ZIP and building value (`parks.Clusters`), the matching of clusters to the parks already held, with what is updated, created, marked gone and grown (`parks.Match`), a park's **name** (`parks.Name`), its **map URL** (`parks.MapURL`), the view words (walked, gone and sort) and their validators, and the validators of the rules' values. It also owns the behavior of the store's park id validator `store.ValidParkID`, as `D14-prospect-lists` owns `store.ValidProspectListID`'s, and the terms the other documents read: the **derivation**, the **candidate keys**, the **seed rules**, the **counties known**, the **name of a park** in a store, the **park order** and **sort key**, and the **park filter**. It states no store method's, pass's, tool's or page's behavior but one, that none of them adds, changes or removes anything of a candidate (parks outcome 9). `D04-store` declares `store.ParkIDPrefix`, `store.ValidParkID`, `store.Park`, `store.ParkParcel`, `store.ParkFilter`, `store.ParkRulesEdit`, the parks tables, migration `0023`, the seed rows, and every park method; `D06-passes` owns the parks pass, started by a `scrape.finished` delivery of `mn_parcels`, by `edit_park_rules` and at start; `D07-events` the widened pass events; `D03-serve` the start-time read and the two routes; `D08-pages` the `parks` and `park` pages and the landing's park count; `D10-tools` the tools `parks`, `park`, `park_rules` and `edit_park_rules`; `D01-layout-and-run-seam` the package's place in the layout and the counts; `D11-on-a-space` the checks.

"Parks decision N" is entry N of the decisions of the parks change; "parks outcome N" is outcome N of its intent.

## Parcels

A row of `mn_parcels` is one parcel. Its **PIN** is the row's source id, the state pin. Its site address is the row's address (an Isanti row has no ZIP); its point is the row's latitude and longitude, absent when either is; its owners are the names of the row's people whose role is `owner`, in order. From the row's facts it takes `use_class`, a list of labels (a county's `useclass1` to `useclass4` joined; Mille Lacs puts several labels into one entry separated by commas, so a single string is read as a one-entry list), `homestead` (`Yes`, `No`, `Fractional` or nothing; nothing on every parcel of Chisago, Isanti, Stearns, Benton and Morrison) and `emv_bldg`, the building value in whole dollars (nothing on every parcel of Carlton and Benton, where every value field is null). A fact that is absent, null or of another type is read as nothing; a malformed fact never fails a read.

## The rules

The **rules** are one settings row, the **distance** in meters (seed 600), the **minimum parcels** (seed 3) and the **value threshold** in dollars (seed 1,000,000), and the **label rows**, each a county and a label. A county with at least one label row uses labels only: a parcel there is industrial when some entry of its use class, split on commas and each piece trimmed, equals one of its county's labels exactly, case and spacing as the source sends them; the rule is `label`. A county with no label row uses the two **fallbacks**: a parcel is industrial under `value` when it is not a homestead (its `homestead` is neither `Yes` nor `Fractional`, nothing counting as not a homestead) and its building value is known and above the threshold; otherwise under `candidate` when its address key equals the address key of an active candidate, any source's. `value` is tried before `candidate`, so a parcel both would pick is recorded `value`. The rule that picked each parcel is recorded on it.

The **address key** of a street and a ZIP is the street uppercased, every rune that is not a letter, a digit or white space removed and its white space collapsed to single spaces, then `|` and the five-digit ZIP; there is no key when either part is missing, so a parcel without a ZIP (every Isanti parcel) never matches a candidate, and a candidate without one is never matched.

The seed rules are data in the migration: the three numbers, and the labels Chisago `3a Industrial Land And Building`; Stearns `3a Industrial Land And Building`; Anoka `3a INDUSTIAL PREFERENTIAL` (the source's spelling); Aitkin `Industrial Preferred`; St. Louis `Industrial Preferred` and `Industrial Non-Preferred`; Sherburne `Industrial Preferred`, `Industrial Non-Preferred`, `INDUSTRIAL (with Buildings)` and `Industrial - Bare Land`; Mille Lacs `Indust Land/Building`; Isanti and Morrison `3A-Commercial/Industrial/Public Utility`. Carlton, Benton, Washington and Crow Wing have no labels and use the fallbacks; in Carlton and Benton, where the building value is always null, only `candidate` can pick a parcel.

## Clusters and parks

Per county, over its industrial parcels that have a point, two parcels within the distance of one another join one **cluster**, and so do their neighbors' neighbors (single linkage). A cluster of at least the minimum parcels is a park's worth. Its **center** is the mean of its parcels' latitudes and longitudes; its **radius** the greatest distance of a parcel from the center; its **town** the most common city among its parcels, the county when none has a city; its **ZIP** the most common five-digit ZIP among them, none when none has one; its **building value** the sum of its parcels' known building values. Distance is great-circle meters by the haversine formula over a sphere of the mean Earth radius.

## Identity

A park has an id, `park_` and 16 lowercase hexadecimal digits from the store's random source, minted once. Each derivation matches its clusters to the parks the store holds, active or gone, by shared parcels: a cluster sharing at least one PIN with a park **overlaps** it. Each park is claimed by at most one cluster and each cluster claims at most one park: the pairs are taken most shared parcels first, then the older park, so a cluster overlapping several parks updates the one it shares most with, the older on a tie, and the others are marked gone unless another cluster claims them. A claimed park is **updated**: its parcels become the cluster's, each with its rule, and a gone park comes back active. A cluster claiming none is a **new** park. An active park no cluster claims is marked **gone**, never deleted; it keeps its parcels, so a later cluster that overlaps it brings it back. A park that grows past the count it held carries **grown from**, the count before that growth, kept, not re-set by a later growth, until it is walked.

A park's town, county and number are fixed when it is minted. Its **number** is one more than the greatest number of a park in its county and town, gone ones counted, so numbers follow creation order within a town and never change. Its **name** is its town alone while the store holds one park in that county and town, and the town, a space and its number once it holds more, gone ones counted, so the first park of a town is renamed from `Wyoming` to `Wyoming 1` when a second is minted there.

A park is **walked** when a later walking run has visited it; it then carries when, the run and the places found. Nothing in this release walks a park, so every park is unwalked.

## The view

A list of parks is filtered by county, by **walked** (`all`, the default; `unwalked`; `walked`) and by **gone** (`active`, the default; `gone`; `all`), and sorted by **minutes** (the default, ascending, an unknown drive time last), **value** (building value, descending) or **parcels** (descending), the park id ascending breaking every tie, so a keyset over the sort key and the id pages it.

## Recorded decisions

- **The pure rules are a package** (parks decisions 2 to 4): `internal/parks` holds the reading of a parcel, the address key, the classification, the distance, the clustering, the matching and the name as functions of values, as `internal/judging` holds the judgment rules, so a test proves each by calling it without a database or a scrapes; the store keeps the records and the pass applies the functions.
- **The park id and its validator are the store's**, beside the candidate, pass and prospect list ids (`store.ParkIDPrefix`, `store.ValidParkID`), since the store mints the id; this document owns the validator's behavior, as `D14-prospect-lists` owns `store.ValidProspectListID`'s.
- **A pass kind reads a scraper outside the source registry** (departure): `mn_parcels` is not a source and stays unregistered, since a park is not a candidate and a parcel row becomes no match key, link or fact. The parks pass reads its rows through the same scrapes client, and the scraper's name, `parks.Scraper`, is this package's constant, not a registry entry, so `sources.Get` still answers false for it and an ingest still refuses it.
- **A scrapes read at start** (departure): after the start settles what a dead process left, candidates asks scrapes once for the current `mn_parcels` run and derives the parks in the background when that run differs from the one they were last derived from, so a release that brings new rules or a store that missed a delivery catches up without the owner; scrapes unreachable or holding no run starts nothing and the start proceeds (`D03-serve`, `D06-passes`).
- **The run the parks were last derived from is kept with the rules** (departure from reading it off the newest done parks pass): the prune keeps the newest passes of every kind together, so a parks pass is pruned within days of daily judges and enriches, and the derived run would be lost with it. The store records the run each derivation applies in the same transaction that finishes the pass done (`D04-store`), which equals the newest done parks pass's run while that pass is kept.
- **Code-built hrefs on the parks page** (departure, design's third paging form): every link of the parks page carries the view, county, walked, gone and sort, beside the page token, so the code builds each href and the template only draws them (`D08-pages`).
- **Labels match exactly, as the source spells them** (parks decision 2): no case folding, no fuzzy match, so `3a INDUSTIAL PREFERENTIAL` is kept with its misspelling. A label can hold no comma, since a use class entry is split on commas before it is compared, and never begins or ends with white space, since each piece is trimmed.
- **`value` before `candidate`**: a fallback parcel both would pick is recorded `value`, the one that needs no candidate, so the count by candidate is the parcels only a candidate's address found.
- **The bounds of the rules** are this document's: distance 1 to 5,000 meters (beyond 5 km single linkage chains whole towns); minimum parcels 2 to 100 (one parcel is not a park); value threshold 0 to 1,000,000,000 dollars; a county name and a label at most 100 and 200 bytes, valid UTF-8 with no control character and no surrounding white space; at most 100 labels per county, no two equal. A county need not be one the parcel records hold, so labels can be set before scrapes holds a county's records; such a county shows among the counties known.
- **A park's town, county and number are fixed at mint**, so a number never changes and two parks never share a name; an update moves the center, radius, ZIP, building value, parcels and run, never the name.
- **Splits are matched pairwise**: when one park's parcels fall into two clusters, the cluster sharing more of them keeps the park and the other claims its next best overlapping park or becomes a new one; this is the decision's most-shared rule applied from both sides.
- **A gone park keeps its parcels**, so a cluster that forms again where it stood overlaps it and brings it back active with its id, name and number.
- **Every claimed park counts as updated**, whether or not anything about it changed, and is rewritten with the run; a park counts as grown in the derivation in which its count rises.

## Consumer tasks

Proposed. The parks pass (`D06-passes`) reads `rules, _, err := st.ParkRules(ctx)`, then the candidate keys a batch at a time, `keys, after, err = st.ActiveAddressKeys(ctx, after, passes.BatchSize)` until `after` is empty, into `k := map[string]bool{}`, then `prior, err := st.PriorParks(ctx)`, then streams `sc.Rows(ctx, run, fn)` where `fn` does `p := parks.ParcelOf(row); p.Rule = parks.Classify(p, rules, k)`, adds `p.County` to the distinct `counties` of the rows read, and keeps `p` only when `p.Rule != ""`; then `cs := parks.Clusters(kept, rules)`, `plan := parks.Match(cs, prior)` and `st.ApplyParks(ctx, pass.ID, run, counties, plan, counts)`. A test of the package calls each function with values it builds: `parks.Classify(parks.Parcel{County: "Chisago", UseClass: []string{"3a Industrial Land And Building"}}, r, nil)` answers `parks.RuleLabel`; `parks.Clusters` over three parcels 500 m apart in one county with `r.Distance` 600 and `r.MinParcels` 3 answers one cluster; `parks.Match` over that cluster and a prior park sharing one PIN answers one update of that park.
