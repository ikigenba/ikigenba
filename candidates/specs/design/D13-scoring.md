# D13-scoring

Judgments: what `candidates` says about each candidate, as two separate answers, under rules version 3. The first is **operating confidence**: how sure we are that it is a real business operating today, from 0 to 1, with the evidence behind it. The second is the **revenue estimate**: a low, a high and how much we trust the range. Beside them an assessment carries the facts the cohort filters will read: when the business is first known to have existed, the newest filing, its categories, the government name pattern it matched, the fleet test and the drive minutes. This document owns the pure functions of `internal/scoring` (the combined estimate, the freshness of a filing, the judged weight of a piece of evidence, the combining rule and the order key), what a judge writes (`store.Judge`, `store.JudgeAll`, and the judging `store.Decide` does of what it touches), and the **shortlist**: which candidates `store.Shortlist` answers and in which order. What a source reads from one row (its estimate, evidence, dates, category and government match) is `D05-sources`'; which links a candidate holds and in which order is `D12-resolution`'s; the records are `D04-store`'s; when a judge runs is `D06-passes`'.

## Why two judgments

Rules version 2 ranked the shortlist by one number, confidence times estimated revenue, and that number mixed two questions. Carrier type says how well a truck count predicts revenue; filing age says whether the business still exists. Blended, a well-documented trucking company always outranked a real manufacturer, and nobody could tell from the number why. Version 3 answers the two questions separately, and filing age touches only the first.

## Operating confidence

A source reads, from each row, the **row evidence** it gives: each piece a kind (`fmcsa_active`, `fmcsa_activity`, `ppp_loan`, `own_domain`), its source, its weight before freshness and a short description (`D05-sources`). At a judge, each piece of each link not gone is weighed: an `fmcsa_active` piece is multiplied by the **freshness** of its row's filing date, 1 within two years of the date (the MCS-150 cycle), falling linearly to 0 at five years, and 0 without a date; every other kind keeps its weight. A piece whose judged weight is not above 0 says nothing and is left out. Each kind counts once per candidate, from the link that gives it the greatest weight, and the kept pieces are listed by weight, highest first.

The **operating evidence** of a candidate is that list. Today it is exactly the row evidence; it is the seam for evidence that comes from enrichment rather than a scraped row (the website check, `website_live`, a later change), which would add pieces here with its own source name. Nothing of enrichment is designed now.

The combining rule closes part of the gap to certainty with each piece: `1 − (1 − w₁) × (1 − w₂) × …`. Weights 0.6, 0.3 and 0.2 give 0.776. **Missing evidence is never zero**: a candidate with no evidence has no operating confidence, not 0, and sorts after every candidate that has one.

## Revenue estimate

Each source gives each candidate one **estimate**: for FMCSA the estimate of its freshest link not gone, unchanged from version 2 except that it is no longer multiplied by freshness; for PPP the estimate of the link with the largest amount, which is the largest low since PPP's low is proportional to the amount. The **combined** estimate weights by confidence: `low` is the confidence-weighted mean of the sources' lows, `high` the same over the sources that give one and absent when none does, the confidence the highest of theirs. A candidate with no estimate has no revenue fields at all. Disagreement between sources is still deferred.

## The assessment

A judge writes, for each active candidate it is given, reading by its id the candidate's record, its own links and rulings, and the ZIP table's minutes for its display ZIP, its estimates and one **assessment**: the operating confidence with its order key and its operating evidence; the combined revenue low, high and confidence; **known since**, the earliest date any link not gone shows the business existing; the **newest filing** among its links not gone; its **categories**, each category a link not gone gives with the scraper that gave it, `other` a real category and an empty list unknown; the **government match**, the first pattern a link's name matched in link order; the **fleet** pass (some source's freshest link not gone passes the fleet test); the drive minutes from its display ZIP (absent when the ZIP table lacks it); whether it is excluded; the rules version and when it was calculated. Only links not gone count, for every field.

**Nothing is removed automatically.** The government name patterns now fill the government match and exclude nothing; the fleet test is stored and gates no list; an old filing, a missing estimate or missing minutes hide nothing. A candidate is excluded only when the owner's latest `exclude` or `include` on it is an `exclude`.

**Judgments are written only by a judge.** The judge pass (`D06-passes`, daily from the `judge_candidates` trigger), a rebuild (which ingests every source's current run, then judges every candidate), and an owner's ruling (`merge`, `split`, `exclude`, `include`, which judges the candidates it touches in the same call, so the list answers a ruling at once; a `not_same` judges nothing) are the only writers. An ingest pass stores rows and judges nothing. There is no refresh: the pass-end statement of version 2 that re-applied the filing-age arithmetic in SQL to every stored score, and its agreement test with Go, are gone, so the formulas live once, in Go. Between judges nothing decays: a stored judgment is what the last judge wrote, and the daily judge keeps it within a day of true.

**Why daily, not on change and not on read.** The candidates whose `fmcsa_active` weight is moving are the ones whose filing nobody updated, so their rows never change: 2,431 of the 16,706 active MN and WI carriers are in the two-to-five-year band, and 2,663 change weight within 30 days. Judging only changed candidates would never revisit them. Judging on read would make the first read after a quiet stretch wait for a full judge (18 seconds for the 16,580 candidates on `us`) and would add a staleness check to every read path, to save 18 seconds of CPU a day. A daily trigger is the pattern the suite already has.

## The shortlist

The **shortlist** stays until the cohorts replace it, so the owner can see the new judgments. It is every active, judged candidate the owner has not excluded, ranked by operating confidence, highest first, empty last, the candidate id breaking ties. Only the filters a caller passes apply, read against the new fields: maximum drive minutes, minimum revenue (against the revenue low), class and cargo. A candidate whose value for a filter is unknown is left out only when that filter is passed. Every threshold lives in the query, never in stored data.

## Rules version 3

The rules version is `sources.Rules`, and its value is 3; no requirement spells it, and tests read the value the source declares. Every estimate and assessment written carries it. Version 3 is a change of rules, so it needs a rebuild: every row is read again and every candidate judged again, the owner's rulings reapplied as always. Old assessments are discarded by the migration and rewritten by the rebuild (`D04-store`).

## Recorded decisions

- **Ordering departure**: the shortlist is ordered by the **operating key**, the operating confidence scaled by a million and truncated to an integer, −1 when there is none, then by candidate id in byte order. appkit's paging token carries only integers and strings, so the keyset position must be exact; two confidences less than a millionth apart may share a key and then fall to id order, a departure from ordering by the float (intent decision 18) that no person can see. An empty confidence's key of −1 sorts after every judged value.
- **Rounding**: the combined low and high are the weighted means rounded half away from zero to whole dollars (`math.Round`), saturating at the `int64` bounds. With confidences summing to 0 the mean is unweighted; no rule of version 3 makes one.
- **Two and five years are calendar years** from the filing date (`AddDate(2, 0, 0)` and `AddDate(5, 0, 0)`), linear in elapsed time between them; a filing dated after the moment weighs 1. Freshness is measured at the judge's moment in UTC truncated to the second.
- **Evidence weighing nothing is left out**: a stale or undated `fmcsa_active` adds no piece, so a candidate with nothing else has no operating confidence rather than 0 (intent decision 2).
- **Evidence order** is weight, highest first, then kind name in byte order; one kind's best piece is the first in link order among equal weights.
- **PPP's estimate is its largest**: the PPP link whose estimate has the greatest low (the largest amount) gives PPP's estimate, the first in link order on ties; every other source keeps its freshest link's.
- **The government match is the first in link order** among links not gone, from any source's name.
- **Unranged now means no revenue**: a judge counts the candidates it judged whose revenue low is absent; missing minutes no longer matter to any count.
- **A candidate not yet judged is not on the shortlist**: with no assessment there is nothing to rank; a rebuild or the next judge gives it one.
- **A filter is applied as given**: the store has no defaults; a filter not passed is nil and not applied, and a passed maximum of 0 keeps only candidates at minute 0. The tools pass what the caller passed; the landing page passes no filter.
- **The class and cargo filters compare exactly** with the candidate's display tags (`D12-resolution`): `for_hire`, and cargo words as scrapes spells them (`genfreight`).
- **Judge ignores ids it cannot judge**: an id naming no candidate or a merged one is skipped, not refused, since a ruling may merge a candidate between a pass's work and its judge.
- **A merged candidate keeps its estimates and last assessment** as they stood at the merge and is never judged again; the survivor's are recomputed; the shortlist answers active candidates only.
- **The old score fields are not written**: confidence, base, effective, score, score key, filed and exclusions stay as columns this release leaves empty and the next drops (`D04-store`).

## Consumer tasks

Current: the engine (`D06-passes`) called `st.Rescore(ctx, touched)` after every run and `st.RescoreAll(ctx)` for a rebuild, then `st.RefreshShortlist(ctx)` at every pass end; the start refreshed before ready; `shortlist` and the landing page read `Effective`, `Score` and `ScoreKey` under `scoring.DefaultMaxMinutes` and `scoring.DefaultMinRevenue`.

Proposed: an ingest pass calls neither; the judge pass and a rebuild call `c, err := st.JudgeAll(ctx)` once and add `c.Judged` and `c.Unranged` to the pass's counts; nothing refreshes. `Decide` judges what it touches itself. The tools (`D10-tools`) answer `shortlist` from `rs, err := st.Shortlist(ctx, f, key, id, n+1)`, `f` holding only the filters the caller passed, each entry showing `r.Assessment.Operating`, the three revenue fields, `Categories`, `Fleet`, `Minutes` and `GovernmentMatch`, the token over `(r.Assessment.OperatingKey, r.Candidate.ID)`; the landing page (`D08-pages`) passes `store.ShortlistFilter{}`. `candidate` and the candidate page show `GetAssessment`'s fields. A test of `internal/scoring` calls `scoring.Operating([]sources.Evidence{{Weight: 0.6}, {Weight: 0.3}, {Weight: 0.2}})` and compares within 1e-9 of 0.776. A test of a judge attaches observations whose readings it builds, sets its clock, calls `st.Judge(ctx, ids)` and compares `GetAssessment` with the assessment it expects.
