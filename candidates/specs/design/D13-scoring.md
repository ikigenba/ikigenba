# D13-scoring

Scoring: what a candidate is worth against the fit test, rules version 1 (decisions 8 and 9, and the effect of decision 10's `exclude` and `include`). It owns the pure functions of `internal/scoring` (the combined estimate, the freshness weight, the effective confidence, the point estimate, the score), the rules version `sources.Rules` and the ruling exclusion word `sources.ExcludeRuling`, what a re-score writes (`store.Rescore`, `store.RescoreAll`, and the re-score `store.Decide` makes of what it touches), and the **shortlist**: which candidates `store.Shortlist` answers, in which order, with which numbers. What a source estimates from one row is `D05-sources`'; which links a candidate holds and which is its freshest is `D12-resolution`'s; the records are `D04-store`'s. The fit test is in range, a fleet, and ten million dollars or more flowing through it.

## Estimates and the combined guess

Each source gives each candidate it has evidence on one **estimate**: the estimate its source read from the candidate's freshest link of that source that is not gone (for FMCSA, `n` the power units of that link), with its low, its high (absent when the source gives only a floor), its confidence, the facts it used, its filing date and the rules version. A source whose freshest link gives none, or whose links are all gone, gives the candidate none.

The **combined** estimate weights by confidence rather than averaging: `low` is the confidence-weighted mean of the sources' lows, `high` the same over the sources that give one and absent when none does, `confidence` the highest of theirs. Disagreement between sources is deferred until there is a second source.

## Freshness, the point and the score

Freshness is judged when a number is asked for, never stored: a filing's weight is 1 within two years of its date (the MCS-150 cycle), falls linearly to 0 at five years, and is 0 without a date. The **effective confidence** is the stored confidence times the weight of the newest filing among the candidate's links not gone, so a candidate whose only evidence is stale or gone has effective confidence 0 and drops off the shortlist until another source shows it alive, and nothing is reprocessed as time passes. The **point** estimate is the midpoint when `high` is present and `low` otherwise; the **score** is the point times a confidence: the stored **base** is the point times the stored confidence, and the score at a moment is the point times the effective confidence then.

## The assessment

A re-score writes, for each active candidate it is given, its estimates and one **assessment**: the fleet pass (a source's freshest link not gone passes the fleet test), the combined low, high and confidence, the newest filing date among its links not gone, the drive minutes from its display ZIP, its exclusions with their reasons and whether it is excluded, the base, the rules version and when it was scored.

**Range is a ZIP table** (decision 9): the minutes are the `zip_drive` table's for the candidate's display ZIP, the table the start loads whole from the release (`D04-store`, `D03-serve`). A ZIP the table lacks, an empty one included, leaves the minutes absent: the candidate is **unranged**, shows no minutes and is never on the shortlist; a re-score counts the unranged candidates it wrote.

**Exclusions**: the exclusion signals of its links not gone, each reason and detail once; and, when the owner's latest `exclude` or `include` on it is an `exclude`, the `ruling` reason. It is **excluded** when its latest such ruling is an `exclude`, not excluded when it is an `include` (a reason found by the rules stays listed but no longer counts), and, with no such ruling, excluded when any exclusion was found. Rulings are applied at every re-score, so a rerun never overwrites one.

`decide` re-scores the candidates a ruling touches in the same call (coordinator decision C2): the survivor of a merge, both sides of a split, the candidate of an `exclude` or `include`; so the shortlist answers a ruling at once. A `not_same` changes no score.

## The shortlist

The **shortlist** of a filter at a moment is every active candidate with an assessment that is in range (drive minutes present and at most the filter's maximum), big enough (`high` absent or at least the filter's minimum revenue: near-line candidates are kept, not cut), passes the fleet test, is not excluded, has an effective confidence above 0 at that moment, and carries the filter's class tag and cargo tag when it names them; ranked by its score at that moment, highest first. Every threshold lives in the query, never in stored data: loosening a cutoff is a number in a call, not a reprocess. The defaults the tools and the landing page use are `scoring.DefaultMaxMinutes` (120) and `scoring.DefaultMinRevenue` (10,000,000).

## Recorded decisions

- **Rounding**: the combined low and high are the weighted means rounded half away from zero to whole dollars (`math.Round`), saturating at the `int64` bounds; the point of a range is the exact midpoint truncated toward zero, computed without overflow. With confidences summing to 0 (no rule of version 1 makes one) the mean is unweighted.
- **Two and five years are calendar years** from the filing date (`AddDate(2, 0, 0)` and `AddDate(5, 0, 0)`), linear in elapsed time between them; a filing dated after the moment weighs 1.
- **The fleet pass is any source's**: true when the freshest not-gone link of some source passes; with one source, the freshest FMCSA link's.
- **The filing date that weighs is the newest among all links not gone**, as decision 8 says, even when that link's source gave no estimate.
- **Exclusions come from links not gone** only: a gone row no longer counts for the score, and its exclusions are part of the score.
- **The ruling exclusion carries no detail**: the why of a ruling stays in the ruling, kept in the store and nowhere else (decision 10), so an assessment never repeats it.
- **The shortlist is ordered by score at the moment, then by candidate id** in byte order, so equal scores have one order on every call and a page boundary is stable.
- **A filter is applied as given**: the store has no defaults of its own; a `MaxMinutes` of 0 keeps only candidates at minute 0. The tools and pages pass the defaults.
- **The class and cargo filters compare exactly** with the candidate's display tags (`D12-resolution`): `for_hire`, and cargo words as scrapes spells them (`genfreight`).
- **Rescore ignores ids it cannot score**: an id naming no candidate or a merged one is skipped, not refused, since a ruling may merge a candidate between a pass's attach and its re-score.
- **A merged candidate keeps its estimates and last assessment** as they stood at the merge and is never re-scored (`D12-resolution`); the survivor's are recomputed; the shortlist answers active candidates only.

## Consumer tasks

The engine (`D06-passes`) calls `c, err := st.Rescore(ctx, touched)` after a run's batches and its `MarkGone`, or `st.RescoreAll(ctx)` for a rebuild, and adds `c.Rescored` and `c.Unranged` to the pass's counts. The tools (`D10-tools`) answer `shortlist` from `rs, err := st.Shortlist(ctx, store.ShortlistFilter{MaxMinutes: scoring.DefaultMaxMinutes, MinRevenue: scoring.DefaultMinRevenue, Class: sources.ClassForHire, Cargo: "genfreight"}, 25*page, 26)`, each entry showing `r.Candidate`, `r.Assessment.Minutes`, `r.Assessment.Low`, `r.Assessment.High`, `r.Effective` and `r.Score`; `candidate` shows `a, _ := st.GetAssessment(ctx, id)` with `e := scoring.Effective(a.Confidence, a.Filed, cfg.Now())` and `scoring.Score(scoring.Point(a.Low, a.High), e)`. A page shows the freshness as `scoring.Freshness(a.Filed, now)` and each estimate from `st.Estimates(ctx, id)`. A test of `internal/scoring` calls `scoring.Combine([]sources.Estimate{{Low: 100, Confidence: 0.25}, {Low: 400, High: &h, Confidence: 0.75}})` and compares `Combined{Low: 325, High: &h, Confidence: 0.75}`.
