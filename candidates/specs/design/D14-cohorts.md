# D14-cohorts

Cohorts: what a cohort is, as terms the rest of the design reads. A **cohort** is a saved search the owner creates and grooms: a name, filters, a sort order and a size, with a reject list kept beside it. Nothing is stored about who is in it: its **members** are worked out every time it is read, from the candidates and their assessments, each carrying the candidate's facts as its facts row holds them at that read, its opinions as the last judge wrote them and when its site text and its brief were last written, the cohort's standing rejects and the clock's date at that read. This document owns the words: the cohort id's form, the size bounds and default, each filter with its switch for unknowns, the date a read measures filing age at, the order rule, the **matching** candidates, the **members** with the site text and brief times each carries, the **classified** count of the members whose categories a brief gave, the **standing reject** and the exclusion rule; and what the validators `store.ValidCohortID`, `store.ValidCohortName`, `store.ValidCohortSize`, `store.ValidCohortFilters` and `store.ValidCohortOrder` accept. It states no method's behavior. The names it uses (`store.Cohort`, `store.CohortFilters`, `store.CohortOrder`, `store.Reject`, the size, order and kind constants and the validators' signatures) are declared in `D04-store`, which also owns every store method: creating, editing and deleting a cohort, reading one (`ReadCohort` answers this document's members, matching, classified and standing rejects at the moment it reads `Config.Now`), recording rejects and un-rejects, and the errors. The tools that create, groom and read cohorts are `D10-tools`'; the landing's cohort list and the cohort page are `D08-pages`'. The assessment's opinions (operating confidence, revenue, exclusion) are `D13-scoring`'s; the facts it carries (known since, newest filing, categories, government match, fleet verdict with power units and drivers, `website` and drive minutes) are what the enrichers derive or fetch (`D16-enrichers`, `D15-website-check`), and the facts row that holds them, with `GetAssessment` answering them from it, is `D04-store`'s; `store.CohortFilters.Website`, `store.CohortFilters.WebsiteUnknown` and the assessment's `Website` are declared in `D04-store`, and the `website` and `website_unknown` tool arguments are `D10-tools`'. `store.Ranked.SiteText` is declared in `D04-store`, the `site_text` kind and its results are `D16-enrichers`', and the member's `site_text` answer and the cohort page's site text column are `D10-tools`' and `D08-pages`'. `store.Ranked.Brief` is declared in `D04-store` (R-L490-XD2E), the `brief` kind and its results are `D16-enrichers`', how a brief's summary and categories reach the facts row, and `store.ClassifiedBy`, which reads from a set of categories whether a brief or the links gave it, are `D04-store`'s, and the member's `brief` answer and the cohort page's brief column are `D10-tools`' and `D08-pages`'; a targeted enrich over a cohort walks this document's members (`D06-passes`).

Decisions cited by number are the decisions document of the cohorts change; "log N" is entry N of its decisions log. "Website decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the website-check change, "site text decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the site-text change, "brief decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the brief change, and "category evidence decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the category-evidence change.

## What a cohort is

A cohort's id is `coh_` and 16 lowercase hexadecimal digits, minted like a candidate's and never reused (decision 1); the name is free text, required, and not unique, since the id is the handle. Its size is 1 to 100, 30 when its creator passes none, and it is read whole, since its size bounds it (decision 2). Cohorts belong to the space: every signed-in caller sees and may change every one (decision 8).

## Filters

Each filter applies only when the cohort carries it, and a cohort with no filters is the ranked view of every judged active candidate the owner has not excluded. Every filter on a fact that can be unknown has a switch, false unless set, that admits the candidates whose fact is unknown; a switch admits unknowns for its own filter only, the filters a cohort carries must all pass, and a switch set without its filter's value carries nothing and tests nothing (log 1), except `category_unknown`, which alone keeps only the candidates with no category. The filters read the assessment as `GetAssessment` answers it, and the class filter reads the candidate's class tag. The assessment's facts are the candidate's facts row's at the read, every one unknown, and the government match empty, when the candidate has no row yet; its operating confidence, revenue and exclusion are the last judge's:

- **revenue**, carried when either bound is set: a known revenue low at least the minimum (0 when absent) and at most the maximum (unbounded when absent), both inclusive (log 2); no estimate passes only with `revenue_unknown`.
- **drive time**, a maximum only: known minutes at most it; no minutes only with `minutes_unknown`.
- **category**, a list of category names, any of the twenty-three `sources` declares, `other` among them: some category of the candidate's in the list; no category only with `category_unknown`. An empty list with `category_unknown` keeps only the candidates with no category; an empty list without it carries nothing. A candidate's categories are its facts row's, which hold one set or the other, never both: the categories of its newest good brief, each with source `brief`, when that brief gives at least one, and otherwise those its links and its `name` give through `link_facts`, each with the source that gave it (category evidence decisions 1 and 2; the projection is `D04-store`'s). The filter matches by name whatever the source (category evidence decision 6), so a candidate whose brief decides is admitted by the brief's categories alone, and one with no brief, or whose brief gives none, by its links'.
- **fleet**, `pass` or `fail`: the fleet verdict equal to it; an `unknown` verdict only with `fleet_unknown`. `unknown` is not a value the filter takes, since the switch covers it (log 4).
- **website**, a list of states drawn from `https`, `http` and `none`: the assessment's `website` fact equal to some state in the list, so `https` alone is a secure site, `https` with `http` is any site, and `none` alone is an own domain with no site; a fact `unknown` only with `website_unknown`, never through `none` (website decision 16).
- **government**, true or false: true keeps the candidates whose name matched a government pattern, false those whose did not. It is never unknown.
- **filing age**, a number of days: the newest filing at most that many whole days before the date of the read; no newest filing only with `filing_unknown`.
- **carrier class**, `for_hire`, `private` or `mixed`: the class tag equal to it; a candidate with no class tag, or the tag `unknown`, only with `class_unknown` (log 26).
- **excluded**: unless the cohort shows excluded candidates, a candidate the owner's standing ruling excludes is left out. It is never unknown.

Cargo is not a filter; it stays a label on the candidate. The website state is a filter but not an order field (website decision 16).

## The clock

Filing age is the one place a read measures against a clock. The date of a read is the UTC calendar date of the moment the store reads its own clock, once per read (log 3), so the tool and the page each measure on the day they are called, and a test sets the date through the clock it hands the store.

## Order

A cohort's order is a list of fields, each with a direction, over `operating_confidence`, `revenue_low`, `revenue_confidence`, `drive_minutes`, `newest_filing` and `known_since`, each at most once. The first entry under which two candidates differ decides; a candidate whose value is unknown comes after every candidate whose value is known, whatever the direction; and the candidate id, ascending, breaks every remaining tie, so the same records always give the same list. An empty order is `operating_confidence` descending (decision 5).

## Members and matching

The **matching** candidates of a cohort at a read are the active candidates that have been judged (that hold an assessment, log 5), that the cohort has no standing reject of, that the exclusion rule admits and that pass every filter the cohort carries, in the cohort's order. **matching** is their number, which tells the owner whether the filters are too tight or too loose; the **members** are the first `size` of them (decision 3). A new scrape, a new enrichment, a new judgment, a ruling or a reject changes the members at the next read, an enrichment through the facts it writes before any judge runs, and a candidate may be a member of several cohorts.

Each member also carries when site text was last written for it: the time of its newest `site_text` result, none when it has never had one (site text decision 18). It is carried, not filtered or ordered on: no filter or order field reads it, so a `site_text` result changes no cohort's matching, members or order, and the cohort query is unchanged (site text decision 11).

Each member carries when its brief was last written the same way: the time of its newest `brief` result, failed or not, none when it has never had one (brief decisions 8 and 11). The time is carried, not filtered or ordered on, and the cohort query is unchanged (brief decision 8). A brief's categories are another matter: when the brief is good and gives at least one, they replace the links' in the facts row (category evidence decision 1), so a `brief` result can change a cohort's matching and members through the category filter, admitting a candidate or leaving one out, as any enrichment does through the facts it writes.

A read also counts the members a brief has classified: **classified** is the number of members whose assessment's categories are the brief's set, which `store.ClassifiedBy` of them answers as `brief` (category evidence decisions 3 and 5). A member's classification is that same answer over the same categories the category filter reads, so the label and the filter cannot disagree. It is counted, not filtered or ordered on, and `store.Ranked` gains no field for it: the member's tool answer and its page row read it from the member's assessment (`D10-tools`, `D08-pages`). When classified equals the number of members, every member is classified by its brief.

## Rejects

A reject or un-reject is a record naming a cohort, a candidate, who, when and why; the latest on a cohort-and-candidate pair stands (decision 10). The **standing reject** of a pair is that latest record when it is a reject. A reject may name a candidate that is not a member, so the owner can keep out one that will surface later; reject records are never removed, not when un-rejected and not when their cohort is deleted (decision 11). Which calls record what, and what they answer, is `D04-store`'s.

## Validation

The five validators are pure, read no store, and are the one place each rule lives: the tools call them before the store and answer `invalid`, and the store calls them again and answers `ErrInvalid`, so the two cannot disagree (log 7). Refused: a malformed id (a `not_found`, since it can name no cohort), a blank name, a size outside 1 to 100, a negative money, minutes or days value, a minimum revenue above the maximum, a category that is not one of the twenty-three category names, a fleet value other than `pass` or `fail`, a website list that is empty, repeats a state or holds anything but `https`, `http` or `none` (website decision 16), a class other than `for_hire`, `private` or `mixed`, an order field or direction outside its set, and a field repeated in the order (decision 9). A blank reason is the tools' and `Reject`'s own check (`D10-tools`, `D04-store`), not a cohort's.

## Recorded decisions

- **Switches are per filter** and carry nothing alone (log 1), `category_unknown` excepted: a switch set without its filter's value is not refused, and every other one admits nothing because nothing is filtered; `category_unknown` alone keeps only the candidates with no category.
- **Revenue bounds** default to 0 and unbounded and are inclusive (log 2); a known revenue low below 0, which no source gives, fails a carried revenue filter.
- **Filing age counts whole UTC days** (log 3): a newest filing 730 days before the date of the read passes a limit of 730, one 731 days before fails, and a filing on or after that date passes every limit. The rule is stated as a count of days between two dates rather than as a date moved back, so no limit a direct store caller passes can overflow a time.
- **The fleet filter takes `pass` or `fail`** (log 4); a verdict `unknown` is admitted only by the switch.
- **The website filter takes `https`, `http` and `none`** (website decision 16): `unknown` is not a value it takes, since `website_unknown` covers it, as the fleet filter leaves `unknown` to its switch (log 4). The switch is per filter and carries nothing alone (log 1), so a cohort with `website_unknown` and no list admits every candidate whatever its state.
- **An empty website list is refused, unlike an empty category list** (website decision 16): an empty category list is read as an absent one because the stored filters cannot tell the two apart, and the website list is stored the same way, but a caller who passes an empty website list has asked for no state at all, so it is refused rather than read as no filter. A repeated state is refused too. The list is kept as given, not sorted or deduplicated, so the store's normal form (`D04-store`) is unchanged.
- **"Judged" is an assessment row** (log 5), as the shortlist's base was. A candidate with a facts row and no assessment row is not judged and is no member.
- **Facts come from the facts row, opinions from the last judge.** Every filter and order term reads `a`, what `GetAssessment` answers, so moving the facts' source from the assessment's columns to the facts row (`D04-store`) changed no term here: an enrichment's facts reach every cohort at the next read, and the judge that follows it changes only the opinions. A candidate with no facts row reads every fact unknown and no government match, so among the fact filters only the unknown switches and a government filter of false admit it.
- **`class_unknown` admits no tag and the tag `unknown`** (log 26): both say the class is not known, and nothing is left out silently.
- **An empty category list is an absent one**: the stored filters omit empty members, so an empty list cannot be told from an absent one when read back; it carries no category filter unless `category_unknown` is set.
- **Operating confidence orders by the operating key**, `D13-scoring`'s exact integer of the rounded confidence; a candidate with no operating confidence is unknown and comes last in either direction.
- **The excluded filter reads the assessment's `Excluded`**, which every exclusion or inclusion re-judges in the same call (`D13-scoring`), so an exclusion takes effect in every cohort at once and an inclusion brings the candidate back everywhere (decision 12).
- **Standing rejects are listed whatever the candidate's status**: a reject recorded before its candidate was merged still stands and is still shown, and it cannot be cleared, since `Reject` refuses a merged candidate either way (`D04-store`); only active candidates can be members, so it affects no member.
- **Negative numbers inside `filters` reach the validators only from a direct store caller**: on the tool path appkit's schema refuses them first (log 15; `D10-tools` records the departure from decision 9's word), and the validators still refuse them so the store's own rule is whole.
- **A member's site text is the newest result's time, not a fact** (site text decisions 11 and 18): it is the `At` of the candidate's newest `site_text` result, the same time the facts row's `site_text` entry holds, and the zero time when there is none, which the tool answers as null and the page as blank. It is neither a filter nor an order field, since site text feeds no judgment and the decisions leave the cohort query unchanged; a failed run is still a result, so its member shows site text as written.
- **A member's brief is the newest result's time, not its text** (brief decisions 8 and 11): it is the `At` of the candidate's newest `brief` result, the same time the facts row's `brief_at` holds, and the zero time when there is none, which the tool answers as null and the page as blank. A failed run is still a result (brief decision 6), so its member shows a brief as written while the facts row's `brief` text stays the newest non-failed one's (`D04-store`). It is neither a filter nor an order field; the brief reaches the filters only through the categories it gives the facts row.
- **The category filter on words is unchanged; what the row holds changed** (category evidence decisions 1 and 6): R-BENE-ZGE3 reads `a.Categories`, which holds either the newest good brief's categories alone or, when there is no good brief or it gives none, those from the links and the name, never a mix, so with words given its meaning is unchanged; a filter on a word passes a candidate whose brief decides only when the brief gives that word, whatever its links say, and `category_unknown` admits only a candidate whose row holds no category.
- **Departure from category evidence decision 6, "the filter is unchanged"**: `category_unknown` with no category words keeps only the candidates with no category, rather than carrying nothing (R-BENE-ZGE3, R-BFVB-D84S). That document's finish line and smoke test require a cohort of `category_unknown` with no category words, `fleet` pass and `website` any to show the private carriers the records cannot place; under the old rule that cohort had no category filter and held every candidate, 16,471 on `us`. With words given the filter is as that decision says.
- **Classified is a new term beside matching, not a change to it** (category evidence decision 5): which candidates match and what each `store.Ranked` carries are defined as before (R-BH37-QZVH), since only the facts row's categories changed, so classified is its own term (R-BIB4-4RM6) over the members, read from the assessment each already carries; `D04-store` declares `store.CohortRead.Classified` and has `ReadCohort` answer it.
- **Editing and deleting are `D04-store`'s**: a passed filter set or order replacing the whole, an empty or unchanged edit writing nothing, and a deleted cohort's reject records remaining are store-method behavior, not terms.
- **Departure from `shape/` Listing**: a cohort's members and its standing rejects are answered whole, not paged. Members are bounded by the cohort's size, at most 100 (decision 2); the standing rejects of a cohort are what the owner rejected while grooming a list of 20 or 30, and a reject list long enough to need paging is a cohort to start again. Counting `matching` is one count over the same condition, filtered by the database as `shape/` Storage asks (decision 25).

## Consumer tasks

Proposed. The tools (`D10-tools`) check a `create_cohort` before the store: `store.ValidCohortName(a.Name)`, `store.ValidCohortSize(n)` with `n` the passed size or `store.CohortSizeDefault` when none is passed, `store.ValidCohortFilters(f)` over the filters converted to a `store.CohortFilters`, and `store.ValidCohortOrder(o)`; any false is `invalid`. `edit_cohort` checks each part passed the same way, then `store.ValidCohortID(a.Cohort)`, false being `not_found`; `cohort`, `delete_cohort`, `reject` and `unreject` check the id the same way. The store (`D04-store`) answers `cr, err := st.ReadCohort(ctx, id)` with `cr.Members` this document's members, each with its site text and brief times, `cr.Matching` its matching and `cr.Classified` its classified at the moment it reads `Config.Now`, and `cr.Rejects` the cohort's standing rejects; a test of the store builds candidates, records their enrichments with `st.AddEnrichments` or `st.Derive`, `site_text` and `brief` results among them at clock times it chooses, judges them with its clock set, records rejects, sets the clock to the date of the read and compares `cr` with the members, matching, classified and standing rejects this document defines. The landing and the cohort page (`D08-pages`) show what `ReadCohort` and `ListCohorts` answer and compute nothing of their own. A test of the validators calls them directly: `store.ValidCohortFilters(store.CohortFilters{Fleet: sources.FleetUnknown})` is false, `store.ValidCohortFilters(store.CohortFilters{Website: []string{}})` is false, and `store.ValidCohortOrder([]store.CohortOrder{{Field: store.OrderRevenueLow, Direction: store.DirectionAsc}, {Field: store.OrderRevenueLow, Direction: store.DirectionDesc}})` is false.
