# D05-sources

The sources: the fixed registry that knows sources by name (`sources.Registry`), and the one source this pass registers, `fmcsa`, which reads a row of scrapes' FMCSA scraper (decisions 3 and 7). A **source** is Go code, not configuration: it knows how one row of its scraper becomes a **reading** (`sources.Reading`), the row's match keys, its common fields as candidates keeps them, its class and cargo tags, its filing date, whether it passes the fleet test, the size estimate it gives with the facts it used, and its exclusion signals. A source reads one row at a time and needs nothing else: no other row, no candidate, no clock, no read of scrapes. Adding a source is adding one to the registry; the candidate, the links, the pass, the tools and the pages hold every source without change. The types a source makes, the `Source` interface and the registry's shape are `D01-layout-and-run-seam`'s; the normalized keys a reading carries (`sources.NameKey` and its siblings) and the rules of matching are `D12-resolution`'s; what a re-score does with the readings, and the rules version, are `D13-scoring`'s. The engine (`D06-passes`) hands each streamed row to the source the registry holds for its scraper and skips a run of a scraper the registry does not hold.

## The registry

The registry is fixed: `Run` builds it as `sources.New(fmcsa.New())`, so the one source is `fmcsa` and a `scrape.finished` for any other scraper is skipped (`D06-passes`). A test builds a registry of its own sources with `sources.New`. A source's name is the name of the scraper whose rows it reads, so a link's scraper, an estimate's source and the registry's name are one word.

## FMCSA

The FMCSA source reads the row envelope appkit's `scrapes.Row` decodes, as scrapes' FMCSA scraper fills it: the common fields as the scraper normalized them, and `facts`, the source's strings as received, null when absent, with `cargo` a sorted list of the cargo codes the record marks. A **string fact** is a fact whose value is a JSON string; a fact that is absent, null, a number or anything else gives no value, and no rule reads it.

- **Identity.** The reading's name, names, street, city, state and ZIP are the row's as streamed; its county is `<state>-<fips>` built from the row's state and FIPS code (`MN-115`); its phone and email are the string facts `phone` and `email`; its people are the row's. The keys are the normalized forms of the name, the phone and the email's domain (`D12-resolution`).
- **Power units** is the string fact `power_units` read as a whole number: one or more ASCII digits and nothing else. Any other value gives no fleet pass and no estimate. A value so large that the for-hire high would not fit a signed 64-bit integer is not read either.
- **Filing date** is the string fact `mcs150_date` read as `YYYYMMDD`, a real calendar date, midnight UTC. An unreadable or absent value means no filing date, which the rules treat as past the cutoff (`D13-scoring`).
- **Class tag** from the string fact `class`, split on `;`, each part trimmed and compared without regard to case: `AUTHORIZED FOR HIRE` or `EXEMPT FOR HIRE` is for hire, `PRIVATE PROPERTY` is private; both kinds is `mixed`, neither (no class, or passenger and government classes alone) `unknown`.
- **Fleet pass**: the string fact `status` is `A` and power units is 2 or more. Owner-operators with one truck drop out; the floor stays low because FMCSA sees registered road vehicles only.
- **Cargo tags** are the fact `cargo` as given: its string elements in order (`genfreight`, `bldgmat` and the like), none when it is not a list.
- **Estimate**, `n` the power units: for hire `150,000 × n` to `250,000 × n` at confidence 0.7; private `150,000 × n` and no high at 0.3; mixed `150,000 × n` and no high at 0.5; unknown as private at 0.2. It carries the facts it used, copied as the row held them (`power_units`, `class`, `mcs150_date`), the filing date, and the rules version (`sources.Rules`, `D13-scoring`). A row whose power units do not read gives no estimate.
- **Exclusion signals**, each recorded with its reason word and what matched: government by class, one `government_class` per government class word among the class parts (`STATE GOVERNMENT`, `LOCAL GOVERNMENT`, `FEDERAL GOVERNMENT`, `INDIAN NATION`); government by name, one `government_name` per pattern found as whole words in the normalized name (`COUNTY`, `CITY OF`, `TOWN OF`, `VILLAGE OF`, `TOWNSHIP`, `SCHOOL DISTRICT`, `STATE OF`, `DEPT`, `DEPARTMENT`, `UNIVERSITY`, `PUBLIC WORKS`), exactly the document's list. Public companies and franchises are left to `decide`.

These numbers and lists are rules version 1: contract, not copy. A revision is a new rules version and a rebuild (decision 8).

## Recorded decisions

- **The facts an estimate copies** are `power_units`, `class` and `mcs150_date`, the three the formula and its confidence read, each present only when the row's facts hold it; `status` decides the fleet pass, not the estimate, and is not copied.
- **An estimate does not need the fleet pass.** A row whose power units read gives an estimate whatever its status or count (decision 7 ties only an unreadable count to "no estimate"); the shortlist's fleet filter keeps the one-truck and inactive rows out (`D13-scoring`).
- **Class parts are compared trimmed and case-folded**, and the government class words the same way, so a stray space or a lower-cased export cannot turn a carrier `unknown`.
- **Government name patterns match whole words** of the normalized name (`D12-resolution`'s `NameKey`, punctuation already stripped): `COUNTY` matches `PINE COUNTY HIGHWAY` and not `COUNTYLINE`, and `DEPT` not `DEPTFORD`. The list is exactly the document's, which closes it; a private business a pattern catches (a `COUNTY MATERIALS`) is returned with `include` (`D12-resolution`).
- **Power units has a ceiling** of 36,893,488,147,419 (the largest `n` with `250,000 × n` in an `int64`); a larger value reads as no whole number rather than overflowing.
- **A filing date must be a real date**: `20250230` is no filing date, so a corrupt field counts as past the cutoff rather than as a guess.
- **A source shares nothing with its row**: the reading's slices and maps are its own, so the link the store writes cannot change when the streamed row is reused.

## Consumer tasks

`cli.Run` builds `reg := sources.New(fmcsa.New())` and hands it to `passes.New`. The engine (`D06-passes`) skips a run with `_, ok := reg.Get(scraper); !ok`, lists `reg.Names()` for a rebuild, and for each streamed row `r` builds `store.Observation{Scraper: r.Scraper, SourceID: r.SourceID, Digest: r.Digest, Reading: src.Read(r)}`. A test of `internal/fmcsa` builds `scrapes.Row` values with `Facts` of its own, calls `fmcsa.New().Read(r)` and compares the `sources.Reading` field by field: `rd.Class == sources.ClassForHire`, `*rd.Estimate.High == 250000*14`, `rd.Estimate.Facts["power_units"]`, `rd.Exclusions[0].Reason == sources.ExcludeGovernmentName`. A test of the registry builds `sources.New(a, b)` from fakes and calls `Names` and `Get`.

- R-FLWK-VBKE: `sources.New(list...)` MUST return a registry whose `Names()` returns, on every call, a new non-nil slice of the distinct names the elements of `list` answer from `Name()`, in ascending byte order, and whose `Get(n)` returns the first element of `list` whose `Name()` is `n` and true, or a nil `Source` and false when none is; so that `sources.New(fmcsa.New()).Get("bogus")` answers false and its `Names()` is exactly `fmcsa`.
- R-FN4H-93B3: When `cli.Run` (`D01-layout-and-run-seam`) serves, the sources its passes read rows with MUST be exactly those of `sources.New(fmcsa.New())`: a `rebuild` (`D10-tools`) over a fake scrapes asks for the current run of `fmcsa` and of no other scraper, and every link the pass writes holds as its `Reading` what `fmcsa.New().Read` returns for the row streamed.
- R-FOCD-MV1S: `(*fmcsa.Source).Name` MUST return `fmcsa.Name`.
- R-FPKA-0MSH: candidates' design defines, for a `scrapes.Row` `r` and a key `k`, the **string fact** `k` of `r` as the string a JSON string decodes to when `r.Facts` holds `k` and its value, once leading and trailing JSON white space is ignored, is a JSON string, and as **none** otherwise (`r.Facts` nil, `k` absent, or its value `null`, a number, a boolean, an array or an object); every requirement of candidates' design naming a string fact MUST denote this.
- R-FQS6-EEJ6: candidates' design defines the FMCSA **power units** of a `scrapes.Row` `r` as the integer the string fact `power_units` of `r` denotes in decimal when that fact is one or more ASCII digits and no other character and that integer is at most 36893488147419, and as none otherwise; so that `"14"` is 14, `"014"` is 14, `"0"` is 0 and `"36893488147419"` is 36893488147419, and `""`, `" 14"`, `"14.0"`, `"-3"`, `"1e2"`, `"36893488147420"`, the JSON number `14` and `null` are none; every requirement of candidates' design naming power units MUST denote this.
- R-FS02-S69V: candidates' design defines the FMCSA **filing date** of a `scrapes.Row` `r` as the `time.Time` that `time.Parse("20060102", s)` returns, which is midnight UTC of that date, when the string fact `mcs150_date` of `r` is a string `s` of exactly eight ASCII digits that that call accepts with a nil error, and as none otherwise; so that `"20250318"` is 2025-03-18 00:00:00 UTC, and `"20250230"`, `"2025-03-18"`, `"2025031"`, `""` and an absent fact are none; every requirement of candidates' design naming a filing date of an FMCSA row MUST denote this.
- R-FT7Z-5Y0K: candidates' design defines the FMCSA **class tag** of a `scrapes.Row` `r` as follows, its **class parts** being the strings `strings.ToUpper(strings.TrimSpace(p))` for each `p` of `strings.Split(c, ";")` when the string fact `class` of `r` is `c`, and none when that fact is none: `sources.ClassMixed` when the class parts hold `AUTHORIZED FOR HIRE` or `EXEMPT FOR HIRE` and also `PRIVATE PROPERTY`; otherwise `sources.ClassForHire` when they hold `AUTHORIZED FOR HIRE` or `EXEMPT FOR HIRE`; otherwise `sources.ClassPrivate` when they hold `PRIVATE PROPERTY`; otherwise `sources.ClassUnknown`; so that `PRIVATE PROPERTY` is private, `AUTHORIZED FOR HIRE;EXEMPT FOR HIRE` and `exempt for hire ` are for hire, `PRIVATE PROPERTY;AUTHORIZED FOR HIRE` is mixed, and `PRIVATE PASSENGER, BUSINESS`, `STATE GOVERNMENT` and an absent class are unknown; every requirement of candidates' design naming a class tag or class parts of an FMCSA row MUST denote these.
- R-FUFV-JPR9: For every `scrapes.Row` `r`, `fmcsa.New().Read(r)` MUST return a `sources.Reading` whose `NameKey` is `sources.NameKey(r.Name)`; whose `Name` is `r.Name`; whose `Names` is a non-nil slice holding the elements of `r.Names` in order; whose `Street`, `City`, `State` and `Zip` are the fields of the same name of `r.Address`; whose `County` is `sources.County(r.Address.State, r.Address.County)`; whose `Phone` and `Email` are the string facts `phone` and `email` of `r`, each empty when none; whose `PhoneKey` is `sources.PhoneKey` of that `Phone` and whose `DomainKey` is `sources.DomainKey` of that `Email`; and whose `People` is a non-nil slice holding, in order, for each element of `r.People`, the `sources.Person` with the same `Name` and `Role`.
