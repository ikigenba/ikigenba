# D13-judging

Judging: what `candidates` judges about each candidate, as two separate **judgments**, under rules version 5. The first is **operating confidence**: how sure we are that it is a real business operating today, from 0 to 1, with the evidence behind it. The second is the **revenue estimate**: a low, a high and how much we trust the range. The facts a prospect list's filters and order read beside them (when the business is first known to have existed, the newest filing, its categories, the government name pattern it matched, the **fleet verdict** with the power units and drivers behind it, the **website** state and address with the source that decided it, and the drive minutes) are not judged: they are enrichments, which enrichers derive or fetch (`D16-enrichers`) and the store keeps as the candidate's **facts row** (`D04-store`). A judge reads the facts row and writes only the judgments (funnel rename decisions 12 and 13). This document owns the pure functions of `internal/judging` (the combined estimate, the freshness of a filing, the judged weight of a piece of evidence, the combining rule and the order key) and what a judge writes (`store.Judge`, `store.JudgeAll`, and the judging `store.Decide` does of what it touches): the judgments, worked out from the candidate's links, rulings and facts row. What a source reads from one row (its estimate, evidence, dates, category, government match, and its fleet verdict and counts) is `D05-sources`'; how the link facts and the drive minutes are derived from a candidate, the fleet verdict among them, is `D16-enrichers`'; which links a candidate holds and in which order, and which derived results a ruling records, is `D12-ingest`'s; how and when a website is checked is `D15-website-check`'s, and how and when one is searched for is `D16-enrichers`'; the records, the enrichments and the facts row among them, are `D04-store`'s; when a judge runs is `D06-passes`'; which candidates a prospect list answers, and in which order, is `D14-prospect-lists`'.

"Intent decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the judgments change, *two judgments and the PPP reader*; "cohorts decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the cohorts change, and "cohorts log N" entry N of its decisions log. "Website decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the website-check change, and "enrichers decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the enrichers change. "Category evidence decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the category-evidence change. "Website search decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the website-search change. "Funnel rename decision N" is decision N of the decisions document of the funnel rename, which gives what candidates stores, answers and shows the funnel's stage names, and "funnel rename log DN" entry DN of its delivery's decisions log.

## Why two judgments

Rules version 2 ranked every candidate in one list by one number, confidence times estimated revenue, and that number mixed two questions. Carrier type says how well a truck count predicts revenue; filing age says whether the business still exists. Blended, a well-documented trucking company always outranked a real manufacturer, and nobody could tell from the number why. Version 3 answers the two questions separately, and filing age touches only the first.

## Operating confidence

A source reads, from each row, the **row evidence** it gives: each piece a kind (`fmcsa_active`, `fmcsa_activity`, `ppp_loan`, `own_domain`), its source, its weight before freshness and a short description (`D05-sources`). At a judge, each piece of each link not gone is weighed: an `fmcsa_active` piece is multiplied by the **freshness** of its row's filing date, 1 within two years of the date (the MCS-150 cycle), falling linearly to 0 at five years, and 0 without a date; every other kind keeps its weight. A piece whose judged weight is not above 0 says nothing and is left out. Each kind counts once per candidate, from the link that gives it the greatest weight, and the kept pieces are listed by weight, highest first.

The **operating evidence** of a candidate is that list plus what enrichment adds, which today is the website. A judge reads the website state of the candidate's facts row and the source that decided it, check or search, which are the website decision over its results (`D04-store`): the newest `website_check` result's state when it found a site, otherwise the newest confirmed `website_search` result's (`D15-website-check`, `D16-enrichers`). When that state is `https` or `http` and its source is not the search, the candidate gains one `website_live` piece, source `website_check`, weighing 0.5, described by `sources.DescribeWebsiteLive`. A newest result of `none` adds nothing, even when an older result found a site, and it leaves `own_domain` alone: missing evidence is never counted against a company. No result, so no row or a row whose website reads `unknown`, adds nothing. A site the search found adds nothing either (website search decision 12): the judgment is unchanged, and its website evidence still reads the website check alone, so a company the search found a site for judges as it did before the search. A row a release before the website search wrote has an empty source, and its site counts, since only the check could have set it. The piece joins the row evidence under the same rule, one per kind, listed by weight, so an FMCSA carrier filed within two years with its own domain judges 0.68, and 0.84 once its site answers. The judge reads neither the enrichments nor the ZIP table: a website result reaches the judgment only through the facts row it writes, its source included.

The combining rule closes part of the gap to certainty with each piece: `1 − (1 − w₁) × (1 − w₂) × …`. The result is rounded to six decimal places, so weights 0.6, 0.3 and 0.2 give 0.776 and one piece weighing 0.2 gives 0.2. **Missing evidence is never zero**: a candidate with no evidence has no operating confidence, not 0, and a prospect list ordered by operating confidence puts it after every candidate that has one (`D14-prospect-lists`).

## Revenue estimate

Each source gives each candidate one **estimate**: for FMCSA the estimate of its freshest link not gone, unchanged from version 2 except that it is no longer multiplied by freshness; for PPP the estimate of the link with the largest amount, which is the largest low since PPP's low is proportional to the amount. The **combined** estimate weights by confidence: `low` is the confidence-weighted mean of the sources' lows, `high` the same over the sources that give one and absent when none does, the confidence the highest of theirs. A candidate with no estimate has no revenue fields at all. Disagreement between sources is still deferred.

## The judgments

A judge writes, for each active candidate it is given, reading by its id the candidate's record, its own links, its rulings and its facts row, and nothing else, its estimates and its **judgments** (`store.Judgments`): the operating confidence with its order key and its operating evidence; the combined revenue low, high and confidence; whether it is excluded; the rules version and when it was calculated. Only links not gone count, for every judgment the links give.

The judgments hold no fact (funnel rename decision 13). A judge reads the facts row for the website's operating evidence and nothing else of it, and writes no fact anywhere: the facts live in the facts row alone, which only enrichers write (`D04-store`). A prospect list's member carries its judgments and its facts side by side (`store.Ranked`), the `candidate` tool answers them as two members, `enrichments` and `judgments` (`D10-tools`), and the candidate page shows them apart (`D08-pages`), so a fact moves as soon as an enrich writes it, and only the judgments wait for a judge. A merged candidate keeps the judgments its last judge wrote, and its facts row is deleted at the merge, so it answers no facts.

## The facts

How each fact is worked out is `D16-enrichers`'. Known since, the newest filing, the government match, the categories the links give and the fleet verdict with its power units and drivers are the `link_facts` kind's, derived from the candidate's links not gone under the rules this document used to own and moved there unchanged: among them, the verdict and counts of the freshest FMCSA link not gone, `unknown` with no counts for a candidate known only from PPP, never `fail`. The categories are instead the `brief` kind's, read from the candidate's kept pages, when the good brief gives at least one; how the facts row chooses between the two kinds' categories is `D04-store`'s, and a judge reads none of them. The drive minutes are the `drive_minutes` kind's, from the display ZIP. The website, its address and its source are the website decision over the `website_check` and `website_search` kinds' results (`D04-store`, `D15-website-check`, `D16-enrichers`): the check's site when it found one, otherwise the newest confirmed search's, so the `website` filter admits a company whose site the search found (website search decision 9), and a judge reads the state and source for the website's operating evidence. A judge neither derives nor fetches; a full ingest and a merge or split re-derive before they judge (`D06-passes`, `D12-ingest`).

**Nothing is removed automatically.** The government name patterns fill the government match and exclude nothing; the fleet verdict, an old filing, a missing estimate or missing minutes leave a candidate out of a prospect list only when that prospect list carries the filter that reads them, and each filter on a fact that can be unknown has its own switch for the unknowns (`D14-prospect-lists`). A candidate is excluded only when the owner's latest `exclude` or `include` on it is an `exclude`.

**Judgments are written only by a judge.** The judge pass (`D06-passes`, daily from the `judge_candidates` trigger, started by hand by the `judge` tool, and started as an enrich's follow-on under the rule stated there), a full ingest (the `ingest` tool naming no scraper, which ingests every scraper's current run, re-derives every derived kind for every candidate, then judges every candidate), and an owner's ruling (`merge`, `split`, `exclude`, `include`, which judges the candidates it touches in the same call, a merge or a split after re-deriving them (`D12-ingest`), so every prospect list answers a ruling at its next read; a `not_same` judges nothing) are the only writers. The rulings reach `Decide` through the `decide`, `exclude` and `include` tools (`D10-tools`); which tool records which kind changes nothing here. An ingest of one run or of one scraper stores rows and an enrich pass records results and their facts (`D06-passes`, `D16-enrichers`), and neither judges. There is no refresh: the pass-end statement of version 2 that re-applied the filing-age arithmetic in SQL to every stored ranking number, and its agreement test with Go, are gone, so the formulas live once, in Go. Between judges nothing decays: a stored judgment is what the last judge wrote, and the daily judge keeps it within a day of true, while the facts are always the newest results'.

**Why daily, not on change and not on read.** The candidates whose `fmcsa_active` weight is moving are the ones whose filing nobody updated, so their rows never change: 2,431 of the 16,706 active MN and WI carriers are in the two-to-five-year band, and 2,663 change weight within 30 days. Judging only changed candidates would never revisit them. Judging on read would make the first read after a quiet stretch wait for a full judge (18 seconds for the 16,580 candidates on `us`) and would add a staleness check to every read path, to save 18 seconds of CPU a day. A daily trigger is the pattern the suite already has. A prospect list read is the one place the service reads a clock at a read, for the filing-age filter, and it reads the stored newest filing against it; it judges nothing (`D14-prospect-lists`).

## No single ranking

Version 3's judgments were first read through one interim ranked list with one fixed order and the caller's filters applied per call. Prospect lists replace it (cohorts decision 15): which candidates a prospect list answers, its filters, unknown switches and order, is `D14-prospect-lists`', reading the judgments this document defines beside the facts. This document defines no list.

## Rules version 5

The rules version is `sources.Rules`, and its value is 5; no requirement spells it, and tests read the value the source declares. As with versions 3 and 4, the number lives in this prose alone: the requirement declaring `sources.Rules` (`D01-layout-and-run-seam`) states no value, so a new version opens no gap, and the delivery sets `sources.Rules` to 5 by direct instruction. Every estimate and judgments written carries it. Version 5 changed what a judge writes, the `website_live` evidence and the two website facts, and no formula: an estimate's arithmetic is version 3's, and its formula is the same number stamped when a source reads the row (cohorts log 14). Version 4 brought the three-valued fleet verdict and its counts. The enrichers change keeps version 5: from the same links, rulings and newest website result a judge writes the same opinions, and the facts are the same values, derived by enrichers instead of the judge. The website-search change keeps version 5 too (website search decision 12): a new version marks the judge writing other opinions from the same inputs, and this one does not. Every website result an earlier release could hold is a website check's, and from those the website decision gives the same state and URL and a source that is the check or, for a row an earlier release wrote, empty, so every existing candidate's evidence and opinions are what they were. Only a site the search found, which no earlier release could hold, is left out of the evidence, and it gives the same evidence the candidate had without it; it moves only the website facts, which are not judged.

An active candidate's facts read unknown until it has a facts row (`D04-store`, `D03-serve`), and the first enrich after a release that adds a kind derives every candidate's link facts and minutes; its follow-on judge then rewrites the judgments. A full ingest re-ingests, re-derives and re-judges but never fetches or deletes a website result (`D04-store`), so the judge it ends with reads the website results already held, as it reads the owner's rulings; a merge moves the merged candidate's results to the survivor and re-derives the survivor, whose newest website result is then the one recorded last among them all.

## Recorded decisions

- **Ordering departure**: operating confidence is ordered by the **operating key**, the operating confidence, already rounded to six decimal places, scaled by a million and rounded to an integer, so the key is exact (0.776 keys 776000, 0.2 keys 200000), −1 when there is none. A prospect list ordered by operating confidence orders by the key, the candidate id breaking ties (`D14-prospect-lists`), and the stored key is indexed, so the order is one exact comparison in the query; two combined values less than a millionth apart may round to one confidence and one key and then fall to id order, a departure from ordering by the unrounded float (intent decision 18) that no person can see. An empty confidence is unknown and sorts after every judged value whatever the direction.
- **Operating confidence is rounded to six decimal places** (`math.Round(x*1e6)/1e6` of the combined value `x`, half away from zero), and the operating key is derived from the rounded value. The sandbox showed one 0.2 piece answering 0.19999999999999996, float residue no reader should see; the orchestrator ruled to store and answer the rounded value. A millionth is finer than any weight the sources give, so no ranking a person can see changes. Evidence weights and revenue confidence are not rounded. A candidate whose combined value is below half a millionth (only a piece weighing under 0.0000005 can do it) has an operating confidence of 0, keyed 0: it is still present, and still sorts before a candidate with none.
- **Rounding**: the combined low and high are the weighted means rounded half away from zero to whole dollars (`math.Round`), saturating at the `int64` bounds. With confidences summing to 0 the mean is unweighted; no rule of version 3 or 4 makes one.
- **Two and five years are calendar years** from the filing date (`AddDate(2, 0, 0)` and `AddDate(5, 0, 0)`), linear in elapsed time between them; a filing dated after the moment weighs 1. Freshness is measured at the judge's moment in UTC truncated to the second.
- **Evidence weighing nothing is left out**: a stale or undated `fmcsa_active` adds no piece, so a candidate with nothing else has no operating confidence rather than 0 (intent decision 2).
- **Evidence order** is weight, highest first, then kind name in byte order; one kind's best piece is the first in link order among equal weights.
- **PPP's estimate is its largest**: the PPP link whose estimate has the greatest low (the largest amount) gives PPP's estimate, the first in link order on ties; every other source keeps its freshest link's.
- **The link facts' rules moved to `D16-enrichers`** (enrichers decisions 2 and 6): the earliest known since, the newest filing, the first government match in link order, the categories with their sources, and the verdict and counts copied from the freshest FMCSA link's reading (an empty verdict read as unknown, a PPP reading's never consulted) are now what the `link_facts` kind derives, with the examples that proved them. Moving them changes no value; the judge reads their result from the facts row.
- **No fleet boolean**: the Go type of the facts holds the verdict, and the migration of the release that brought the verdict dropped the old `fleet` column with the unwritten `low` and `high` (`D04-store`, which records that departure from expand then contract).
- **The website piece weighs 0.5 whatever the result's age** (website decision 13, which sets no age): freshness is FMCSA's filing cycle, and `judging.Weight` leaves every other kind's weight alone. There are no refresh ages any more: a website result is renewed only when the candidate's domain changes (`D15-website-check`). A candidate that stops being checked keeps its last result as the newest: one whose display email turns free-provider or blank, or a survivor without its own domain that a merge gave an `https` result. That result then keeps 0.5 and its state indefinitely. This is accepted: the result is still what was last seen, and a later change may add an age if real results show it matters.
- **The newest check decides alone** (website decision 13): a site that answered once and has since stopped answering gives no evidence, and nothing averages over a candidate's results; the facts row holds only the newest check's state, or a site the search found, which gives no evidence.
- **The facts come from the facts row at the read** (enrichers decisions 5 and 7, replacing website decision 14's "the website facts come from the judge"): a prospect list's filters and rows, the `candidate` tool and the candidate page answer what the newest results say as soon as they are recorded, while the judgments say what the last judge saw. An enrich that counted a derived result or an `https`, `http` or `none` website result starts a judge unless a judge is running (`D06-passes`), so the website piece and the website fact seldom disagree for long. A `website_search` pass starts no judge (website search decision 1, `D06-passes`), and needs none: a site it finds moves the website facts at the next read and no judgment.
- **A site the search found gives no evidence** (website search decision 12): the `website_live` piece reads the check alone, so a company whose only site is the search's judges as one with none, while its website facts read the found site and the `website` filter admits it (website search decision 9). Evidence from a searched site, with its own source word and weight, is deferred until the first prospect list's results show how often the agent is right; adding it is a new rules version.
- **The piece is withheld by source, not granted by it**: the condition is a source other than the search, not a source equal to the check, so a facts row a release before the website search wrote, whose source is empty, keeps its piece. The migration that adds the source sets it to the check on every row holding a site (`D04-store`), so only a row the previous release writes after a rollback is empty.
- **During a rollback the previous release's judge counts a search-found site** as live evidence until it re-projects that row from the check alone, which drops the site; the rollback's limits are `D04-store`'s known limits.
- **Rules version 5 is kept** (website search decision 12): no existing candidate's evidence changes, since only search-found sites, new in this release, are left out, and those give the evidence the candidate had without them (see Rules version 5).
- **The judge no longer copies facts** (funnel rename decisions 12 and 13): until this release a judge copied the facts row into the stored judgments for the release before and for a merged candidate. The judgments now live in their own table holding the judgment fields alone (`D04-store`, migration `0017_prospect_lists.sql`), the old table stays for the previous release and goes stale after the upgrade (`D04-store`, Known limits), and a merged candidate answers its last judgments and no facts.
- **Unranged now means no revenue**: a judge counts the candidates it judged whose revenue low is absent; missing minutes no longer matter to any count.
- **A candidate not yet judged is in no prospect list**: with no judgments there is nothing to filter or order (`D14-prospect-lists`); a full ingest or the next judge gives it judgments, even when its facts row is already written.
- **Judge ignores ids it cannot judge**: an id naming no candidate or a merged one is skipped, not refused, since a ruling may merge a candidate between a pass's work and its judge.
- **A judge touches no prospect list, no reject, no enrichment and no facts row**: what a prospect list answers changes at its next read because the judgments or the facts changed, never because a judge wrote the prospect list, and only enrichers write the facts.
- **A merged candidate keeps its estimates and last judgments** as they stood at the merge and is never judged again; its facts row is deleted, so it answers no facts; the survivor's are recomputed; prospect lists answer active candidates only.
- **`JudgeAll` no longer promises the `candidate_cargo` repair** (cohorts log 30): its one observable was the cargo filter of the interim ranked list, which is gone, and cargo is not a prospect list filter. Whether the table is still kept is `D04-store`'s; it is a finding to drop it in the next release.
- **The old ranking fields are gone**: confidence, base, effective, the combined number and its key, filed and exclusions, which version 3 left empty, were dropped by the cohorts release's migration (`D04-store`, cohorts decision 16).
