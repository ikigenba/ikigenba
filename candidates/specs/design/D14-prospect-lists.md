# D14-prospect-lists

Prospect lists: what a prospect list is, as terms the rest of the design reads. A **prospect list** is a saved search the owner creates and grooms: a name, filters, a sort order and a size, with a reject list kept beside it. Nothing is stored about who is in it: its **members** are worked out every time it is read, from the candidates, each member carrying the candidate's facts as its facts row holds them at that read, its judgments as the last judge wrote them and when its site text and its brief were last written, the prospect list's standing rejects, the candidates' campaign uses and the clock's date at that read. This document owns the words: the prospect list id's form, the size bounds and default, each filter with its switch for unknowns, the date a read measures filing age and campaign age at, the order rule, the **matching** candidates, the **members** with the site text and brief times each carries, the **classified** count of the members whose categories a brief gave, the **standing reject** and the exclusion rule, each member's **basis** and the **by basis** counts, the **basis filter**, the **trend filter**, an **open** and the **previous open**, and the **changes** since it: the **since**, the **definition changed**, the **joined** with why each joined, the **left** with why each left, `gone` among them, and the **first failing filter**; and what the validators `store.ValidProspectListID`, `store.ValidProspectListName`, `store.ValidProspectListSize`, `store.ValidProspectListFilters` and `store.ValidProspectListOrder` and the pure functions `store.BasisOf` and `store.KindsOf` answer. It states no method's behavior. The names it uses (`store.ProspectList`, `store.ProspectListFilters`, `store.ProspectListOrder`, `store.Reject`, `store.Ranked`, `store.BasisCounts`, `store.Changes`, `store.Joined`, `store.Left`, `store.Open`, `store.OpenMember`, the size, order, kind, basis, definition and why constants and the validators' and pure functions' signatures) are declared in `D04-store`, which also owns every store method: creating, editing and deleting a prospect list, reading one (`ReadProspectList` answers this document's members, matching, classified, by basis, changes and standing rejects at the moment it reads `Config.Now`, and records nothing), opening one (`OpenProspectList` answers what `ReadProspectList` would and records one open), recording rejects and un-rejects, the `opens` table and the `Opens` read, and the errors. The tools that create, groom and read prospect lists are `D10-tools`'; the landing's prospect list list and the prospect list page are `D08-pages`'. The judgments (operating confidence, revenue, the headcount, exclusion) are `D13-judging`'s; the facts a member carries (known since, newest filing, categories, government match, fleet verdict with power units and drivers, the headcounts, `website` and drive minutes, and the brief labels) are what the enrichers derive or fetch (`D16-enrichers`, `D15-website-check`), and the facts row that holds them, with `GetFacts` answering them from it, is `D04-store`'s; `store.ProspectListFilters.Website`, `store.ProspectListFilters.WebsiteUnknown` and the facts' `Website`, `WebsiteURL` and `WebsiteSource` are declared in `D04-store`, which also owns the website decision of a list of results (a term `D04-store` defines, not one of the numbered website decisions of the website-check change cited below), which sets the facts row's website from a `website_check` that found a site, a confirmed `website_search` result or a `website_search` result that found nothing, and otherwise leaves it `unknown` (website states outcomes 1 to 3), and the source words `store.WebsiteByCheck` and `store.WebsiteBySearch`; the `website_search` kind and its results are `D16-enrichers`', and the member's `website_source` answer and the prospect list page's mark of a site found by search are `D10-tools`' and `D08-pages`'; and the `website` and `website_unknown` tool arguments are `D10-tools`'. `store.Ranked.SiteText` is declared in `D04-store`, the `site_text` kind and its results are `D16-enrichers`', and the member's `site_text` answer and the prospect list page's site text column are `D10-tools`' and `D08-pages`'. `store.Ranked.Brief` is declared in `D04-store`, the `brief` kind and its results are `D16-enrichers`', how a brief's summary and categories reach the facts row, and `store.ClassifiedBy`, which reads from a set of categories whether a brief or the links gave it, are `D04-store`'s, and the member's `brief` answer and the prospect list page's brief column are `D10-tools`' and `D08-pages`'; a targeted enrich over a prospect list walks this document's members (`D06-passes`). `store.ProspectListFilters.MinCampaignAgeDays`, `store.CampaignUse`, `store.CampaignUses` and a use's effective time are declared in `D04-store`, which records the uses from the campaigns events `D06-passes` accepts; the `min_campaign_age_days` tool argument is `D10-tools`', and the prospect list page's showing of it `D08-pages`'. A prospect list was called otherwise until the funnel rename gave it the stage name (funnel rename decision 4); its ids moved from the old prefix to `pl_` with the same 16 digits (funnel rename decision 5). `store.ProspectListFilters.Trend`, `store.ProspectListFilters.TrendUnknown`, `store.OrderTrend`, the judgments' `Trend` and `TrendChange` and the normal form that orders a trend list are declared in `D04-store`; the trend words and how a judge reads a trend from the headcount series are `judging`'s (`D01-layout-and-run-seam`, `D13-judging`); the `trend` and `trend_unknown` tool arguments, the `trend` order field and a member's `trend` and `trend_change` answers are `D10-tools`', and the prospect list page's trend column and filter `D08-pages`'.

"Headcount trend decision N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the headcount trend change, which has the judge write a headcount series per kind and a trend, growing, flat, shrinking or unknown, with the years and figures behind it, and makes the trend a prospect list filter and an order field. "Revenue containment outcome N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the revenue containment change, which has a prospect list's revenue minimum check the low end of a member's estimate and its maximum the high end.

## What a prospect list is

A prospect list's id is `pl_` and 16 lowercase hexadecimal digits, minted like a candidate's and never reused (decision 1); the name is free text, required, and not unique, since the id is the handle. Its size is 1 to 100, 30 when its creator passes none, and it is read whole, since its size bounds it (decision 2). Prospect lists belong to the space: every signed-in caller sees and may change every one (decision 8).

## Filters

Each filter applies only when the prospect list carries it, and a prospect list with no filters is the ranked view of every judged active candidate the owner has not excluded. Every filter on a fact that can be unknown has a switch, false unless set, that admits the candidates whose fact is unknown; a switch admits unknowns for its own filter only, the filters a prospect list carries must all pass, and a switch set without its filter's value carries nothing and tests nothing (log 1), except `category_unknown`, which alone keeps only the candidates with no category. The filters read a member's judgments and facts as `store.Ranked` carries them, and the class filter reads the candidate's class tag. The facts are the candidate's facts row's at the read, every one unknown, and the government match empty, when the candidate has no row yet; the operating confidence, revenue and exclusion are the last judge's:

- **revenue**, carried when either bound is set: a known revenue low at least the minimum (0 when absent) and, when a maximum is set, a known revenue high at most it, both inclusive (log 2, revenue containment outcome 1), so the whole estimate sits inside the window; no estimate, or with a maximum set an estimate with no high end, is unknown and passes only with `revenue_unknown` (revenue containment outcome 2).
- **drive time**, a maximum only: known minutes at most it; no minutes only with `minutes_unknown`. A candidate's minutes are its facts row's, which are a route's when a `drive_route` result routed it from the current origin to its current address, and otherwise the ZIP estimate's (drive route decision 7; the drive decision is `D04-store`'s); the filter reads the minutes whatever decided them.
- **category**, a list of category names, any of the twenty-three `sources` declares, `other` among them: some category of the candidate's in the list; no category only with `category_unknown`. An empty list with `category_unknown` keeps only the candidates with no category; an empty list without it carries nothing. A candidate's categories are its facts row's, which hold one set or the other, never both: the categories of its newest good brief, each with source `brief`, when that brief gives at least one, and otherwise those its links and its `name` give through `link_facts`, each with the source that gave it (category evidence decisions 1 and 2; the projection is `D04-store`'s). The filter matches by name whatever the source (category evidence decision 6), so a candidate whose brief decides is admitted by the brief's categories alone, and one with no brief, or whose brief gives none, by its links'.
- **fleet**, `pass` or `fail`: the fleet verdict equal to it; an `unknown` verdict only with `fleet_unknown`. `unknown` is not a value the filter takes, since the switch covers it (log 4).
- **website**, a list of states drawn from `https`, `http` and `none`: the member's `website` fact equal to some state in the list, so `https` alone is a secure site, `https` with `http` is any site, and `none` alone is a company the website search ran for and found nothing, or named a site that did not load (website states outcomes 1 and 5); a fact `unknown`, a company no search has come back empty for and no site was found for, only with `website_unknown`, never through `none` (website decision 16). A candidate's `website` fact is its facts row's: the site the newest check found, when it found one; otherwise the site of the newest search whose site loaded when fetched; otherwise `none`, when a search that did not fail found nothing or named a site that did not load; and otherwise `unknown`, a check that found nothing included (website search decision 7, website states outcomes 2 and 3; the website decision is `D04-store`'s), so a site the search found and confirmed admits the candidate to `https` or `http` exactly as a checked one does (website search decision 9).
- **trend**, a list of trends drawn from `growing`, `flat` and `shrinking`: the member's trend, as the last judge wrote it, equal to some trend in the list, so `growing` alone is a business whose headcount within one kind rose at least a fifth and at least five people over about three years; a trend `unknown`, a business with no kind whose series gives a comparison year, only with `trend_unknown` (headcount trend decision 10). `unknown` is not a value the filter takes, since the switch covers it, as with the fleet and website filters (log 4). An absent or empty list carries nothing, and a repeated trend is accepted, as with the basis filter. How a judge reads the trend is `D13-judging`'s; the filter reads only the word.
- **government**, true or false: true keeps the candidates whose name matched a government pattern, false those whose did not. It is never unknown.
- **filing age**, a number of days: the newest filing at most that many whole days before the date of the read; no newest filing only with `filing_unknown`.
- **campaign age**, `min_campaign_age_days`, a number of days: every campaign use of the candidate at least that many whole days before the date of the read, or any use when it is 0, a use's day being that of its effective time, when it was added or, when its added event has not arrived, the earlier of when it was rejected and when something was delivered (`D04-store`). A rejected use counts, and a candidate never in a campaign always passes, so the filter has no switch for unknowns (campaigns decision 18): 30 leaves out a candidate added to any campaign in the 30 days before the read, counting the read's own day as 0, and keeps one added 30 days before or never; a use dated after the read is left out by any number above 0, and 0 keeps all.
- **carrier class**, `for_hire`, `private` or `mixed`: the class tag equal to it; a candidate with no class tag, or the tag `unknown`, only with `class_unknown` (log 26).
- **basis**, a list of bases drawn from `records`, `partial` and `enriched`: the member's basis equal to some basis in the list; an absent or empty list admits all three (basis decision 3). The basis is never unknown, so the filter has no switch.
- **excluded**: unless the prospect list shows excluded candidates, a candidate the owner's standing ruling excludes is left out. It is never unknown.

Cargo is not a filter; it stays a label on the candidate. The website state is a filter but not an order field (website decision 16).

## The clock

Filing age and campaign age are the two places a read measures against a clock, and both measure against the same date. The date of a read is the UTC calendar date of the moment the store reads its own clock, once per read (log 3), so the tool and the page each measure on the day they are called, and a test sets the date through the clock it hands the store.

## Order

A prospect list's order is a list of fields, each with a direction, over `operating_confidence`, `revenue_low`, `revenue_confidence`, `drive_minutes`, `trend`, `newest_filing` and `known_since`, each at most once. `trend` orders on the change behind the trend, the newest figure less the comparison figure, so under descending the largest growth comes first and the largest fall last, and a member whose trend is unknown, which has no change, comes last either way (headcount trend decision 10). The first entry under which two candidates differ decides; a candidate whose value is unknown comes after every candidate whose value is known, whatever the direction; and the candidate id, ascending, breaks every remaining tie, so the same records always give the same list. An empty order is `operating_confidence` descending (decision 5).

No filter and no order field reads the headcount itself: a member carries its judgments' headcount low as it carries every judgment, and filters and sort on the headcount are deferred to step 8 of the size and coverage plan (revenue bands decisions 7 and 17). The trend filter and the `trend` order field read the trend judgment and its change, which the judge works out from the headcount series, not the current headcount (headcount trend decisions 5 and 10).

## Members and matching

The **matching** candidates of a prospect list at a read are the active candidates that have been judged (that hold judgments, log 5), that the prospect list has no standing reject of, that the exclusion rule admits and that pass every filter the prospect list carries, in the prospect list's order. **matching** is their number, which tells the owner whether the filters are too tight or too loose; the **members** are the first `size` of them (decision 3). A new scrape, a new enrichment, a new judgment, a ruling, a reject or a campaign use changes the members at the next read, an enrichment through the facts it writes before any judge runs, and a candidate may be a member of several prospect lists.

Each member also carries when site text was last written for it: the time of its newest `site_text` result, none when it has never had one (site text decision 18). It is carried, not filtered or ordered on: no filter or order field reads it, so a `site_text` result changes no prospect list's matching, members or order, and the prospect list query is unchanged (site text decision 11).

Each member carries when its brief was last written the same way: the time of its newest `brief` result, failed or not, none when it has never had one (brief decisions 8 and 11). The time is carried, not filtered or ordered on, and the prospect list query is unchanged (brief decision 8). A brief's categories are another matter: when the brief is good and gives at least one, they replace the links' in the facts row (category evidence decision 1), so a `brief` result can change a prospect list's matching and members through the category filter, admitting a candidate or leaving one out, as any enrichment does through the facts it writes.

A `website_search` result reaches the members the same way and no other. When its site loads and the newest check found no site, the facts row's `website` becomes that site's state and its `website_source` `search`, so the result can admit a candidate to a prospect list filtered on `https` or `http` and leave it out of one filtered on `none`, or out of one that admitted it only through `website_unknown` (website search decision 9). When it did not fail but found nothing or named a site that did not load, and neither the newest check nor a confirmed search gives a site, the row's `website` becomes `none` with an empty source, so the result admits the candidate to a prospect list filtered on `none` and takes it out of one that admitted it only through `website_unknown` (website states outcomes 3 and 5). A check that finds nothing makes no `none`: it leaves the row `unknown` until a search runs (website states outcome 2). The search adds no filter, order field or member time, and the judge gives a searched site no operating evidence (website search decision 12, `D13-judging`), so it changes no judgment and no operating order. Each member's facts carry which source gave its website, the facts row's `WebsiteSource`, beside its `Website` and `WebsiteURL` (website search decision 10, S 6).

A `drive_route` result reaches the members the same way and no other: when it routed and its inputs match the candidate's current address and the origin, the facts row's minutes become the route's, with the source `route`, so a member the route puts above a prospect list's drive limit leaves it at the next read and the next in order takes its place, one it puts within the limit may join, and the `drive_minutes` order places it by the route's minutes (drive route decision 8). The kind adds no filter, order field or member time, and the judge takes no evidence from drive time (drive route decision 12, `D13-judging`), so it changes no judgment. Each member's facts carry which decided its minutes, the facts row's `MinutesSource`, beside its `Minutes`, with the route's miles and resolved address (drive route decision 10).

A read also counts the members a brief has classified: **classified** is the number of members whose facts' categories are the brief's set, which `store.ClassifiedBy` of them answers as `brief` (category evidence decisions 3 and 5). A member's classification is that same answer over the same categories the category filter reads, so the label and the filter cannot disagree. It is counted, not filtered or ordered on, and `store.Ranked` gains no field for it: the member's tool answer and its page row read it from the member's facts (`D10-tools`, `D08-pages`). When classified equals the number of members, every member is classified by its brief.

## Basis

Each member carries its **basis**, what its judgments stand on, worked out at every read from the enrichment results the candidate holds and never stored as a judgment (basis decisions 1 and 2). The four **targeted kinds** are `website_search`, `site_text`, `brief` and `drive_route`, the kinds an agent asks for; `website_check`, `link_facts` and `drive_minutes`, which run on their own, never count. The basis is `records` when the candidate has no result of a targeted kind, so its judgments rest on scraped rows and the automatic enrichers alone; `enriched` when it has some brief that did not fail, any one, not only the newest, and some `drive_route` result that routed it, one with minutes; and `partial` otherwise, which takes in every other result of a targeted kind, a failed website search, a failed brief and a failed or unroutable route among them. A company with no website cannot be `enriched` until website search finds one, since its brief has nothing to read. `store.BasisOf` states this over a list of results, and a member's basis is `store.BasisOf` of what `Enrichments` answers for it, results a merge moved to it included, so a survivor stands on the results of both.

The **kinds present and missing** explain a basis: `store.KindsOf` lists the targeted kinds the candidate has any result of, failed ones included, and the rest, both in the order `website_search`, `site_text`, `brief`, `drive_route`. The card and the `candidate` tool show them (`D08-pages`, `D10-tools`); a prospect list does not.

The **basis filter** admits the members whose basis is in its list, and **by basis** counts the members, after the size cut, on each basis, so the three counts sum to the number of members, as classified counts members and not matching (basis decision 4).

## Opens and changes

An **open** of a prospect list is the record of one opening of it, a `store.Open`: when, who, the list's filters, order and size as they were, the members it answered, each with its basis then, and its **passing**, every candidate that matched then, in rank order, before the size cut (basis decision 7; the passing was added by the owner during design). `OpenProspectList` records one (`D04-store`); the `prospect_list` tool records one on every call that does not peek (`D10-tools`), and the prospect list page on every `GET` and `HEAD` (`D08-pages`). A peek, the legacy `cohort` tool, a refused call and a failed read record nothing. Opens are never pruned, and deleting a prospect list leaves its opens, as it leaves its rejects; since a prospect list id is never reused, a deleted list's opens are never compared again (basis decisions 10 and 12).

The **previous open** of a list at a read is its open recorded last before that read, the one with the greatest `Seq`, by any caller, through the tool or the page, whatever the list's definition was then (basis decision 8). The **changes** at a read compare the members now with the previous open's members:

- **since** is the previous open's time. A **first open**, one with no previous open, has a zero since and reports nothing else: no definition change, no joined and no left.
- **definition changed** names which of the list's own filters, order and size differ between the previous open's record and the list now, in that order, so the owner can tell a change of their own from enrichment's (basis decision 9). Both sides are compared in their normal form, so an edit to an equal definition is no change; a website list in another order is, since it is stored as given.
- **joined** are the members now that were not members then, in rank order now, each with its name and basis now and why it joined: `new` when the candidate was created after the previous open, else `moved_up` when it was in the previous open's passing, so it matched then and was below the size cut, and `passes` otherwise, since it did not match then and does now.
- **left** are the members then that are not members now, in their rank at the previous open, each with its name now, its basis then and now, and why it left, judged against the list's definition now and the candidate as it stands: `gone` when it is no longer in the corpus, merged away or no longer judged, its basis now empty; else `excluded` when the owner's exclusion now leaves it out, else `rejected` when it has a standing reject in this list, else `filter` when it fails a filter, naming the **first failing filter** by the name the list's filters give it, else `cut`, since it still passes and the order pushed it below the size cut.

The first failing filter is checked in the order the filters are declared, revenue, drive time, category, fleet, website, trend, government, filing age, campaign age, class and basis, the exclusion rule left out since it is `excluded`. Its names are the filters' own JSON names: `revenue_min` when the low is below the minimum or unknown with a minimum set, else `revenue_max`, which covers a high end above the maximum and a high end missing under a maximum (revenue containment outcome 3); `max_minutes`; `categories`, or `category_unknown` when only that switch is set; then `fleet`, `website`, `trend`, `government`, `max_filing_age_days`, `min_campaign_age_days`, `class` and `basis`.

## Rejects

A reject or un-reject is a record naming a prospect list, a candidate, who, when and why; the latest on a prospect-list-and-candidate pair stands (decision 10). The **standing reject** of a pair is that latest record when it is a reject. A reject may name a candidate that is not a member, so the owner can keep out one that will surface later; reject records are never removed, not when un-rejected and not when their prospect list is deleted (decision 11). Which calls record what, and what they answer, is `D04-store`'s.

## Validation

The five validators are pure, read no store, and are the one place each rule lives: the tools call them before the store and answer `invalid`, and the store calls them again and answers `ErrInvalid`, so the two cannot disagree (log 7). Refused: a malformed id (a `not_found`, since it can name no prospect list), a blank name, a size outside 1 to 100, a negative money, minutes or days value (filing age and campaign age alike), a minimum revenue above the maximum, a category that is not one of the twenty-three category names, a fleet value other than `pass` or `fail`, a website list that is empty, repeats a state or holds anything but `https`, `http` or `none` (website decision 16), a class other than `for_hire`, `private` or `mixed`, a basis other than `records`, `partial` or `enriched` (basis decision 3), a trend other than `growing`, `flat` or `shrinking` (headcount trend decision 10), an order field or direction outside its set, and a field repeated in the order (decision 9). A blank reason is the tools' and `Reject`'s own check (`D10-tools`, `D04-store`), not a prospect list's.

## Recorded decisions

- **Switches are per filter** and carry nothing alone (log 1), `category_unknown` excepted: a switch set without its filter's value is not refused, and every other one admits nothing because nothing is filtered; `category_unknown` alone keeps only the candidates with no category.
- **Revenue bounds** default to 0 and unbounded and are inclusive (log 2); a known revenue low below 0, which no source gives, fails a carried revenue filter.
- **Each revenue limit checks its own end of the estimate** (revenue containment outcomes 1, 4 and 5): the minimum reads `revenue_low` and the maximum `revenue_high`, so a member passes only when its whole estimate sits inside the window, and an $8M to $80M estimate no longer passes a $5M to $10M window; overlap and the midpoint were rejected. The filter keeps its shape, `revenue_min`, `revenue_max` and `revenue_unknown` with their names and types, and no column or migration changes, so the previous release runs on this schema; what changes is which members a stored list has at its next read. The order stays on `revenue_low`; separate floor and ceiling filters and a `revenue_high` order field are deferred.
- **A missing high end is unknown only under a maximum** (revenue containment outcome 2): with a maximum set, a member whose estimate has no `revenue_high`, today a mixed carrier's truck estimate, cannot be checked against the ceiling, so it passes only with `revenue_unknown`; with only a minimum set it is judged on its low end. A known low end is still checked: a low below the minimum fails whatever the switch, since the switch admits what cannot be checked, not what is known to fail (decided for this change, so nothing is admitted silently).
- **The excluded-for names follow the ends** (revenue containment outcome 3): the first failing filter (R-LXEB-91XC) keeps its conditions, `revenue_min` when the low is below the minimum or missing under one and otherwise `revenue_max`, which now also names a high end above the maximum and a high end missing under a maximum with the switch off, the latter shown with the existing reason and no new copy. The names and their place before the other filters are unchanged; the terms citing the revenue filter were re-minted with it, otherwise unchanged.
- **Filing age counts whole UTC days** (log 3): a newest filing 730 days before the date of the read passes a limit of 730, one 731 days before fails, and a filing on or after that date passes every limit. The rule is stated as a count of days between two dates rather than as a date moved back, so no limit a direct store caller passes can overflow a time.
- **The fleet filter takes `pass` or `fail`** (log 4); a verdict `unknown` is admitted only by the switch.
- **The website filter takes `https`, `http` and `none`** (website decision 16), `none` being a search that found nothing, not a check that did (website states outcome 5): `unknown` is not a value it takes, since `website_unknown` covers it, as the fleet filter leaves `unknown` to its switch (log 4). The switch is per filter and carries nothing alone (log 1), so a prospect list with `website_unknown` and no list admits every candidate whatever its state.
- **An empty website list is refused, unlike an empty category list** (website decision 16): an empty category list is read as an absent one because the stored filters cannot tell the two apart, and the website list is stored the same way, but a caller who passes an empty website list has asked for no state at all, so it is refused rather than read as no filter. A repeated state is refused too. The list is kept as given, not sorted or deduplicated, so the store's normal form (`D04-store`) is unchanged.
- **"Judged" is holding judgments** (log 5), as the interim ranked list's base was. A candidate with a facts row and no judgments is not judged and is no member.
- **Facts come from the facts row, judgments from the last judge** (funnel rename decision 13). Every filter and order term reads a member's `Facts` or its `Judgments`, each where it lives: an enrichment's facts reach every prospect list at the next read, and the judge that follows it changes only the judgments. A candidate with no facts row reads every fact unknown and no government match, so among the fact filters only the unknown switches and a government filter of false admit it. Until the funnel rename one object carried both; the terms here were re-minted to read the two apart, and none changed in meaning.
- **`class_unknown` admits no tag and the tag `unknown`** (log 26): both say the class is not known, and nothing is left out silently.
- **An empty category list is an absent one**: the stored filters omit empty members, so an empty list cannot be told from an absent one when read back; it carries no category filter unless `category_unknown` is set.
- **Operating confidence orders by the operating key**, `D13-judging`'s exact integer of the rounded confidence; a candidate with no operating confidence is unknown and comes last in either direction.
