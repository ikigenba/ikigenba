# D10-tools

The twenty-three MCP tools agents reach candidates by through the MCP gateway: `internal/tools` (decision 14). An agent lists the owner's prospect lists with `prospect_lists`, reads one with `prospect_list` (its definition, `matching`, how many of its members the brief classified, how many stand on each basis, its members in rank order, each with its basis, the changes since the list was last opened and its standing rejects), which records the open unless it is a peek, and keeps them with `create_prospect_list`, `edit_prospect_list` and `delete_prospect_list`; it keeps a candidate out of one prospect list with `reject` and lets it back with `unreject`; it finds candidates by name, ZIP or phone with `candidates`, reads one with its enrichments (its basis and the targeted kinds it has and lacks, its facts, each with where it came from, and its brief) and, apart from them, its judgments, with its evidence, estimates, rulings, kept pages, enrichment results of every kind, reject records and campaign history with `candidate` (funnel rename decision 13), reads the passes with `passes` and `pass`, starts an ingest pass by hand with `ingest`, over one scraper's current run or every registered scraper's (funnel rename decision 9), starts a judge pass over every active candidate with `judge` (funnel rename decision 10), starts an enrich pass with `enrich`, over every candidate due one, of one kind or of every kind but the three prompts kinds, website search, site text and the brief, and the drive route, or over one prospect list's members or one candidate, of one kind, keeps a page of a candidate's website with `add_page`, records a merge, split or not-same ruling with `decide`, and excludes a candidate from every prospect list with `exclude` or brings it back with `include` (cohorts decision 17); and it lists the industrial parks candidates derives from the Minnesota parcel records with `parks`, reads one with its parcels with `park`, reads the rules that pick a county's industrial parcels with `park_rules` and changes them with `edit_park_rules`, which starts a parks pass that derives the parks again (parks decisions 2 and 5); and it walks named parks with `walk`, which searches them with Google Places and keeps the places found (`D19-walk`). Every signed-in caller sees and may change everything: the evidence, the rulings, the prospect lists and the rejects are the space's, not a user's, so no read answer depends on who asks, and the caller a tool is handed is used only to start an ingest, a judge, an enrich, a parks pass or a walk as that caller (`D06-passes`), which a targeted pass of a prompts kind forwards to prompts, and to record who opened a prospect list, who made a ruling, who added a page, who created or last changed a prospect list, and who rejected or un-rejected. The pass record is `D06-passes`' (`passes.Record`), answered as that document encodes it. What a prospect list is, its filters, its order, its members and `matching`, what a standing reject is, a member's basis, the kinds present and missing, the counts by basis, the previous open and the changes since it, joined and left with their reasons, are `D14-prospect-lists`'; what each store method answers is `D04-store`'s; which kinds an enrich pass walks and in what order is `D06-passes`'; what each kind takes as its inputs and what it finds is `D16-enrichers`', and what the website check and `add_page` fetch, and how a page's host is held to the candidate's website, is `D15-website-check`'s; what a page is and how its copies are kept is `D04-store`'s; what a campaign use is, which candidate it lands on and the order uses are read in is `D04-store`'s, how a merge moves them `D12-ingest`'s, and which of campaigns' events record them `D06-passes`'; and how the facts row projects the newest result of each kind, decides the website between the website check and the website search and the drive minutes between the drive route and the ZIP table, which `GetFacts` and `ReadProspectList` answer the facts from, is `D04-store`'s. What a park is, its parcels and the rule that picked each, its name, the rules, the counties known, the park order and a park's sort key are `D18-parks`'; how a parks pass reads the parcel records and derives the parks is `D06-passes`'. This document states the tools' arguments and schemas, how each call maps onto one store method, the words a store error becomes, and the answers' shapes. Beside them, and listed nowhere, `/mcp` still answers the one tool a sibling of the previous release calls by its old name, `cohort`, answering it as a `prospect_list` peek of the mapped id answers, recording no open, through a dispatcher in front of two appkit servers; it goes in the next release (funnel rename decisions 6 and 17, `D03-serve`). Every argument naming a prospect list accepts the previous release's `coh_` form of a list's id beside its `pl_` form, mapped by `store.MapListID`, and every answer carries the `pl_` id.

Decisions cited by number are the decisions document that delivered candidates; "cohorts decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the cohorts change, and "log N" entry N of its decisions log. "Website decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the website-check change, "website log X" entry X of the decisions log of the website-check change, "enrichers decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the enrichers change, "site text decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the site-text change, "brief decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the brief change, "category evidence decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the category-evidence change, "website search decision N" outcome N of the decisions document of the website-search change, "drive route decision N" outcome N of the decisions document of the drive-route change, "S N" entry DN of the decisions log of the website-search change, "campaigns decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the campaigns change, and "campaigns log DN" entry DN of its decisions log. "Funnel rename decision N" is decision N of the decisions document of the funnel rename, which gives the tools, their members and their answers the funnel's stage names. "Drive route links outcome N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the drive route links change, and "links log DN" entry DN of the decisions log of its delivery. "Basis decision N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the prospect basis change, which adds the basis and the changes since the previous open; a choice that change's delivery made with no outcome behind it is cited as settled for this delivery. "Revenue bands decision N" is decision N of the decisions document of the revenue bands change, which makes a candidate's revenue its headcount times a per-employee band for its kind of business. "Form 5500 decision N" is candidates decision N of the decisions document of the Form 5500 change, which adds Form 5500 filings as a third source and matches a filing to a candidate by its EIN. "Website states outcome N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the website states change, which makes `none` mean a website search found nothing and `unknown` mean none has run. "Headcount trend decision N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the headcount series and trend change, which has the judge write each candidate's yearly headcount series, one per kind, and a trend, growing, flat, shrinking or unknown, with the kind, the two years and the two figures behind it. "OSHA ITA outcome N" is outcome N of the reader's section of the intent document of the OSHA ITA change, which adds OSHA's 300A summaries as a fourth source and reads each establishment's employees and hours worked, and "OSHA ITA P N" entry PN of that change's delivery decisions log. "Parks decision N" is decision N of the decisions document of the parks change, which derives industrial parks from the Minnesota parcel records scrapes holds. "Empty sites outcome N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the empty sites change, which runs the website search again for a company whose site text kept no pages from the website on record, and "empty sites decision N" decision N of its delivery.

## The tools

- `prospect_lists` takes optional `page` and `limit` and answers `prospect_lists`, the held prospect lists' definitions in ascending byte order of name and then of id, `limit` at a time with `next` when more remain (Paging, below). Read-kind.
- `prospect_list` takes `prospect_list` and optional `peek`, a boolean, absent and false alike, and answers `prospect_list` (the definition), `matching`, `classified`, how many of the members answered the brief classified (category evidence decision 5), `by_basis`, how many of the members answered stand on each basis, `changes`, the changes since the previous recorded open of the list, `members` and `rejects`. Without `peek` it is an open: it answers as `OpenProspectList` does (`D04-store`), which records the open, with the caller as the one who opened it and the members answered with their bases, so the next open compares against it; with `peek` true it answers as `ReadProspectList` does, the same answer, and records nothing, so an agent reading the list on the owner's behalf does not move the point the next open's changes are counted from. Either way the store reads its own clock once, for the filing-age and campaign-age filters and the open's time. `changes` holds `since`, the previous open's time, `null` on a first open, when nothing else is reported; `definition_changed`, which of `filters`, `order` and `size` differ from that open's; `joined`, each with its name, basis and why (`new`, `passes`, `moved_up`); and `left`, each with its name, its basis then and now, why (`gone`, for a candidate no longer active and judged, with no basis now, `excluded`, `rejected`, `filter`, with the first failing filter's name, `cut`) (`D14-prospect-lists`). A prospect list is read whole: its size is at most 100, so it is not paged. Each member is the candidate with its display fields and tags, its basis, `records`, `partial` or `enriched`, its operating confidence, revenue range and the confidence in it, `headcount_low`, the low end of the headcount its judge used (revenue bands decision 17), `trend`, the trend its judge wrote, and `trend_change`, the change behind it, absent when the trend is unknown (headcount trend decisions 10 and 11), its category names and `classified_by`, which source decided them: `brief` when every one is the brief's, `links` when they are the link facts' and empty when it has none (category evidence decision 3), its fleet verdict, website state and the URL it landed on, when site text was last written for it and when the brief was (each `null` when never), drive minutes with `minutes_source`, which decided them, `route` or `zip` (drive route decision 10), government match, known-since date, newest filing and whether it is excluded. Read-kind, though an open records itself (Recorded decisions).
- `candidates` takes optional `name` (a case-insensitive substring of any of its names), `zip`, `phone` (matched on the phone key), `page` and `limit`, and answers `candidates`, active candidates newest first, `limit` at a time with `next`. Read-kind.
- `candidate` takes `candidate` and answers it with its `enrichments` and its `judgments` as two members (funnel rename decision 13). `enrichments` is what the enrichers produced: `basis`, as a member carries it, and `kinds`, which of the four targeted kinds, `website_search`, `site_text`, `brief` and `drive_route`, it has a result of, failed ones included, `present`, and which it has none of, `missing`, so an agent sees what the basis rests on; `facts`, its facts row as the store holds it at the read, each fact with where it came from (known-since date and `known_since_source`, newest filing and `newest_filing_source`, the source identifier each was taken from by its order of sources (`D05-sources`), categories each with its source and `classified_by`, as a member carries it, `records_differ`, whether the brief decided them and the link facts' categories name a different set, and `record_categories`, the link facts' categories with their sources when `records_differ` is true and `[]` otherwise (category evidence decisions 3 and 4), government match, fleet verdict with the power units and drivers behind it, `headcounts`, one per link not gone that carries one, each with its scraper, kind, count and the `date` it is as of, and `brief_labels`, the labels of the newest brief that did not fail (revenue bands decisions 4 and 5), website state, the URL it landed on and `website_source`, what decided it, the website check, the website search, `places` for a Places-reported website the check has not yet been given, or `owner`, which nothing records yet (`D04-store`), and `website_checked_url`, the URL the deciding check was given and began from, the Places URL or the email domain, only when it differs from the URL landed on; the website facts are decided at the read, at the candidate's Places URL then, as the candidate page decides them, so the answer and the page agree, drive minutes with `minutes_source`, which result decided them, the drive route or the ZIP table, and, when the route decided, its `miles` and `route_address`, the address the routing service resolved (drive route decision 10), and `written`, when each kind last wrote the row, the website search's time among them), absent when the candidate has no facts row; and `brief`, its newest brief result that did not fail, with the summary, the categories it gave each with why, its labels and when, absent when there is none. `judgments` is what the last judge wrote: operating confidence with the evidence behind it, the revenue range with its confidence, the headcount the judge used, `headcount_low`, `headcount_high` and `headcount_basis`, the kinds and scrapers that gave it (revenue bands decision 7), `headcount_series`, one element per kind with a dated figure, each with its kind, the scraper behind its newest figure and its figures oldest year first, one per year that has one, and `trend` with its basis, `trend_kind`, `trend_from_year`, `trend_to_year`, `trend_from`, `trend_to` and `trend_change` (headcount trend decisions 4, 9 and 11), the owner's exclusion, the rules version and when it was calculated, absent when the candidate has never been judged. Beside them it answers `sources`, what the candidate's display facts were taken from: the source identifier of its `name`, its `address` (street, city, state and ZIP together), its `phone` and its `email`, and `other_names`, `other_addresses`, `other_phones` and `other_emails`, every other value its records hold for each, with the source identifier that holds it, so an agent sees what the page shows beside each fact; each of its `people` carries `source` too, absent when empty, as on a candidate a previous release stored and on every link's people. It answers the park it was found in (`park`), its links, and each places link's `walk` and `park`, estimates, the judge's `headcount` estimate among them in its source's place (revenue bands decisions 8 and 16), rulings, the pages kept on it (the newest copy of each URL, newest first, each with its number, URL, title, size read, when it was fetched, who added it and the path of its text, never the text itself), its `enrichment_results` of every kind newest first (each with its kind, source, inputs and cost and when; a website check's with its state, landed URL, title, status code, size read, last error and domain; a derived kind's, and a site text, brief, website search or drive route result's, with its `found` object), reject records, every reject and un-reject naming it, each with its prospect list's name while the prospect list exists, and its campaign history, one use per campaign the business was put in, newest added first, each with the campaign's id, the prospect list it was added from (empty when added one at a time), when it was added, when the campaign rejected it and why, and when a piece was last delivered to it, each time absent when unknown (campaigns decision 17); a merged id answers the merged record with its `merged_into`, no `sources`, no enrichments, no judgments, no links, no estimates, no pages, no results, no campaign history, and its rulings and reject records. Read-kind.
- `passes` takes optional `page` and `limit` and answers `passes`, pass records in pass order (`D04-store`), `limit` at a time with `next`; `pass` takes `pass` and answers its record; a walk's record carries its counts, and `pass` of a walk adds `parks`, the parks it covered. Read-kind.
- `ingest` takes optional `scraper`, a registered scraper's name, and answers the pass record it started, `running`: named one, an ingest of that scraper's current run, which an enrich follows when it ends `done`, as one follows an automatic ingest; named none, a full ingest of every registered scraper's current run that re-derives every derived kind and judges every candidate; the work lands in the background (`D06-passes`). Additive.

- `judge` takes nothing and answers the record of the judge pass it started over every active candidate, `running`, the same pass the daily trigger starts (`D06-passes`). Additive.
- `enrich` takes optional `kind`, one of `drive_minutes`, `link_facts`, `website_check`, `website_search`, `site_text`, `brief` and `drive_route`, and optional `prospect_list`, `candidate` and `force`, and answers the pass record it started, `running`, as `ingest` does. Without a target it is the corpus enrich the previous release had: with a `kind` the pass runs that kind alone, and with none, or an empty one, every kind but the prompts kinds, `website_search`, `site_text` and `brief`, and `drive_route`, the derived ones first and the website check only when the engine has a checker (`D06-passes`); a prompts kind, `drive_route` and `force` are refused there. With a `prospect_list` or a `candidate`, never both, and a `kind`, it starts a pass of kind `enrich_target` over the prospect list's members at the call (`D14-prospect-lists`) or the one candidate, running that kind for those that are due, or for every one of them with `force`; site text runs the caller's `site-text` prompt in prompts once per company, the brief the caller's `brief` prompt, and the website search the caller's `website-search` prompt, confirming the site its agent names with the website check's fetcher before it is recorded, and the drive route asks the routing service for the drive from the origin to each company's street address (`D06-passes`, `D16-enrichers`, `D17-drive-route`; drive route decisions 1 and 10). A candidate is due a kind when its inputs for it are not empty and it has no result of the kind or its newest one was computed from other inputs (`D04-store`). `older_than_days`, which an earlier release took, is no longer a member. A corpus enrich is `busy` only while another corpus enrich runs, and a targeted one only while another runs over the same target (`D06-passes`). Additive: a run adds results and never removes one.
- `add_page` takes `candidate` and `url`, fetches the URL itself with the website check's fetcher and rules (`D15-website-check`), and keeps the page on the candidate, its title, its text and the size read, with the caller as the one who added it; it answers the page it kept without the text, whose text is served at the page's own path (`D08-pages`). The URL's host must be the candidate's website host or a subdomain of it, and a candidate keeps at most `store.PageCap` distinct URLs; adding a URL it holds keeps a newer copy. It ties the page to no pass and no prompts run, so any signed-in caller may call it, an agent inside a prompts run or the owner's own. Additive: it adds a copy and never removes one.
- `decide` takes `kind`, `why` and `candidate`, and by kind `other` (`merge`, `not_same`) or `links` (`split`); it records the ruling with the caller as its maker and answers `ruling` and `candidates`, the candidates as they stand after it (`store.Decide`, `D12-ingest`). `exclude` and `include` are no longer its kinds. Additive.
- `exclude` and `include` take `candidate` and `why` and record the owner's exclusion or inclusion through the same `store.Decide`, answering as `decide` does. The ruling re-judges the candidate in the same call, so it leaves or rejoins every prospect list that does not show excluded candidates at once. Additive.
- `create_prospect_list` takes `name` and optional `filters`, `order` and `size` (the default size, 30, when absent, `D14-prospect-lists`) and answers the new prospect list's definition; called twice it makes two prospect lists. Additive.
- `edit_prospect_list` takes `prospect_list` and any of `name`, `filters`, `order` and `size`; a passed `filters` or `order` replaces the whole of it (`{}` clears every filter, `[]` sets the default order), an absent or null one keeps it, and the answer is the definition after the call. An edit that changes nothing writes nothing and answers the definition as it was. Additive.
- `delete_prospect_list` takes `prospect_list` and answers the definition it removed; its reject records stay. Destructive.
- `reject` and `unreject` take `prospect_list`, `candidate` and `why` and answer the standing record for the pair after the call; repeating either writes nothing. Additive.
- `parks` takes optional `county`, `walked` (`all`, the default, `unwalked` or `walked`), `gone` (`active`, the default, `gone` or `all`), `sort` (`minutes`, the default, nearest first with unknown drive minutes last, `value`, the most building value first, or `parcels`, the most parcels first, each with the id breaking ties), `page` and `limit`, and answers `parks`, each park with its name, county, town, ZIP, state, center, radius, parcel count, building value, drive minutes, `null` when unknown, `grown_from`, its newest walk, with its new candidates and whether the ceiling cut it, the run it was last derived from, its map link and its times, `limit` at a time with `next` (Paging, below). Read-kind.
- `park` takes `park` and answers `park`, as `parks` answers it, and `parcels`, `walks`, `places` and `found_candidates`, its walks newest first, the places they found and the candidates found in it, every parcel of the park with its PIN, owners, site address, building value, `null` when unknown, the rule that picked it (`label`, `value` or `candidate`) and its point, the most building value first. A park marked gone answers its state and the parcels it kept. Read-kind.
- `park_rules` takes nothing and answers the distance in meters, the minimum parcels, the value threshold in dollars and `walk_ceiling`, and `counties`, every county known (a county with a label, or one the last derivation read) in byte order, each with its labels, `[]` for a county that uses the fallbacks (`D18-parks`). Read-kind.
- `edit_park_rules` takes any of `distance`, `min_parcels`, `value_threshold` and `walk_ceiling`, and `county` with `labels`, the whole label list of that one county, `[]` removing every label so the county uses the fallbacks; it lands the edit and starts a parks pass of origin `rules` over the current parcel run, and answers `rules`, the rules after it as `park_rules` answers them, and `pass`, the record of the pass it started, `running` (`D06-passes`). An edit with nothing set changes no rule and still derives the parks again. While a parks pass runs it is `busy` and changes nothing. Additive.
- `walk` takes `parks`, a list of park ids, and optional `again`, a boolean, absent and false alike, and answers the record of the walk pass it started, `running`; nothing else walks, and a walked park needs `again`. Additive.

## Paging

`candidates`, `passes`, `prospect_lists` and `parks` page as the suite's listing convention asks (`shape/`, Listing and Storage), on the contract of appkit's `paging` package (`appkit/paging`, appkit's D25), which this document cites and never restates. Each input declares `page`, an opaque string, and `limit`, an integer, as pointer fields carrying the shared descriptions `paging.PageDescription` and `paging.LimitDescription`; each answer is its list then `next`, an opaque string present exactly when at least one entry follows, whose field's tag carries `paging.NextDescription`. An absent, null or empty `page` is the first page, and `next` is handed back as `page` unchanged; an absent or null `limit` is `paging.DefaultLimit`.

Each tool's token is `paging.Token{Tool: <its name>}` over the key of the last entry the page answered, in the order the store windows by (`D04-store`): for `candidates` the candidate's created time as RFC 3339 in UTC to the second and its id; for `passes` the pass's started time so formatted and its id; for `prospect_lists` the prospect list's name and its id. Those three keys are two strings. `parks` pages by the park order under its sort (`D18-parks`), so its token is three strings: the sort it was issued under, the last park's sort key under that sort (its drive minutes, empty when unknown, its building value or its parcel count, as a decimal) and its id. The tool asks the store for one entry more than the page, from the decoded position, and sets `next` only when the extra entry came back. A token binds no filter: a caller hands `next` back with the same `county`, `walked` and `gone`, and a changed filter reads on from the same position. A `parks` token binds its sort: one handed back under another sort is the page refusal, since its key means nothing there, as is one whose id is not a park id or whose key is not a decimal number; an empty key is accepted only under `minutes`, where it stands for unknown drive minutes. A token one of the four issued is refused by the other three, because the checksum covers the tool's name; the parks page's before-token (`D08-pages`) is refused by the tool alike.

Before the store is read, a `limit` outside 1 to `paging.MaxLimit` is refused with the text of `paging.Window`'s error, then a `page` the tool's token does not decode with `paging.BadPage`; each refusal is that text alone, not one of the five words below, and is the same while the database is unreachable. Paging records nothing: no token appears in any event or log line (`D09-trail`).

## Arguments

Every argument type is typed, so appkit derives each tool's input schema and refuses malformed arguments before the handler runs. A prospect list's filters are one nested object, `filters`, whose members carry the prospect lists document's spellings: `revenue_min`, `revenue_max`, `max_minutes`, `max_filing_age_days` and `min_campaign_age_days` as non-negative integers, `categories` as a list of names, `fleet` and `class` as strings, `government` as a boolean that may be absent, and the switches and `show_excluded` as booleans, `basis` as a pointer to a list of the three words, so that absent, null and `[]` alike are no filter, and `website` as a pointer to a list of states, so that absent or null is no filter and a passed `[]` an empty list, with its switch `website_unknown`, and `trend` as a pointer to a list of the three directions, so that absent, null and `[]` alike are no filter, with its switch `trend_unknown`. `enrich`'s arguments are flat: `kind`, `prospect_list` and `candidate`, strings, each absent and empty alike, and `force`, a boolean, absent and false alike. `add_page`'s are `candidate` and `url`, both required strings. `prospect_list`'s are `prospect_list`, a required string, and `peek`, a boolean, absent and false alike; `delete_prospect_list` takes no `peek`. An order is a list of entries, each an object with `field` and `direction`, both required. On both tools `filters` and `order` are pointers, so an absent or null member reaches the handler as nil and a passed `{}` or `[]` as an empty value; `size` is a pointer to an `int`.

The handler converts the arguments to the store's types (the filter set and the order list) and checks them with the store's validators before it reads the store: what each validator accepts is `D14-prospect-lists`' validation set, and a value it refuses is `invalid`. The schema already refuses a member `filters` does not name, an `order` entry missing `field` or `direction` or naming another member, and a negative number in `filters`. A `website` list's states, and that it is not empty and repeats none, and that every element of `basis` is one of the three words and every element of `trend` one of the three directions, `unknown` not among them since the switch carries it, are the store's validator's to check (`D14-prospect-lists`), which accepts a repeated or an empty `basis` or `trend` and stores its normal form (`D04-store`); an absent `website` reaches the store as nil and a passed `[]` as an empty list, which is `invalid`.

## Answers

Times are RFC 3339 UTC to the second; a date (a filing date, the known-since date, the newest filing) is `YYYY-MM-DD`, absent when there is none. Money is whole dollars. A confidence or an evidence weight is the stored `float64` itself, never rounded at the call. Missing is never zero: a candidate with no evidence answers no `operating_confidence`, one with no estimate no `revenue_low`, `revenue_high` or `revenue_confidence`, one with no known date no `known_since`, one with no FMCSA counts no `power_units` or `drivers`, one whose judge used no headcount no `headcount_low`, `headcount_high` or `headcount_basis`, on the judgments and, for `headcount_low`, on a member, one whose trend is unknown no `trend_kind`, `trend_from_year`, `trend_to_year`, `trend_from`, `trend_to` or `trend_change` on the judgments and no `trend_change` on a member, one whose rows give no category an empty `categories`, one with no facts row no `facts`, one whose drive minutes the route did not decide no `miles` or `route_address`, one with no brief result that did not fail no `brief`, and one never judged no `judgments`; `written` holds only the kinds that wrote the row. `headcounts` and `brief_labels` are always present on the facts, `[]` when there is none, as `headcount_series` is on the judgments; `trend` is always present on the judgments and on a member, `unknown` when no kind gives one; and a headcount's `date` is absent when its source gives none, as is its `hours`, which only an OSHA ITA summary gives. `website` is always present: `https` or `http` when the newest website check or a confirmed website search found a site, `none` when no such result did and a website search that did not fail found nothing or named a site that did not load, and `unknown` otherwise, so a check that found no site leaves it `unknown` (website states outcomes 1 to 3); `website_url` is absent unless the state is `https` or `http`. `website_source` is always present, `check` or `search` for whichever found the site, and the empty string when none did, a searched `none` included (`D04-store`'s website decision; website search decision 10). `minutes_source` is always present, on the facts and on a member, `route` or `zip` for whichever result decided the drive minutes and the empty string when neither did (`D04-store`'s drive decision; drive route decision 7). `classified_by` is always present, the empty string for a candidate with no category, and is derived at the read from the sources of the categories the same answer carries, never stored (category evidence decisions 3 and 11); `records_differ` and `record_categories` are always present too, the list `[]` unless the brief decided and the records say otherwise. A website check result's `url`, `title` and `error` are absent when empty, its `status_code` when no response came, and it carries no `found`; any other kind's result carries `found`, the object it stored (`{}` included), and none of the website members. Every judgment answered (operating confidence and its evidence, revenue range, exclusion) is the one the store holds, written by the last judge of the candidate (`D13-judging`, `D06-passes`); every fact (dates, categories, government match, fleet verdict and counts, website, drive minutes, their source, the miles and the route address) is the facts row's at the read, and the brief the newest good brief result's, written by the newest result of its kind, the website and its source by the website decision over the website check's and the website search's results (`D04-store`). A campaign use answers `campaign` and `prospect_list` always, `prospect_list` the empty string for a business added one at a time, and `added`, `rejected` and `delivered` as times, each absent when the store holds none, as when a delivered or rejected event arrived before the added one, with `reason` absent unless the campaign rejected it.

A prospect list's definition answers its `filters` exactly as the store holds them: the store's own filter type encoded with its members omitted when empty, so a prospect list with no filters answers `{}`, and a stored `government` of false is present. Its `order` is never empty, since the store keeps the default order explicitly (`D04-store`). `created_by` and `changed_by` are user ids, beside `created_email` and `changed_email`. A member carries `excluded` always, since a prospect list that shows excluded candidates lists them beside the rest, and `website_source` always, the empty string included, as the facts do. A member carries `basis` always, and the read answers `by_basis` with all three counts, which sum to the members answered. `changes` carries `since` always, a time or `null`, and `definition_changed`, `joined` and `left` always, `[]` when empty; a left member carries `filter` only when its `why` is `filter` and `basis_now` only when its `why` is not `gone`, and its `name` is the candidate's name now. The kinds in `enrichments` are always two arrays in the order website search, site text, brief, drive route. A reject record carries its prospect list's name while the prospect list exists, and omits `why`, `user`, `email` and `at` in the one answer that has none: an `unreject` of a pair that has no standing reject, which writes nothing.

A kept page answers its `number`, the store's number of that copy, its `url`, the URL the fetch landed on, its `title`, absent when empty, its `size`, its `fetched` time, `by` and `email`, the user who added it, and `path`, the candidate's page path for that number; it never carries the text, which the page path serves. A candidate's `pages` are every URL it holds, the newest copy of each, newest first, `[]` when none and for a merged candidate. A member's `site_text` is always present: the time the site text kind was last written for the member, `null` when never; so is its `brief`, the time the brief kind was last written for it, a failed result's time included, `null` when never (`D14-prospect-lists`). A site text, brief or drive route result answers its stored `found` as any non-website kind does, a failed one included, and a drive route result with nothing routable too.

A candidate is answered whole, phone and email included: a tool's answer is product, given to a signed-in caller, and the secrecy of decision 18 governs the trail, the bus, refusals and the logs (`D09-trail`), not the answer.

## Refusals

Apart from the two paging refusals above, a refusal is an MCP error result whose text is `<word>: <sentence>`. The words are the five `D01-layout-and-run-seam` declares as `passes.Refusal*`: `not_found`, `invalid`, `busy`, `stopping` and `unavailable`. Each sentence is a copy constant, `tools.Refuse*`: one paired with each word, used unless a requirement names another, and four more for `add_page`, `RefuseNoWebsite`, `RefuseOffSite`, `RefusePageCap`, which names the cap, and `RefuseFetch`, which opens a fetch failure's text. A refusal names no id, business name, prospect list name, address, phone, email or reason the store supplied (decision 18), with three exceptions, recorded below: an `enrich` refused for a missing prompt carries the engine's text, naming the prompt and quoting prompts' refusal, an `add_page` whose fetch failed carries the failure, and a `walk` refused for a gone or a walked park names the parks' ids (walk decision 7).

- `candidate` and `pass`: an id that is not well formed, and an unknown or pruned one, are `not_found`.
- `prospect_list` and `delete_prospect_list`: a malformed prospect list id is `not_found` without reading the store; then an unknown or deleted one is `not_found`.
- `create_prospect_list`: a name, size, filter set or order a validator refuses is `invalid`, before the store.
- `edit_prospect_list`: a passed name, size, filter set or order a validator refuses is `invalid`; then a malformed prospect list id `not_found`; both before the store; then an unknown or deleted prospect list `not_found`.
- `reject` and `unreject`: a blank `why` is `invalid`; then a malformed prospect list or candidate id `not_found`; both before the store; then what `Reject` refuses: an unknown or deleted prospect list or an unknown candidate `not_found`, a merged candidate `invalid`.
- `exclude` and `include`: a blank `why` is `invalid`; then a malformed candidate id `not_found`; both before the store; then what `store.Decide` refuses.
- `decide`: a `kind` other than `merge`, `split` and `not_same`, a blank `why`, `other` absent for `merge` or `not_same` and `links` empty for `split` are `invalid`, decided first; then a malformed `candidate` or `other` is `not_found`, without reading the store; then what `store.Decide` refuses.
- `ingest`: what `Engine.Ingest` refuses, with its word, and with the engine's text when it carries one (`D06-passes`): an unknown scraper `invalid`, no scrapes `unavailable`, a running ingest of the same scraper, or, for a full ingest, another running full ingest, `busy`, the drain `stopping`.

- `judge`: what `Engine.Judge` refuses: a running judge `busy`, the drain `stopping`.
- `enrich`: a non-empty `kind` that names no kind, both `prospect_list` and `candidate`, `force` with neither, a prompts kind (`website_search`, `site_text`, `brief`) with neither, and a target with no `kind` are `invalid`, decided first; then a malformed `prospect_list` or `candidate` is `not_found`; both without the store. Then, without a target, what `Engine.Enrich` refuses, with its word (`D06-passes`): `stopping` while draining, `invalid` for `drive_route`, `unavailable` for `website_check` with no checker, `busy` while a corpus enrich runs; and with one, what `Engine.EnrichTarget` refuses, with its word and its text when it carries one: `stopping`, an unknown or deleted prospect list or an unknown candidate `not_found`, a merged candidate `invalid`, a kind its engine cannot run `unavailable` (`website_search` without a checker or without a prompts client, `drive_route` with a routes client that is not ready), `drive_route` on an engine with no routes client, since the maps key was missing at start, `invalid` with the text of `passes.NoRouteKey`, a caller with no prompt named for the kind `invalid` with a text naming it, and `busy` while a pass over the same target runs. `older_than_days` and any other member the arguments do not name are appkit's schema refusal.
- `add_page`: a malformed `candidate` is `not_found`, then a `url` that is not an absolute `http` or `https` URL with a host `invalid`, then no fetcher `unavailable`, all without the store; then, before fetching, an unknown candidate `not_found`, a candidate with no website, a merged one among them, `invalid` with `RefuseNoWebsite`, a URL off its website `invalid` with `RefuseOffSite`, and a twenty-first distinct URL `invalid` with `RefusePageCap`; then a fetch that fails `unavailable` with `RefuseFetch` and the failure, and one that was redirected off the website `invalid` with `RefuseOffSite`; each keeps nothing.
- `candidates`, `passes`, `prospect_lists` and `parks`: the limit refusal, then the page refusal, before the store is read.
- `parks`: then a `walked`, `gone` or `sort` that is not one of its words, or a `county` that is not well formed, `invalid`, without the store. A well-formed county no park is in is no refusal; it lists none.
- `park`: a malformed id is `not_found` without reading the store; then an unknown one is `not_found`. A gone park is answered.
- `edit_park_rules`: a number outside its bounds, a `county` that is not well formed, `labels` without `county` or `county` without `labels`, and a label list a validator refuses (an empty or untrimmed label, one with a comma, a repeated one or too many) are `invalid`, before the store (`D18-parks`); then what `Engine.EditParkRules` refuses, with its word alone: the drain `stopping`, no scrapes `unavailable`, a running parks pass `busy`; each changes no rule and records no pass. A negative number is appkit's schema refusal.
- `walk`: what `Engine.Walk` refuses, with its word and its text when it carries one (`D19-walk`): the drain `stopping`, no maps key `invalid` with `passes.NoPlacesKey`, no park, more than `passes.MaxWalkParks` or a repeated one `invalid`, a malformed or unknown park `not_found`, a gone park `invalid` with `passes.ParkGone` and its id, a walked park without `again` `invalid` with `passes.AlreadyWalked` and its id, another walk running `busy`.
- Every tool: the database failing is `unavailable`. Malformed arguments, an unknown member in `filters` or in an order entry and a negative number in `filters` among them, are appkit's schema refusal.
