# D10-tools

The eighteen MCP tools agents reach candidates by through the MCP gateway: `internal/tools` (decision 14). An agent lists the owner's prospect lists with `prospect_lists`, reads one with `prospect_list` (its definition, `matching`, how many of its members the brief classified, its members in rank order and its standing rejects), and keeps them with `create_prospect_list`, `edit_prospect_list` and `delete_prospect_list`; it keeps a candidate out of one prospect list with `reject` and lets it back with `unreject`; it finds candidates by name, ZIP or phone with `candidates`, reads one with its enrichments (its facts, each with where it came from, and its brief) and, apart from them, its judgments, with its evidence, estimates, rulings, kept pages, enrichment results of every kind, reject records and campaign history with `candidate` (funnel rename decision 13), reads the passes with `passes` and `pass`, starts an ingest pass by hand with `ingest`, over one scraper's current run or every registered scraper's (funnel rename decision 9), starts a judge pass over every active candidate with `judge` (funnel rename decision 10), starts an enrich pass with `enrich`, over every candidate due one, of one kind or of every kind but the three prompts kinds, website search, site text and the brief, or over one prospect list's members or one candidate, of one kind, keeps a page of a candidate's website with `add_page`, records a merge, split or not-same ruling with `decide`, and excludes a candidate from every prospect list with `exclude` or brings it back with `include` (cohorts decision 17). Every signed-in caller sees and may change everything: the evidence, the rulings, the prospect lists and the rejects are the space's, not a user's, so no read answer depends on who asks, and the caller a tool is handed is used only to start an ingest, a judge or an enrich as that caller (`D06-passes`), which a targeted pass of a prompts kind forwards to prompts, and to record who made a ruling, who added a page, who created or last changed a prospect list, and who rejected or un-rejected. The pass record is `D06-passes`' (`passes.Record`), answered as that document encodes it. What a prospect list is, its filters, its order, its members and `matching`, and what a standing reject is, are `D14-prospect-lists`'; what each store method answers is `D04-store`'s; which kinds an enrich pass walks and in what order is `D06-passes`'; what each kind takes as its inputs and what it finds is `D16-enrichers`', and what the website check and `add_page` fetch, and how a page's host is held to the candidate's website, is `D15-website-check`'s; what a page is and how its copies are kept is `D04-store`'s; what a campaign use is, which candidate it lands on and the order uses are read in is `D04-store`'s, how a merge moves them `D12-ingest`'s, and which of campaigns' events record them `D06-passes`'; and how the facts row projects the newest result of each kind and decides the website between the website check and the website search, which `GetFacts` and `ReadProspectList` answer the facts from, is `D04-store`'s. This document states the tools' arguments and schemas, how each call maps onto one store method, the words a store error becomes, and the answers' shapes. Beside them, and listed nowhere, `/mcp` still answers the one tool a sibling of the previous release calls by its old name, `cohort`, answering it as `prospect_list` answers the mapped id, through a dispatcher in front of two appkit servers; it goes in the next release (funnel rename decisions 6 and 17, `D03-serve`). Every argument naming a prospect list accepts the previous release's `coh_` form of a list's id beside its `pl_` form, mapped by `store.MapListID`, and every answer carries the `pl_` id.

Decisions cited by number are the decisions document that delivered candidates; "cohorts decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the cohorts change, and "log N" entry N of its decisions log. "Website decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the website-check change, "website log X" entry X of the decisions log of the website-check change, "enrichers decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the enrichers change, "site text decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the site-text change, "brief decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the brief change, "category evidence decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the category-evidence change, "website search decision N" outcome N of the decisions document of the website-search change, "S N" entry DN of the decisions log of the website-search change, "campaigns decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the campaigns change, and "campaigns log DN" entry DN of its decisions log. "Funnel rename decision N" is decision N of the decisions document of the funnel rename, which gives the tools, their members and their answers the funnel's stage names.

## The tools

- `prospect_lists` takes optional `page` and `limit` and answers `prospect_lists`, the held prospect lists' definitions in ascending byte order of name and then of id, `limit` at a time with `next` when more remain (Paging, below). Read-kind.
- `prospect_list` takes `prospect_list` and answers `prospect_list` (the definition), `matching`, `classified`, how many of the members answered the brief classified (category evidence decision 5), `members` and `rejects`, as `ReadProspectList` answers (`D04-store`), the store reading its own clock once for the filing-age and campaign-age filters. A prospect list is read whole: its size is at most 100, so it is not paged. Each member is the candidate with its display fields and tags, its operating confidence, revenue range and the confidence in it, its category names and `classified_by`, which source decided them: `brief` when every one is the brief's, `links` when they are the link facts' and empty when it has none (category evidence decision 3), its fleet verdict, website state and the URL it landed on, when site text was last written for it and when the brief was (each `null` when never), drive minutes, government match, known-since date, newest filing and whether it is excluded. Read-kind.
- `candidates` takes optional `name` (a case-insensitive substring of any of its names), `zip`, `phone` (matched on the phone key), `page` and `limit`, and answers `candidates`, active candidates newest first, `limit` at a time with `next`. Read-kind.
- `candidate` takes `candidate` and answers it with its `enrichments` and its `judgments` as two members (funnel rename decision 13). `enrichments` is what the enrichers produced: `facts`, its facts row as the store holds it at the read, each fact with where it came from (known-since date, newest filing, categories each with its source and `classified_by`, as a member carries it, `records_differ`, whether the brief decided them and the link facts' categories name a different set, and `record_categories`, the link facts' categories with their sources when `records_differ` is true and `[]` otherwise (category evidence decisions 3 and 4), government match, fleet verdict with the power units and drivers behind it, website state, the URL it landed on and `website_source`, which result decided it, the website check or the website search, drive minutes, and `written`, when each kind last wrote the row, the website search's time among them), absent when the candidate has no facts row; and `brief`, its newest brief result that did not fail, with the summary, the categories it gave each with why, its labels and when, absent when there is none. `judgments` is what the last judge wrote: operating confidence with the evidence behind it, the revenue range with its confidence, the owner's exclusion, the rules version and when it was calculated, absent when the candidate has never been judged. Beside them it answers its links, estimates, rulings, the pages kept on it (the newest copy of each URL, newest first, each with its number, URL, title, size read, when it was fetched, who added it and the path of its text, never the text itself), its `enrichment_results` of every kind newest first (each with its kind, source, inputs and cost and when; a website check's with its state, landed URL, title, status code, size read, last error and domain; a derived kind's, and a site text, brief or website search result's, with its `found` object), reject records, every reject and un-reject naming it, each with its prospect list's name while the prospect list exists, and its campaign history, one use per campaign the business was put in, newest added first, each with the campaign's id, the prospect list it was added from (empty when added one at a time), when it was added, when the campaign rejected it and why, and when a piece was last delivered to it, each time absent when unknown (campaigns decision 17); a merged id answers the merged record with its `merged_into`, no enrichments, no judgments, no links, no estimates, no pages, no results, no campaign history, and its rulings and reject records. Read-kind.
- `passes` takes optional `page` and `limit` and answers `passes`, pass records in pass order (`D04-store`), `limit` at a time with `next`; `pass` takes `pass` and answers its record. Read-kind.
- `ingest` takes optional `scraper`, a registered scraper's name, and answers the pass record it started, `running`: named one, an ingest of that scraper's current run, which an enrich follows when it ends `done`, as one follows an automatic ingest; named none, a full ingest of every registered scraper's current run that re-derives every derived kind and judges every candidate; the work lands in the background (`D06-passes`). Additive.

- `judge` takes nothing and answers the record of the judge pass it started over every active candidate, `running`, the same pass the daily trigger starts (`D06-passes`). Additive.
- `enrich` takes optional `kind`, one of `drive_minutes`, `link_facts`, `website_check`, `website_search`, `site_text` and `brief`, and optional `prospect_list`, `candidate` and `force`, and answers the pass record it started, `running`, as `ingest` does. Without a target it is the corpus enrich the previous release had: with a `kind` the pass runs that kind alone, and with none, or an empty one, every kind but the prompts kinds, `website_search`, `site_text` and `brief`, the derived ones first and the website check only when the engine has a checker (`D06-passes`); a prompts kind and `force` are refused there. With a `prospect_list` or a `candidate`, never both, and a `kind`, it starts a pass of kind `enrich_target` over the prospect list's members at the call (`D14-prospect-lists`) or the one candidate, running that kind for those that are due, or for every one of them with `force`; site text runs the caller's `site-text` prompt in prompts once per company, the brief the caller's `brief` prompt, and the website search the caller's `website-search` prompt, confirming the site its agent names with the website check's fetcher before it is recorded (`D06-passes`, `D16-enrichers`). A candidate is due a kind when its inputs for it are not empty and it has no result of the kind or its newest one was computed from other inputs (`D04-store`). `older_than_days`, which an earlier release took, is no longer a member. A corpus enrich is `busy` only while another corpus enrich runs, and a targeted one only while another runs over the same target (`D06-passes`). Additive: a run adds results and never removes one.
- `add_page` takes `candidate` and `url`, fetches the URL itself with the website check's fetcher and rules (`D15-website-check`), and keeps the page on the candidate, its title, its text and the size read, with the caller as the one who added it; it answers the page it kept without the text, whose text is served at the page's own path (`D08-pages`). The URL's host must be the candidate's website host or a subdomain of it, and a candidate keeps at most `store.PageCap` distinct URLs; adding a URL it holds keeps a newer copy. It ties the page to no pass and no prompts run, so any signed-in caller may call it, an agent inside a prompts run or the owner's own. Additive: it adds a copy and never removes one.
- `decide` takes `kind`, `why` and `candidate`, and by kind `other` (`merge`, `not_same`) or `links` (`split`); it records the ruling with the caller as its maker and answers `ruling` and `candidates`, the candidates as they stand after it (`store.Decide`, `D12-ingest`). `exclude` and `include` are no longer its kinds. Additive.
- `exclude` and `include` take `candidate` and `why` and record the owner's exclusion or inclusion through the same `store.Decide`, answering as `decide` does. The ruling re-judges the candidate in the same call, so it leaves or rejoins every prospect list that does not show excluded candidates at once. Additive.
- `create_prospect_list` takes `name` and optional `filters`, `order` and `size` (the default size, 30, when absent, `D14-prospect-lists`) and answers the new prospect list's definition; called twice it makes two prospect lists. Additive.
- `edit_prospect_list` takes `prospect_list` and any of `name`, `filters`, `order` and `size`; a passed `filters` or `order` replaces the whole of it (`{}` clears every filter, `[]` sets the default order), an absent or null one keeps it, and the answer is the definition after the call. An edit that changes nothing writes nothing and answers the definition as it was. Additive.
- `delete_prospect_list` takes `prospect_list` and answers the definition it removed; its reject records stay. Destructive.
- `reject` and `unreject` take `prospect_list`, `candidate` and `why` and answer the standing record for the pair after the call; repeating either writes nothing. Additive.

## Paging

`candidates`, `passes` and `prospect_lists` page as the suite's listing convention asks (`shape/`, Listing and Storage), on the contract of appkit's `paging` package (`appkit/paging`, appkit's D25), which this document cites and never restates. Each input declares `page`, an opaque string, and `limit`, an integer, as pointer fields carrying the shared descriptions `paging.PageDescription` and `paging.LimitDescription`; each answer is its list then `next`, an opaque string present exactly when at least one entry follows, whose field's tag carries `paging.NextDescription`. An absent, null or empty `page` is the first page, and `next` is handed back as `page` unchanged; an absent or null `limit` is `paging.DefaultLimit`.

Each tool's token is `paging.Token{Tool: <its name>}` over the key of the last entry the page answered, in the order the store windows by (`D04-store`): for `candidates` the candidate's created time as RFC 3339 in UTC to the second and its id; for `passes` the pass's started time so formatted and its id; for `prospect_lists` the prospect list's name and its id. All three keys are two strings. The tool asks the store for one entry more than the page, from the decoded position, and sets `next` only when the extra entry came back. A token binds no filter. A token one of the three issued is refused by the other two, because the checksum covers the tool's name.

Before the store is read, a `limit` outside 1 to `paging.MaxLimit` is refused with the text of `paging.Window`'s error, then a `page` the tool's token does not decode with `paging.BadPage`; each refusal is that text alone, not one of the five words below, and is the same while the database is unreachable. Paging records nothing: no token appears in any event or log line (`D09-trail`).

## Arguments

Every argument type is typed, so appkit derives each tool's input schema and refuses malformed arguments before the handler runs. A prospect list's filters are one nested object, `filters`, whose members carry the prospect lists document's spellings: `revenue_min`, `revenue_max`, `max_minutes`, `max_filing_age_days` and `min_campaign_age_days` as non-negative integers, `categories` as a list of names, `fleet` and `class` as strings, `government` as a boolean that may be absent, and the switches and `show_excluded` as booleans, and `website` as a pointer to a list of states, so that absent or null is no filter and a passed `[]` an empty list, with its switch `website_unknown`. `enrich`'s arguments are flat: `kind`, `prospect_list` and `candidate`, strings, each absent and empty alike, and `force`, a boolean, absent and false alike. `add_page`'s are `candidate` and `url`, both required strings. An order is a list of entries, each an object with `field` and `direction`, both required. On both tools `filters` and `order` are pointers, so an absent or null member reaches the handler as nil and a passed `{}` or `[]` as an empty value; `size` is a pointer to an `int`.

The handler converts the arguments to the store's types (the filter set and the order list) and checks them with the store's validators before it reads the store: what each validator accepts is `D14-prospect-lists`' validation set, and a value it refuses is `invalid`. The schema already refuses a member `filters` does not name, an `order` entry missing `field` or `direction` or naming another member, and a negative number in `filters`. A `website` list's states, and that it is not empty and repeats none, are the store's validator's to check (`D14-prospect-lists`); an absent `website` reaches the store as nil and a passed `[]` as an empty list, which is `invalid`.

## Answers

Times are RFC 3339 UTC to the second; a date (a filing date, the known-since date, the newest filing) is `YYYY-MM-DD`, absent when there is none. Money is whole dollars. A confidence or an evidence weight is the stored `float64` itself, never rounded at the call. Missing is never zero: a candidate with no evidence answers no `operating_confidence`, one with no estimate no `revenue_low`, `revenue_high` or `revenue_confidence`, one with no known date no `known_since`, one with no FMCSA counts no `power_units` or `drivers`, one whose rows give no category an empty `categories`, one with no facts row no `facts`, one with no brief result that did not fail no `brief`, and one never judged no `judgments`; `written` holds only the kinds that wrote the row. `website` is always present, `unknown` before the candidate's first website check result, and `website_url` is absent unless the state is `https` or `http`. `website_source` is always present, `check` or `search` when one of them decided the website, the empty string when neither did (`D04-store`'s website decision; website search decision 10). `classified_by` is always present, the empty string for a candidate with no category, and is derived at the read from the sources of the categories the same answer carries, never stored (category evidence decisions 3 and 11); `records_differ` and `record_categories` are always present too, the list `[]` unless the brief decided and the records say otherwise. A website check result's `url`, `title` and `error` are absent when empty, its `status_code` when no response came, and it carries no `found`; any other kind's result carries `found`, the object it stored (`{}` included), and none of the website members. Every judgment answered (operating confidence and its evidence, revenue range, exclusion) is the one the store holds, written by the last judge of the candidate (`D13-judging`, `D06-passes`); every fact (dates, categories, government match, fleet verdict and counts, website, drive minutes) is the facts row's at the read, and the brief the newest good brief result's, written by the newest result of its kind, the website and its source by the website decision over the website check's and the website search's results (`D04-store`). A campaign use answers `campaign` and `prospect_list` always, `prospect_list` the empty string for a business added one at a time, and `added`, `rejected` and `delivered` as times, each absent when the store holds none, as when a delivered or rejected event arrived before the added one, with `reason` absent unless the campaign rejected it.

A prospect list's definition answers its `filters` exactly as the store holds them: the store's own filter type encoded with its members omitted when empty, so a prospect list with no filters answers `{}`, and a stored `government` of false is present. Its `order` is never empty, since the store keeps the default order explicitly (`D04-store`). `created_by` and `changed_by` are user ids, beside `created_email` and `changed_email`. A member carries `excluded` always, since a prospect list that shows excluded candidates lists them beside the rest, and `website_source` always, the empty string included, as the facts do. A reject record carries its prospect list's name while the prospect list exists, and omits `why`, `user`, `email` and `at` in the one answer that has none: an `unreject` of a pair that has no standing reject, which writes nothing.

A kept page answers its `number`, the store's number of that copy, its `url`, the URL the fetch landed on, its `title`, absent when empty, its `size`, its `fetched` time, `by` and `email`, the user who added it, and `path`, the candidate's page path for that number; it never carries the text, which the page path serves. A candidate's `pages` are every URL it holds, the newest copy of each, newest first, `[]` when none and for a merged candidate. A member's `site_text` is always present: the time the site text kind was last written for the member, `null` when never; so is its `brief`, the time the brief kind was last written for it, a failed result's time included, `null` when never (`D14-prospect-lists`). A site text or brief result answers its stored `found` as any non-website kind does, a failed one included.

A candidate is answered whole, phone and email included: a tool's answer is product, given to a signed-in caller, and the secrecy of decision 18 governs the trail, the bus, refusals and the logs (`D09-trail`), not the answer.

## Refusals

Apart from the two paging refusals above, a refusal is an MCP error result whose text is `<word>: <sentence>`. The words are the five `D01-layout-and-run-seam` declares as `passes.Refusal*`: `not_found`, `invalid`, `busy`, `stopping` and `unavailable`. Each sentence is a copy constant, `tools.Refuse*`: one paired with each word, used unless a requirement names another, and four more for `add_page`, `RefuseNoWebsite`, `RefuseOffSite`, `RefusePageCap`, which names the cap, and `RefuseFetch`, which opens a fetch failure's text. A refusal names no id, business name, prospect list name, address, phone, email or reason the store supplied (decision 18), with two exceptions, recorded below: an `enrich` refused for a missing prompt carries the engine's text, naming the prompt and quoting prompts' refusal, and an `add_page` whose fetch failed carries the failure.

- `candidate` and `pass`: an id that is not well formed, and an unknown or pruned one, are `not_found`.
- `prospect_list` and `delete_prospect_list`: a malformed prospect list id is `not_found` without reading the store; then an unknown or deleted one is `not_found`.
- `create_prospect_list`: a name, size, filter set or order a validator refuses is `invalid`, before the store.
- `edit_prospect_list`: a passed name, size, filter set or order a validator refuses is `invalid`; then a malformed prospect list id `not_found`; both before the store; then an unknown or deleted prospect list `not_found`.
- `reject` and `unreject`: a blank `why` is `invalid`; then a malformed prospect list or candidate id `not_found`; both before the store; then what `Reject` refuses: an unknown or deleted prospect list or an unknown candidate `not_found`, a merged candidate `invalid`.
- `exclude` and `include`: a blank `why` is `invalid`; then a malformed candidate id `not_found`; both before the store; then what `store.Decide` refuses.
- `decide`: a `kind` other than `merge`, `split` and `not_same`, a blank `why`, `other` absent for `merge` or `not_same` and `links` empty for `split` are `invalid`, decided first; then a malformed `candidate` or `other` is `not_found`, without reading the store; then what `store.Decide` refuses.
- `ingest`: what `Engine.Ingest` refuses, with its word, and with the engine's text when it carries one (`D06-passes`): an unknown scraper `invalid`, no scrapes `unavailable`, a running ingest of the same scraper, or, for a full ingest, another running full ingest, `busy`, the drain `stopping`.

- `judge`: what `Engine.Judge` refuses: a running judge `busy`, the drain `stopping`.
- `enrich`: a non-empty `kind` that names no kind, both `prospect_list` and `candidate`, `force` with neither, a prompts kind (`website_search`, `site_text`, `brief`) with neither, and a target with no `kind` are `invalid`, decided first; then a malformed `prospect_list` or `candidate` is `not_found`; both without the store. Then, without a target, what `Engine.Enrich` refuses, with its word (`D06-passes`): `stopping` while draining, `unavailable` for `website_check` with no checker, `busy` while a corpus enrich runs; and with one, what `Engine.EnrichTarget` refuses, with its word and its text when it carries one: `stopping`, an unknown or deleted prospect list or an unknown candidate `not_found`, a merged candidate `invalid`, a kind its engine cannot run `unavailable` (`website_search` without a checker or without a prompts client), a caller with no prompt named for the kind `invalid` with a text naming it, and `busy` while a pass over the same target runs. `older_than_days` and any other member the arguments do not name are appkit's schema refusal.
- `add_page`: a malformed `candidate` is `not_found`, then a `url` that is not an absolute `http` or `https` URL with a host `invalid`, then no fetcher `unavailable`, all without the store; then, before fetching, an unknown candidate `not_found`, a candidate with no website, a merged one among them, `invalid` with `RefuseNoWebsite`, a URL off its website `invalid` with `RefuseOffSite`, and a twenty-first distinct URL `invalid` with `RefusePageCap`; then a fetch that fails `unavailable` with `RefuseFetch` and the failure, and one that was redirected off the website `invalid` with `RefuseOffSite`; each keeps nothing.
- `candidates`, `passes` and `prospect_lists`: the limit refusal, then the page refusal, before the store is read.
- Every tool: the database failing is `unavailable`. Malformed arguments, an unknown member in `filters` or in an order entry and a negative number in `filters` among them, are appkit's schema refusal.

## Descriptions

Every tool's description is one line, a copy constant `tools.Describe<Tool>`, which the gateway's `describe` answers as the tool's first line. `tools.Catalog()` answers each tool's name with that line, in the order `prospect_lists`, `prospect_list`, `candidates`, `candidate`, `passes`, `pass`, `ingest`, `judge`, `enrich`, `add_page`, `decide`, `exclude`, `include`, `create_prospect_list`, `edit_prospect_list`, `delete_prospect_list`, `reject`, `unreject`, the eighteen, for the tools page (`D08-pages`).

## Recorded decisions

- **Raw registration**, as scrapes: all eighteen tools are registered with appkit's `mcp.AddRawTool` and answer what `encoding/json` writes, the JSON as the one text block and as `structuredContent`; the answer types use `omitempty`, which appkit's typed `mcp.AddTool` refuses, so they carry no output schema, and their JSON is stated here. The argument types are typed: each field carries a `json:"<name>"` tag naming its property, with no `omitempty`, and each required property also `mcp:"required"`, the order entry's `field` and `direction` included; the paged tools' `Page` and `Limit` also carry a `description` tag repeating `paging.PageDescription` and `paging.LimitDescription` word for word, as `spec`'s one exception to copy living in one place allows.
- **The interim ranking tool went in one release** (cohorts decision 15): the tool, its arguments, its operating-key token and its answer types left with no expand step, since tool answers are not persisted; the ranked view of everything is a prospect list with no filters. The funnel rename follows that precedent for the `candidate` answer's members and for the tool names, and keeps only the one old tool name a sibling calls, unlisted, for one release (funnel rename decisions 6 and 17).
- **`exclude` and `include` are tools of their own; `decide` keeps three kinds** (cohorts decision 12). Both record the same rulings through the same `store.Decide`, with the same effect and the same re-judge, and answer the same `Decision` `decide` does, so nothing about a ruling changes but the tool that takes it. `decide` refuses `exclude` and `include` as `invalid`, as it refuses any kind it does not take.
- **Filters are one nested typed object; an order is a list of typed entries** (log 15). The cutoffs are pointers to `uint32`: absent is not carried, `0` is a value, and appkit's schema gives each `"minimum":0`. A probe against appkit's released `mcp` at the release candidates requires showed, for the argument types stated here: the nested `filters` and order entry objects get `additionalProperties:false`; a negative cutoff, an unknown member and an entry missing `field` or `direction` are refused by appkit's schema before the handler; `filters` and `order` as pointers reach the handler as nil when absent or null, a passed `{}` as a non-nil empty filter object, and a passed `[]` as a non-nil empty list; `size`, `name` and the nested pointer members likewise reach it as nil when null. So log 8's "absent or null keeps" holds as written for `edit_prospect_list`. Decision 9 asks for every validation to be `invalid`; the unknown filter name and the negative value are appkit's schema refusal instead, a departure from its word and not its intent (log 15): both are refused before the store, and no caller is left without an answer. Every other check of cohorts decision 9 is the handler's `invalid`.
- **`size` has no schema bound**: it is a pointer to `int`, listed as a plain integer, and a value outside 1 to 100 is the handler's `invalid`, by `store.ValidProspectListSize`, so the bound lives once, in the store's validator, which the store applies again for a direct caller (log 7).
- **The tools check with the store's validators, the store checks again** (log 7): one rule in one place, so a tool's `invalid` and the store's `ErrInvalid` cannot disagree, and a prospect list part the tool lets through never fails at the store for its shape.
- **`edit_prospect_list` screens shape before the id**, as `decide` does: a passed part the validators refuse is `invalid` whatever the id, then a malformed id is `not_found`. **`reject` and `unreject` follow log 6**: blank `why` first, then the ids' form, then the store's precedence (`D04-store`). A blank `why` or name is one with no character but white space, kept verbatim otherwise (log 7).
- **`RefuseInvalid` is reworded** to a sentence that fits any argument refused `invalid`, a ruling's, a prospect list's or a reject's (log 7). The requirement that declares it states no value, so it is re-minted to say what it now covers, and the definition of a refusal that cites it with it (log 29).
- **A member is narrower than the whole candidate answer** (log 17). Decision 17 says a member carries its whole record of judgments and facts, in the shape the interim list's entry had; the log settles that entry's fields with `fleet_verdict` in place of `fleet`, plus `known_since`, `newest_filing` and `excluded`, which is what the prospect list page's row and the dashboard's row need. - **`fleet_verdict` replaces `fleet`** on the facts and on the link (log 10): a string in `fleet` would pass a caller's truthiness check silently, a missing member fails loudly. A link a previous release stored has no verdict on its reading and answers `unknown`. The facts answer `power_units` and `drivers` beside it (log 20), absent when not known.
- **A prospect list's definition encodes the store's own filter type** (log 16), so the answered `filters` is the stored text and an agent reads back exactly the filters that apply. `created_by` and `changed_by` carry the user id, paired with the email as rulings carry `user` and `email`; creating stamps the changed fields equal to the created ones (log 24), so they are always present.
- **`unreject` of a pair with no standing reject** writes nothing and answers a record of kind `unreject` with the prospect list, its name and the candidate, and no `why`, `user`, `email` or `at` (log 27): repeating it is harmless and the answer still says the pair is not rejected.
- **`prospect_lists` pages by name, then id**, in byte order (log 18), so its token is two strings, like the other two lists'. `prospect_list` is not paged: a prospect list is at most 100 members (cohorts decision 2).
- **Effects**: the six reads are `mcp.Read`; `ingest`, `judge`, `enrich`, `add_page`, `decide`, `exclude`, `include`, `create_prospect_list`, `edit_prospect_list`, `reject` and `unreject` are `mcp.Additive`, since each adds a record, a page copy among them, or rewrites a prospect list's definition and none removes a record; `delete_prospect_list` is `mcp.Destructive`, since it removes a prospect list (prompts' precedent for a delete).
- **Every number shown is the store's**: the tools compute nothing at the call, so `tools.Config` carries no clock; the one clock a prospect list read uses is the store's own, for the filing-age and campaign-age filters (`D14-prospect-lists`). The candidate answer, the prospect list answer and the pages agree: judgments as of the last judge, facts as of the facts row.
- **A link answers its reading's common fields and keys**, so an agent can see why a row matched and what a later pass will match on, and not the estimate, evidence, known-since date, category or government match it carries, which the candidate's estimates and judgments answer.
- **`judge` follows `ingest`, and `enrich` follows `judge`,** in the catalog (website log A3, funnel rename decisions 9 and 10): the three start a pass and answer its record, so they sit together, in the funnel's order. **`add_page` follows `enrich`** (site text decision 2), since the site text pass's agent calls it, and the tools page lists eighteen.
- **`older_than_days` leaves the schema** (site text decision 12). The previous release kept it as a member only so that a caller passing it was told `invalid` by the handler; it is now an unknown member like any other, refused by appkit's schema before the handler, whatever its value, and no pass is recorded. That is the departure from cohorts decision 9's word, and not its intent, that the nested filters already make (log 15): the call is refused before anything runs and no caller is left without an answer.
- **`enrich` takes its target flat** (site text decision 12): `prospect_list` and `candidate` are two strings rather than one id whose prefix says which, so the schema tells a caller what each names, and naming both is the handler's `invalid`. `kind` is a plain string, so absent and empty are one value; without a target it still means every kind (but the prompts kinds, below) and a non-empty one that is not an element of `enrichers.Kinds()` is the handler's `invalid`, which `Enrich` and `EnrichTarget` refuse again for a direct caller. A nil checker makes only `website_check` and `website_search` `unavailable`, and a nil prompts client only `website_search`, `site_text` and `brief` (website search decision 1, S 1).
- **The tool decides the shape of a call before the engine** (site text decisions 6, 12 and 14; brief decision 10; website search decision 1): a prompts kind, `website_search`, `site_text` or `brief`, with no target, `force` with no target and a target with no `kind` are `invalid`, and a malformed target `not_found`, all without the store, so a draining engine or a failing store answers them as it answers a malformed ruling. A pass over every candidate would be thousands of paid runs by accident (site text decision 6); a targeted pass records one kind, its `enrich_kind` (site text decision 14), and walks exactly that one, so a target with no kind has nothing to walk. The engine refuses each again for a direct caller (`D06-passes`); an unknown target and a merged candidate are its refusals, since they need the store; a merged candidate is `invalid`, as `reject` refuses one, since its pages and results have moved to the survivor.
- **Departure: refusals that carry variable text** (site text decisions 2 and 7): the parent's decision 18 and this document's rule make a refusal a word and a fixed sentence. Two now carry a detail, because the caller needs it to act: the missing prompt names the prompt the caller must create and quotes prompts' refusal, as `D06-passes` builds the engine's `Refusal.Text`, which the tool answers after the word; and a fetch that failed answers `RefuseFetch` and the failure `website.Page.Error` holds (`D15-website-check`), which may quote the URL the caller gave, so the agent can try another URL. `RefusePageCap` names the cap, the one copy constant whose content a requirement constrains.
