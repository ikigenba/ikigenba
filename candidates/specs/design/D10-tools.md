# D10-tools

The seven MCP tools agents reach candidates by through the MCP gateway: `internal/tools` (decision 14). An agent reads the ranked shortlist with `shortlist`, finds candidates by name, ZIP or phone with `candidates`, reads one with its evidence, estimates, assessment and rulings with `candidate`, reads the passes with `passes` and `pass`, starts a pass over every registered scraper's current run with `rebuild`, and records one of the owner's rulings with `decide` (decision 10's tool surface). Every signed-in caller sees every candidate: the evidence and the rulings are the funnel's, not a user's, so no answer depends on who asks, and the caller a tool is handed is used only to start a rebuild as that caller (`D06-passes`) and to record who made a ruling. The pass record is `D06-passes`' (`passes.Record`), and the tools answer it as that document encodes it; the candidate, its evidence and its rulings are answered in this document's shapes.

## The tools

- `shortlist` takes optional `max_minutes` (absent: `scoring.DefaultMaxMinutes`, 120), `min_revenue` (absent: `scoring.DefaultMinRevenue`, 10,000,000), `class`, `cargo` and `page`, and answers `shortlist`, the candidates that pass decision 8's filters, ranked by score (`D13-scoring`), 25 at a time with `next` when more remain; each entry is the candidate with its display fields and tags, its drive minutes, the combined estimate, the point, and the effective confidence and score computed at the call. Read-kind.
- `candidates` takes optional `name` (a case-insensitive substring of any of its names), `zip`, `phone` (matched on the phone key) and `page`, and answers `candidates`, active candidates newest first, 25 at a time with `next`. Read-kind.
- `candidate` takes `candidate` and answers it with its links, estimates, assessment (with the freshness, effective confidence, point and score at the call) and rulings; a merged id answers the merged record with its `merged_into`, no links, no estimates, no assessment, and its rulings. Read-kind.
- `passes` takes optional `page` and answers `passes`, pass records in pass order (`D04-store`), 25 at a time with `next`; `pass` takes `pass` and answers its record. Read-kind.
- `rebuild` takes nothing and answers the pass record it started, `running`; the work lands in the background (`D06-passes`). Additive.
- `decide` takes `kind`, `why` and `candidate`, and by kind `other` (`merge`, `not_same`) or `links` (`split`, the source keys to move out); it records the ruling with the caller as its maker and answers `ruling` and `candidates`, the candidates as they stand after it, the new candidate among them for a split (`store.Decide`, `D12-resolution`). The ruling is applied and the candidates it touches re-scored in the same call, so the shortlist reflects it at once. Additive.

A **page** is a non-negative integer, `0` the first and also what an absent `page` means; page `p` holds the 25 entries that follow the first `25 × p`. `next` is the number of the page after this one, present only when at least one entry follows this page, so an agent hands `next` back as `page`.

## Answers

Times are RFC 3339 UTC to the second; a filing date is `YYYY-MM-DD`, absent when there is none. Money is whole dollars: a score and a base, which are not whole, are rounded to the nearest dollar. A confidence or a freshness is the `float64` itself. A candidate is answered whole, phone and email included: a tool's answer is product, given to a signed-in caller, and the secrecy of decision 18 governs the trail, the bus, refusals and the logs (`D09-trail`), not the answer.

## Refusals

A refusal is an MCP error result whose text is `<word>: <sentence>` (decision 14, as scrapes'). The words are the five `D01-layout-and-run-seam` declares as `passes.Refusal*`: `not_found`, `invalid`, `busy`, `stopping` and `unavailable`. Each sentence is a copy constant, `tools.Refuse*`, one per word, so a refusal names no id, business name, address, phone, email or ruling text the call or the store supplied (decision 18).

- `candidate` and `pass`: an id that is not a well-formed candidate or pass id, and an unknown or pruned one, are `not_found`.
- `rebuild`: what `Engine.Rebuild` refuses, with its word (`D06-passes`): the drain `stopping`, no `scrapes` entry in the services file at the start `unavailable`, a pass already running `busy`; anything else `unavailable`.
- `decide`: a `kind` that is none of the five, a `why` with no character but white space, `other` absent for `merge` or `not_same`, and `links` empty for `split` are `invalid`, decided first, as `store.Decide` decides a malformed ruling (`D12-resolution`); an argument the kind does not take is ignored, as `store.Decide` ignores it; then a `candidate`, or an `other` the kind takes, that is not a well-formed candidate id is `not_found`, without reading the store; then what `store.Decide` refuses: an unknown candidate `not_found`, a ruling the records forbid `invalid` (a ruling on a merged candidate, a merge of a candidate into itself, a split naming keys the candidate does not hold or all of them; `D12-resolution`).
- Every tool: the database failing is `unavailable`. Malformed arguments, a negative `page` among them, are appkit's schema refusal. A `class` or `cargo` no candidate carries answers an empty list.

## Descriptions

Every tool's description is one line, a copy constant `tools.Describe<Tool>`, which the gateway's `describe` answers as the tool's first line (Verification). `tools.Catalog()` answers each tool's name with that line, in the order `shortlist`, `candidates`, `candidate`, `passes`, `pass`, `rebuild`, `decide`, for the tools page (`D08-pages`).

## Recorded decisions

- **Raw registration**, as scrapes: all seven tools are registered with appkit's `mcp.AddRawTool` and answer what `encoding/json` writes, the JSON as the one text block and as `structuredContent`; the answer types use `omitempty`, which appkit's typed `mcp.AddTool` refuses, so they carry no output schema, and their JSON is stated here. The argument types are typed, so appkit derives the input schema and refuses malformed arguments before a handler runs: each field carries a `json:"<name>"` tag naming its property, with no `omitempty`, and each required property also `mcp:"required"`.
- **`max_minutes` and `min_revenue` are pointers to `uint32`**, so an absent or `null` member is the query's default and `0` is a value: `min_revenue` 0 lifts the revenue cutoff, which an agent reviewing near-line candidates wants. `uint32` holds every sensible cutoff (more than four billion dollars) and lets appkit's schema refuse a negative one; the conversion to the store's `int64` cannot overflow. The proof: a probe against appkit's released `mcp` derived `{"type":"integer","minimum":0}` for a `*uint32` field and handed the handler nil for an absent and a `null` member.
- **`page` is an integer from 0** (`uint32`), as scrapes', so `25 × page` always fits the store's `int64` offset.
- **`decide` screens its arguments' shape before the store**, by the rule `store.Decide` applies to a malformed ruling (`D12-resolution`), so the word is the same whichever answers: `invalid` (decision 10); an id that cannot name a candidate is `not_found`, as scrapes answers a malformed run id; `store.Decide` decides the rest, an unknown candidate `not_found` before a forbidden ruling `invalid`, so a merge on `cand_0000000000000000` with any well-formed `other` is `not_found` (Verification). `why` is kept verbatim; one of only white space is no reason.
- **`decide` answers what `store.Decide` returns**, in its order: the candidates as they stand after the ruling, which for a split includes the new candidate (decision 14); which candidates and in which order is `D12-resolution`'s.
- **A merged candidate's `candidate` answer is its record**, display fields as the store keeps them (binding decision: a merged candidate keeps the display fields it had at the merge), with `merged_into`, no links, estimates or assessment, and its rulings, which record the merge (decision 10: "its record as merged with `merged_into` and no evidence"). The page blanks the display fields as its template asks (`D08-pages`); the tool answers the record.
- **The shortlist's numbers are the store's** (`store.Ranked.Effective` and `Score`, at the store's `Now`); `candidate`'s are computed with `scoring` at `Config.Now`. In `Run` both are `Process.Now`.
- **A link answers its reading's common fields and keys**, so an agent can see why a row matched (`name_key`) and what a later pass will match on (`phone_key`, `domain_key`), and not the estimate or exclusions it carries, which the candidate's estimates and assessment answer.
- **Effects**: `shortlist`, `candidates`, `candidate`, `passes` and `pass` are `mcp.Read`; `rebuild` and `decide` are `mcp.Additive`: a ruling adds a record and never removes one (decision 10).
- **Descriptions are one line**, one sentence of at most 120 characters ending with a full stop, which appkit's `mcp` requires; **refusal sentences are constants**, so decision 18's secrecy holds by construction.

## Consumer tasks

`D03-serve`'s handler calls `tools.Register(srv, tools.Config{Passes: eng, Store: st, Now: p.Now})` once on the server `Process.MCP` made; the tools page reads `tools.Catalog()`. A model calls `shortlist` with `{}` and gets `{"shortlist":[{"candidate":{"id":"cand_…","status":"active","name":"…",…},"minutes":84,"low":7800000,"high":13000000,"confidence":0.7,"effective":0.7,"point":10400000,"score":7280000}],"next":1}`, then `{"page":1}`; `shortlist` with `{"class":"for_hire","max_minutes":30}`; `candidates` with `{"name":"trucking"}`; `candidate` with `{"candidate":"cand_…"}` and gets `{"candidate":{…},"assessment":{…},"estimates":[{"source":"fmcsa",…,"facts":{"power_units":"52",…},"formula":1}],"links":[{"scraper":"fmcsa","source_id":"…","reason":"source_key",…}],"rulings":[]}`; `decide` with `{"kind":"merge","candidate":"cand_A","other":"cand_B","why":"Same officers and phone."}` and gets `{"ruling":{"kind":"merge",…,"user":"…","email":"…"},"candidates":[…]}`; `decide` with `{"kind":"split","candidate":"cand_A","links":["fmcsa:1204457"],"why":"…"}`; `rebuild` with `{}` and gets a pass record `running`, then `pass` with `{"pass":"pass_…"}` until `done`; or it is refused `busy: <sentence>`. A test builds a store over its own database, replaces the ZIP table with `candidates.ZipDrive()`, attaches observations and re-scores them, builds an engine over a `scrapes` client on a fake's socket and captures, an `mcp.Server` from `mcp.NewServer`, registers the tools, serves the server inside `identity.Require`, calls `mcp.Client.CallTool` with JSON arguments as two callers, decodes a success's `structuredContent`, compares it and the text block with `json.Marshal` of the expected `tools.ShortlistPage`, `tools.CandidateList`, `tools.Detail`, `tools.PassList`, `tools.Decision` or `passes.Record`, and compares a refusal's text with the word, `": "` and the `tools.Refuse*` constant.
