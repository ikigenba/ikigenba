# D12-ingest

Ingest: how rows become candidates and how the owner's rulings move them (decisions 4, 5, 6 and 10, form5500 decisions 14, 15 and 21, OSHA ITA outcomes 6, 10 and 12 and OSHA ITA P2 and P11, and estimate inversion outcomes 1, 3 and 4 and estimate inversion log D1). It owns the normalized keys a reading carries (`sources.NameKey`, `sources.NameKeys`, `sources.EINKey`, `sources.PhoneKey`, `sources.DomainKey`, `sources.County`, `sources.SourceKey`), the **match key**, a candidate's **display** (the fields and tags it shows, from its freshest evidence that gives each), and the three store writes that change which candidate a row belongs to: `store.Attach` (the attach rule), `store.MarkGone` (a row absent from its scraper's latest run) and `store.Decide` (the five rulings, made through the `decide`, `exclude` and `include` tools), with what a `merge` or a `split` does to the enrichment results, facts rows, pages and campaign uses of the candidates it touches: it re-runs the derived kinds for them, and a `merge` moves the other's pages to the survivor while a `split` leaves them where they are (site text decision 1), and a `merge` moves the other's campaign uses to the survivor, combining two uses of one campaign into one, while a `split`, an `exclude` and an `include` leave them where they are (campaigns decisions 3 and 17, campaigns log D1). The records and their shapes, the enrichment results, the facts row, the pages and the campaign uses (`store.CampaignUse`, recorded from campaigns' events by `store.AddCampaignUse`, `store.RejectCampaignUse` and `store.DeliverCampaignUse` and read by `store.CampaignUses`) are `D04-store`'s; what each enricher names as its inputs and computes is `D16-enrichers`'; what a source reads from a row is `D05-sources`'; what a judge writes, and the effect of `exclude` and `include` on the judgments, are `D13-judging`'s; the engine that calls `Attach` and `MarkGone` is `D06-passes`'; the `decide`, `exclude` and `include` tools are `D10-tools`'; which candidates a prospect list holds, an excluded one included, is `D14-prospect-lists`'. "Enrichers decision N" is decision N of the decisions document that delivered the enrichers; "site text decision N" is decision N of the one that delivered the `site_text` kind and the pages; "brief decision N" is decision N of the one that delivered the `brief` kind; "category evidence decision N" is decision N of the one that made a company's good brief decide its categories; "website search decision N" is decision N of the one that delivered the `website_search` kind, and "S N" is entry DN of that delivery's decisions log; "website states outcome N" is outcome N of the one that made `none` mean that a search found nothing; "form5500 decision N" is candidates decision N of the decisions document that delivered the `form5500` source; "OSHA ITA outcome N" is outcome N of the decisions document that delivered the `osha_ita` source, and "OSHA ITA P N" is entry P N of that delivery's decisions log; "estimate inversion outcome N" is outcome N of the decisions document of the estimate-inversion change, which brings rules version 10, and "estimate inversion log DN" is entry DN of that change's decisions log.

## Candidates

A candidate is a record with an opaque, permanent id, `cand_` and 16 lowercase hex digits drawn from the random source when it is created, never derived from a name, an address or a DOT number (`D04-store` mints it). It is `active` or `merged`, with the id it was merged into; it is never deleted, its id never changes, and a merged candidate never becomes active again. The valid set is every candidate not merged; excluded is a flag on the judgments, so an excluded candidate is still valid (`D13-judging`).

An active candidate shows its **display**. Its name and address (street, city, state, ZIP and county) are its **display link**'s, its freshest evidence, which is the first of its links in the order `store.Links` answers them: not gone first, then newest filing date, then newest last seen (decision 4), so a candidate whose every link is gone shows its freshest gone link. Its phone, email, people and class tag each come from the first of its **giving links** that gives a value for it: the giving links are its links not gone, in that order, or all of its links when every one is gone. So a link that says nothing about a field never blanks it: a PPP row, which carries no phone, email, people, class or cargo, can lead a candidate's name and address without hiding the carrier's phone or its `for_hire` class. Its cargo tags are, as before, the union of its links' cargo tags, gone ones included. Its `names` are every name any of its links uses, each link's name before its other names, the display name first. Every write that changes a candidate's links leaves its display current, and `updated` moves only when a field of the candidate changed. A merged candidate keeps the display fields and tags it had at the merge; only its links move.

## Keys

The **name key** is the name upper-cased, punctuation stripped, white space collapsed, and the legal suffixes `INC`, `LLC`, `CO`, `CORP`, `LTD` and `COMPANY` dropped from the end. A reading carries one name key per name it carries (form5500 decision 14): `sources.NameKeys` keys each of the reading's names in order, dropping an empty key and a repeat, so its first is usually the reading's `NameKey` (a link holds both its `NameKey` and its `NameKeys`, so matching tries either), and an FMCSA reading carries its DBA's key beside its legal name's, a PPP reading its one key, a Form 5500 reading the keys of its sponsor name, its DBA and its plan-derived name, and an OSHA ITA reading the key of its company name alone, or of its establishment name when the company name is empty; the establishment name is not a name key (OSHA ITA outcomes 6 and 10, OSHA ITA P11, `D05-sources`). The **EIN key** is the employer identification number when it is exactly nine ASCII digits and not all zeros, and none otherwise; `form5500` and `osha_ita` fill it. An OSHA ITA row that gives its EIN as eight digits, the leading zero dropped, is read as nine with a leading zero before it is keyed, so `10739420` keys as `010739420` (OSHA ITA outcome 10); that padding is the source's own (`D05-sources`), and `sources.EINKey` is the same for every source. The **match key** is the name key with the row's state and five-digit ZIP; a reading without all three has none, so a row with no name or no ZIP never joins another by name (under-merging is the intended failure). A row's **place** is its state and five-digit ZIP. The name keys a row **tries** are its `NameKeys`, or its `NameKey` alone when it has none; the name keys a link **holds** are its reading's `NameKey` together with its reading's `NameKeys`, so a link an earlier release wrote, whose reading has no `NameKeys`, still holds its `NameKey`; the EIN key a link holds is its reading's. City and street are evidence, not key. The phone key (digits, last ten), the domain key (the email's domain, lower-cased, none for a free provider) and the county (`MN-115`) are stored for later use and take no part in matching this pass. The **source key** is `<scraper>:<source_id>`, how a split names the links it moves.

## The attach rule

`Attach` takes a batch of observations, rows as their source read them, and applies each in order, in one transaction (decision 5):

1. A link already holding the row's source key keeps the row on its candidate, whatever its keys now say. The row is **unchanged** when its digest is unchanged, or, when the attach is forced, as a full ingest forces it, when its new reading is byte for byte the reading the link stores (OSHA ITA P15): last seen moves, the run is recorded, a gone link returns, and the link's reading, digest and keys are not rewritten. Otherwise it is **changed**: the link takes the new reading and digest as well, and the keys that reading carries.
2. Otherwise the row is **new**, and it joins a candidate by one of its keys (form5500 decision 15):
   1. by EIN: when it has an EIN key and an active candidate one of whose links, gone or not, holds the same EIN key, the oldest such candidate takes it, by `ein` with confidence 0.9, whatever its name keys and place say;
   2. otherwise by name: when it has a place, for each name key it tries, in order, when an active candidate one of whose links, gone or not, holds that name key with the same state and ZIP exists, the oldest such candidate takes it, by `match_key` with confidence 0.8, the first name key that finds one deciding.

   A `not_same` ruling is honored at both: a row whose EIN key, or whose deciding name key, links of both of a ruled pair hold goes to the older, and the pair stays two candidates.
3. Otherwise a candidate is created for it, and the link that founds it attaches by `source_key` with confidence 1.0.

Each step reads by key, never the whole of the links (`D04-store`): the first step finds the row's link by its source key, the second the oldest active candidate holding the row's EIN key, through the links' index on EIN key, and otherwise, name key by name key, the oldest holding that name key at the row's place, through the links' name keys and their index on name key (`D04-store`); and the display of each candidate one of whose links the batch wrote is re-derived from that candidate's own links once, at the end of the batch, those links read in one query by the candidate's id and folded as read, so the read is bounded by the one candidate, the largest on the `us` space holding 3,224 links (`D04-store`, `shape/`'s Bounded work). Identical keys collapse at creation, since a later row of the same batch meets the candidate an earlier one created; so there are never more candidates than rows. A link moved by a ruling attaches by `decision` with confidence 1.0, and a link keeps how it came to its candidate until a ruling moves it. Matching reads only the links: it never calls scrapes. A batch the database answers busy, as when litestream's checkpoint holds the write lock, is tried again by the store after a pause, a few times, and a failed try changes nothing (`D04-store`); only when the tries are spent does `Attach` fail.

`MarkGone` marks gone every link the run a pass just read no longer holds, a page at a time (`D04-store`, `shape/`'s Bounded work): each call reads at most `n` of its scraper's links after its cursor, in source id order, marks those of them last seen in a run other than its run and not already gone, leaves the others of its page as they were, and answers the source id of the last link of its page as the next cursor, empty once none remain, all in one transaction; the caller walks it from an empty cursor with `passes.BatchSize` until the cursor comes back empty, so no statement, transaction or open cursor spans the scraper's links. A page may mark nothing, and its cursor may be a link it did not mark. A gone link is kept, with its last seen; it still counts for identity and no longer for the judgments (decision 6). In the same transaction `MarkGone` re-derives by key, from their own links, read in one query per candidate and folded as read, the display of the candidates holding a link the call marked, bounded by those candidates, since a link that is gone no longer leads the display or gives a field of it. It no longer answers those candidates' ids: they are the candidates holding a link it marked, which a caller that needs them reads by key. A row that returns is unmarked by `Attach`.

## Rulings

A ruling is made only by hand, through the `decide`, `exclude` and `include` tools (`D10-tools`), each of which calls `Decide`; nothing in the pipeline rules on its own. Each records its kind, the candidates involved, who, when and why, and is never changed or removed (decision 10).

- `merge` names the survivor and the other: the other's links, its enrichment results of every kind and its pages move to the survivor (`D04-store`), each page keeping its number and every other field, and its campaign uses move to the survivor in the same write, a campaign both hold becoming one use that keeps the earliest added time with its prospect list, the earliest rejection with its reason and the latest delivery; its facts row is dropped; the derived kinds are then run again for the survivor, from its links and display after the move, its facts row is rewritten from its newest results, and it is judged again; the other is marked `merged` into it and keeps everything else it had at the merge (display fields, tags, estimates and its last judgments).
- `split` names a candidate and the source keys to move out, a proper subset of its links: a new candidate is created for them, and the pair is recorded `not_same` in the same call, so matching never rejoins them. Its enrichment results, its pages and its campaign uses stay on it, so a fetched result such as the website check's, and every page an agent added, stays with the original and the new candidate has none until an enrich finds it due or an agent adds one; the derived kinds are then run again for both, from each one's own links and display, both facts rows are rewritten, and both are judged.
- `not_same` names two active candidates and changes nothing else.
- `exclude` and `include` name one candidate; the latest of the two on it stands (`D13-judging`). Each judges that candidate again, records no enrichment result and leaves its campaign uses as they were. An excluded candidate leaves every prospect list, unless a prospect list shows excluded candidates, and an `include` returns it (the exclusion rule, `D14-prospect-lists`).

A ruling on a merged candidate, a merge or `not_same` of a candidate with itself, a split naming keys the candidate does not hold or all of them, are refused `ErrInvalid`. Rulings are applied throughout and never overwritten: a pass or a full ingest moves no link a ruling moved, revives no merged candidate and changes no ruling, since the attach rule keeps every row on the candidate its link names. `Decide` judges the candidates it touches in the same call (`D13-judging`), so a ruling shows in every prospect list at once; nothing else in this document judges. The candidates it touches are those it answers: `r.Candidate`, `r.Other` where the kind takes one, and a split's new candidate. A `merge` or a `split` moves links, so it re-derives the display of the active candidates it answers, records one new result of each derived kind for each of them whose inputs are not empty (`D16-enrichers`), rewrites their facts rows and then judges them, all in the one write; an `exclude` or an `include` moves none, records no result, writes no facts row and judges `r.Candidate` alone; a `not_same` judges none and records nothing but its ruling (`D13-judging`). It reads and writes each candidate it touches by key (its record, its own links, its rulings, its enrichment results, its facts row, and the ZIP table's minutes for its display ZIP, which the drive-minutes kind reads), so its work is bounded by those candidates' links and results, each candidate's own links read in one query and folded as read, the largest candidate on the `us` space holding 3,224 links; it reads no table whole and leaves every other candidate as it was.

## Recorded decisions

- **Punctuation is removed, not spaced**: every rune that is not a letter, a digit or white space is dropped, so `J.B. HUNT` keys as `JB HUNT` and `SMITH-JONES` as `SMITHJONES`; a variant spelled with spaces then misses, which under-merging accepts. Suffixes drop repeatedly from the end while more than one word is left, so `ACME CO INC` keys as `ACME` and a business named `COMPANY` keeps its name.
- **A match key needs all three parts**, a non-empty name key, a state and a ZIP of five ASCII digits; the document's "name plus place" is read as all of the place. An empty name key never matches, as before: a row tries the non-empty elements of its `NameKeys` when it has one, or else its `NameKey` when that is not empty, which for every reading a source builds is the same as trying its `NameKeys`, or its `NameKey` when `NameKeys` is empty, since `sources.NameKeys` never answers an empty key.
- **The EIN step comes first and needs no place** (form5500 decision 15): one EIN is one employer, so two filings of one sponsor join after a move, a rename or across plans, whatever their ZIPs; 0.9 records that an EIN is stronger evidence than a name at a place (0.8) and weaker than the row's own source key (1.0). A row with an EIN key no link holds falls through to the name step, so a Form 5500 or OSHA ITA row still joins an FMCSA or PPP candidate, which hold no EIN key, by name, and an OSHA ITA row whose EIN a Form 5500 link holds joins that sponsor's candidate by EIN, whatever its site's name and ZIP.
- **A row tries its own keys; a link holds all of its keys.** The row's side is its `NameKeys` in order, so its first key, the legal or sponsor name, is tried before a DBA or a plan-derived name, and the first that finds a candidate decides even when a later one would find an older candidate; the link's side is every key its reading carries, its `NameKey` included, so a link written before a reading carried `NameKeys` still matches by its first key. Both sides keep "gone or not" at the name step as at the EIN step, as the match-key step had it: a gone link still counts for identity (decision 6).
- **Rules version 7 and the links that exist** (form5500 decision 21): every source's reading gained keys and a source joined, so `sources.Rules` becomes 7 and the next full ingest rereads every row and, attaching with force, rewrites every link's reading and keys, since every reading changed. Until that ingest runs on a space, a link written before carries an empty EIN key and no name key beyond its `NameKey`, so the new steps find nothing extra through it and the name step finds exactly what the match-key step did; that is safe, as under-merging is the intended failure. A link's stored keys are what `D04-store` keeps.
- **Several sites of one EIN are one candidate, with one link per row** (OSHA ITA outcomes 10 and 12): an OSHA ITA row is one establishment's summary for one year, with a source key of its own, so each row is its own link, and a site whose EIN key a link already holds joins that link's candidate at the EIN step, whatever its name and place say. So KTM Paving's rows across three establishment ids, with its EIN written in eight digits in some years and nine in others, are one candidate, its 2017 row, which carries no EIN since no row of 2016 to 2018 carries one, joining it by its name at its ZIP instead, and Design Ready Controls' sites under its main EIN, at different names and ZIPs, are one candidate with one link per site and per year; summing the sites' counts is the judge's (`D13-judging`), and the links stay one per row so the card shows each site. A site that files under another EIN meets by EIN only the links holding that EIN, and by name only those at its own place, so Design Ready Controls' Champlin site, under a second EIN, is its own candidate until the owner merges it (recorded, not fixed).
- **An EIN-less site matches by its company name at its place** (OSHA ITA outcome 10): a row without an EIN key, every row of 2016 to 2018 and about a tenth since, tries its name, the company name, at its state and ZIP as every row does, so a business that never gives an EIN joins across years only where its name and place stay the same. Molin Concrete is one candidate at its Lino Lakes ZIP, its rows joining by company name; its Ramsey site, at another ZIP through 2021, is a second candidate at that ZIP, which is accepted (recorded, not fixed): under-merging is the intended failure, and the owner can merge it.
- **The establishment id is not a key** (OSHA ITA outcome 10): it changes for a fifth to a quarter of employers each year and one business can hold three, so it is a fact the row keeps (`D05-sources`) and no step of the attach rule reads it. Joining EIN-less rows across years by establishment id, where their names and place did not join them, is deferred.
- **A site's name is not a name key** (OSHA ITA outcomes 6 and 10, OSHA ITA P11): an OSHA ITA reading's names hold only the row's name, the company name, so its company name alone keys it; the establishment name keys only when it is the row's name because the company name is empty, and otherwise it is shown, not matched (`Reading.Establishment`, `D05-sources`). scrapes publishes a row's names as its name then the establishment name when it differs, and this design first keyed both, accepting the risk as form5500 accepted plan-derived names. It diverges because the sandbox check found place-like site names keying unrelated businesses into one candidate at a ZIP: Molin Concrete's five `Ramsey` rows joined Forpak Automation's candidate with Multisource, Life Fitness, Cybex and others through names such as `Ramsey` and `Delavan`, and 795 candidates held OSHA ITA links under two or more EINs.
- **Rules version 8** (OSHA ITA P2): `osha_ita` joins the sources and adds evidence the judge reads, so `sources.Rules` becomes 8, and a full ingest rereads every row under one version and, attaching with force, rewrites every link whose reading the new rules change. No key rule of an existing source changes, and every row a link already holds keeps its candidate by its source key, so that ingest moves no link; on a space it runs before the first `osha_ita` run is ingested (`D11-on-a-space`).
- **Rules version 10** (estimate inversion outcomes 1, 3 and 4, estimate inversion log D1): FMCSA's reading stops giving a mixed carrier a truck estimate and the judge's combination changes (`D05-sources`, `D13-judging`), so `sources.Rules` becomes 10, and a full ingest rereads every row under one version and, attaching with force, rewrites every link whose reading the new rules change, so the mixed carriers' links lose their estimate, then records a fresh result of every derived kind and judges every candidate under the new combination. No key rule changes, and every row a link already holds keeps its candidate by its source key, so that ingest moves no link. A judge alone would not do: it reads the links as stored, so it would keep the mixed truck estimates on them.
- **The free-provider list** is exact domains, lower-cased: `aol.com`, `arvig.net`, `att.net`, `bellsouth.net`, `centurylink.net`, `centurytel.net`, `charter.net`, `comcast.net`, `earthlink.net`, `embarqmail.com`, `frontier.com`, `frontiernet.net`, `gmail.com`, `gmx.com`, `googlemail.com`, `hotmail.com`, `hughes.net`, `icloud.com`, `live.com`, `mac.com`, `mail.com`, `mchsi.com`, `me.com`, `midco.net`, `msn.com`, `outlook.com`, `protonmail.com`, `q.com`, `qwest.net`, `rocketmail.com`, `sbcglobal.net`, `tds.net`, `usfamily.net`, `verizon.net`, `windstream.net`, `yahoo.com` and `ymail.com`: the document's providers with their sister domains and the residential ISPs of Minnesota and Wisconsin.
- **The phone key keeps fewer than ten digits** when that is all there is: the document says digits only, last ten, and names no floor.
- **A link keeps its reason** when a later pass re-attaches it by source key: a founding link is `source_key`, a key match stays `match_key` at 0.8 and an EIN match `ein` at 0.9 until a ruling moves it, and a moved link stays `decision`. Rewriting a key match as `source_key` at the next pass would erase the uncertainty the confidence records and the trace of a ruling.
- **`linked` counts every changed row and every new row that joined a candidate**, so `created + linked = new + changed` on every batch; the first pass has `created + linked = rows`, a full ingest `linked` the rows whose readings it changed and `unchanged` the rest.
- **An unchanged row keeps its stored reading**: outside a full ingest its digest says the row is the same, and a change of rules is a full ingest, which rereads every row and rewrites each link whose reading the new rules change (decision 11).
- **A forced row whose reading did not change is unchanged** (OSHA ITA P15): a full ingest still reads every row and compares, but a row whose new reading encodes to exactly the bytes its link stores, the JSON the store keeps, member names included, touches only its link's run and last seen (and returns a gone link), rewrites neither the reading nor the keys, and counts `unchanged`; a row whose reading differs is `changed` and rewritten, as before. Earlier, a forced attach rewrote every link and its key rows whether or not its reading changed, and counted every row `changed`; on `us`, under a 256 MiB memory cap that stays, that was most of the some 23 GB a full ingest wrote, and the full ingest failed `store` after every row was read. The comparison is with the stored bytes, not the decoded reading, so a link an earlier release wrote, whose stored reading lacks members this release's reading has, is changed and rewritten once. The digest is left as it was: a row whose digest moved but whose reading did not keeps its old digest, so the next scraper ingest of it finds it changed and rewrites it once, which costs one row. The candidates a call founds, the touched ids it answers and the display re-derived at the end of the batch are as before, so a forced call still touches, and re-derives the display of, every candidate one of whose links it holds; R-7DIN-CZDO, the unforced unchanged row, keeps its text, and the forced one is a requirement of its own.
- **Every candidate a call writes a link of is touched**, the unchanged ones included; `Attach` answers their ids, and `MarkGone`'s are the candidates holding a link it marked, which it no longer answers (`D04-store`). An ingest pass judges nothing (`D06-passes`): the daily judge, the judge an enrich that wrote anything starts, a full ingest or a ruling brings their judgments up to date.
- **A display link exists for every active candidate**: one is created with a link, a merge leaves the survivor all of both, and a split moves a proper subset; so the name and address are always one link's. When every link is gone, the freshest gone link shows.
- **A link that gives no value does not blank one** (root ruling, intent decision 20): a PPP row "adds no phone, email or people", yet a PPP link approved after a carrier's last filing leads the `Links` order, and as the display link it would blank the candidate's phone, email, people and class, so the class and cargo filters and the phone search would lose a candidate whose newest evidence is a loan. Phone, email, people and class therefore each come from the first giving link that gives a value; name and address stay the display link's, so the shown place is always one row's whole address and the drive minutes read its ZIP. Matching is unchanged. Each of those fields is one link's, never a merge of several, since the class is one carrier's registration. Cargo stays the union over every link, gone ones included, as decision 4 says "its links" and as the previous release had it: a PPP link has no cargo, so the union already keeps it, and this is the smallest change. The giving links are those not gone, as a gone row no longer counts (decision 6), except when every link is gone: then they are all of them, so such a candidate shows what its rows last said rather than nothing.
- **A site can lead the display** (accepted, coordinator ruling): an OSHA ITA link is one establishment's row, so when it has the newest filing date among the links not gone it is the display link, and a candidate with several sites shows that site's name and address, which may not be the business's main one; the display rule is unchanged for it, as for every source.
- **A merged candidate keeps everything it had at the merge but its links, its enrichment results, its facts row and its pages** (coordinator ruling, and site text decision 1 for the pages): its display fields, tags, estimates and last judgments stay as they were, never judged again, so its judgments stay as the last judge wrote them, and it answers no facts, since its facts row is deleted (`D04-store`, `D13-judging`), so a result recorded on it after its last judge and before the merge shows on the survivor, not on it; and no prospect list holds it, since a prospect list's members are active candidates (`D14-prospect-lists`); its links, its results of every kind, its `brief` results among them, and its pages move (`D04-store`) and its facts row is deleted, one row per active candidate (enrichers decision 5), so "no evidence" is no links and no results. Chains of merges are left as they are, each record naming the id it merged into.
- **A split writes two rulings**: the `split` (naming the new candidate as its other, with the keys it moved) and then a `not_same` between the pair, with the same who, when and why; `Decide` answers the `split`. The sandbox check sees "a new candidate and a `not_same` ruling".
- **`Decide` refuses in this order**: a context done and a failing store (`D04-store`); a ruling with no user id (`ErrUnreachable`, a value no consumer sends, since the tool always has a caller); a malformed ruling (`ErrInvalid`: an unknown kind, a why that is blank, a merge or `not_same` with no other, a split with no keys); a candidate or other the store does not hold (`ErrNotFound`); a ruling the records forbid (`ErrInvalid`); then a random source that fails while minting a split's candidate. A field the kind does not take is ignored.
- **`include` needs no earlier `exclude`**: the latest of the two stands, so an `include` on a candidate never excluded changes nothing shown. The rules no longer exclude (`D13-judging`): a government name pattern is a fact on the judgments, so only a ruling hides a candidate.
- **`Decide` keeps all five kinds while the tools divide them** (cohorts decision 12): `decide` takes `merge`, `split` and `not_same`, and `exclude` and `include` are tools of their own (`D10-tools`), but each records the same ruling through the same `Decide` call, with the same effect and history, so the store method and its requirements are unchanged.
- **A merge of a pair ruled `not_same` is not refused**: the owner may change their mind, and the merge is the later ruling.
- **A ruling touches only the candidates it names** (orchestrator ruling, after the sandbox found one ruling taking eight minutes over 43,058 candidates and 53,787 links while the MCP gateway gives up at 50 seconds, so no ruling was saved): the display postcondition had covered every active candidate after any `Attach`, `MarkGone` or `Decide`, and the build met it for `Decide` by re-deriving every candidate. It now covers the candidates each call touches, which for `Attach` is those it answers, the candidates whose links the batch wrote, and for `MarkGone` those holding a link the call marked, which it no longer answers now that it works a page at a time (`D04-store`); for `Decide` it covers only a `merge` or a `split`, the kinds that move links, since a `not_same`, an `exclude` or an `include` changes no candidate (R-RGR7-9XKL). A `merge` or a `split` re-runs the derived kinds for and judges the active candidates it answers, an `exclude` or an `include` judges `r.Candidate` and a `not_same` judges none, as R-NKJR-IPPN (`D13-judging`) states; no ruling records a result on, or writes the facts row of, a candidate it does not answer. Writes keep every display current by keeping each candidate they touch current, so states reached by use are unchanged. A global refresh cannot be told from a bounded one by timing in a test, so the bounded work is required by what it leaves alone: a candidate the ruling did not touch keeps its record and `Updated` even when a `DB.Write` left its stored name differing from its display link, the one state a refresh would visibly repair; its estimates and judgments are `D13-judging`'s, already confined to what `Decide` answers.
- **An enrichment result races a merge as a link does**: `AddEnrichments`, `Derive` and `FillFacts` are among the store's writes that behave as one after the other; `AddEnrichments` skips a candidate already merged and `Derive` and `FillFacts` examine only active ones, so a result for the other of a concurrent `merge` is moved to the survivor or recorded nowhere, no result names a merged candidate, and no merged candidate has a facts row (R-MUPS-FJX3). A derived result moved to the survivor is never its newest of the kind, since the merge records a newer one from the survivor's own subject.
- **A merge or a split re-runs the derived kinds forced** (enrichers decision 15, and the root's answer that a ruling still judges, since rulings are otherwise unchanged by decision 16 and the judge now reads the facts row): a merge or a split changes the links, and often the display, of every active candidate it answers, so their link facts and drive minutes are out of date at once; the ruling records one new result of each derived kind for each of them in its own write, before it judges them, whether or not the inputs differ from its newest result's, so that the judge reads facts from the links as the ruling left them. Choosing "due by inputs" instead would leave the survivor's newest result one moved from the other whenever that one happened to be recorded later and its inputs happened to equal the survivor's. A derived kind whose inputs are empty records nothing, as everywhere (`D16-enrichers`). A fetched kind is never run by a ruling: a ruling is a person's call and must answer at once, without reaching outside; a split's new candidate is checked by the next enrich that finds it due.
- **Pages follow a merge and stay through a split** (site text decision 1): a page is data on a company, like a link, so a merge moves every page of the other to the survivor, whose company it now is, and a split, which cannot tell which company a page was read for, leaves every page with the original, as it leaves the fetched results. A moved page keeps its number, since the number names the copy among every page the store holds (`D04-store`), so its text page's path is found on the survivor and nowhere else; it keeps its URL, text, size, fetch time and who added it, as nothing was fetched again. Two copies of one URL, one from each candidate, are both kept, and the one with the greater number is the one read, as for any two copies.
- **Brief results follow a merge and stay through a split, with the pages** (brief decision 15 and the decision document's Verification): a `brief` result is an enrichment result like any other, so a merge moves the other's `brief` results to the survivor with its pages, and the survivor's facts row is re-projected (`D04-store`), its `brief` text included, from the newest `brief` result of either company for which the kind's `Failed` is false; a split leaves every `brief` result with the original, beside its pages, so the original's facts row keeps its `brief` text and the new candidate's has none. A ruling runs no `brief`, since `brief` is a fetched kind (`D16-enrichers`): the survivor gets a new one only from a targeted `brief` pass, which runs it when it is due for the kind (R-OO40-CCZA, `D04-store`), its newest `brief` result being the one recorded last on either company (R-H5QH-WBC8, `D04-store`), or, with `force`, whenever it holds a page (R-08ON-9XFA, `D06-passes`), and the split's new candidate, holding no pages, has empty inputs for the kind until it holds a page. Rulings are otherwise unchanged (brief decision 15): the merge's move and re-projection are stated over results of every kind and the facts projection.
- **A brief's classification follows its result through a merge and stays through a split** (category evidence decision 1 and the decision document's Verification): the facts projection takes a candidate's categories from its newest good brief when that brief gives at least one, and otherwise from its newest `link_facts` result, never mixing the two (`D04-store`), so a survivor whose other company carried the good brief, holding that result after the merge, is classified by it: its facts row's categories are exactly the brief's, each with source `brief`, and `store.ClassifiedBy` of them answers `brief`, whatever the `link_facts` result the merge's forced derivation recomputes from both companies' links gives. A split leaves the brief with the original, so the original stays classified by it, and the new candidate, holding no `brief` result, takes its categories from its own `link_facts` result or has none. No ruling changes for this; R-LD70-4W25 is re-minted to cite the projection by its document, since `D04-store` re-mints it, and to carry a brief's classification through a merge and a split in its example.
- **Website search results follow a merge and stay through a split** (website search decisions 7 and 13, S 4): a `website_search` result is an enrichment result like any other, so a merge moves the other's `website_search` results to the survivor, and the survivor's facts row is re-projected (`D04-store`): its `Website`, `WebsiteURL` and `WebsiteSource` are the website decision over the survivor's results after the move, so a site the newest `website_check` result of either company found decides first, its source `store.WebsiteByCheck`, otherwise the newest `website_search` result of either company that is not failed and whose confirmation loaded over https or http, its source then `store.WebsiteBySearch`; otherwise the newest `website_search` result of either company that is not failed and named no URL, or one that did not load, which makes the website `none` with no source; and otherwise the website is `unknown`, a check that found nothing included (website states outcomes 1 to 3). A split leaves every `website_search` result with the original, as it leaves the check's, so the original's facts row keeps its website and its source and the new candidate's reads `unknown`, with no source, until an enrich records a result that gives one. A ruling runs no `website_search`, since it is a fetched kind (`D16-enrichers`): the survivor and a split's new candidate are searched only by a targeted `website_search` pass that finds them due for the kind (R-OO40-CCZA, `D04-store`) or forces it. Rulings are otherwise unchanged (website search decision 13), so no requirement here changes for the kind: the move and the re-projection are already stated over results of every kind and the facts projection (R-LD70-4W25). A merged candidate has no facts row, so it answers no facts at all, and its judgments are what its last judge wrote (R-PWRA-LW9H, R-T3DU-KBYR in `D04-store`).
- **Campaign uses follow a merge** (campaigns decisions 3 and 17, campaigns log D1): a candidate's id never changes and a business is in a campaign once, so campaigns keeps the id it was given and candidates follows the merge pointer when it records a campaign event, and the history lands on the survivor. A merge moves the other's uses in the same write as its links, so after a merge this release records `CampaignUses` of the merged candidate answers none and of the survivor every use of both (a merge the previous release recorded leaves the uses under the merged id, `D04-store`'s Known limits); a campaign both held, each having been added to it as its own business, becomes one use, as the business is in that campaign once: the earliest non-zero added time with the prospect list that came with it, so the prospect list age filter (`D14-prospect-lists`) counts from the first use; the earliest non-zero rejection with its reason, as a reject is final (campaigns decision 5) and the store keeps the first; and the latest delivery, as `Delivered` is the last one. A tie keeps the survivor's. Two uses of different campaigns stay two.
- **A use races a merge as a link does**: the store's campaign writes are among the writes that behave as one after the other, and each follows `MergedInto` to the active candidate at the end of the chain, so an event naming the other of a concurrent `merge` is recorded on the other and moved, or recorded on the survivor; either way it lands on the survivor, combined as above, and no use names a candidate this release merged.
- **A refused or failed merge moves no use**: a refusal changes no record (R-GMHK-CFT8), and a failing store changes no record (R-OD8H-2EDG, `D04-store`), so no requirement is added for it; the uses are records like the links.
- **A split, an `exclude`, an `include` and a `not_same` leave every use where it is** (campaigns decision 17): a use is a fact about a business campaigns contacted, and a split cannot tell which of the two companies campaigns meant, as with the pages, so the original keeps them and the new candidate has none; an `exclude` or an `include` changes no campaign history, and a campaign reject is campaigns' own and leaves the owner's exclusion untouched (campaigns log D4).
- **The cap is not re-checked on a merge** (decided for this delivery, recorded in `D04-store`): a merge moves every page of the other whatever the survivor holds, so a survivor may hold more than `PageCap` distinct URLs; refusing or trimming a merge for its pages would make the owner's ruling wait on an agent's crawl, and pages are never deleted. Only `AddPage` enforces the cap, so such a survivor then accepts a newer copy of a URL it holds and no new URL.
- **`exclude`, `include` and `not_same` write no enrichment** (enrichers decision 16 and its verification list): they change no link and no display, so no derived kind's inputs change; `exclude` and `include` judge their candidate again, which reads the facts row as it stands.
- **`MarkGone` works a page at a time** (decisions log P17, `shape/`'s Bounded work): it takes a cursor and an `n`, examines at most `n` of its scraper's links after the cursor in source id order, marks those last seen in another run and not gone, re-derives the display of the candidates holding one it marked, and answers its cursor, in one transaction per call; the caller walks it with `passes.BatchSize`. Earlier version: one bounded statement over all of the scraper's links per call, answering the ids of every candidate it touched. Why: the scraper's links grow with the space, so one statement over them, and a slice of every candidate they touch, grows with it too, where only disk and wall time may. A page may examine links it does not mark, so its cursor may be one of them and a page may mark none (R-KUDX-LYIQ). The display postcondition now names the candidates holding a link the call marked rather than an answered slice (R-HYGP-I8LU), and a walk marks exactly what one call over the whole scraper did (R-RPQ4-8ONN). The display re-derivation and a ruling's work each read a candidate's own links in one query, folded as read, bounded by the one candidate (3,224 links at most on `us`).

## Consumer tasks

The engine (`D06-passes`) attaches each batch with `n, _, err := st.Attach(ctx, run, force, batch)`, summing its counts and keeping no id beyond the batch in hand, since a pass holds no set that grows with the space (`shape/`'s Bounded work), and, once the stream has ended, marks the absent rows by walking `after, g, err = st.MarkGone(ctx, "fmcsa", run, after, passes.BatchSize)` from an empty `after` until it answers an empty one, summing each `g.Gone`; an ingest pass judges nothing. A judge pass reads its judge time `at` once and walks `after, c, err = st.JudgeAll(ctx, after, passes.BatchSize, at)` the same way, summing `c.Judged` and `c.Unranged`, and a full ingest does the same after its ingests (`D13-judging`). The `decide` tool (`D10-tools`) calls `r, cs, err := st.Decide(ctx, store.Ruling{Kind: store.KindSplit, Candidate: a, Keys: []string{sources.SourceKey("fmcsa", id)}, Why: why, UserID: c.UserID, UserEmail: c.Email})` and answers `r` with `cs[1]`, the new candidate; `store.ErrNotFound` is `not_found` and `store.ErrInvalid` `invalid`. The `exclude` tool calls `r, cs, err := st.Decide(ctx, store.Ruling{Kind: store.KindExclude, Candidate: a, Why: why, UserID: c.UserID, UserEmail: c.Email})`, and `include` the same with `store.KindInclude`, each answering `r` with `cs` and mapping the errors as `decide` does. A page names a link's source key with `sources.SourceKey(l.Scraper, l.SourceID)`. A test of a forced attach attaches `o`, then calls `st.Attach(ctx, run2, true, []store.Observation{o})` and checks that the answer counts `Unchanged` 1 and that the link's `Run` is `run2`; it then attaches, forced, an observation of the same source key whose `Reading` differs, and checks `Changed` and `Linked` 1 and the new `Reading`. A test builds `store.Observation{Scraper: "fmcsa", SourceID: "1023916", Digest: "d1", Reading: sources.Reading{NameKey: sources.NameKey("Acme Trucking, Inc."), State: "MN", Zip: "55072", Name: "ACME TRUCKING INC"}}`, attaches it and a second with the same keys and another source id, and checks that `st.Links(ctx, id)` answers both on one candidate, the second with `store.LinkMatchKey`. A test of the EIN step attaches two observations whose readings carry `EINKey: sources.EINKey("411234567")`, `NameKeys: sources.NameKeys(names)` and different ZIPs, and checks that the second link is on the first one's candidate with `store.LinkEIN` and confidence 0.9; a test of an OSHA ITA site's EIN builds the two readings with `oshaita.New().Read` of rows whose `ein` facts are `010739420` and `10739420`, at different ZIPs, and checks the same. A test of a merge decides one with `st.Decide(ctx, store.Ruling{Kind: store.KindMerge, Candidate: s, Other: o, Why: why, UserID: u})` and checks that `st.Enrichments(ctx, s)` answers, before the moved results, one new result of `enrichers.KindLinkFacts` whose `Inputs` equal `enrichers.LinkFacts{}.Inputs` of the survivor's subject, built as `D16-enrichers` says, and that `st.GetFacts(ctx, o)` answers `store.ErrNotFound`. A test of a merge's pages adds pages to both with `st.AddPage(ctx, store.Page{Candidate: o, URL: url, Title: title, Text: text, Size: size, UserID: u1})`, records `st.Pages`, `st.CountPages` and each `st.GetPage(ctx, o, p.Number)` before the merge, and after it asks `st.GetPage(ctx, s, p.Number)` for each and `st.CountPages(ctx, o)`; a test of a split asks the same of the original and the new candidate. A test of a merge's campaign uses records uses on both with `st.AddCampaignUse(ctx, s, "camp_a", "", t1)`, `st.AddCampaignUse(ctx, o, "camp_a", k, t0)`, `st.RejectCampaignUse(ctx, o, "camp_a", reason, t2)` and `st.DeliverCampaignUse(ctx, s, "camp_a", t3)`, merges, and checks that `st.CampaignUses(ctx, o)` answers none and `st.CampaignUses(ctx, s)` one `store.CampaignUse` of `camp_a` with `Added` `t0`, `ProspectList` `k`, `Rejected` `t2`, `Reason` `reason` and `Delivered` `t3`, on that store and on one over a reopened path; a test of a split or an `exclude` asks `st.CampaignUses` of every candidate before and after. A test of the display attaches an FMCSA observation with `Phone: "3205550134"`, `Class: sources.ClassForHire` and `Cargo: []string{"genfreight"}` and a PPP observation sharing its match key with a later `Filed` and no phone, class or cargo, and checks that `st.GetCandidate(ctx, id)` answers the PPP reading's name and address with the FMCSA reading's phone, class and cargo.
