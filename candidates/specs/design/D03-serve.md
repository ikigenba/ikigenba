# D03-serve

The serve path: what happens between `cli.Run` being called with no arguments and the process being gone, the services file, the terms candidates serves on, the listener's life and the route table the handler keeps. It is scrapes' serve path with the pass engine in place of the run engine, the ZIP table loaded at the start, missing facts rows filled from the enrichments at the start, the website check's fetcher chosen at the start, the `prompts` client built at the start, and no sibling read path of its own: candidates is read by people and agents only, through its pages and `/mcp`. `internal/server` is scrapes', unchanged.

`D01-layout-and-run-seam` declares the run seam, the settings, the database path, the addresses and the refusal of a setting; `D02-cli` the exit codes; `D04-store` the store, the ZIP table's write, the facts row and its fill (`FillFacts`), and the prune; `D06-passes` the engine and everything the start and the stop do to passes: the settling before ready (`Engine.Recover`), the prune before ready and on each sweep, the sweep itself, and the drain (`Engine.Drain`); `D16-enrichers` the enrichers, `drive_minutes` among them, which reads the ZIP table; `D15-website-check` the website check, which fetcher it goes through, the stub, and the fetch `add_page` makes; `D07-events` what `/declarations` answers and what a delivery of `scrape.finished`, `cron.judge_candidates.fired` or a campaign event does; `D08-pages` the pages; `D09-trail` the trail and its secrecy; `D10-tools` the tools; `D11-on-a-space` the nginx fragment and the trust. appkit's `prompts` client (D27 of appkit) is installed as appkit states it.

Decisions cited by number are the decisions document that delivered candidates. "Site text decision N" is entry N of the decisions document of the site-text change, "brief decision N" entry N of the decisions document of the brief change, "website search decision N" outcome N of the decisions document of the website-search change, and "S N" entry DN of that change's decisions log. "Campaigns decision N" is entry N of the decisions document that delivered campaigns. "Funnel rename decision N" is decision N of the decisions document of the funnel rename, which gives what candidates stores, answers and shows the funnel's stage names, and "funnel rename log DN" entry DN of its delivery's decisions log.

## The services file

`IKIGENBA_SERVICES` names the services file. candidates uses six of its entries (decision 17, site text decision 21, campaigns decision 17): `scrapes`, whose socket the passes read through appkit's `scrapes` client; `prompts`, whose socket the passes and the `enrich` call reach through appkit's `prompts` client (D27 of appkit) to run the caller's prompt for each company of a `site_text`, `brief` or `website_search` pass (`D06-passes`, brief decision 14, website search decision 1); `events` and `telemetry`, appkit's sinks' (`D01-layout-and-run-seam`); `auth`, read afresh per request by the pages through `urls.Auth` (`D08-pages`); and `campaigns`, read afresh per request by the pages through `urls.Campaigns` only to build the link to a campaign's page (`D01-layout-and-run-seam`, `D08-pages`). The `campaigns` entry is optional: candidates builds no client from it and never calls campaigns, whose events reach it on the bus like any other, so with no such entry it serves as with one, every campaign link empty. The pages also read the entry `scrapes` afresh per request, through `urls.Scrapes`, for the links to scrapes' pages. `Run` reads the file once, when it starts to serve, and builds the `scrapes` client from the entry `scrapes` it finds then, or none (`passes.Config.Scrapes` nil) when there is no such entry; with no client, `ingest` is refused `unavailable` and a delivery of `scrape.finished` is answered as `D06-passes` states; a delivery of `cron.judge_candidates.fired` reads nothing from scrapes and needs no client. It builds the `prompts` client the same way, from the entry `prompts` it finds then, or none (`passes.Config.Prompts` nil) when there is no such entry; with no client, none of `site_text`, `brief` and `website_search` is ready, the three sharing the one client (brief decision 14, S 1), so an `enrich` of any of them with a target is refused `unavailable` and records no pass, as `website_check` is without a checker (site text decision 21, `D06-passes`), and everything else serves as with the client. `website_search` also confirms the site its run names through the website checker the engine is built with (`passes.Config.Website`, `D16-enrichers`, S 1), so with no checker it is not ready either and is refused the same way. Every request the `prompts` client sends carries the caller of the `enrich` call it serves, forwarded from the context the engine hands it (site text decision 7), and is recorded by the client as `sibling.called` (`D09-trail`). A changed `scrapes` or `prompts` entry therefore reaches the passes at the next start. `Run` hands the path to the handler as `web.Config.ServicesPath`. With `IKIGENBA_SERVICES` unset, naming no file, or naming one `services.Read` refuses, the start is not refused, and candidates serves as with a file holding no entry.

## The terms candidates serves on

candidates serves only on a listening socket it inherits, taken as `sd_listen_fds(3)` documents, and reports readiness with `READY=1` to `NOTIFY_SOCKET` as `sd_notify(3)` documents. It trusts `X-User-Id`, `X-User-Email` and `X-Request-Id` as nginx sets them, and trusts the MCP gateway to have forwarded them (decision 21, `D11-on-a-space`). Every request has an id: the one nginx or the caller set, or 32 lowercase hexadecimal digits appkit's telemetry middleware draws from `Process.Rand`.

The events app reaches candidates at its socket on `/events` and `/declarations`. candidates emits the two pass events and accepts exactly `campaign.member.added`, `campaign.member.rejected`, `campaign.piece.delivered`, `cron.judge_candidates.fired` and `scrape.finished` (`D07-events`), so it mounts appkit's `events.DeliveryHandler` over the engine's handlers and `events.DeclarationsHandler` over its emitter, built with `trail.Emits()`, and the same handlers. These two paths sit outside the identity gate, as scrapes' do; the fragment answers both 404 at the public name (`D11-on-a-space`).

## The route table

Every request goes through appkit's `telemetry.Middleware`. Then, by the path alone, compared by hand so that nothing is cleaned and nothing redirected, the bus's two paths, `/mcp` and `/_appkit/` on `URL.Path`: exactly `/events` and exactly `/declarations` go to the event bus's two handlers; every other request goes through `identity.Require`. Past the gate: exactly `/mcp` goes, through `events.Middleware`, to `tools.Dispatch` over two MCP servers, the main one, on which the eighteen tools of `D10-tools` are registered once when the handler is built and which answers everything else, and a legacy one holding only the tool a sibling of the previous release calls by its old name, which answers a `tools/call` naming it (`D10-tools`), so the gateway, listing from the main server, shows only the eighteen; a path beginning `/_appkit/` goes to `page.Static()`; and every other path to the page handler (`D08-pages`), which answers `/`, `/prospect-lists/<id>`, the old list path, which it redirects there (`D08-pages`), `/candidates/<id>`, `/candidates/<id>/pages/<n>` (a kept page's text), `/passes/<id>`, `/about`, `/tools` and, for everything else, the not-found page.

The handler is built from a `web.Config` carrying what the routes need: the banner source, the two MCP servers, the main one and the legacy one, the services file's path, the store, the engine, the writer, the emitter and the website checker, which `add_page` fetches through and which is nil when the run has none (`D01-layout-and-run-seam`, `D10-tools`).

A **read refusal** is the plain-text answer a page gives when the database cannot be read: a status, `text/plain; charset=utf-8`, the line `store.Unreachable` and a newline, no body on `HEAD`, as scrapes answers.

## The start

With `Args` empty, `Run` checks the settings (`D01-layout-and-run-seam`), then whether exactly one socket was passed in, with the diagnostics below. It removes the three `LISTEN_*` variables, takes descriptor 3, and opens the database with `db.Open` at `state/candidates.db`; a failure is `candidates: cannot open database state/candidates.db: ` and the reason, exit 1. It builds the store, the writer, the emitter, the scrapes client, the prompts client, the registry, the website checker and the engine, and records `service.started`, so that it is the first event of every run. The checker is the one `D15-website-check` selects: when `IKIGENBA_SANDBOX` is present, whatever its value, it fetches through the in-process loopback stub that `website.NewStub()` starts; otherwise it fetches through `Process.HTTP` when that is not nil; otherwise there is none, and the engine is built with `passes.Config.Website` nil. A stub that cannot start refuses the start with `candidates: ` and the error's text, exit 1, before `service.started` is recorded (`D15-website-check`). A checker's waits end on `Process.After`, the sweep's timer, and so do the engine's own waits, the result poll and the queue-full wait of a `site_text`, `brief` or `website_search` pass (`passes.Config.After`, `D06-passes`). Building the prompts client does no I/O and cannot fail (D27 of appkit), so an entry `prompts` whose socket nothing serves on refuses nothing at the start; the first `enrich` of `site_text` finds it out (`D06-passes`). Then it loads the ZIP table: the root package's `ZipDrive()` replaces the store's `zip_drive` table whole (`store.ReplaceZipDrive`, decision 9), so the table `drive_minutes` reads in a running candidates is always its release's, whatever an earlier release left (`D16-enrichers`). Then it fills the facts: every active candidate that holds an enrichment and has no facts row gets one built from its newest result of each kind (`store.FillFacts`, `D04-store`), so a database an earlier release wrote, or one whose rows were lost, answers its facts from the first request on. The start judges nothing, enriches nothing and calls prompts for nothing: judgments are written only by a judge pass, a full ingest or a ruling, an ingest pass judges nothing (`D06-passes`, `D13-judging`), and enrichments are written only by an enrich pass, a full ingest or a ruling (`D06-passes`, `D12-ingest`); the fill writes facts rows only, from results already recorded. Then the engine settles what a dead process left, failing every pass still `running` with `interrupted` (`Engine.Recover`), and the store prunes once, as `D06-passes` states. A ZIP load, a facts fill or a settling that fails, the database having failed under it, refuses the start with `candidates: ` and `store.Unreachable`, exit 1; a prune that fails does not, and the start goes on to ready (`D06-passes`). Then `Run` builds the handler, sends `READY=1`, and serves; the sweep waits on `Process.After(cli.SweepInterval)` between prunes (`D06-passes`).

## Stopping

When `ctx` is done (in the binary, on `SIGTERM` or `SIGINT`, the signal's name being the cause) `Run` stops the sweep, drains the engine as `D06-passes` states (a running pass is cancelled and failed `interrupted`; a prompts run a `site_text`, `brief` or `website_search` pass started is abandoned, not cancelled, and ends under prompts' own bound), and at the same time drains the server for `DRAIN_SECONDS`, waits within the same window for the emitter to deliver what it holds, shuts the writer down with the cause's text as `service.stopping`'s reason, shuts the emitter down, closes the stub when the run checks against one, and closes the database. From the drain deadline on, `Run` hands neither sink anything: an event recorded after it reaches standard error as appkit's `lost event` and `undelivered event` lines. A pass interrupted never makes the exit non-zero; a drain that runs out on requests is `candidates: stopped with <n> request(s) unfinished`, exit 1.

## Recorded decisions

- **No sibling read path.** Nothing reads candidates but people and agents (decision 1 names no consumer), so the route table has the pages, `/mcp`, `/_appkit/` and the bus's two paths, and no JSON path of its own; scrapes' read path has no counterpart. prompts reaches candidates only as an agent does: the agent inside a run calls `add_page` through the MCP gateway (site text decision 4), so it needs no path of its own either. A page's text is a page at `/candidates/<id>/pages/<n>`, behind the gate like every other page (site text decision 2).

- **The legacy tool goes through a dispatcher on `/mcp`, not a second path** (funnel rename decisions 6 and 17): appkit's `mcp.Server` lists every tool it holds and has no unlisted tool, and appkit is not changing for this, so `/mcp` hands a `tools/call` naming a legacy tool to a second server holding only that tool and everything else to the main one. candidates has one listener, so the socket a sibling uses cannot be told from the gateway's path; a caller with a credential could call the legacy tool directly, which is accepted, since it is unlisted and goes in the next release. The legacy server is built with a second call of `Process.MCP`, so it carries the same identity and telemetry as the main one.
- **The scrapes and prompts clients are built once, at the start**, from the entries the services file names then: appkit's `scrapes.NewClient` and `prompts.NewClient` (D27 of appkit) each take the entry as a value (`ClientConfig.Service`), and `D01-layout-and-run-seam` reads `IKIGENBA_SERVICES` once. The prompts client is appkit's rather than candidates' own (site text decision 21 left the choice open) because it is scrapes' client's twin, a sibling client over a services entry with the caller forwarded, and the kinds that follow `site_text` reuse it. A services file edited while candidates runs reaches the passes at the next start; the pages, which only build addresses, read it afresh per request as scrapes' pages read `auth`.
- **The ZIP table is loaded and the facts filled after `service.started` and before the settling**, in that order and in the settling's failure class (a failed prune is not one: it is retried at the next sweep, `D06-passes`): the database opened but a write failed, so the start is refused with `store.Unreachable` and the trail holds `service.started` alone, as scrapes refuses a settling that fails. Loading it before the settling means a pass the first delivery begins already derives drive minutes from the release's table; filling the facts before ready means the pages, the tools and the prospect lists read every candidate's facts from the facts row from the first request on (R-GBFU-DOJ9).
- **The start's answer to a failing database is described, not required.** Once the database has opened, a store failure is provoked only with `SetFailing(true)` on a handle the test hands in (candidates/AGENTS.md), and `cli.Run` builds its store over a handle of its own, so no permitted test can fail the ZIP load, the facts fill or the settling under `Run`. The guarantees are stated where the store is handed in: a failing store's `ReplaceZipDrive` answers `ErrUnreachable` and changes nothing (`D04-store`, R-OD8H-2EDG), as its `FillFacts` does (`D04-store`), and so does `Engine.Recover` (`D06-passes`, R-2PU0-C7JL); the refusal of the start that follows is this overview's, as scrapes leaves what its stream does after a failure undeclared.
- **A stub that cannot start is described, not required.** `website.NewStub()` fails only when the stub cannot listen on `127.0.0.1:0` or make its certificate, neither of which a permitted test can bring about (candidates/AGENTS.md), so the refusal is this overview's, as the start's answer to a failing database is. The stub starts with the rest of the checker, after the database opens and before `service.started` is recorded, so the trail of a start the stub refuses is empty and `service.started` stays the first event of every run that records one (R-43KS-69RF, `D15-website-check`).
- **No `prompts` entry serves everything but the prompts kinds** (site text decision 21, brief decision 14, S 1). The start is not refused and the engine is built with `passes.Config.Prompts` nil, so the three kinds that need prompts, `site_text`, `brief` and `website_search`, are `unavailable`, as `website_check` is with no checker, and ingest, judge, full ingest, the other kinds, the tools and the pages serve as before; a space without prompts still serves candidates.
- **The `campaigns` entry is read only for links** (campaigns decision 17). The candidate page links each campaign use to its campaign's page at campaigns, so the pages read the entry afresh per request as they read `scrapes` and `auth`; nothing at the start reads it, no client is built from it, and no request goes to its socket, because what candidates learns of campaigns arrives as the three campaign events on the bus (`D07-events`). A space without campaigns, or a services file without the entry, therefore serves candidates in full, the campaign links empty, and the start does not depend on campaigns being up.
- **A missing identity is `identity.Require`'s 500** on every route behind the gate, as in scrapes; the public name's 302 to sign-in is nginx's (`D11-on-a-space`).
- The bus's two routes are scrapes': outside the gate, inside the telemetry middleware, over the engine's handlers (`passes.Engine.Handlers`), with the emitter given to `events.DeclarationsHandler` because candidates emits as well as accepts.
- `web.Config` carries no clock, as scrapes' carries none, and no random source, which the store, the writer and the emitter hold; it carried `Now` while the pages and the tools computed effective numbers per request. Freshness is not judged at query time (decision 8, departed from; orchestrator decision): a judge writes each candidate's judgments with freshness measured at its own moment (`D13-judging`), and the pages and tools show stored judgments and the stored facts row, so the opinions they show are as fresh as the last judge and the facts as the last enrichment (`D04-store`). A prospect list's clock date is the store's (`store.Config.Now`, `D14-prospect-lists`), so the prospect list tools and the prospect list page add nothing to `web.Config`.
- **Only the sweep's waits stop at the stop.** `Process.After` is the sweep's timer, the website check's, for the pace between checks and each attempt's timeout, and the timeout of the fetch that confirms a `website_search` answer (`D01-layout-and-run-seam`, `D15-website-check`, `D16-enrichers`), and the engine's, for a `site_text`, `brief` or `website_search` pass's result poll and queue-full wait (`D06-passes`, brief decision 10, website search decision 1). The engine's drain begins only once `ctx` is done and ends an enrich or an `enrich_target` only when it reaches it, so a pass still working in that gap may start a wait; what the stop guarantees is that the sweep starts none (R-QR9U-4LFL), and the checker's and the engine's waits end with the drain's cancellation (`D06-passes`). Promising that no wait at all starts once `ctx` is done would need the engine to watch `Run`'s context, which only the drain cancels by design (`D06-passes`).
- **The stub is closed after the drain**, with the sinks and the database, so a check, or a fetch confirming a `website_search` answer, the drain has not yet ended finds its stub still serving (`D15-website-check`).
- The settings, the database path and the sweep interval are `D01-layout-and-run-seam`'s; the settling, the prunes, the sweep and the drain's effect on passes are `D06-passes`'; this document states only the order of the start's steps, the ZIP load, the refusals of the start, and the stop's effect on the process.

## Consumer tasks

`Run`, serving, calls `s, err := settings.Read(p.LookupEnv)`, checks the socket, unsets the three `LISTEN_*` variables and takes `ln, _ := p.Inherit(3)`; opens the database (`D01-layout-and-run-seam`); builds `st := store.New(d, store.Config{Now: p.Now, Rand: rnd})`, the writer `w`, the emitter `em` with `trail.Emits()`, `sc` from `services.Read(path)` and `scrapes.NewClient` (nil without an entry `scrapes`), `pc` from the same list and `prompts.NewClient(prompts.ClientConfig{Service: e, Telemetry: w})` when `list.Find("prompts")` finds `e` (nil otherwise), `reg := sources.New(fmcsa.New(), ppp.New())`, the checker `ck` as `D15-website-check` selects it (`&website.Checker{Client: stub.Client(), After: p.After}` over `stub, err := website.NewStub()` when `p.LookupEnv("IKIGENBA_SANDBOX")` reports present, a non-nil `err` refusing the start, `&website.Checker{Client: p.HTTP, After: p.After}` when `p.HTTP` is not nil, nil otherwise) and `eng := passes.New(passes.Config{Store: st, Sources: reg, Scrapes: sc, Prompts: pc, Telemetry: w, Events: em, Website: ck, After: p.After})`; calls `w.Ready()`; calls `st.ReplaceZipDrive(bg, candidates.ZipDrive())`, `st.FillFacts(bg)`, `eng.Recover(bg)` and `st.PrunePasses(bg, s.KeepPasses)`; builds `h := web.Handler(web.Config{Banner: p.Banner, MCP: p.MCP(w), LegacyMCP: p.MCP(w), ServicesPath: path, Store: st, Passes: eng, Telemetry: w, Events: em, Website: ck})`; sends `READY=1`; and calls `server.Serve(ctx, ln, h, s.Drain(), eng.Drain)`, closing `stub` with `stub.Close()` once it returns when there is one. A test of the handler builds `web.Handler` over a store on its own database, an engine over a `scrapes` client and a `prompts` client built on fakes' sockets and the same checker as `cfg.Website` (or none), a writer and an emitter over captures and a server from `mcp.NewServer`, serves it with `httptest`, and sends raw requests with the identity headers set or left out; it fails the store with `SetFailing(true)` on its handle. A test of the start drives `cli.Run` with a listener, a `NOTIFY_SOCKET` it binds, a `Now`, an `After` and a `Rand` it controls, `HTTP` nil and no `IKIGENBA_SANDBOX` unless it drives an enrich, a services file whose `scrapes` and `prompts` entries name fakes' sockets (or that leaves `prompts` out), and captures; seeds the database first through its own store (a pass left `running`, `done` passes past keeping, a ZIP table of its own, a candidate with a `website_check` result whose facts row it then deletes through `DB.Write`); waits for `READY=1`; drives `/mcp` with `mcp.Client` and `POST /events` with raw HTTP; cancels; and reopens the database. The campaign history needs nothing of the serve path either: `Run` builds nothing from the services file's entry `campaigns`, the three campaign events reach `eng.DeliverCampaign` through `eng.Handlers()` on the same `/events` route, and the candidate page builds each campaign's link with `urls.CampaignPage(urls.Campaigns(cfg.ServicesPath), u.Campaign)` (`D08-pages`). A test of it writes a services file whose `campaigns` entry names a Unix socket it listens on, and after `READY=1` posts a `campaign.member.added` naming a campaign and a candidate its own store seeded, checks the delivery is answered `ok`, calls `candidate` and gets `/candidates/<id>`, finds the campaign in the answer and its page link in the body, then checks that its listener accepted nothing. The prospect list tools and `/prospect-lists/<id>` need nothing of the serve path beyond what is built above: they reach the handler through `cfg.MCP` and the page handler over `cfg.Store`.
