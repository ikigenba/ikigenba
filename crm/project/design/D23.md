# Decision 23 — Adopt the suite lint contract (`root project/design/D30.md`) at tier `cheap`

**Decision.** This tree **adopts the suite lint contract** and registers at
tier **`cheap`**: a committed one-line `crm/.lint-tier` file containing
`cheap`. The contract's content — the tier model and ratchet, the marker
semantics, the cheap tier's rule list, the analyzers-skip-tests /
gofumpt-everywhere policy, the pinned tool, and the enforcement points — is
the umbrella's and is not restated here; the enforcing runner (`bin/lint`)
and its proof live in `bin` (`[proof: bin]`), so this tree carries no lint
test of its own. This Decision owns only the local facts: the marker exists,
says `cheap`, and the tree is clean at that tier.

The tier is deliberately `cheap`, not `strict`, although the tree currently
has zero findings at strict too: entering at strict was considered and set
aside by the operator — the suite's adoption procedure moves every tree
through `cheap` uniformly, and the ratchet (`root project/design/D30.md`)
leaves the strict flip open as a later, separate move.

**Rejected.**

- *Relying on silence-is-conformance with no local Decision.* Silence covers
  conforming to a contract's ambient semantics; registering at a tier is an
  affirmative act (a committed marker plus a conformance obligation) and gets
  its own visible citation-is-adoption Decision, the same treatment given to
  this tree's other adopted suite contracts.
- *Entering at `strict` now.* Operator call, above.
- *Minting local ids for the marker or the clean state.* The behaviors are
  the runner's, proven in `bin`; the local obligation is structural and its
  proof is the phase's deterministic exit condition.

**Verification.** A structural decision: no ids minted, none cited — the lint
contract carries no per-service ids (`root project/design/D30.md`, Rejected:
*Per-service proof ids*). The checkable local facts are the phase's:
`crm/.lint-tier` contains exactly `cheap`, and `bin/lint crm`
(run from the repo root, a read-only check over this tree) exits 0.
