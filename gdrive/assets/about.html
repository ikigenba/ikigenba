How it works

gdrive moves files between Google Drive and artifacts for your agents. An agent finds a file with search, list or show, then pull copies it into an artifact, and push copies an artifact into Drive.

pull and push record a transfer and answer at once. The bytes move on this host in the background, streamed between Drive and artifacts as you, and transfer shows the outcome. When it is done, the artifact's url is where a shell fetches the result.

No byte and no credential passes through the agent: the tools answer references and records only, and gdrive gets a short-lived Google token from connections on the host.

Connect Google at connections first. Until you do, or while the connection needs reconnecting, every tool refuses with the link to connect, for the agent to hand you.
