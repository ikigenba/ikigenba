The panel re-fetches its table every five seconds and sends If-None-Match, so an unchanged set costs a 304 and nothing else. Press Ctrl K to jump to a service, or ? for every shortcut.

$ curl -si https://dummy.acme.ikigenba.com/widgets/table \
    -H 'If-None-Match: W/"3a9f-6"'
HTTP/2 304
etag: W/"3a9f-6"
# unchanged: the page keeps its table
