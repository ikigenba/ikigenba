candidates

Candidate businesses resolved from the records scrapes collects, one per business with every record that describes it as evidence, judged on whether it operates today and how big it is, and read through cohorts, the saved searches the owner grooms.

Cohorts

The space's cohorts, by name. A cohort is a saved search, its filters, order, size and rejects; its members are worked out each time its page is read. Agents make and change cohorts with the tools.
