Banner

The banner every app emits: the favicon and Ikigenba, one link to home; a hairline; the breadcrumb trail, the service's own icon and name linking to its landing page, a chevron that opens the service's pages, then each deeper level, the last unlinked and darker; then at the right edge two icon buttons: the profile, sign out. Every service is a tile on home, where the mark leads; the banner holds no service launcher. The page's own banner is it: its chevron opens the page menu under the trail.

Landing page

The root of the trail: the service alone, with its chevron. At 375px the product name has gone and the favicon stays as the home link.
