MCP tools

Agents manage triggers with these tools, through the MCP gateway's call and mutate.

list
Every trigger in the space, by slug.
show
One trigger, with its schedule, its owner, and when it last fired and fires next.
create
Create a trigger that emits an event on a schedule.
update
Change the schedule of a trigger you own.
pause
Stop a trigger you own from firing.
resume
Start a paused trigger you own firing again, from its next slot.
delete
Delete a trigger you own.
