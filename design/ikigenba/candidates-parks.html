Rules

The rules the parks are derived by. A parcel counts as industrial when its county's use class is one of the county's labels; in a county with no labels it counts when it is not a homestead and its building is worth more than the threshold, or when its address matches a candidate's. Industrial parcels within the distance of one another form a park when there are at least the minimum number of them. Agents change the rules with the tools, and the parks are derived again.
